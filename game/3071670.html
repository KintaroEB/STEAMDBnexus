About this gameFull description from Steam+
Escape into a world of vibrant colors and pixelated charm with Pixel Cross Stitch Color by Number. Choose from a vast collection of delightful images and bring them to life with your artistic touch. Whether you prefer the classic color-by-number approach or the textured beauty of cross-stitch, there's a style for everyone. Features: 100+ Pixel Images: Discover a diverse library of captivating pixel art, from adorable animals to breathtaking landscapes. Color by Number & Cross Stitch: Choose your preferred coloring style and experience the joy of creating pixel masterpieces. Colorblind Mode: An inclusive feature ensuring everyone can enjoy the game, regardless of color vision differences. Relaxing & Rewarding: Unwind with soothing gameplay and the satisfaction of seeing your creations come to life. Perfect for All Ages: Enjoy a creative outlet that's accessible and enjoyable for both kids and adults. Let your imagination run wild and create pixel art that sparkles with color!
