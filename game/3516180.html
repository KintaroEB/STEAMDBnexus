About this gameFull description from Steam+
Plot/Characters [ Don't FOOL ME, Beauties! ] is an FMV dating simulation game where you get to experience unique love stories with four captivating heroines. You, the player, take on the role of Hanbin, a new employee at a luxury hotel, where you’ll spend mischievous and unforgettable moments with four gorgeous and cold-hearted ladies ladies. Can you melt their cold hearts and, in the end, win one of them over? The CEO’s cold-hearted, prideful granddaughter: Ga-eun "Why on earth should I hire someone like you?" A playful senior at work who keeps you wondering if she’s serious: Ah-young "From now on, you’ll wash my baby clean with this toothbrush every day. Got it?" A hotshot influencer and charismatic model: Bit-na "Can’t you even take a proper picture?" An otaku manga artist and your shameless childhood friend who makes you do her laundry: Ji-woo "Since you're doing laundry, can you do mine too?" Which of these irresistibly charming heroines will you manage to win over? One wrong move, and it’s a BAD END... But play your cards right, and you might just catch a glimpse of their sweeter side! Key Features High-Immersion First-Person Dating Simulation Step into the shoes of Han-bin and experience the story entirely from his perspective. Talk, choose, and navigate complex relationships with four captivating heroines. Every decision you make can change the course of the story, leading to sweet romance, unexpected twists, or even a BAD END. Your choices shape the story, so play multiple times to discover every possible ending! Long Playtime, Rich Storyline Each chapter is packed with hidden epilogues, bonus videos, and collectible CG rewards to satisfy completionists. Scattered BAD ENDs throughout the game add tension and spice, making the experience of this dating simulation even more thrilling and immersive. Beautiful Cinematics & Romantic Comedy From lighthearted teasing to heart-pounding moments, this game is filled with scenes designed to keep male players entertained. The heroines will constantly challenge and provoke you, making every interaction unpredictable and fun. Collaborating with professional filmmakers, we’ve captured each moment with stunning visuals that bring their world to life. Four Heroines, Four Irresistible Charms Meet four unique heroines, each with their own personality and irresistible appeal. Whether it's the fiery tsundere, the mischievous troublemaker, the mature beauty, or the girl-next-door type. They won’t make it easy for you, and that's exactly why you’ll want to keep playing. So, who will you manage to win over in the end? About Us Hello! We’re Storytaco, an interactive game developer. We’re excited to greet the Steam community once again with our latest FMV game! We’ll keep creating games that spark your imagination and keep you entertained. So enjoy every moment with the heroines who love to tease you nonstop! Thank you, as always, for your valuable feedback and ongoing support.
