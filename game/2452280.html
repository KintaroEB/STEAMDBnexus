About this gameFull description from Steam+
After the Catastrophe, humanity harnessed the power of Corite—a mysterious carbon-silicon mineral, driving rapid recovery. But its fatally pathogenic nature and relentless spread threaten to consume Earth's last habitable spaces within decades. Should humankind resist? Adapt? Escape? Or follow Corite's call and forge a new path? Unveil the secrets of Corite and safeguard the future! Join S.H.A.D.O.W., and deploy your BREAK Strikers now! Choose from attackers, brawlers, snipers, defenders, or support. Battle at breakneck speed across three action-packed modes: Ace Arena (3v3), Operation VERGE (6v6), and Operation STORM (PvPvE). Evade beams, weave through explosions, and dominate the battlefield! 3v3 or 6v6 Dive into the 3v3 Ace Arena, where victory justifies any cost, or dominate the objective-based 6v6 Operation VERGE, where skill and strategy turn the tide! Brace for adrenaline-fueled battles featuring lightning-fast maneuvers and explosive firepower. Engage in close quarters with a battle-axe, halberd, or laser sword, mastering blocks and deadly parries. Prefer ranged combat? Snipe from the shadows, blast through defenses with energy beams, or rain down destruction with a missile barrage. PvPvE Extraction Go solo or squad up with three mechs to infiltrate the massive, Corite-rich Mashmak Islands and compete against rival teams for resources. Charge into action with your BREAK Strikers—seize supplies, take down colossal targets, infiltrate restricted zones, and annihilate enemy forces. With Pulse Storms spreading fast, it's a race against time. Complete your objectives, upgrade your arsenal with special materials, and return safely! Customization Make your mark on the battlefield with fully customized mechs and pilots! Choose from an endless array of colors, insignias, patterns, and accents to craft your unique mecha masterpiece. These aren't just war machines—they're the embodiment of your spirit! Characters Explore your base and uncover its secrets. Unlock and team up with elite pilots, each with a rich backstory, crucial to humanity's survival. Prepare to fight with your allies and forge a new future!
