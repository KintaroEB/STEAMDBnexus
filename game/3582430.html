About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome aboard Uh Oh Airlines: the delightfully unhinged single player or co-op flight attendant simulator where the skies are friendly, the passengers are feral, and disaster is always cruising at 30,000 feet. Play solo or team up with a friend as you juggle spilled sodas, mysterious smells, and passenger meltdowns, while trying to keep everyone (including yourself) from spiraling into full airborne anarchy. Serve drinks and soothe panicked flyers while pretending you aren’t also mildly panicked. Wrestle open overhead bins that seem to defy the laws of physics, dodge projectile dogs and cats, and clean up mystery liquids. Was it soda? Something worse? Don't ask us! Try not to think too hard about that while still thinking on your feet, because in this full-throttle frenzy, every second counts, and the seatbelt sign is basically just a suggestion. Catastrophes are coming in hot, sometimes literally. You could rise to the occasion and douse the flames (both metaphorical and real)… or just let it all burn in glorious chaos. But, uh, maybe grab the fire extinguisher. Just a thought. Whether you’re flying solo or teaming up with a friend, one thing’s for sure: things are about to get bumpy. Tray tables up and buckle up. This is your captain speaking: good luck. Have any questions? Check out our FAQ's Disclaimer! Thank you for supporting this project. Uh Oh Airlines is currently incomplete and in the alpha phase of development. Please help me get the game to beta by submitting bug reports either via the in-game feedback form or by joining the Discord server. Keep in mind that the game may contain bugs at this stage, so we recommend joining if you’re excited to be part of the journey and don’t mind a little turbulence along the way.
