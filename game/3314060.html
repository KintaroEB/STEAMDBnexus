About this gameFull description from Steam+
The original game you know and love. For Windows 10/11. Create an entire neighborhood of Sims, and run or ruin their lives. Help them pursue careers, make friends, and find romance, or see what happens when you let chaos unfold! Open-ended gameplay gives you the freedom to set your own goals and chart your Sims’ destinies. It is your neighborhood, and they are your Sims. Whether they prosper or perish is completely up to you. The Sims Legacy Collection includes: The Sims As well as the following packs: The Sims Livin’ Large The Sims House Party The Sims Hot Date The Sims Vacation The Sims Unleashed The Sims Superstar The Sims Makin’ Magic
