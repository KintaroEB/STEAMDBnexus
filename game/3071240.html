About this gameFull description from Steam+
Voodoo Fishin’ is a 1–6 player online co-op creepy-cozy fishing game featuring proximity voice chat and swamp-dwelling surprises. Venture into the bayou with friends in search of rare and legendary fish. The deeper you go, the stranger things get. Fish together. Laugh together. Try not to lose your haul. If you die, the swamp keeps your catch. (And any Tackle you have equipped) Craft your puppet angler and hit the dock! It’s chill… until it isn’t. This swamp hunts back. If you make it back alive, the Fish Monger pays by the pound. Upgrade your Bait, Reels, and Rods to chase bigger and rarer fish Play Forever The swamp doesn’t run on corporate servers. Voodoo Fishin’ is self-hosted, so once you’re in, you can always jump back in with friends. No server shutdowns. No “service discontinued.” The swamp stays open as long as you can stay alive. The demo provides access to the full core gameplay systems, but limits world exploration and higher-tier Tackle.
