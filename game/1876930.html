About this gameFull description from Steam+
Start by running a saloon and carve your own place in the Wild West. Shape your bar, hire help, and get drawn into the lives and troubles of the people who gather around your counter. Out here, honest business is rarely enough. See what it takes to survive the frontier. Run a working saloon from behind the counter. Mix drinks, cook meals, and keep supplies moving as the town slowly comes to life. It all starts as honest work, but never stays simple for long. Shape the look of your saloon, clean up after long nights, and decide how much work you take on yourself. Hire staff to keep things running and turn the place into something the town recognizes as yours. The Wild West rarely plays fair. Fights break out, bets are placed, and tempers flare. Survival often depends on how well you handle pressure and chaos. And you may bet, those two things are more than certain. Cross the Line even further… Sometimes, honest work is not enough. In Blueberry, opportunities appear in the gray areas of the frontier. What you choose to do when they surface is a different kind of business altogether. Every face at the bar has a story. Get involved in the lives of Blueberry’s residents and watch personal tales unfold. Funny, tense, and sometimes dark. Your saloon is the hub for all good stories. Core mechanics Saloon customization - personalize your saloon with furniture sets, bar equipment, decorations, color tints, and functional upgrades. Serve Food & Drinks - Prepare meals, mix drinks, and influence customer moods through hands-on bar mechanics. Quest System - Meet unique characters with their own stories, ambitions, and problems that become part of your journey. Criminal Opportunities - Explore the darker side of the Wild West and choose how far you are willing to go. Saloon & Business Management - Expand with gambling tables, rooms for rent, staff hiring, and side ventures. Reputation System - Earn trust, unlock rewards, and watch the town react to your growing influence. Frontier Activities - Encounter side mechanics and unexpected moments that capture the spirit of the Old West.
