About this gameFull description from Steam+
💘Love n Life: Happy Student💘 At Reborn Entertainment, creativity drives everything we do as we’re committed to delivering fresh experiences. For us, fun isn’t just a goal, it’s the rule. ⭐ Main Features Experience dating through the unique Inventory Management system with over 300 unique items. Complete the 5 main and 3 side girls' objectives to experience over 40 romantic moments with over 100 beautiful illustrations. Beautiful girls come to life with Live2D animations. Massive story content and secrets to explore, with more than 200 events. Mini-games: Cooking, fishing, Sports, reading, and more. Stay connected using social apps, texting, collections, taking photos, and a music player on your phone. ⭐ Innovative Gameplay For the first time ever, inventory management meets dating-sim in a bold fusion that delivers a truly unique experience. Your items aren’t just for show. They shape how you engage in every conversation and win hearts, offering you a sensational experience like no other. Each person you encounter brings unique skills and persuasive strategies. Be prepared to adapt and respond to every challenge. Master one of the traits, Confidence, Charm, Kindness, or Knowledge, for great replayability. Each unlocks over 300 powerful items with rich stats. Experiment, combine, and create thrilling builds for endless strategic fun. This addictive core gameplay promises countless hours of entertainment. ⭐ Story You're a third-year college student living in the suburbs. After taking a competitive exam, you’re lucky enough to transfer to the prestigious Lingyun University, located on a small island not far from the mainland coast. The university boasts cutting-edge technology and frequently secures major project contracts with powerful corporations. The school itself also holds many secrets and hidden dramas waiting to be uncovered. ⭐ Romance You're here to gain valuable knowledge… right? But don’t be surprised when the ladies can’t resist your charm. With over 40 romantic encounters, each more tempting than the last, you’ll find yourself drawn into sensational moments far more thrilling than textbooks. And if you’re daring enough to dig deeper, you might just uncover some hidden pleasures they don’t teach in class. ⭐ Social Activities Beyond dating, master your lifestyle with activities like sports, fishing, art, and more. Level up your stats and use your new skills to impress the girls and unlock deeper intimacy. ⭐The ladies: 💘 Liu Yating - College Student You're in the same class as a beautiful, carefree girl who loves to explore new things. She’s destined to become your best friend. 💘 Chen Meiling - Literature Professor The literature teacher at your school and the dorm manager where you live. With her sweet, kind, and playful personality, she’s the teacher every student loves having in class! 💘 Qin Yilin - Student Council President "Only knowledge and discipline create elite students. Without them, you're dust." The Student Council President is cool, elegant, and well-organized. She’s determined to push every student to reach their highest potential—no matter what it takes! 💘 Luna-3X - Virtual Assistant She manages your schedule and informs you of important tasks that need to be done. She also helps with things better left unseen… all with a very glitchy personality. 💘 The Cafe Owner She is your safe haven, offering a space of peace and sanctuary where you don’t have to worry about all the pressure a man is expected to carry on his shoulders. 💘 And Other Mysterious Ladies The island’s full of surprises and the girls are the biggest ones. They’ll charm you, confuse you, maybe even scare you a little... all while dragging you into their delightful mess of mystery and mayhem. Your life’s not just getting turned upside down, but it will get a lot crazier than that...
