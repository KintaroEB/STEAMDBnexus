About this gameFull description from Steam+
Survive the Wild West and Build Your Legacy Step into the unforgiving world of the Wild West. In Westland Survival, you’ll build and expand your own ranch, tame fierce beasts, craft powerful weapons, chase outlaws, and take part in intense PvP showdowns—all in a world teeming with danger, opportunity, and mystery. Build and Expand Your Ranch Establish your homestead from the ground up. Gather resources, craft workbenches, and fortify your property. Grow crops, rear livestock, and turn your ranch into a true frontier stronghold. Tame, Breed, and Fight with Pets Hunt wild beasts, tame them, and turn them into loyal companions. Breed animals at your ranch to unlock stronger perks and build your ultimate pack—whether it's wolves, bears, or other fierce allies. Every predator you meet at the prairies can be your ally—even alligators! Raise and Ride Your Horses Build stables and breed horses with unique traits. Whether you need speed for swift travel or strength for hauling wagons, there’s a wide variety of types and colors to choose from. Craft Weapons and Gear Survive with a handcrafted arsenal. Forge everything from classic revolvers to durable armor. Discover rare blueprints to improve and customize your loadout to fit your playstyle. Engage in PvP and Join Alliances Prove your mettle in duels and clan wars. Join forces with other players in alliances, compete in ladders like Gold Rush, and dominate the Wild West! Explore Mystical and Dangerous Frontiers This land holds more than outlaws—it echoes with the ancient legends of the Indigious, now brought to life. Venture into mysterious locations like the Canyon Trials and the Spirit Cave, where facing mythical forces grants powerful rewards. Uncover a Rich and Twisting Storyline The West holds secrets beyond survival. Follow a gripping storyline filled with frontier justice, ancient mysteries, and personal revenge. Meet unforgettable characters, investigate the disappearance of your sister, and face choices that shape your path through a lawless land. Dive into Regular Events and Challenges Experience rotating in-game events, time-limited challenges, and global activities that keep the frontier full of fresh content and rewards. Answer the call of the Wild West. Build your ranch, ride into battle, and become a legend.
