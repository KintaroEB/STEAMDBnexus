About this gameFull description from Steam+
ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE: Destiny and Strategy Expansion Pack Bundle Note: This bundle includes the products ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE and ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE: Destiny and Strategy Expansion Pack. If you have already purchased ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE, please purchase ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE: Destiny and Strategy Expansion Pack instead of this bundle to avoid making duplicate purchases. A boundless Three Kingdoms experience. To celebrate the series' 40th anniversary, we have improved the quality of and added new content to the "Play as All Officers" feature of ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE, which allows you to play as any officer in the world of the Three Kingdoms. Enjoy the game to the fullest with new elements like Turning Points and Gems, new events, and a new Chinese voice option. • Turmoil and tension abound in the new Turning Point system. Experience new gameplay through various Turning Points, each with their own far-reaching effects. You will need to predict which Turning Points are likely to occur and employ appropriate strategies to work alongside or counter them. • Gems based on status and reputation result in a variety of play styles. Enable a range of possible actions through different Gem types based on your current status. You will have to consider each Gem's effects to decide which to unlock and use for your officer's development. • New features and data make it easier to customize your play experience. We have added the highly requested Chinese voice option and made all cutscenes fully voiced. We have also enhanced the data for Traits, historical events, and Official Titles, and have implemented in-game editing and Registered Specialties features. ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE Theater of war, infinite drama. A masterpiece of historical simulation "ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8" is revived after 20 years. This version includes an improved "All Officers Play" feature, which allows players to play as any officer in the world of the Three Kingdoms. The game is also packed with additional elements that bring the officers to life, such as "Destiny" which can dramatically change the course of events and "Tales" which offer a variety of stories to choose from. Combining 2D and 3D technologies, the game features both new and nostalgic visual effects, as well as many new officer graphics created specifically for this title. Enjoy the greatest military drama in the Three Kingdoms series, with the largest number of officers and scenarios! ■Every officer can be the hero! The "All Officers Play" of ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS VIII returns. More than 55 scenarios spanning over 80 years and featuring the largest number of officers in the series, from rulers to free officers. Play through a vast, deep, and turbulent Three Kingdoms period as you choose. ■Vivid depiction of the drama between officers. A variety of fascinating new features! Many new features have been added to portray a variety of human dramas, such as "Tales" in which players can choose which events unfold. In addition, you can be linked by "Destiny" with other officers, and gain special relationships. The relationship or "Links Forged" between each officer greatly affects how the gameplay unfolds. Changes in that relationship can be ascertained from the "Relationship Chart". ■Graphical enhancements. In addition to newly drawn officer graphics, the scenic beauty of the game is depicted using ink paintings, combining 2D visuals and 3D depth to create a beautiful visual experience of the world of the Three Kingdoms.
