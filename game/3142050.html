About this gameFull description from Steam+
The all-new S2.4: Raid for Riches is live now! The Void Abyss calls to the bold: fighters, treasure hunters, and explorers. If "high risk, high reward" is your creed, you'll leave its depths with coffers overflowing. Remember, only those who survive the Abyss carry their spoils home! Break a leg, Driftmaster! About the game Warborne Above Ashes is a top-down PvP loot-and-extract MMO. You are a Driftmaster roaming the galaxy. Time and again, you dive into the perilous Void Abyss in search of treasure and ever-greater power. Its inhabitants are hardly welcoming… but far more dangerous than the creatures are the hearts of men. Evacuate in one piece, trade your spoils, and grow ever stronger! MMO + DMZ: Claim what you can, then get out fast! High risk, high reward: Danger and opportunity lurk around every corner. Your gear, scavenging route, and battle tactics are all high-stakes bets. Remember: live, and you walk away with gold; die, and your fate turns cold. Mystery & Excitement: Fog blankets the land, hiding friend and foe alike. Each venture brims with danger, and endless possibilities. Multi-layered Maps: Charge into battle or slip unseen to plunder treasure—there's a place for every strategy. To Fight or to Fight, There Is No Question Endless Builds: Recruit over 40 unique Drifters, mix and match from a vast array of equipment and dozens of tactical items to forge a combat style all your own. Skill is Power: Master your skills, exploit terrain, and perfect your movement. Every decision can turn the tide. Bring out your A-game and fight! Scavenge and Arm Yourself to the Teeth Free Trade: A single global Auction House where every treasure you bring back can be exchanged for valuable rewards. Grow with Every Extraction: A diverse tech tree lets you turn every treasure you recover into raw, unbridled power.
