About this gameFull description from Steam+
Oaken Tower is a synergy-heavy PvP auto battler where your tower of items fights other players. Build deadly combos and scale exponentially. Every decision matters. Buy and combine powerful items, unlock game-changings perks and level up to discover rarer items, and build the ultimate tower. KEY FEATURES: Asynchronous roguelike PvP Over 120+ unique items with class specific types Gameplay progression through unlockable perks and cosmetic items 20+ game-changing NPC encounters Deeply synergistic item system with exponential combo potential Casual and Ranked gameplay modes with leaderboards and seasons Endless Mode CHASE THE PERFECT COMBO Discover synergies between items that don't just add up; they multiply! The right combination of items and unlockable perks can create exponential scaling, enabling you to build unstoppable loadouts. CASUAL AND RANKED MODES Jump into casual mode for a pressure-free experience or rise to the challenge of competitive ranked play and see how your build stacks up against the best towers.
