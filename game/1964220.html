About this gameFull description from Steam+
Adventure through the magical world of MergeCrafter! Check out this puzzle merge game for free and meet characters as you quest through the mysterious lands of Runaria, where you can merge everything into better and more powerful items for your magical journey. Runaria Needs You! Merging Manage and Merge - boost production and merge everything into better and more powerful items full of magic for your journey in Runaria! Merge 3 Merge 5 for bonus items Create MEGA MERGES with 9+ items Chain Merge for bonus XP Crafting and Tools Harvest resources with powerful tools. Become the Crafting Expert of Runaria. Grow Trees from Acorns and chop them down. Don't be a pain in the axe! Reforge Pickaxes to mine ores for Iron, Gold, and Diamond Dig up buried treasures hidden deep in Runaria with shovels Biomes Discover unique biomes and assist your friends in Runaria by completing quests Discover and heal all concealed land Meet charming characters Enchant epic Axes, Pickaxes, and Shovels Explore Dungeons Explore a variety of different dungeons in Runaria. Going through the Shady Forest, deeper into the Dark Caverns, and explore the Arid Sands of the Pyramid
