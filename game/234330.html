About this gameFull description from Steam+
MARVEL Puzzle Quest is the ultimate Match 3 Super Hero RPG experience! Collect and level up your favorite characters to discover incredible strategic depth like no other Match-3 has to offer! Become a puzzle combat master as you learn to harness superpowers to bend the gem board to your will. Start your journey to defeat super-powerful cosmic threats to save the universe! Join over 40 million players in deep strategic puzzle combat. Recruit 375+ Super Heroes and Super Villains from across the Marvel Universe, master unique superpowers, and harness the ultimate power of the new 6-Star tier! COLLECT + UNLOCK YOUR FAVORITE MARVEL HEROES - Complete your comic collection and forge an unstoppable team UPGRADE + MAXIMIZE YOUR TEAM'S POWER - Continuously level up your roster to take on bigger challenges FIGHT IN A TACTICAL MATCH-3 BATTLEFIELD - Every move is a tactical decision in this action packed match-3 combat system. MASTER HEAD-TO-HEAD PVP + ENDLESS CHALLENGES - Take part in constant, high-stakes events throughout the year. Assemble your legendary Super Hero roster and master the gem board! Harness iconic Marvel powers and outwit foes in engaging, tactical, puzzle combat COLLECT + UNLOCK YOUR FAVORITE MARVEL HEROES Complete your comic collection and forge an unstoppable team with characters and artwork pulled directly from Marvel Comics history. Recruit 375+ Heroes: Collect original Marvel Comic Covers to instantly unlock and recruit icons like Spider-Man, Captain America, and Doctor Doom. Build Your Ultimate Roster: Collect heroes and villains from across the Marvel Universe, from street-level Wolverine to the cosmic threat of Thanos. UPGRADE + MAXIMIZE YOUR TEAM'S POWER Continuously level up your roster to take on bigger challenges. Make Your Heroes Stronger: Use collected Covers to level up and improve your favorite characters. Every new Cover you add increases their unique Powers, turning a starting hero into a true powerhouse! Master the Strategy: Focus on the strategic pairing of heroes and chase Covers for the latest and rarest 5-Star characters and beyond. Unlock their full potential and dominate your opponents across the game's many modes. FIGHT IN A TACTICAL MATCH-3 BATTLEFIELD The board is your battlefield. Every move is a tactical decision in this action packed match-3 combat system. Unleash Superpowers: Match 3 or more gems to generate Attack Power and fuel your hero’s unique abilities. Deploy powerful skills to deal massive damage or manipulate the board in your favor. Strategic Depth: This is far beyond simple matching. Master team synergies and tactical planning to secure victory. MASTER HEAD-TO-HEAD PVP & ENDLESS CHALLENGES Take your newly assembled Marvel dream team and prove their strength by taking part in constant, high-stakes events throughout the year. Climb the Leaderboards: Battle friends and rivals in seasonal tournaments. Dominate in the newly re-scaled S.H.I.E.L.D. Clearance Levels (SCL), with competitive top tiers designed to test even the strongest teams. New Content Daily: Take on ‘Deadpool’s Daily Challenge’ and many other limited-time story events for epic rewards. New characters, costumes, and major updates are added constantly. START YOUR MARVEL MATCH-3 RPG ADVENTURE TODAY! There’s no time to waste—install now to unleash your inner super hero and save the day!
