About this gameFull description from Steam+
Game made by 1 person without million-dollar funding. Please be patient, we will improve over time <3 Empire Eleven is a premier football management simulator where you take total control of your club, stepping into the shoes of a Manager or even the President/CEO. From tactical decisions on the pitch to high-level financial strategies off it, every choice dictates the success or failure of your organization. Key Features: CEO/President Mode: A deep and complex management system. Oversee club finances, set ticket prices, negotiate sponsorship deals, and much more. Multi-Club Ownership: Create and manage your own group of clubs to dominate multiple leagues simultaneously. Wealth Progression: Your salary as a Manager isn't just a number. Reinvest your earnings to buy Clubs, Stadiums, and even become a Sponsor yourself. Infinite Replayability: Thousands of stories waiting to be told—from leading a bottom-tier side to the heights of the global elite. Experience the match through a specialized 2D tactical view. Be prepared for every moment of the game. Master the pitch. And everything beyond it. Designed to be mechanically complex yet relaxing in execution, Empire Eleven allows you to dive into spreadsheets and financial projections without the pressure of punishing timers. It is pure strategy, distilled into a clean and intuitive interface. Total President Mode: Control the club’s destiny far beyond the touchline. Negotiate multi-million dollar sponsorships, manage stakeholder expectations, and dictate the commercial direction of the franchise. Stakeholder Management: Keep investors satisfied and board members informed. Your financial decisions shape the market’s confidence in your project. World-Class Infrastructure: Design and expand your stadium and centers of excellence. Every upgrade directly impacts revenue and youth development. Customizable Club Culture: Define your team’s philosophy. Will you be a talent-producing powerhouse or a buyer of global superstars? Transform a regional club into a commercial juggernaut. Manage everything from match-day pricing to global naming rights contracts. Strategic Transfer Market: Buy low, sell high. Use data analytics to find undervalued talent and maximize ROI (Return on Investment) with every sale. Deep Financial Ecosystem: A realistic economic model where cash flow is as vital as the scoreline. Navigate carefully through Financial Fair Play regulations! Take total control of the footballing world with our powerful In-Game Editor. Shape the beautiful game exactly how you envision it: Complete Customization: Build your own leagues, create custom clubs, and mold players from scratch. Master the Database: Control every aspect of the football ecosystem with ease. Datapacks & Community: Create your own custom Datapacks and share them with the world! Mod Hub: Join our community and download content from our dedicated Modding Hub, available on our official website. Your legacy starts in the front office. Build more than just a team; construct an institution that stands the test of time. From financial ledgers to the top of the podium, the fate of the club is in your hands. Do you have what it takes to become the greatest CEO in football history? Master the pitch. And everything beyond it. Add to your Wishlist now and start planning your dynasty.
