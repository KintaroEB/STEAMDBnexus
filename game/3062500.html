About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Adventurers is a turn-based tactical RPG set in a rich, high-fantasy world inspired by classic tabletop role-playing games like Dungeons & Dragons. Lead a diverse party of adventurers as they explore an ever-changing world, engage in strategic combat, and manage the complexities of adventuring life. Take on costly quests, battle mythical creatures, and discover many wonders, as you make a name for yourself in a world filled with opportunities and dangers. Turn-Based Tactical Combat Master the art of strategy by harnessing the unique abilities, spells, and skills of your adventurers. Outthink your enemies by using the terrain to your advantage. As the world evolves, so too must your tactics; adapt your strategies and fine-tune your party composition to face increasingly dangerous threats. Every encounter demands sharp thinking, clever planning, and the perfect blend of power and precision to conquer even the mightiest adversaries. Your Epic Saga Is Waiting To Be Written Venture into a dynamic world where every journey holds new surprises. Caravans brave perilous roads, desperately hoping to evade ruthless raiders, while dense forests and towering mountains hide both mythical creatures and untold dangers. Discover ancient ruins brimming with forgotten relics and bustling cities alive with intrigue, where secrets wait to be unearthed. Embark on quests that range from hunting down ferocious beasts and defending vulnerable villages to delving into shadowy conspiracies that could shake the realm. Each quest presents fresh challenges and rich rewards, pushing you closer to legendary status with every choice you make. Become Legends Forge your ultimate team of heroes by recruiting adventurers with distinct classes and powerful skills. As they grow stronger, unlock devastating abilities that can turn the tide of battle. Shape your party's strategy by customizing each character’s talents and attributes to craft a perfect blend of power and synergy. Keep your team in top form by managing their morale, health, and stamina—because every decision could mean the difference between victory and defeat as you prepare for thrilling adventures ahead.
