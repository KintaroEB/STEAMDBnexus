About this gameFull description from Steam+
A relaxing puzzle game in which you build a miniaturized city on a green island in the middle of a vast azure lake. Get away from your daily routine and immerse yourself in the world of URBO: Dream One. Watch your city grow house by house. This is just one of the worlds of URBO. Each one offers the opportunity to build mysterious cities in an atmospheric, dreamlike setting. Focus on the game when you feel your mind needs a rest. Follow our news via the link below, perhaps it is exactly URBO's realms that you need for inspiration... URBO: Dream One features a grassy island with a mystical Tower, willing to reveal its secrets to those who are wise. The strange monuments that are part of the landscape hint at the mystical origins of this realm. Look at the world from different angles, at different times of day. After all, the familiar always appears in a new light when night changes to day. In URBO: Dream One, the architectural style and color palette of your city perfectly blends in with the beauty of the surrounding world. Every house built, every lucky move will be seen by the Tower. Using its bonuses will add a nice touch to the gameplay, and you will build a truly prosperous city that many inhabitants will want to call home.
