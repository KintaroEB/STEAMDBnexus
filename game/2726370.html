Use Your Voice to Trick and Bite Talk your way into homes using your real voice. Choose your words carefully. Charm your way inside or blow your cover. Master the Art of Disguise Dress up as anything: a knight, a superhero, even your last victim. Use costumes and props to earn trust and fool your targets.
