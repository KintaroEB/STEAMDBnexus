About this gameFull description from Steam+
Experience the BAFTA award-winning story of Life is Strange now beautifully remastered with enhanced visuals and vastly improved animation using mocap technology. Play as Max Caulfield, a photography senior who discovers she can rewind time while saving her best friend Chloe Price from a violent altercation. The pair soon find themselves investigating the mysterious disappearance of fellow student Rachel Amber, uncovering a dark side to life in Arcadia Bay. Meanwhile, Max must quickly learn that changing the past can sometimes lead to a devastating future.

Content notice

The protagonist of Life is Strange Remastered, Max Caulfield, can rewind time at will. But her ability to prevent the futures she sees does not stop her from experiencing them, nor does it shield her from the emotional fallout of those events. This Game deals honestly with complex emotions, difficult themes, and rare but shocking acts of violence. As such, it may contain content not appropriate for all ages, and may not be appropriate for viewing at work, with cautions suggested for Intense Violence, Blood, Sexual Themes, Strong Language, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol.
