About this gameFull description from Steam+
This game is part of the Bahamut 30th Anniversary Indie Game Promotion Program. A twist of fate lands you in a charming plant shop called Carol's House, where you are unexpectedly hired by its mysterious owner, Carol. There, you share a home with three furry roommates—each with strikingly different personalities. Across three changing seasons and countless small happenings, profound bonds are forged among you. Trivia About You is a heartwarming, slice-of-life romance adventure game that chronicles the everyday happenings of roommates in Carol's House. Step into the pixel-charmed world of Carol's House, make choices that shape your relationships with furry roommates... Or will an unexpected choice change everything? Endless Little Stories Your unique choices shape the narrative flow, allowing you to discover entirely new scenes and stories. Rich events, charming surprises, and beautifully illustrated CGs make each playthrough fresh and memorable. Multiple Endings Build affection with any of the three distinct characters. The crucial decision you make at the turning point will lead the story. Who stays by your side, where your journey is going...will totally be determined by you. Pixel Objects to Discover More than just a visual novel! It's an open invitation to wander through Carol's House. Interact with pixel objects in the scene to uncover hidden stories and delicate details. Every seemingly ordinary pixel could be guarding a character's unspoken secrets. Charming Mini-Games Step into Carol's House and truly live the life in a plant shop! Engage in light, interactive tasks like watering, pruning, and greeting customers. Find a small sense of accomplishment and calm in the daily, soothing routine. Via An easygoing yet sincere gray wolf young man. Via is gentle, sensitive, and passionate about coffee and photography. He tackles his new job with eager dedication. With his arrival at Carol's House, a brand-new chapter of daily life begins to unfold. Kyle A sunshine-bright, straightforward tiger firefighter . Kyle is an outgoing, warm person who loves to chat and cook. He projects a bold exterior, eager to be the reliable "big brother," but his romantic views are surprisingly traditional. Beneath this straightforward facade, however, Kyle harbors a secret worry and anxiety, ignited the moment he realized a certain someone means more than he expected. Mick A quiet, reserved jackal student. Quiet and often struggling for words, Mick serves as the gentle, unassuming helper in the background of everyday life. He is timid, self-doubting, and lacking confidence, yet he notices everything. However when Mick meets someone he can truly trust and lean on, he finally finds the courage to dream and move forward. Shin A rational, cool-headed goat accountant. Sharp-tongued and aloof, Shin looks stingy and keeps everyone at arm's length. His life is defined by strict boundaries and personal principles. Only in those rare moments when his concern for someone slips out can you glimpse the genuine, gradually warming affection hidden behind the walls he's built. Carol A free-spirited and open-minded sheep who runs Carol's House Carol is the heart and soul of Carol's House. She is the owner of a plant shop and also a notably caring landlady. She enjoys drinking, acts on impulse, and is capable of dropping profound life advice into the most casual conversation.
