About this gameFull description from Steam+
Everwind is a novel take on the First-Person RPG and Sandbox Survival genre. Build a base on a flying ship, gather resources, craft, loot, and fight alongside your friends as you rise above the clouds to uncover the mysteries of this world. Your airship is truly your home in the skies. Expand it to your heart's desire: build walls, workstations, gardens, even whole towers! It's your flying fortress and the only limit is your creativity. Everything you build travels with you, so no more long treks back to base, ever. Play solo or in online co-op with up to 4 friends on one shared ship. One steers, one builds, one brews potions, one holds the line. Because every journey is better with friends! Personalize your character, adjust the facial features, select from a variety of hairstyles, and choose the skin tone that matches your liking! Take the helm and steer your ship yourself through an infinite, procedurally generated world of multiple unique biomes. Each with its own landscapes and distinct enemies. No two skies are ever alike, and there's always another horizon waiting. Visit 1,000+ hand-crafted points of interest, and delve into the procedurally generated dungeons that are never the same twice. The world bends to you - everything is fully destructible, so tear it down and reshape it to your vision. Dive into battle with a sword in one hand and a shield in the other. Fight through dungeons where fearsome bosses stand between you and the treasure. Choose from a wide range of weapons, from daggers and bows to powerful magical staves and enchanted books, to gain the edge against challenging enemies, or support your friends through the arcane art of alchemy.
