About this gameFull description from Steam+
Unlock full face tracking on your avatars with VRCFT (VRChat Face Tracking)--a powerful, modular desktop application that unleashes face-tracking support from multiple VR headsets and trackers. Simply install VRCFT, choose the module(s) that apply to your hardware, and use a Unified Expression enabled avatar in VRChat. Key Features Integrates seamlessly with VRChat using OSC Introduced the Unified Expression standard (142 supported blendshapes) Works with a wide variety of free, paid, and custom avatars Works with the Quest Pro, Vive Pro Eye, Vive Facial Tracker, and other popular hardware Easy setup and light footprint VRCFT is the go-to standard for adding face tracking to VRChat.
