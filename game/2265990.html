About this gameFull description from Steam+
ENDALOR is a challenging survival game with RPG elements for 1-4 players, set in an immersive medieval dark fantasy world. Explore, craft, fight, loot and above all - survive! THE WORLD Explore, scavenge and conquer a vast hand crafted world, full of secrets and unimaginable dangers. From the mountain peaks to the depths of a mysterious underground, the unfolding adventure is yours to shape. SURVIVAL Gather resources to not only make yourself stronger, but to keep yourself alive. With swinging, smashing and slashing the game presents an unforgiving stamina based combat system that tests even the most experienced warriors. Your skills will be tested by ever-greater foes and your power challenged by fearsome bosses. CONQUERING Build a camp to regain your strength, craft weapons, armor, workstations and more. Seek out rare and powerful artifacts, magic, and weapons to overcome ever greater threats.  Brave the journey alone, or gather a party of four friends to face the adventure together. Key Features A vast immersive hand crafted open world, filled with secrets and danger that is seamlessly connected from the huge mountains and towering castles to dark dungeons and even darker caverns, where every corner is filled with unique enemies and rare treasure. Play alone offline or create a party of 4 adventurers. Stamina based combat that tests your skills and lets you choose your own way of dealing with danger. Wield a dagger or a mighty war hammer, throw spears or cast magic. Earn experience and increase your strength. Gather resources provided by the land or take them form the bodies of your enemies, scavenge ancient dungeons and castles for loot and treasure. Build a camp to regain strength and protect your loot, grow and cook food, prepare for your next adventure. Prepare and survive mighty boss encounters.
