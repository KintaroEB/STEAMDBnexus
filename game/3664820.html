About this gameFull description from Steam+
Sandboxels is a falling-sand game featuring heat simulation, electricity, density, chemical reactions, cooking, and fire spread. With over 500 unique elements to play with, Sandboxels is a great simulator to pass the time and relax. Play the classic Sandboxels browser game on Steam with extra perks: Steam Workshop support - Browse and publish saves directly in-game Unlimited save file management - No more save slots! No advertisements Bigger screen space Rich Presence for Discord Support the developer! There are thousands of interactions between the many materials, plants, animals, fluids, and powders in the game. Experiment with chemistry, cook food, grow an ecosystem, and more. Simply click or use your touchscreen to select a category, like Liquids, then pick an element, like Water. Drag on the canvas above to place your pixels, and watch them interact with others!
