About this gameFull description from Steam+
Two geese. One fragile egg. Infinite chaos. Work together to carry it through unpredictable challenges without breaking it, every crack makes it harder, every mistake more hilarious. A co-op physics game about teamwork, clumsiness, and the unstoppable power of honking. Cracks = Chaos With every crack, the egg becomes harder to control and easier to lose. Each impact makes it wobblier, bouncier, and far more unpredictable. Handle it carefully, or watch it explode in a glorious mess. Not all is lost When the egg slips away, you can still recover it. Every fall is a chance to try again. Fragile, cracked, but never truly broken. Teamwork at its finest Pick up your useless partner, throw them back on track, and pretend you didn’t almost drop them on purpose. FEATURES One egg, endless chaos – Keep it safe (or try) through unpredictable physics and tricky obstacles. Cracks that change everything – Each hit makes the egg weaker and the game harder. Teamwork and betrayal – Lift, toss, honk, and sometimes sabotage your partner. Physics-fueled fun – Clumsy controls and goose energy combine for hilarious moments. A true test of patience – Rage, laugh, and celebrate every inch of progress.
