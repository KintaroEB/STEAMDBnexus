About this gameFull description from Steam+
Stay Out is a multiplayer role-playing game with shooter elements, immersing you in a dark and mysterious world where the spirit of stalkerism reigns. Here you will find abandoned territories full of dangers, mysteries and abnormal phenomena that defy nature itself. In this world, the deadliest threats come not only from anomalies or mutants, but also from people like you - desperate, hungry and unscrupulous. A world where every step is a challenge The Alienation Zone is not just a place, but a living, breathing organism with its own laws. Abandoned cities, secret laboratories, military bases and contaminated wastelands hide not only deadly threats, but also rare artifacts that can change your fate. Become one of the stalkers Survival in Extreme Conditions - Fight hunger, thirst, radiation, and disease. Beware of other players for whom you're an extra mouth, an unwanted witness, or just easy prey. Anomalies and Artifacts - Explore dangerous zones where the usual laws of physics are broken. Hunt for rare artifacts, but remember: anomalies do not forgive mistakes. Mutants and Mysterious Life Forms - Meet creatures born from unexplained phenomena. Some will hunt you down, others will become part of mysteries yet unsolved. Freedom of choice and competition for resources Character Development - Create a unique stalker by choosing your path: lone explorer, mercenary, merchant or faction leader. PvP and Interaction - Trust only those who deserve it. In the Zone, you can team up for survival or fight for valuable resources - it all depends on your decisions. PvE and Fighting the Zone itself - Infiltrate abandoned bunkers and scientific complexes where valuable artifacts and items are stored, destroy dangerous creatures to obtain rare resources and materials for crafting. Risk your life by mining unique artifacts in the epicenters of deadly anomalies. Economy and Trade - Rare trophies, weapons and gear become currency in this brutal world. Learn how to find bargains or take your loot by force. Deep mechanics and a dynamic world Crafting and Modification - Find and improve weapons, find the right armor and gear, to increase your chances of survival you'll have to cook food and create medicines. Every item you create and find is another step towards survival in the world of Stay Out. Bases and Shelters - Team up and take over bases, but be prepared to defend them from other players and the threats of the Zone. Events and Disasters - Mutant invasions and sudden abnormal activity will make each of your stints in the game unique. Stay Out isn't just a game. It's a challenge. Will you dare to look beyond the edge of the unknown? Can you survive where others have already perished? The world of the Zone awaits its heroes... and its victims.
