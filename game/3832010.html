About this gameFull description from Steam+
SaveSync is a co-op focused save syncing, backup, and sharing tool designed to make continuing shared game worlds easy and effortless. SaveSync ensures everyone in your group can pick up where you left off, without relying on the original host. We built SaveSync after seeing how often players asked, “How can we keep playing our co-op world if the host isn’t available?” The usual answers, setting up a paid dedicated server or manually transferring files, are either expensive or frustrating. SaveSync solves this with a simple, automated and affordable solution. Key Features ☁️ Cloud Save: The saves you choose are stored on Steam Workshop, encrypted, unlisted, and private by default. 🔄 Cross-User Syncing: Share your saves with your friends so anyone can keep hosting. Save files are scanned with Windows Defender before uploading and downloading, for an extra layer of safety. 🧭 Host Transfer Made Easy: The save’s host changes automatically when activated. 🗂️ Save Activation Control: Activate or deactivate specific saves to get around in-game save limits–great for games that restrict the number of save slots. 🕒 Save History & Revert: View the complete history of your save files, including when and who modified them, and easily revert to any previous version. 📂 Complements Steam Cloud: Works alongside Steam Cloud without conflict. Save activation/deactivation and host changes reflect properly, even on unsupported platforms. 🖧 LAN Sync: Seamlessly play Minecraft Java edition, Hytale and Vintage Story LAN with your Steam friends. 🌐 WAN Sync (experimental): Host Minecraft Java Edition, Hytale or Vintage Story for friends outside your network. Share a public address and our tunnel servers handle the rest. 💻 Platform Support: Windows and Linux (via Proton) 🎮 Games Currently supported: Schedule I Stardew Valley Minecraft: Java Edition, Prism Launcher, CurseForge, Modrinth, Feed The Beast, PineconeMC Launcher Satisfactory Valheim Don't Starve Together Enshrouded Vintage Story Core Keeper MISERY Project Zomboid Palworld Necesse Terraria, tModLoader The Planet Crafter Abiotic Factor ICARUS StarRupture Hytale ASKA Raft Alchemy Factory Roadside Research Windrose Factorio Space Engineers Stationeers Castle Story Supermarket Simulator Soulmask 7 Days to Die Crusader Kings III Subnautica 2 Sunkenland Romestead Astroneer RuneScape: Dragonwilds HumanitZ Scrap Mechanic Big Walk Waterpark Simulator How to Fish Barotrauma Allumeria Medieval Dynasty Eden Crafters Anime Shop Simulator Sons Of The Forest Dimraeth Permafrost Japanese Ramen Simulator Moon Sweepers Main Sequence How They Built the Pyramids Ludusavi We're adding more games regularly. Want your favorite co-op game supported next? Request it on our Discord , our subreddit, or the Community section on Steam!
