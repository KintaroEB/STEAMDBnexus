About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Mash Box – a prototype sandbox game developed by an indie team. Immerse yourself in a world where creativity knows no bounds and every update introduces fresh features and improvements. Mash Box is more than just a game; it's the technology foundation for our future projects. As an evolving prototype, it’s designed to grow with constant updates and community feedback. Your input helps shape both the game and the tech that will drive our current and upcoming titles. Features: Limitless Possibilities: Dive into a sandbox environment with endless creative potential. Ongoing Evolution: Enjoy a game that transforms with each update, driven by player feedback and continuous development. Tech Foundation: Influence the core technology that will power future games, shaping the direction of upcoming projects.
