About this gameFull description from Steam+
Co-Op Gameplay Experience Explore, drill, and produce oil on your own or team up with up to five friends. Work together for faster progress and make strategic decisions to run a successful operation. Oil Exploration and Extraction Lease land from the municipality to search for oil. Extract crude oil and transport it to your storage facilities using barrels or tanker trucks. Item Production and Market Sales Process crude oil into petroleum products using industrial machinery, then set your own prices and sell them in your shop. Fuel Production and Sales Refine crude oil into a variety of fuels such as diesel and gasoline. Negotiate with gas stations to sell your products and maximize your profits. Valuable Discoveries During excavations, you’ll uncover not only oil but also precious gems, fossils, and minerals. Add them to your collection and earn extra revenue. Equipment Upgrades Enhance everything from your detectors and vehicles to your oil pumps. Expand your brand and grow your factory to increase efficiency and output. Ultimate Goal: Become the City’s Leading Oil Supplier As you earn more money, unlock new regions, acquire larger equipment, and aim to become the sole provider of oil that powers the entire city. Get ready for a strategic, cooperative, and exploratory adventure!
