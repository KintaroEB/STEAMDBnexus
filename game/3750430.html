About this gameFull description from Steam+
Subverting the famous figures of Eastern history Combines many famous historical figures of the East, allowing you to experience the dream moment of being like-minded with the heroes. Play card combinations and hero summoning Summon heroes, collect cards, cultivate heroes, and add skill cards and equipment cards to hero cards to strengthen their abilities, making the game rich and diversified. The Funny Riding System Discover the Funny and Cute Riding System in the history of the game! Whether it's a red rabbit horse, a water dragon head or a panda head, choose your favorite horse and ride around the world to maximize your ideas! Unique A.RPG Battle System “Mystina : Heroes Online” adopts a closed real-time battle system, with random access to the battlefield, completely real-time battles, and A.PRG battles that can show a variety of attack strategies.
