About this gameFull description from Steam+
OPEN ALPHA - PLEASE READ Legend of Edda: Pegasus is currently running an Open Alpha. Expect bugs, expect balance to move, and expect all characters and progress to be wiped before the official launch. Anything you buy is tied to your account rather than your character, and carries across the wipe. Legend of Edda: Pegasus is a free-to-play PC MMORPG set in the Heroic Age of Greek mythology, with the gods of Olympus and the Titans at war. Choose a side, evolve your class, and venture into a world full of magic and challenges. DYNAMIC CHARACTER CLASSES Begin as a Fighter, Rogue or Mage and keep branching - Warrior, Swordsman, Assassin, Archer, Cleric and Wizard, and on into named specialisations such as Templar, Sniper, Arcanist and Necromancer. Every class has its own Tome of Power identity bonuses, and a sidearm slot lets you swap weapons mid-fight to fire six weapon-gated skills. An elemental attack and defence layer runs through every hit, skill and piece of gear. BEASTS, DUNGEONS AND THE COLOSSEUM Fight monsters for the gear only they drop. Run daily dungeons across difficulty tiers, multi-stage mission dungeons with switches, waves and bosses, and raids - with party bots so a short group can still enter. Duel anywhere in the field, or take organised PvP to the Colosseum. TWELVE PROFESSIONS Gathering and crafting trades - herbalism, mining, fishing, cooking, alchemy, gem cutting, woodworking, enchanting and more. Practise every trade at once; each keeps its own level, perk ladder and recipe book. Crafting is a real skill you drag to your hotbar and use in the field, and gathering nodes, ore veins and fishing spots are scattered across the world. COLLECT EVERYTHING Codex - an in-game encyclopedia of monsters, pets, mounts and costumes, every entry with its own portrait Monster Cards - one for every monster in the game: take its form for 60 seconds, then keep a buff drawn from its own stats Wardrobe - every costume set you own in one place, account-wide, with live preview Pets and Mounts - summon, ride, collect Marbles and Soul Jewels - permanent stat power, slotted and combined GUILDS, TRADE AND PROGRESSION Enchant, reinforce and identify your equipment. Found a guild, donate gear and materials for Guild Points, and unlock guild skills the whole roster shares. Open a street stall that keeps selling while you are offline. Work through a seasonal Battle Pass with free and premium tracks, chase achievements tracked server-side, and climb the rankings. THE EPIC CONFRONTATION When you have the power for it, the Sacred War awaits - a realm-wide clash between Olympus and the Titans in three modes, including Conquest, where neutral shrines fall to whichever god does the most damage to them. Embrace the challenges, refine your skills, customise your character, build your catalog of pets, and show your godlike power in the Sacred War. Step into the enchanted world of Legend of Edda and become the legend you were destined to be.
