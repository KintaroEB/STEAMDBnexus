About this gameFull description from Steam+
Hey, adventurers, welcome to California Escapades! Explore historical California's rich and dangerous lands while building up and maintaining your own farm and city with Jim, a gold miner, and Emma, a country farm girl. Everyone knows chasing after gold and treasure has its risks, no matter how careful you are. Join Jim and Emma on their adventures and find some rare treasure along the way! Features: • Build up your city with unique buildings, factories, production buildings, and decorations! • Complete various missions to explore and unlock the secrets of Gold Rush California! • Build and maintain your own farm with animals and crops! • Meet all kinds of bizarre characters and join special events during your adventure exploring the mysterious land! • Collect and produce items to build your own commercial empire through quests and completing orders! • Explore mysterious and magical lands! Let's explore historical Gold Rush California! Enter the game and start your adventure through a magical land of lush valleys, vast deserts, pristine snowfields. The home base has the perfect land to build your own farm where you can raise animals and harvest crops. Expand your farm into a city where you can buy and sell real estate, decorate with unique decorations, and conduct business transactions. When you're feeling adventurous, embark on a journey through the mysterious wilderness. Who knows what you'll find and who you'll meet! Play California Escapades now!
