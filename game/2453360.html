About this gameFull description from Steam+
Diverse Cluckmechs, endless combinations. Unleash your creativity with over 100 defensive structures and 300+ upgrade paths! Combine different towers and upgrades to create unique and powerful strategies. The possibilities are truly endless! Enjoy fast-paced and addictive gameplay with a wide range of characters and abilities. From the firepower of the Warcluck to the trap-laying expertise of the Mouscle, each Character has unique skills and tactics. With endless replayability, build and upgrade your defenses, lead your troops, and defeat waves of enemies. Choose from a variety of unique heroes and discover new strategies with each playthrough. Unleash your creativity then outsmart your enemies with clever strategies. Enjoy complete freedom to place your defenses and base anywhere on the map. Strategically plan your enemy's route and build your defenses accordingly. Unpredictable adventures, full of wacky humor and unexpected twists. Embark on a journey filled with humor and wonder, explore unpredictable landscapes and encounter bizarre creatures. From deserts to glaciers, you'll never know what awaits you. Discover a wacky animal world and hidden surprises at every turn!
