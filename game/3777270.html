About this gameFull description from Steam+
“Lots of Cats on an Adventure” is a game where you find hidden cats in comic strips, meow! On a peaceful day, humans (a.k.a. cat servants) were abducted by aliens! One brave cat couldn’t just sit back and watch, so they set off on an adventure to gather a crew to rescue their beloved humans…. Find the cats hidden in the beautiful and diverse hand-drawn landscapes, and join the cat friends on their wacky adventure, meow! Find Over 2200 Cats and Special Items! Part 1 (free): 5+1 main landscapes, 5 interactable comic strips, 850+ cats, 250 items Part 2 (DLC): 5+1 main landscapes, 5 interactable comic strips, 850+ cats, 250 items Main Features All comic strips and main landscapes are connected through story progression. Hints can be used once per minute. No penalty for clicking on a spot without a cat. Search freely. Has an original soundtrack, with unique tracks featured in each episode. Another BGM set is also available, so you can choose the one that you like best. Every illustration is 100% hand-drawn and crafted with care. (For more details, please check the pinned topic in Discussions.)
