About this gameFull description from Steam+
Test your friendships via local multiplayer or compete with runners worldwide in this sequel to the acclaimed competitive platformer – now with up to 8 players per online lobby, upgraded netcode, new characters and power-ups, high-stakes elimination tournaments, explosive story campaign, and a variety of new multiplayer modes. GOTTA GO FAST Master the movement: build momentum, swing with a grappling hook, and outmaneuver opponents using speed boosters, shortcuts, and traps scattered across maps. TOOLS OF DESTRUCTION Unleash an arsenal of power-ups, featuring fan-favorites like fireballs, freeze rays, and golden hooks alongside new additions like blasters and portable teleporters. SAVE THE DAY Save New Rush City, meet new and returning heroes, and gear up for multiplayer mayhem in a campaign that expands the original story and lore. HILARIOUS PVP CHAOS Kick back in Quick Play, get sweaty in Ranked, compete in brand-new 64-player elimination tournaments, or check out a variety of rule-bending modes in the Playground. Features • Chaotic 2D racing for up to 8 players online • Fluid, high-skill movement system • 12 playable characters • Dozens of unlockable cosmetic items • Expanded arsenal of new and returning power-ups • 16 map layouts with unique challenges • Local multiplayer & custom games • New experimental, rule-bending modes • Explosive story campaign • 64-player elimination tournaments • Competitive Ranked play
