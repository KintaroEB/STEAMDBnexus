About this gameFull description from Steam+
Hand Simulator is a game in which you control your own hands (You do not need a VR helmet, you just need a keyboard and mouse). There are many different levels where you can play with spinners, plunge into the subtleties of handling weapons, milk a cow, take part in a Mexican duel with your friends, have a good time fishing, play chess or simply chat in an anonymous club. Hand Simulator is a game where everyone finds entertainment for their tastes. Features: - A lot of different levels. The game is developed every day; players' most interesting ideas are inevitably realized. - Multiplayer. For online games fans there are many levels implemented in Hand Simulator; there you can have fun with your friends. - Detailed mechanics of weapons. In order to make at least one shot, you will have to work hard. - Achievements. In each level, both online and offline, there are tasks in the performance of which, you will receive achievements. - Collectible cards.
