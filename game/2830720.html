About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into the ultimate squad warfare experience in this real-time, fast paced tactical top-down shooter. Use momentum to your advantage and to your enemies detriment as your bullets and explosive shrapnel ricochet using FreeInfantry's Newtonian physics model. Plan your attack from the shadows and ambush your enemy thanks to Infantry's real-time Fog of War visibility model. FreeInfantry features several zone types, providing players with exciting and varying experiences in this sci-fi military universe that starts with the war between the Titan Militia and Collective Military. Fight alongside your team or as a solo trooper in Io's Landing against a neverending tide of enemy bots in escalating challenge modes. Join a squad and test your skills and strategy in the competitive deathmatch league that's 50 plus seasons running. Survive against the swarm of aliens in Bug Hunt. FreeInfantry is most active daily between 9 PM and 2 AM EST, but may be enjoyed at other times as well through the featured bots zones. FreeInfantry is 100% free with zero paid unlockables. Prepare for battle and experience it today.
