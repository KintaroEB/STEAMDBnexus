About this gameFull description from Steam+
『Mini Cozy Room : Lo-Fi』is a productivity game that blends calming Lo-Fi music with creative room decoration. Cute avatars and pets, along with convenient desktop tools like memos, to-do lists, and a Pomodoro timer, are designed to boost your focus and efficiency. 1. Your Own Cozy Workspace Create the workspace you’ve always dreamed of! Choose from a wide variety of rooms, furniture, and decorations. The beautiful workspace you design will help enhance your concentration and productivity. 2. Soothing Lo-Fi Music & Ambience Fill your workspace with diverse Lo-Fi music genres and calming ambient sounds. You'll soon find yourself relaxed and deeply immersed in your tasks. 3. Multiplayer Mode Feeling lonely while working alone? Work together with your friends! Invite friends to your workspace, or visit rooms shared by other players. Chat freely with people from around the world using text, emojis, and mini notes. For those who prefer a quieter environment, a Focus Mode is also available. 4. Innovative Desktop UI and Mini Mode Freely reposition and resize notes and to-do lists on your desktop. Feeling overwhelmed by the large game screen? Switch to Mini Mode and conveniently place it in your taskbar! 5. Cute Avatars and Pets Express your unique style with diverse fashion options, hairstyles, and accessories. Move adorable avatars to chairs, beds, or beanbags and assign tasks. Lovely pets like cats, dogs, and hedgehogs will keep you company. 6. Key Features Enjoy Lo-Fi music across various genres Mix different ambient sounds to create your perfect atmosphere Use productivity tools such as Memo, To-Do List, and Pomodoro Timer (with both count-up and count-down modes) Customize your own avatar with various hairstyles and outfits Design your personal workspace with different wallpapers and furniture Add external music files (MP3, WAV, etc.) to create your own playlist Bookmark your favorite channels using the YouTube embedded player for easy access Earn experience by listening to music. As you level up, gift deliveries arrive in your room Level up to unlock new items and content
