About this gameFull description from Steam+
This is an open-world criminal empire simulator. Build Your Criminal Empire From the Streets Up. Steal cars, run illegal businesses, recruit your own crime family and conquer the city in a seamless open-world criminal empire simulator. Every crime, business and territory is connected, allowing you to build your organization from a small-time hustler into the city's most powerful kingpin. A True Criminal Playground Choose how your empire begins. Become a car thief, drug producer, nightclub owner, dealer or crime boss. Every path leads to the same goal: control the city. Build Your Criminal Empire Commit Crimes Steal Cars Pickpocket Evade the Police Shootouts Street Races Run Illegal Businesses Warehouse Management Nightclub Management Production Chains Business Automation Office Operations & Criminal Jobs Rent cheap motel rooms or apartments to start your first illegal operation, then expand into warehouses, businesses, and a city-wide criminal network. Grow from a small-time hustler into the city's most powerful crime boss. Rule The City Rival Families Territory Control Recruit Gangsters Expand Your Influence Become The Boss Key Features • Build Your Criminal Empire: Start with nothing and rise from a small-time hustler to the city's most powerful crime boss. • Commit Crimes Your Way: Steal cars, pickpocket civilians, rob targets, evade the police, and complete criminal jobs across the city. • Run Illegal Businesses: Build warehouses, manage nightclubs, automate production chains, and expand your criminal network. • Recruit Your Crime Family: Hire gangsters, assign criminal operations, eliminate rival families, and take control of every district. • Conquer the City: Fight for control of 11 unique districts, eliminate rival families, earn Prestige, and dominate the city’s criminal underworld. • Living Open World: NPCs, police, traffic, businesses, and rival organizations react dynamically to your actions. • Vehicle Theft & Customization: Steal, customize, upgrade, race, or dismantle vehicles for profit. • Deep Progression System: Unlock skills, improve your abilities, expand your businesses, and discover new ways to build your empire. • Every System is Connected: Crimes, businesses, territories, economy, and progression work together to create one seamless criminal empire simulation.
