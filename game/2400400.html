About this gameFull description from Steam+
" Feed the Cups " will make you experience every bit of a drink shop. You will work in various beverage stores, make drinks as accurately as possible according to customers' needs, and manage inventory and purchase , avoid capital shortage, and control the " pressure " that you can bear. You can cooperate with up to 2 people on the screen! This is a game made of light Roguelite drinks. According to the type of drinks sold, you need to prepare materials, order for customers, make drinks, make cups, even clean the shop, and respond to the review of the municipal supervision bureau. The drinks included in the game include a variety of beans, tea, milk, coffee, bubbles, soda, etc., which will be gradually presented with the progress of the level. Main features ·At different levels, it is necessary to understand the way and process of making drinks sold by beverage stores, and prepare materials in advance so as to make drinks more quickly after customers order. ·Each day in the level will trigger a variety of events. Each day will become stronger and stronger according to your business situation, and there will also be corresponding costs to make the level difficult. ·You need to ensure that there is no deficit in the store's funds, and your pressure will not exceed the upper limit, and insist on completing all the days to pass the level. ·Each time you complete the checkpoint, you will get money according to the turnover of the beverage store, which will be used to upgrade your home, expand more and larger rooms, purchase various kinds of furniture, and improve your ability and experience of making drinks.
