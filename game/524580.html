About this gameFull description from Steam+
Long ago, a war raged between two powerful deities. Each was sealed away, their weapons scattered throughout the world. Our hero in this RPG, Fang, happens upon one such weapon and now he alone must choose which deity will awaken again! Key Features 1. In the three stories to play, friends become foes, foes become friends, and, based on your choices, multiple endings can be unlocked! 2. Customize weapons by fusing them with fairies and change dungeons stats through World-Shaping. Even transform with the “Fairize” skill and become stronger in battle! 3. With up to six party members on the battlefield at a time, you can play as all your favorite Fencers! 4. From Amateur to Hell, choose the difficulty level that suits your style!
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 3 regional ratings+

Content notice

This is a role-playing game in which players assume the role of a fighter battling enemy forces. Players explore dungeon environments, interact with characters, and battle enemies in turn-based combat. Players select attack moves from a menu screen as characters use magical sword-like weapons against human and fantastical creatures (e.g., dragons, robots). Cutscenes also depict instances of violence (mostly still-screen images): characters impaled by swords/spikes/lances; blood trickling from characters' mouths. A handful of scenes include suggestive dialogue (e.g.,“[Y]our boobs are the perfect size and shape. . .” and “My boobs are way bigger than yours are.”). One scene depicts characters in a hot spring with bubbles or hair barely covering their breasts. During the course of the game, wine is referenced several times, and is consumed by a character, who has become addicted. The words “sh*t” and “a*shole” appear in the dialogue.
