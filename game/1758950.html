About this gameFull description from Steam+
Banzai Escape 2 Subterranean is a sequel game based on the events of Banzai Escape 2 where the Draugr labs have been captured by SPECTRE. All of the sisters (Inchester, Imingter and Iekler) take in refugees to a location deep underground. In order to survive, they must defeat the Lanun occupying the underground dungeon networks along with other factions such as SPECTRE and others You are an Elite Imingter agent with special access to roaming around underground networks to dispose any incoming threat. Lets Journey Underground Subterranean network Engaging Enemies Completing Missions Features: 1P or 2P Single-player / Co-op mode to allow friend to play together. Fun! Chaotic! Intense! Player will always be encountering action moment with rogue and aggressive like of enemies while finishing your objectives Customizable Customizable costumes / skins. Lots varieties of options to choose to make your character looks prettier. Random Generated Level Random generate map segments to allow different path taken toward objectives area inside underground dungeon RNG Arsenals Random generate gun & powerups or loot-able for grinding. Lots of guns and about 56++ nano-plugin addon powerups skills with lots of random stats that can be obtainable. ..And Many more!
