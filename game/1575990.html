About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step right up to the Twisted Tower! Careful though! Our silly mascots may be looking for a hug, and our carnival rides might need some screws tightened… So what do you say, chap? Are you tough enough to make it to the top of the tower and save the love of your life? Here’s what your ticket will get you upon entering the magical resort: Explore an abandoned 1950’s resort reminiscent of BioShock and Disney World Get lost in 5 hauntingly beautiful levels: the hotel, the waterpark, the clown casino, the carnival forest, and the space station Punchy, bloody gunplay with corrupted fairy-tale mascots Immerse yourself in a mysterious and emotional story Choose from a variety of unique paths per playthrough, ensuring the world feels like a fresh new experience Upgrade your toyish arsenal, including: a whack-a mole mallet, rubber-band pistol, dart throwin’ Tommy Gun, fartin’ shotgun, bubblegum spittin’ chain-gun, sniper slingshot, skull-poppin’ ray gun, and more Collect bizarre toys to assist you in jumping, dashing, and grappling your way up the tower
