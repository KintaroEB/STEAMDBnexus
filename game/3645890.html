About this gameFull description from Steam+
Cast your line to feed an ancient eldritch beast. Bring friends or go solo. It doesn’t care who is feeding it, only that it is fed. With over 100 different species of creatures to catch and dozens of live animals to use as bait. Grill it, smoke it, turn it into a pearl, or bet it all in the gambling pit. Sell the extra fish you catch and spend the profits on upgrades, better gear, and items from the traders found around the map. You must sacrifice a certain amount of fish to the beast every day if you want to survive, but the beast is not the only thing out there. Be wary of territorial beasts, stalking nightmares, and ambushing predators. Learn the creatures of the land to avoid being killed, you can't feed the hungering abomination if you are dead... Drink beer, explore multiple maps, customize your characters, play instruments, catch fish and legendary creatures like the Lochness monster, explode monsters (and people), AND GAMBLE. Fight back against the Lake Entity and stop the beast!
