About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into the shoes of a helpless father who has to balance completing a series of house chores and babysitting, before his wife gets home. PLEASE STOP CRYING is a horror game with a design of emulating the stress one may experience when looking out for a baby. Ever considered having kids? Think of this as a demo for what you will have to go through at times. FEATURES: This is a fast paced FPS lighthearted horror game where your goals are to complete as many chores as you can before being interrupted by the baby. Complete all the chores as quickly as you can to achieve a record time and the honorable title of "DAD OF THE YEAR". Enjoy the crispy 90's era graphics for the best experience.
