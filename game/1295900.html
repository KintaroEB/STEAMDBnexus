About this gameFull description from Steam+
Become the Dragon Step into the scales of a mighty dragon and claim the skies, land, or sea as your own. Choose from a variety of species, each with its own perks and specializations. Customize Your Dragon Make your character stand out by giving it a unique name, custom colors, and even custom body parts such as horns, tail tips, and claws. Choose from a growing list of customization options to create your perfect dragon. Survival of the Fittest Hunt for sustenance and locate vital water sources to survive while amassing a hoard of valuable resources through the gathering and inventory system. Utilize your treasure trove to craft useful items that improve your dragon's chances of survival and abilities. Your World, Your Rules Draconia respects your playstyle. Grow your dragon by completing quests or battling enemies. Explore the world in PvE or opt into optional PvP, enabling or disabling it at will through your character profile. Safely explore the world of Draconia without fear of unwanted confrontation, and engage in battles only when you choose. Embark on the Ultimate Adventure Unravel the story of Draconia in single-player or multiplayer. Form groups and clans, and invite friends and like-minded players to accompany you on your journey.
