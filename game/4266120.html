About this gameFull description from Steam+
🧩 2025: Mosaic Retrospective is the follow-up to 2024: Mosaic Retrospective. A free, meditative game about completing a single enormous 57,600 piece grid puzzle based on global news events of the year. 💣 If you’re familiar with minesweeper, you’re ready to dive in! Use the numbered clues (0-9) to uncover light tiles that gradually reveal an intricate, detailed mosaic. 🎶 Whether you're engrossed in your favourite podcasts or listening to your favourite tunes, 2025: Mosaic Retrospective serves as the perfect backdrop to enhance your experience. Or enjoy the soothing built-in jazz  soundtrack composed and performed by multi-instrumentalist Kim Nahuel. ⏳ Dozens of Hours of Addictive Gameplay! 2025: Mosaic Retrospective offers hours of meditative, yet challenging gameplay as you piece together the events that shaped the year.
