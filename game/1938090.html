The Call of Duty® experience supports Modern Warfare® 4, Black Ops 7 and Call of Duty®: Warzone™. Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® 4 brings players Warfare Without Limits. In Call of Duty®: Black Ops 7, Treyarch and Raven Software are bringing players the biggest Black Ops ever. Call of Duty®: Warzone™ is the massive free-to-play combat arena, featuring Battle Royale and Resurgence. Call of Duty® Points (CP*) are the in-game currency that can be used in Modern Warfare® 4, Black Ops 7, Black Ops 6, Modern Warfare® III, Modern Warfare® II and Call of Duty®: Warzone™ to obtain new content. Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® 4, Call of Duty®: Black Ops 7, Call of Duty®: Black Ops 6, Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® III, Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® II or Call of Duty®: Warzone™ required to redeem.
