About this gameFull description from Steam+
That One Otter Game is a mischievous co-op adventure where two young otters take on a wild rite of passage to prove their worth. Complete silly park challenges to fill up your stamp card—catch a panicked penguin, race a hyped-up zebra, or haul a stolen radio to a mountaintop. Yeet stuff everywhere, hijack a tiny car to smash fences (or bump into a few unsuspecting bystanders), and meet all kinds of animals: shy penguins, chill elephants, or chatty otters to bicker with. Explore a vibrant world full of surprises, unlock hidden hats to strut in style, and let your imagination run wild—you never know what’ll happen next. Built for two players on the same couch, the game thrives on exploration, dumb ideas, and chaotic fun. Everything reacts in weird ways, and every corner hides a new giggle. That One Otter Game is a fuzzy mix of nonsense, cuteness, and wet fur—a joyful sandbox where the goal isn’t just to win, but to cause delightful mayhem like two otters on a sugar rush.
