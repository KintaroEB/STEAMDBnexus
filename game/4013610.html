About this gameFull description from Steam+
Pure Badminton brings the speed, precision, and energy of real-world badminton to life. Experience the fastest racket sport in the world like never before, in a realistic and accessible game. 🏸 REALISTIC GAMEPLAY & PHYSICS Authentic shuttle physics and realistic player animations recreate the flow and intensity of real matches. Expect a challenge that feels natural, with performance shaped by positioning, timing, and shot selection. 👥 LOCAL MULTIPLAYER (UP TO 4 PLAYERS) Play singles or doubles with your friends. Pure Badminton supports local multiplayer for up to 4 players. This demo allows you to play singles or doubles matches, alone or in multiplayer, in 2 of the available stadiums, with 4 available characters.
