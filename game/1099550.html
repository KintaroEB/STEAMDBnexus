About this gameFull description from Steam+
3D Organon is the world’s leading medical XR and healthcare education platform, transforming how anatomy and clinical knowledge are explored, taught, and mastered. With its immersive 3D environments, lifelike models, and powerful AI-driven learning tools, 3D Organon sets a new standard for healthcare education. 3D Organon goes beyond traditional anatomy apps by delivering an all-in-one suite of features—from full-body 3D anatomy exploration and XR Medical Imaging to ultrasound simulation, interactive quizzes, and the Medverse multi-user learning environment. New in This Version: AI Study Assistant Meet your intelligent anatomy tutor. The newly introduced AI Study Assistant delivers real-time learning support, helping users navigate complex topics, receive personalized guidance, and get anatomy explanations instantly—revolutionizing self-directed study and classroom integration. With interactive tools, clinical correlations, upmarket visualization technologies, and a multilingual anatomical knowledge base, 3D Organon bridges the gap between theoretical concepts and clinical application. Its definitions are aligned with the official Terminologia Anatomica and translated into 16 languages by academic experts. Trusted by over 600 prestigious institutions and endorsed by industry leaders such as Meta’s Mark Zuckerberg and HTC’s Cher Wang, 3D Organon has been spotlighted in global keynotes and featured in leading publications like Forbes. Guest Mode Access (Free): Full access to all features for the first 2 hours Unlimited access to: Male & female skeletal systems Connective tissues Joint movement animations Select anatomical definitions Platform updates and UI enhancements Subscription Unlocks Full Access to: All 15 body systems (male/female) XR Medical Imaging with DICOM integration Detailed microscopic anatomy models Cadaveric image library USMLE-style quizzes and clinical questions Organ, brain, and bone mapping tools Body actions with 550+ animations Tumor growth and pain visualization tools Multi-user Medverse sessions Advanced features like slicing, exploding, and diagram tools Medverse: A Paradigm Shift in Medical Education The 3D Organon Medverse is a next-generation virtual learning environment where students and educators collaborate in real time. With multi-user access, cross-platform compatibility, and integrated teaching tools, Medverse enables institutions to host dynamic, interactive lessons beyond physical boundaries. Who Is 3D Organon For? Designed for medical and allied health students, educators, physicians, patients, artists, and curious minds, 3D Organon supports a wide range of educational needs. With accurate anatomical modeling, intuitive navigation, and tools developed by medical professionals, it simplifies complex concepts for beginners and experts alike. Key Highlights: Blazing fast performance and redesigned UI Over 17,000 detailed anatomical structures 1,000s of microscopic structures in 3D AI Study Assistant Accurate anatomical terminology and definitions Real-time male/female model switching Explode, slice, and annotate tools Diagram and mapping features Favorites, multi-select, and quick scene switching X-ray mode, and live search Video recording, osteophyte simulation, tumor and pain tools Microscopic Anatomy Models Include: Glomerulus, Nephron, Liver lobule, Bone cross-section, Skin, Tooth, Capillary bed, Bronchial tree, Small intestine, Peripheral nerve, Skeletal muscle fiber, Cranial nerve nuclei, and many more… Available Languages: English, Latin (terminology), Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, Russian, Ukrainian, Georgian, Polish, Thai, Greek, Dutch. Content notice

3D Organon Anatomy is a medical anatomy software which contains detailed 3D anatomy models of the male and female human body including the Reproductive and Integumentary (skin) systems. All 3D models in the software are designed under the supervision of anatomy professors and medical professionals.
