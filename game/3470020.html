About this gameFull description from Steam+
Currently, Eutolant Saga will be available in Japanese only. Future localization plans are undecided. Path of Domination or Liberation? — A Classic Fantasy SRPG A peaceful nation engulfed in war. You are an apprentice knight who rises to face this crisis. Join the princess in escaping the advancing army, rally your allies, and travel across the world to reclaim your homeland. In a meticulously crafted fantasy world, only you can command the battlefield, strategize, and restore peace to the continent! A Simple Yet Strategic System ✅ Fast-paced turn-based battles with no screen transitions! ✅ Form a party of over 40 unique allies! ✅ Characters' stats grow randomly! ✅️ A revolutionary backup system that boosts your growth rate when progress starts to stall! ✅ Special items available to support struggling characters! ✅ No permadeath—no need to reset! ✅ Even without permadeath, injuries carry consequences! Lively Character Interactions A war chronicle brought to life by over 120 unique characters, blending moments of deep drama with lighthearted banter! With a rich and engaging script, the story unfolds through lively interactions and dynamic character dialogue. Regarding Voice Acting Characters speak when selected, but there is no voice acting during story scenarios. Regarding Mouse Control Mouse support is only available at a resolution of 1920x1080px. If you wish to use the mouse in full-screen mode, please open the "game.ini" file in the game folder and change the setting to: HardwareFullScreen=0 However, please note the following side effects: - You will not be able to change the window size. - The screen will be stretched to fit your monitor size. When Your Controller Is Not Working Properly Installing the Xbox Extended Feature Support Driver may resolve the issue. ▼ Steps Launch Steam in Big Picture Mode.
