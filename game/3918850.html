About this gameFull description from Steam+
AN ORIGINAL MIGHT & MAGIC STRATEGY CARD GAME! Might & Magic Fates TCG is an original strategy card game rooted in the legendary Might & Magic universe. Build your deck, summon mythical creatures, cast devastating spells, and lead iconic heroes into battle. Each card is part of a living legacy shaped by decades of fantasy lore and player imagination. EXPLORE THE SEA OF FATES A fractured multiverse where timelines collide and destinies unravel. Lead the charge with powerful heroes, command diverse armies, and outwit your opponents in tactical duels that reward creativity and skill. Whether you're a longtime fan or new to card games, Fates offers a fresh take on a legendary world. COMMAND MIGHT & MAGIC HEROES Lead with iconic heroes drawn from the Might & Magic universe. Progress each hero like an RPG character, unlocking game-changing abilities and evolving your strategy over time. COLLECT HUNDRED OF CARDS Build your arsenal with powerful spells, creatures, and artifacts — as well as unique hero cards and strategic building cards that shape the battlefield in your favor. MASTER ICONIC FACTIONS Fight for the glory of Haven, raise the dead in Necropolis, unleash Inferno’s fury, or command the arcane power of Academy. PLAY WITH STRATEGY AND FREEDOM Craft your own tactics with a flexible deckbuilding system, then test your skill in battles where synergy, positioning, and timing matter more than luck. PLAY SOLO OR PVP Climb the ranks in competitive multiplayer or enjoy seasonal solo events and faction-based challenges. FREE TO PLAY, FAIR FOR ALL Play and progress without paywalls. In-game purchases are optional and never required to compete. Your cards are more than tools. They are echoes of fallen worlds, bound to fate. Are you truly prepared to discover your fate?
