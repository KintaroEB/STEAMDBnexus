About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Prologue offers a limited slice of the Scam Centre Simulator: Under Kingdom, featuring selected mechanics and early content. It is designed to showcase the core gameplay experience and set the stage for the complete release. Scam Centre Simulator: Prologue is a dark management simulation game where survival comes at a cost. Burdened by crushing debt, you’re given a chance to escape into a mysterious town, but only if you can run its underground scam empire. Recruit workers with unique skills, manage shady operations, and expand your influence. From bribing police to running scams and keeping workers under control. Will you repay your debts and escape, or be consumed by the kingdom of scams? Manage Your Scam Centre – Feed, discipline, and control workers while scaling your operation. Recruit Unique Talent – Find workers with different backgrounds and abilities. Engage in Illegal Activities – From street hustling and police bribery to scams. Generate Electricity – With power in short supply, keep your centre running by producing electricity through garbage recycling and manpower: hand-crank generator, bicycle-powered generator, human hamster wheel. Expand and Customize – Upgrade facilities, decorate your hideout, and expand your empire.
