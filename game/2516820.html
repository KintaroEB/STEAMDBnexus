About this gameFull description from Steam+
The lucky land is already in chaos. In order to restore order to this land, brave adventurers should embark on a journey. Game gameplay + LuckLand is a strategy casual game that combines elements such as card building and Roguelike. + You need to accumulate energy in the battle and defeat the enemy to achieve victory. + The game operation is simple, just click the rotation button, and the system will automatically calculate energy based on the combination of chess pieces, and engage in a life and death battle with the enemy after several rounds. + Each round, different pieces can be selected to join the chessboard, forming various combinations to gain high energy and defeat enemies with increasing difficulty. Collect pieces and construct combinations + Collect pieces and treasures from different regions and confront enemies on a 4x5 chessboard. + Completing orders at the Color Workshop, searching for treasure chests in the Treasure Bay, or all in at the Destiny Casino is all up to you. Unlock treasures & talents to elevate + As the game progresses, the difficulty will gradually increase. + Defeat higher difficulty enemies, unlock exclusive treasures, and give your deck a qualitative leap. + Defeat higher difficulty enemies, unlock powerful talents, and they will make you more adept in the exploration process. Good luck!
