About this gameFull description from Steam+
Become the ultimate football manager in Soccer Manager 2027. Step into the dugout in Soccer Manager 2027, a realistic football manager game where you take charge as manager of elite clubs and world-class players. Navigate the dynamic transfer market, master matchday tactics, and compete across 54 leagues in 36 countries worldwide. Experience the thrilling action unfold on the pitch with our improved 3D Match Motion Engine, bringing every football game to life with greater realism and immersion. SM27 delivers an authentic management experience for fans of football games and football management games. KEY FEATURES • Build your ultimate dream team from real players in this immersive soccer manager experience. • Master formations and player instructions as you manage elite teams like Manchester City, Bayern Munich, and Borussia Dortmund. • Compete across 54 football leagues globally, including the official Bundesliga. • Develop your football club through training facilities and youth academy investments. • Experience stunning 3D football games powered by the upgraded Match Motion Engine. • Step onto the global stage and lead one of over 100 national football teams. NEW FOR THE 2027 SEASON • The improved 3D Engine features remastered player models and brand-new animations that bring matchday action to life. • Refreshed UI with a sleek new colour theme for a more intuitive football management experience. • Exciting additions: Live Scores, Squad Planner, and World Player of the Year awards. • Enhanced tactics with a redesigned interface and robust club planning tools. • New Club Focuses, immersive inbox messaging, and authentic continental cup draws add incredible depth to your football management career. BUILD YOUR DREAM TEAM Take control of the world's biggest clubs. Sign elite superstars, discover hidden wonderkids, and make critical transfer market decisions. Shape a squad capable of competing for the biggest trophies in this immersive football manager game. MASTER MATCHDAY TACTICS Take charge of your squad's formations and playing style. Select your top eleven, adapt your strategies on the fly, and guide your club to domestic titles and continental glory. Watch your tactical genius unfold in stunning 3D. DEVELOP YOUR CLUB Upgrade stadium facilities, improve your youth academy, and craft a long-term sporting strategy. Make every administrative decision count as you secure the future and financial success of your club. AUTHENTIC COMPETITIONS & LEAGUES Choose from over 900 clubs across 54 leagues in 36 countries worldwide, including the fully licensed Bundesliga. Take control of your chosen club, conquer domestic leagues and continental competitions, and build your managerial legacy. INTERNATIONAL MANAGEMENT Take the reins of over 100 national teams. Call up your country's best, shape the international squad, and compete for international glory in major global tournaments. CREATE YOUR OWN CLUB Want to start from scratch? Create and customise your very own club. Compete in authentic leagues, fight your way up through the lower divisions, and forge your unique legacy as a top football manager. Do you have what it takes to become a legend of the beautiful game? Download Soccer Manager 2027 now and lead your club to glory!
