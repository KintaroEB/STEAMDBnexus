About this gameFull description from Steam+
Players are born in "Henapoland" as pioneer lords. The objective is to find the golden land of "Jipongu". Although it is an idol game, all the various contents do not end with idols alone. It is a free game! On the left side of the screen, members continue to collect the chosen materials they have already found. Use these to break through the obstacles and problems that stand in your way! Other side contents are also abundant. Petit Combat Harvesting in the field Harvesting in the sea There are also hard areas that are difficult to clear. This game is Idle Game, making it ideal for those with time to spare or in-between activities!
