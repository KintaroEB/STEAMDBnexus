About this gameFull description from Steam+
Venture into a hauntingly liminal forest in Mycelium: The Silent Contract, as an unwilling contractor for a mysterious corporation. Your handler demands results as per your signed contract: harvest the specimens and extract. In this co-op/single-player extraction game death is just the beginning and nothing is as it seems. Fans of the Backrooms, SCP, and Alan Wake universes will feel right at... home? But the mysterious nature and the cryptic fine print of the Silent Contract will keep even them on their toes. What did you sign, exactly? ABOUT THE GAME Single and CO-OP multiplayer with an advanced voice chat and other means of coordination Huge, procedurally generated, liminal forest with dynamic weather conditions and spatial sound Dozens of unique locations with their own mysteries, puzzles, and dangers More than 10 twisted monsters and other dangers to stand in your way Upgradable equipment to aid in your missions MYCELIUM There is no life after death but there's always more work to do. Having signed a contract in exchange for a successful life, now that it has ended, you are obliged to perform tasks for a company called Mycelium. YOU ARRIVE Each mission you arrive at a dark and motionless forest where silence is your best friend. It will never be the same, so you'll have to find your bearings over and over again. Don't get lost - or you might never come back. YOU GATHER Look for mycelium as that's what you need to complete the contract and free your soul. Buy equipment the company provides and find valuables to get more time in the future missions. The more time you get, the more mysteries you'll face and can solve. YOU EXTRACT Use the mycelium you've got to extract from the mission. But hurry up - you can't stay in the forest forever. The longer you stay, the more dangerous it becomes, as there is always someone or something that wants your mycelium more than you do. YOU IMPROVE Each successful mission will let you get more advanced equipment and more time to uncover the mysteries of the forest. Use that wisely, as it will not be getting easier. IF YOU LOVE TO WANDER, LOST IN THE FOREST - GET MYCELIUM: THE SILENT CONTRACT NOW!
