About this gameFull description from Steam+
After surviving a shipwreck on the very edge of the charted seas, you stand before the daunting challenges of mysterious lands waiting to be discovered. FIGHT AND SURVIVE Forge your own path without the constraints of traditional classes. Level up your skills to unlock new baseline abilities, and search the world for rare spellbooks that grant you extraordinary powers! GATHER, BUILD AND CRAFT Establish your own base and master a variety of professions to craft and upgrade your armor and weapons. Create potions and enchantments that will be essential for your survival in this unforgiving environment. EXPLORE Explore a vast, procedurally generated world and progress through distinct biomes, each filled with unique enemies, resources, and challenges.
