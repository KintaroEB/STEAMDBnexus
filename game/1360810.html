About this gameFull description from Steam+
Define Your Own Legacy No matter how harsh reality gets, here, you are the main character. Office Life is not just a simulator; it’s an interactive "Zero-to-Hero" saga featuring over 2 million words of branching narratives. Start Your Corporate Legend Experience a deeply realistic simulation of the corporate world. Master Corporate Warfare: Learn to pick sides in complex office politics, win high-stakes project bids, and navigate a minefield of cooperation and betrayal. Complex Relationships: Interact with hundreds of unique NPCs who are more than just quest givers. Will you make powerful allies or dangerous enemies? The choice is yours. Immersive "Zero-to-Hero" Journey You start as a fresh college graduate facing a world of infinite possibilities. Infinite Career Paths: Experience the thrill of a meteoric rise. Climb the ladder to become an industry elite, or go rogue and start your own company to dominate the market in intense commercial battles. Financial Freedom: Success isn't just about the 9-to-5. Trade stocks, invest in real estate, and amass a fortune to realize your dream of true financial freedom. Romantic Choices Life isn't just about work. Over a 10-year timeline, you will meet multiple charismatic and independent women. Deep Interaction: Unlock exclusive storylines and dating events for each heroine. Emotional Bonds: Will you stay loyal to one true love, or navigate a complex web of relationships? Get married, have children, and build a warm home in the cold city. Build Your Workplace Strategy We’ve gamified office politics! Face difficult clients, shifting blame, and demanding bosses with a unique card system. Strategic Deckbuilding: Build your deck from 500+ cards representing work actions like "Overtime," "Slacking Off," and "Report." Wit Wins: Use strategy to resolve crises and enjoy the thrill of outsmarting your opponents! Rich Personal Life Life begins after 5 PM! Side Hustles: Write web novels, become a streamer, or deliver food. There are many ways to get rich! Work-Life Balance: Hit the gym, travel the world, buy luxury cars and mansions. Live your best life in the big city.
