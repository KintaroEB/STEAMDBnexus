About this gameFull description from Steam+
Ever wondered what one quadrillion damage per second feels like? No? Well, you’re about to find out anyway. Welcome to Clicker Heroes, the OG idle game that will turn you from a casual clicker into a number-obsessed maniac. This isn't just your finger versus infinity. It’s a clicker extravaganza where numbers go up, monsters explode into gold, and your mouse begs for mercy. Prepare your meaty appendages, it’s clicking time! Hire 35 unforgettable heroes to do the heavy lifting while you sit back and watch the gold pour in. From Athena to King Midas, legendary figures join your cause and bring unique powers that crank your damage through the roof. Upgrade them and unlock abilities so strong they make your MMO characters look like kindergarteners. Click to crush endless waves of monsters and watch them explode into piles of gold. Each new zone brings tougher enemies and bosses that put your damage to the test. Smash through them, claim their treasure, and keep climbing ever higher... until even the Ancients stir in their idle slumber. Your heroes never quit, even when you do, so gold piles up while you sleep, eat, or pretend to be productive. Then comes Ascension, the big reset that trades all your progress for shiny Hero Souls and permanent power. The first time feels like a prank, but soon you'll come back stronger, crushing bosses that once made you cry, and treating trillions of DPS like spare change. Recruit and upgrade more than 35 heroes, each with their own powers. Keep the adventure going even while you are away, as your heroes fight nonstop and bring back mountains of gold while you're AFK. Ascend to reset your run, earn Hero Souls, and return stronger every time. Push through endless zones filled with tougher monsters and bigger rewards. Play for free and enjoy the full game without spending a cent.
