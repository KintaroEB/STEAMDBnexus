About this gameFull description from Steam+
Car Dealer Simulator Early Days is a free prologue to the full Car Dealer Simulator game. Little Sam, the quirky owner of the business, will introduce you to its secrets. He’s not great at keeping things in order, so your task is to buy low, fix up, and sell high. In the full game, if you handle the initial tasks well, you’ll have the chance to take over the business, and then… Everything will be in your hands! Build a business that fulfills your customers’ automotive fantasies. From budget-friendly cars to extravagant rides, seize opportunities, hire the best, provide excellent service, and maximize profits. Grow your company day by day. The key to achieving high profits lies in negotiating when buying old cars. Look for great deals, and during price haggling, stay firm and professional. Not all purchased cars are in working condition, so you’ll need to use a tow truck to transport them. Every car requires diagnostics and preparation before being sold. At Little Sam’s request, you’ll perform your first mechanical and bodywork repairs. In the prologue, the available tools are quite limited. Presenting a car to the customer requires a few tricks. Washing, good photos, and an engaging advertisement will be your allies. Little Sam will guide you through tasks such as washing, taking photos, and creating a sales listing. Remember, customers buy with their eyes, so use the shine of the paint, a full tank, and quality photos to your advantage. Your profits will allow you to continually grow your company. In the prologue, you’ll have the chance to upgrade the car wash, while in the full game, you’ll unlock additional tools, repair zones, and the customer service office.
