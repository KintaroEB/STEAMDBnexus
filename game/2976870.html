About this gameFull description from Steam+
💗Taiwan Love Story ⁵💗 The Story You're a college senior studying humanities at a university, and during a camping event held by your department, you meet five women from different parts of Taiwan. While you're hanging around the bonfire, a mysterious kiss leaves you lovestruck and yearning for more. Inspiration strikes, and you finally settle on a thesis topic: "The Binary Equation of Love and Happiness." In order to both finish your thesis and find true love, you set out on an adventure around the island. Along the way, you meet up with the women and experience a series of bittersweet romantic encounters bursting with youthful naivety. Your very own Taiwanese love story is about to begin... Features Light, fun simulation gameplay Well-crafted, rich storylines for all 5 women Exciting mini-games that match the game's theme Full voice acting for the women! Deep insights into local Taiwanese life and culture Fully dynamic, immersive CGs Download the free DLCs to unlock even more content! Characters Amy Lin A female professional who's traveled to the big city for work. She's an absent-minded and somewhat clumsy white-collar employee. Though she appears innocent and sweet, deep down she has a rather stubborn side. Amanda Zhang An aristocratic bookworm from Kaohsiung who's quiet and reserved, having been brought up in a strict household. She loves singing, but her father's plan has no room for art, so she has to give up on her dream... Zoe Hai A lively, exuberant girl from Penghu. She loves diving and seems to have endless energy. Ain't nobody don't love Zoe! Lil' Blu A hot betel nut babe from Taichung who acts like she won't let anyone get close. She's got a strong personality and is tough as shit, but deep down, she's just a girl who longs for affection. Sarah Wang The co-owner of a small eel noodle shop in Tainan with her husband. Mrs. Wang is the essence of maternal warmth, and is a caring, captivating milf.
