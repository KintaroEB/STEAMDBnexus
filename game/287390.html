About this gameFull description from Steam+
It is the year 2034. Beneath the ruins of post-apocalyptic Moscow, in the tunnels of the Metro, the remnants of mankind are besieged by deadly threats from outside – and within. Mutants stalk the catacombs beneath the desolate surface, and hunt amidst the poisoned skies above. But rather than stand united, the station-cities of the Metro are locked in a struggle for the ultimate power, a doomsday device from the military vaults of D6. A civil war is stirring that could wipe humanity from the face of the earth forever. As Artyom, burdened by guilt but driven by hope, you hold the key to our survival – the last light in our darkest hour… UPDATED FOR STEAM Experience the post-apocalyptic depths of the Moscow Metro like never before with the Next-Gen Update. METRO Last Light Redux has been enhanced with numerous visual and technical improvements, including 4K Textures; TSR, DLSS and FSR support; increased Geometry and Texture details; improved PBR materials; spatial audio support; improved Animations and sound quality, and more! METRO Last Light Redux also adds supports for the Xbox Controller Impulse Triggers and DualSense controller Adaptive Triggers, immersing you further in the fight for survival. Game Features METRO Last Light Redux continues Artyom’s epic journey in this definitive version – experience the 2013 classic, rebuilt in 2014 and now further updated to take advantage of modern hardware while preserving the gameplay of the original Redux experience Brave the horrors of the apocalypse, and immerse yourself in the Moscow Metro – equip your gasmask and an arsenal of hand-made weaponry as you face the threat of deadly mutants, human foes, and supernatural phenomena in one of the most atmospheric worlds in gaming Two unique Play Styles : ‘Spartan’ and ‘Survival’ – approach the campaign as a slow burn Survival Horror , or tackle it with the combat skills of a Spartan Ranger in these two unique modes The legendary Ranger Mode returns – dare you play the fearsome Ranger Mode? No HUD, UI, deadlier combat and limited resources combine to create the ultimate immersive experience Based on the internationally bestselling novel series by Dmitry Glukhovsky
