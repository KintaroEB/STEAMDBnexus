About this gameFull description from Steam+
Oasis is a Windows 11 driver for SteamVR for VR headsets of the Windows Mixed Reality family, such as the HP Reverb, Samsung Odyssey, Lenovo Explorer, or Dell Visor. This driver does not require the Mixed Reality Portal application and is therefore compatible with the latest versions of Windows 11 (24H2 and future). The driver offers full headset and motion controller tracking as well as a native SteamVR rendering pipeline. It can run OpenVR and OpenXR applications through SteamVR. FULL DOCUMENTATION: Please see the "Visit Website" or "View Manual" links. IMPORTANT: This driver requires and NVIDIA GRAPHICS CARDS or an AMD GRAPHICS CARD. There is no support for INTEL GRAPHICS CARDS due to limitations in the Intel drivers. IMPORTANT: This driver is targeted at Windows 11 users. If you are unwilling to upgrade from Windows 10 to Windows 11, this program is not for you, you may stay on Windows 10 and use the Mixed Reality Portal instead. Both of these restrictions are listed in the "System Requirements" below, under "Minimum". If you cannot meet the minimum requirements, it is your problem, not the developer's problem nor a shortcoming of the application.
