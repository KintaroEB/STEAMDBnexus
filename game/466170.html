About this gameFull description from Steam+
Idling to Rule the Gods is a free to play idle game with a lot of content and some minigames. Depending on your playstyle, you can play it fast or slow. It is mostly text based and offers lots of content, but please don't expect overly good graphics and animations. Idling to Rule the Gods can provide lots of fun for years if you delve into it, but might also not be as easy to understand as some other games. If there is something unclear or you have trouble to progress efficiently, feel free to join our Discord Community which can help you, or check the feedback button ingame. Unleash your inner god in Idling to Rule the Gods, the ultimate idle game where incremental progress reigns supreme! In the year 9001, humanity's folly led to the destruction of countless worlds. You are the last survivor, escaped to a dimension ruled by powerful deities. Now, armed with the unique ability to create shadow clones, you must embark on an epic idle quest for ultimate power. Begin your journey by creating an army of shadow clones. Watch as they tirelessly train, learn powerful skills, and battle fearsome monsters, all while you effortlessly absorb their strength. Your first target: Hyperion, the god who decimated your people. Overcome him and unlock a universe teeming with even stronger deities, each a new challenge to conquer. Idling to Rule the Gods offers a deeply rewarding idle experience: Passive Progression: Sit back and watch your power grow exponentially, even when you're offline. Perfect for busy gamers! Strategic Depth: Manage your shadow clones, upgrade their skills, and optimize your idle gains to become an unstoppable force. Endless Content: Confront a pantheon of gods, discover new worlds, and unlock powerful upgrades in your quest for divine dominance. Rebirth and Ascend: Prestige your progress to unlock powerful bonuses and accelerate your idle climb to the top. Collect and Train: Discover loyal pets and build magnificent monuments to further enhance your power. Are you ready to ascend from lone survivor to supreme deity? Download Idling to Rule the Gods and experience the addictive thrill of idle gaming today!
