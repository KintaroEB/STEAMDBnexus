About this gameFull description from Steam+
Nova-Life is a simulation video game mainly focused on city roleplay in an open world. THE FIRST FRENCH ROLEPLAY GAME! In Nova-Life you can create your own character, find a job and go to work to gain money. The Nova-Life project will have around ten dozen or so jobs in its final version, including the possibility of becoming a self-entrepreneur in order to create your own store, and/or your own company which will offer a wide range of choices in the roleplay. The roleplay of Nova-Life is a French roleplay. Features A pleasant driving system Written communication and VO-IP Multiple vehicles Many professions The jobs have a level hierarchy, which allows you to get a rank in your job and therefore increase your salary
