About this gameFull description from Steam+
Countless souls wander the ring road at night. The title of “fastest” never fades, no matter how times change. The stage of battle is a sealed future Tokyo. Race through the night highways weaving through the city in your customized machine. Defeat the countless rivals and aim to become the fastest on the Shuto Expressway. A One-of-a-Kind SP Battle A unique system that sets itself apart from other racing games. In battles where drivers clash by reducing “Spirit Points,” a numerical measure of mental strength, speed alone is not enough to secure victory. Strategy is required to wear down your opponent’s Spirit Points — an experience where only the rules for winning matter. A Realistic Experience Linked to Reality Over 30 officially licensed manufacturers are featured, including real-world vehicles and aero parts. Enjoy the thrill of speed on faithfully recreated courses of the Shuto Expressway. Customize to Your Style Enhance performance through tuning and refine driving feel with detailed settings to create your ultimate machine. A wide range of customization options — including vinyl graphics, stickers, and body colors — let you personalize your ride. Stories of Unique Rivals A world where over 450 rivals clash in pursuit of supremacy. Even those battling for the top reveal their true selves during moments of respite at parking areas. Souls driven by the desire to be “the fastest” are drawn together, sometimes colliding as they create their own rhythm. Witness the birth of a new “legend” with your own eyes. New Feature in This Title: The “PERK” System A skill-tree system that allows you to unlock various functions using BP earned from winning battles. Permanently enhance driver abilities, increase rewards, and improve convenience.
