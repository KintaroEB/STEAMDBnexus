About this gameFull description from Steam+
The definitive social deduction party game in three dimensions! Among Us 3D is a party game of teamwork and betrayal. Grab your crew of 4-10 players and launch into a fully 3D version of the beloved classic. Note: Among Us 3D is NOT compatible and does not feature crossplay with the original Among Us game. Experience the core Among Us gameplay you know and love, such as tasks, sabotages, Emergency Meetings, and venting — now in 3D! In Classic gameplay, play as either Crewmate or Impostor. — Crewmate Mission: Complete all tasks or eject the Impostor. Keep your eyes and ears open — someone could be lurking right around the corner. Can you and the crew survive?! — Impostor Mission: Sabotage, deceive, and kill all Crewmates! Sneak through vents, craft the perfect lie, frame the innocent, and survive at any means necessary. Enjoy classic Crewmate tasks, reimagined with innovative and immersive first-person controls. Beware the Impostor while you try your hand at all new tasks! Customize your gameplay, comfort, and safety settings to build the best experience possible. Switch things up with Roles, Tag Mode, and Custom Lobbies! Add even more Roles into the mix with fan-favorites like the vent-using Engineer from the original Among Us or some AU3D exclusives, such as the vengeful Wraith or justice-seeking Vigilante! In Tag Mode, it’s Infected versus Crewmates. The Infected try to catch and infect all Crewmates to expand their ranks before the Crewmates can finish a set number of tasks. Lobby up and connect with other Crewmates — including Among Us 3D: VR players! — with native crossplay. Create custom lobbies for you and your closest pals with personalized gameplay, comfort, and moderation settings. Note: Among Us 3D is NOT compatible and does not feature crossplay with the original Among Us game. Fashion your Crewmate with an endless number of color, hat, glove, and skin combinations. Expand your wardrobe even more with Stardust. Purchase this in-game currency* to spend on additional cosmetics and let your true style shine! Note: Stardust is not compatible with and cannot be converted to or from currencies in the original Among Us game. Team up with or betray your friends using your preferred method of communication: — Yap away with native, proximity voice chat. — Setup private lobbies with your friends without needing external chatting apps. — Not a talker? Use intuitive and efficient Quick Text Chat options to stay communicative. — Express yourself through your hands with the all-new Gesture system.
