Build your car, earn sponsors, and compete on the world stage in ONLINE TANDEM BATTLES to become a DRIFT KING! MULTIPLAYER DRIFTING Experience REAL drifting physics! Smoke it up in tournaments of up to 32 players, or kickback on the squad with up to 10 players in Open Lobbies! TUNE YOUR RIDE TO PERFECTION Get your hands DIRTY!
