About this gameFull description from Steam+
Incremental Infinity is a game about reaching infinity. In an abstract world ruled by numbers, you’ll grow exponentially stronger through a truly infinite, procedurally generated skill tree. You fire automatically. No action is required other than interacting with the skill tree. Your number isn’t just your Health—it’s also your size. The bigger your number, the larger and more powerful you become. But be careful: one defeat sends you all the way back to “1”, though you’ll keep your upgrades when you restart. Unlock new sectors of the infinite skill tree, discover unique upgrades, and keep pushing your growth farther and faster. Features An infinite, procedurally generated skill tree Unlock new sectors to expand the skill tree endlessly Add new upgrades to the pool to customize your build Play in full screen or as a side-window companion while doing other things Complete quests to earn more resources Ascend to unlock permanent upgrades and boost XP gain No limit to your power
