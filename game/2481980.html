About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome To The Hunt Welcome to Untamed Kingdom, a multiplayer animal survival game! Explore immersive maps and Hunt for food or avoid predators to keep yourself alive. Will you be up for the challenge? Diverse Playable Animals Choose from a variety of animals, each with its own unique strengths and weaknesses, as you attempt to survive in the wild. Whether you're a cunning wolf, a graceful gazelle, or a powerful bear, your choices will significantly impact your gameplay experience. Realistic Ecosystem Immerse yourself in beautifully rendered and highly detailed ecosystem, ranging from lush forests to harsh deserts. Every environment presents distinct challenges and opportunities, and the changing seasons and weather patterns affect your survival strategy. Encounter a dynamic ecosystem filled with a wide range of wildlife, both predators and prey, all with realistic behaviours and interactions. Fight For Your Survival Test your instincts and adaptability as you navigate the dangers of the wild. Hunt for food, avoid predators, and manage your energy and health to stay alive. Complete various challenges to unlock new abilities and improve your chances of survival. Establish and defend your territory from rival animals. Engage in territorial disputes, and use your wits and abilities to maintain your claim.
