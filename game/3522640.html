About this gameFull description from Steam+
Shape limitless stories, share memorable adventures Embrace complete freedom and forge truly unique sagas with AI narration adapting seamlessly to any input. Evolve beyond multiple choice responses and guiding narratives to embrace the subtle nuances of storytelling. Simply type genuine responses and actions to engage with the story as naturally as playing a tabletop RPG. Take advantage of a free-form character creation system, filling out CRPG-style character sheets with as much detail desired. Type out characters’ personalities, class, speech patterns, and more to forge distinct heroes and villains alike. Import and export character sheets to share your original characters on social media, or invite others’ to join the adventure. Design any adventure, from a legendary campaign to a cheeky side quest, and complete missions to gather memories and skills meticulously generated to fit each character and storyline. Once completed, share a Quest Log with the world, or load and play original scenarios created by fellow adventurers to continue the search for the ultimate story. Just by Typing, You Can Be a Hero or a Villain There are no multiple-choice options in this game. Your actions and dialogue are dictated entirely by your own free-form input. No matter the content or length of what you write, the AI will adapt and expand the story accordingly. Describe your character's movements in painstaking detail, or watch as a single, casual line of dialogue turns the tide of the entire situation. In Saga & Seeker, it's not just the story that's dynamic. The background music and sound effects are also controlled by the AI, changing to match the mood of the narrative. You can even shape the atmosphere of the story as you see fit. Your Ideal Characters Come to Life. Pour In All Your Headcanon. Just like its gameplay, Saga & Seeker features a free-form character creation system, allowing you to fill pages with detailed, hand-written settings. Subtle nuances that are difficult to express in typical multiple-choice creation systems can be perfectly replicated through your own words. (And if you can't think of anything, it's fine to leave fields blank! You can always edit or add more details later.) Furthermore, you can create not only your protagonist but also the party members who join the story. They will not only converse with you but also interact naturally with each other, acting according to their own personalities. Characters that once lived only in your mind will leave your hands and begin to live and breathe on their own. That is the essence of character creation in Saga & Seeker. Share Your Creativity and Expand the World Saga & Seeker includes a "Character Sheet" import/export feature, allowing you to easily share the characters you've created. Show off your prized characters, or invite a friend's creation to join your party. And if you want to unleash even more creativity, you can load and play original scenarios created by players. Simply fill out the official text file template. Craft your own scenarios in your search for the ultimate story. Create Your Own Stories, Whenever and However You Want In Saga & Seeker, you decide when a scenario ends. If you're enjoying a particular story, you can continue it for as long as you like, until you're completely satisfied. You can pause a scenario midway through, or even scrap a story and start over if it's not going well. Enjoy creating stories just for yourself, without worrying about anyone else, whenever and however you please. Additionally, upon clearing a scenario, a "Log" is saved for you to revisit anytime. These logs can be exported as files, so you can share your favorite stories with friends! About In-Game Currency In Saga & Seeker, "Ink" is consumed to utilize generative AI features. The cost of Ink covers generative AI API fees, server maintenance, and other operational costs. Please note that the amount of Ink required for generation may fluctuate based on changes in AI service pricing and currency exchange rates. The base game includes an initial amount of Ink.
