About this gameFull description from Steam+
Become a Ninja Master Start your adventure as a Ninja in this incremental game, defeat incoming enemies with a well-timed Katana Slash, research new technologies and build up your own village. Recruit and Conquer Embark on an enemy controlled island to establish your very own settlement. Recruit legendary heroes and send them out on missions to conquer enemy provinces to unite the land. Collect a zoo Collect and befriend various pets that will help you out on your adventures. Have your friend by your side to raise your mutual bond through your journeys and make your friends even more powerful. Key Features Defeat incoming enemies, collect coins, earn gold Build up your village to earn even more gold even while you are offline! Buy Research Upgrades to increase your power Ascend and learn epic and permanent Skill Upgrades Collect pets and award them with treats Recruit heroes and unite the land Perfect various Challenge levels Complete Quests for rewards Solve weekly riddles Unlock hundreds of Achievements Unlock characters and customize them with dyes Crossplatform cloud saving - take your game on the go!
