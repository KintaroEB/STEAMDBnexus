About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to ROCKET BOT ROYALE, a fun new take on the online battle royale formula. Powerful, wall-climbing, rocket-jumping, artillery-pumping Robo-Tanks are the vehicle of choice in this fast-paced shootout, where the goal is to survive longer than the competition. Collect the loot to upgrade your arsenal, tunnel into the terrain to take cover and collect buried treasure, and avoid the rising water levels to be the BLAST ONE STANDING! Launch deadly rockets from your custom robo-tank to destroy the competition in this cross-platform Battle Royale set on massive sinking islands. Compete on the global leaderboards to earn bragging rights and prestigious awards. These islands are populated by strange enemy tanks. They aren't the smartest, but they are certainly a nuisance. Destroy them quickly before they get a lucky shot off at you. They will drop any loot they've collected when destroyed, so target them when you're low on power-ups or coins! Explore the islands for hidden loot and items to help you in your quest for the Victory Royale. Crates dropped from above can contain weapons and health which can make the difference between winning and losing! FEATURES: Explosive Tank Battles – Blast other players off sinking islands in chaotic, physics-driven combat! Dynamic Islands – The battlefield never stays still — terrain is destructible, forcing you to keep moving. Skill-Based Matchmaking – Face off against players at your level, or rise through the ranks to prove your mastery. Play with Friends – Squad up in custom lobbies, spectate your friends’ matches, or invite the community for private tournaments. Daily Rotating Game Modes – Keep it fresh with new twists every day, from Zero Gravity to Rapid Fire and more. Cross-Platform Play – Jump in from PC, mobile, or browser — all players share the same battlefield. Leaderboards & Seasons – Compete for global glory, climb seasonal ladders, and earn unique rewards each season. Collect & Customize – Unlock and equip unique tanks, trails, and gliders to stand out on the battlefield. Community-Powered Development – From fan art making it into the game to live dev streams and community votes — players shape the future of Rocket Bot Royale.
