Liz and her mentor Aisha, are talented witches. But despite their talent, a thief manages to steal a powerful tome called the “Grimoire of Darkness” from them. Chasing after the man, they end up near the town of Bramach. Aisha senses danger emanating from the area and orders Liz to stay behind.
