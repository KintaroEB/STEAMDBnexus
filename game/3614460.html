About this gameFull description from Steam+
Hypermarket Simulator is an immersive first-person retail sim where you design, manage, and grow a massive one-stop shopping paradise that goes way beyond groceries. Build Your Dream Hypermarket Turn an empty shell into a buzzing retail empire. From electronics and clothing to sportswear, frozen food, home décor, plants and more, you’ll manage a growing catalog of 540+ products under one roof. Lay out your aisles, design the floor plan, choose where deliveries arrive, decorate and name your store, and craft the perfect shopping experience for your customers. Master Inventory & Realistic Storage Stock hundreds of different products, but think like a real store manager. Frozen and refrigerated goods must be stored correctly or they’ll spoil and become unsellable. Set up backroom storage with different rack types, create custom stock areas anywhere in the store, and let your staff use them to keep shelves full and your operation running smoothly. Hire, Train & Automate As your hypermarket grows, so does the chaos. Keep things under control by: Installing self-checkout kiosks to reduce queues Hiring staff to restock, assist customers, and run the checkouts Assigning specific tasks and training employees to move faster, stock more efficiently, and work as cashiers Find the perfect balance between speed, efficiency, profit, and service quality. Stock, Sell & Strategize You decide what your store is known for. Will you be the go-to place for high-end electronics, or the neighborhood favorite for everyday essentials? Set prices, monitor what’s flying off the shelves, optimize shelf organization, and fine-tune your restocking strategy. Smart decisions lead to happy customers and a booming bottom line. Expand & Evolve Start with a modest floor space and grow into a sprawling hypermarket. Reinvest your profits to expand the store, unlock new departments and product categories, and upgrade your equipment. Step by step, turn your small shop into a full-scale retail empire built around smart planning and customer focus. Make It Uniquely Yours Name your store, customize floors and walls, and make creative use of windows and doors to shape the perfect interior. Add decorations, build dedicated staff areas and offices, and give your hypermarket its own personality, both on the shop floor and behind the scenes. Step inside, grab your scanner, and see if you have what it takes to run the ultimate hypermarket.
