About this gameFull description from Steam+
Lootbound: take risks, build your squad and create your build Every run starts with you alone. As you move across the map, choose a route that leads to valuable loot. Along the way, you will meet companions who will help you fight this difficult battle! Team and equipment You need to collect a separate set of equipment for the hero and each companion. Items do not have fixed slots. You can rotate them and place them in the inventory as long as there is free space. Item auras Items become truly powerful when they start affecting one another. Some items have auras that strengthen weapons or armor in adjacent cells. Sometimes moving an item by a single cell is enough to change the entire build. Companions and abilities In combat, you control only the main hero. Companions act on their own. They already know what to do... Create the final GUARDIAN At the end of each biome, you choose one of two boss parts. Each part changes the Guardian's appearance and power. After completing all biomes, you will have to face the final boss. Risk and reward Some rewards in Lootbound cannot be obtained just like that. In special rooms, you are offered several options with different difficulty levels. The more valuable the reward, the harder it is to obtain with a dice roll. This is Lootbound...
