About this gameFull description from Steam+
Last Group Out is a casual top-down extraction shooter set in a collapsed society where only survival matters. Inspired by "Escape from Tarkov" and "The Escapists". Scavenge & Survive Enter a hostile, abandoned land teeming with danger and opportunity. Grab what you can before time runs out, or risk losing it all. Use your loot runs to level up, grow your wealth, and prepare for greater threats. Loot, Sell, Upgrade Find gear, weapons, and oddities scattered across the wasteland. Sell what you don’t need to buy stronger equipment. Customize and enhance your weapons with powerful buffs. Level Up & Evolve Gain XP by defeating enemies, looting gear, and completing quests. Unlock new weapons, equipment, and boss challenges. Upgrade your loadout to match your playstyle: stealthy, aggressive, or something in between. Use All Your Senses Danger lurks in every shadow. Stop. Look. Listen. Smell. Use your instincts and stealth to stay one step ahead of enemies and rivals. Solo or Co-op Go it alone, or team up with up to 3 friends. Take on missions, level up, and extract with hard-earned loot, together.
