About this gameFull description from Steam+
I Wanna Maker is a precision platformer with a robust level editor and online level sharing, based on I Wanna Be The Guy fangames. Create complex levels using a wide variety of objects, a powerful events system, and customizable visuals. Browse levels online, traverse a hand-crafted campaign, take on challenge modes, and more! Level Editor Features Intuitive but powerful level editor with 70+ objects A flexible events system which enables endless possibilities Dozens of visuals for blocks, spikes, and backgrounds, each color-customizable 150+ songs to choose from Props, lights, and particles to visually enhance your levels Add optional objectives to your levels with collectible gems Gameplay Features Play 240,000+ user-created levels made for a variety of skill levels Speedrun leaderboards for each level with watchable replays Campaign mode with dozens of high-quality levels Incredibly precise and responsive controls Take on challenge modes consisting of randomly chosen levels Save levels to custom playlists and share them online Follow your favorite makers so you don't miss their new levels Customize your character's appearance No monetization I Wanna Maker is free to play, with no monetization of any kind. We hope you enjoy making levels and participating in the community!
