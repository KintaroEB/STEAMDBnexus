About this gameFull description from Steam+
A relaxing mushroom-foraging simulator that immerses you in a peaceful forest atmosphere through meditative gameplay. If you miss mushroom picking—or have never tried it—here’s your chance to escape the city bustle and find inner peace. Numerous mushroom species await you From chanterelles and porcini to russulas and toadstools—collect everything in sight or hunt only for valuable specimens. Pickle your mushrooms A single mushroom, a whole batch of one species, or a signature mix—the choice is yours. Dry your mushrooms Chop firewood, light the stove, and dry mushrooms over the coals.
