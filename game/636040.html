About this gameFull description from Steam+
Pixel Worlds is back. Built by many of the original developers, Pixel Worlds returns as a free-to-play online sandbox MMO where players create, trade, explore, and shape the game together. This is a fresh start for everyone — with progression and the in-game economy reset to create a fair and stable foundation for the future. Pixel Worlds Pixel Worlds is a free to play online mmo sandbox indie game that let’s you create, play, craft and build. Have unique adventures, get gems, play with friends, get friends along the way and collect items to show off! Join millions of other players in this free to play online multiplatform MMO sandbox adventure and be a part of the friendly online community. BUILD - Create your own worlds - from parkour and adventure maps to farms, social hubs, and more - Mine resources and craft blocks, props, and items to shape your world - Build spaces for others to explore, play, and interact CUSTOMIZE - Create your own unique look with a wide range of items - Craft, trade, and collect gear, outfits, and accessories - Discover new styles through gameplay and the player-driven economy ADVENTURE - Explore dungeons, fight enemies, and collect rewards - Progress your character and unlock stronger items over time FARM - You can crossbreed, farm, grow and combine almost anything in Pixel Worlds. Building blocks, furniture, tools, items, weapons and even clothes. - While farming you also get gems and collect items. Use them to buy, trade and craft other items. FISHING - Compete in weekly fishing tournaments to win huge prizes and get gems. - Get gems by selling the fish or use them to craft new fishing gear to catch even bigger and more valuable fish! TRADE - A fully player-driven economy - Trade items, gear, and worlds with other players - Value is shaped by the community — not fixed systems * But be careful! Other players will try to profit from the trade as well so make sure you won’t get scammed! * PLAY TOGETHER - Explore worlds created by other players - Take part in community-made games and activities - Build, trade, and play together in a shared online world COMMUNITY FIRST Pixel Worlds has always been shaped by its players. This relaunch focuses on: - Fair progression - A stable in-game economy - A stronger foundation for long-term play We’re rebuilding the game together — with the community at its core.
