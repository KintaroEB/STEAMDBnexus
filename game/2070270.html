About this gameFull description from Steam+
Ragnarok shattered the world. Rise as a Runari to rebuild it. Master chaotic physics-based combat inspired by Zelda and Devil May Cry. Explore islands and dungeons, smash monsters, loot and craft powerful gear, and grow your home in the sky. Play solo or online co-op with up to 4 players. Master the Chaos Unleash mayhem in a reactive, breakable world. Chain abilities into brutal team combos. Smash pillars onto monsters, lasso enemies into tornadoes, send enemies flying across the map, fire Greebles out of cannons, or kick exploding barrels into the fray. Rise Together Join up to three friends in online co-op. Share every weapon and ability you unlock as you grow stronger together and discover new playable Runari. Turn on Unbridled Chaos to lasso your friends in combat, because they deserve it. Forge Infinite Possibilities Build your workshop on Odin Shell, the ancient turtle who has summoned you to restore the world. With visual crafting, you'll never have to wait for a friend stuck in a menu. Forge tons of weapons, gear, and powerful abilities to use in battle or sell them to fund your journey. Explore the Realms Link the Bifrost gates to unlock new islands and handcrafted dungeons that rearrange every visit, including that one room you swore you cleared already. Solve puzzles, fight challenging enemies, and grab valuable loot as you travel from the icy peaks of Frostvaldr to the deserts of Dustlanger to find the gods. Run a Fantasy Shop Turn a tiny stall into the most famous shop in the skies. Stock shelves, fill orders, charm customers, and watch your reputation spread as the best (and only) store in the skies. Team Up with Fantastic Creatures Blins keep your shop running and Gnasher devours anything you find (and vomits up treasure in return, a fair trade). Chaos is great for business.
