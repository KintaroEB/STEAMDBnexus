About this gameFull description from Steam+
You’re not just running a video rental store. You’re running out of time. RUN THE WORLD'S LAST VIDEO RENTAL STORE You’ve inherited the last video rental store in town. You're broke, drowning in late returns, and streaming giant RentNet just opened across the street. Run your store by day. Do whatever it takes to survive by night. The longer you stay in business, the more chaotic things become. BUILD YOUR STORE Order movies, snacks, toys, and rare collectibles. Stock shelves, manage customers, and expand your store from a dying shop into a thriving business. Hire employees, automate operations, and optimize every inch of your store. Every decision determines whether you survive another week. Oh and did we mention we have PLENTY of store customization options? HUSTLE TO SURVIVE The store alone isn’t enough. Run underground fight clubs. Gamble on scratcher tickets. Install a claw machine. Take on shady side hustles across the city. Some will make you rich. Some will ruin you. CREATE YOUR OWN FILM EMPIRE Produce and release your own original films to compete with RentNet. Manage production, release films, and turn your store into something far bigger than a rental shop. EXPLORE A CITY FULL OF OPPORTUNITY Explore a low-poly open city by foot or scooter. Discover secrets, track NPC schedules, and unlock increasingly dangerous and lucrative opportunities. PLAY SOLO OR WITH FRIENDS Run the store alone or with friends in co-op multiplayer. PLEASE NOTE: Co-op is temporarily disabled in the main build due to performance instability. Saves you create now will be co-op compatible when it returns. To play co-op today, switch to the Legacy branch (right-click the game in your Library → Properties → Betas → "Legacy"), which runs the June build and your older saves. Restoring co-op is our top priority. Timeline to follow here and in our Discord once 1.0 hotfixes settle. Divide responsibilities, expand faster, and take bigger risks together. 100% HUMAN-MADE Every asset, illustration, and piece of marketing was created by real artists.
