About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Walking Dead: No Man's Land is a tactical RPG that plunges you into the harsh and unforgiving world overrun by the undead. Your mission is to lead a group of survivors through endless dangers, manage resources, and make tough decisions that will determine their fate. ▸ FORM THE ULTIMATE TEAM Gather your ultimate team of survivors, featuring iconic characters from the show, including Rick, Michonne, Daryl, Maggie, Glenn, Negan, Carl, and so many more! Train and upgrade your team to take on hordes of zombies and relive the most intense scenes from The Walking Dead. ▸ FORGE ALLIANCES In this brutal world, your guild is your strongest shield, guiding you through the apocalypse and pushing you forward. Team up with your friends and join the Guild Wars to battle against real players worldwide. Whose team of survivors will come out on top? ▸ BUILD A SAFE CAMP Build a fortified camp to safeguard your survivors, enhance their arsenal, and tend to their wounds after strategic battles and PVP raids. Keep your camp thriving by managing resources and providing food, ensuring your survivors endure the harsh world of The Walking Dead. ▸ EXPLORE Explore various game modes: Season, Story, Distance, Outpost, and weekly challenges. Gather exclusive resources, gain unique skills, and acquire powerful weapons. Master each mode to keep your team ahead of the undead! ▸ STRATEGIC COMBAT Take on hordes of walkers in iconic locations from The Walking Dead, such as Hilltop and Alexandria! Each new battleground introduces unique enemies designed to challenge your tactics. Your survival hinges on every tactical decision and strategic move you make! Survive the Zombie Apocalypse in the World of The Walking Dead!
