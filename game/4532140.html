About this gameFull description from Steam+
HUSHED is a 1–4 player online co-op horror game where your team is split by different senses, tied together by a rope, and hunted by a monster that can hear your voice. Work with your team, complete the objectives, and escape while a monster hunts you. In Senses Mode, each player has a different role. The Eye sees the monster. The Listener hears the monster. The Stunner carries the stun gun, but cannot see or hear the monster. No player has the full picture. Your team must guide each other and use every role correctly. All players are connected by a rope. Stay close, move together, and survive together. One player’s mistake can put the whole team in danger. Your microphone is part of the game. Talking, screaming, or making noise can reveal your location and attract the monster. You can only hear teammates who are near you. If someone moves too far away, their voice fades with distance. FEATURES : • 1–4 player online co-op horror • Senses Mode with unique player roles • Optional Tied Together mode • Monster reacts to your microphone • Proximity voice chat • Different objectives • Randomized items • Dynamic scares WARNING: This game contains horror elements, loud jump scares, sudden scares, and flashing lights. Player discretion is advised.
