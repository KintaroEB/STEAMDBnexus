About this gameFull description from Steam+
“Ziji Liu, a native of Nanyang, is a famous person with good moral character. After hearing about this, he plans to go there.” "During the reign-period Taiyuan of the Jin dynasty there lived in Wu‑ling a certain fisherman. One day, as he followed the course of a stream, he became unconscious of the distance he had traveled. All at once, he came upon a grove of blossoming peach trees that lined either bank for hundreds of paces..." For thousands of years, countless travelers have lost in the mountains, looking for the legendary paradise. They lingered, lost their way, or died soon after. In the description they left: It seems to be a perfect utopia. But if this utopia is eternal, is it not another form of hell? What is the price for the extinct Hosha kingdom, which possessed the secret of immortality that even the emperor could not hope for? Peach blossoms wither, dead bones are all over the ground, and equinox flowers wait a thousand years to bloom The nine hundred souls left here are longing for the arrival of the "Man for Flower Burial“. Shuang Hua in the daytime looks forward to going to the outside world with you and taking a look at the endless stream of horses and carriages. Shuang Hua at night hopes you bury everything… "Can you take me to the outside world?" The goddess in charge of the sacrifices is said to be the descendant of the mountain god who guided villagers to take refuge in the Peach Blossom Spring. She is gentle and kind, curious about the outside world. “The rules of the day do not apply to the night.” A mysterious girl appears at night and has the same appearance as Shuang Hua in the daytime, but their temperaments are quite different. Sometimes indifferent and noble, sometimes charming and affectionate. She is the master of the Peach Blossom Spring and has the power of omniscience and omnipotence. An amnestic girl in the village that looks related to those mysteries. A gentle widow during the daytime, and an intrepid blade master at night. A kind village head during the daytime, always hungry at night. He is a hunter and butcher in the village who slaughtered the livestock. A naughty kid in the village who like to play cricket fighting. - High degree of freedom, - Text with 200,000 non-repeating words - 20 different endings Choose your favorite line to experience, and explore the village from different perspectives The Goddess, amnestic little sister, pretty widow, an aged village head, and strong hunter are the main characters. They all have their own backstories and unique endings (love or friendship). Some options are locked in the first playthrough, and hidden options will be unlocked when replaying. Japanese-style impasto style, the pursuit of the ultimate fineness. The clothing and village environments are designed by referring to the clothing characteristics of the Qin and Han dynasties in China. The art involves many opposing themes such as "day and night", "peach blossom and equinox flower" and "life and death". Full-text dubbing Hanser, a well-known KOL and voice actor, dubs the heroine for more than 600 lines. 森中人 serves as the dubbing director, ensuring the professionalism of the dubbing. Assisted development was provided by Nekoday, the developer of "Christmas Tina: Ephemeral Views Of Winter". Nekoday is responsible for UI design, PV design, art design, sound effects, and soundtrack. 【Staff】 Development Team: ZeroCreation Games · Development Team of "Lay a Beauty to Rest: The Darkness Peach Blossom Spring" Developer/Scriptwriter/Performer: 嵇零Zero Scene Concept Designer: fronzenX Character Concept Designer: Man Man CG: Man Man、Cha Zi、fronzenX Programmers: Xin Hun, Yu Ye, San Jin Executive producer: Xiong Zhen Art Assistant: Xiao Gu Tou Soundtrack: Candy_wind Dubbing Director: Sen Zhong Ren Voice Actors: Hanser, Jing Zhu, Wu Gu You You, Yifan Wei, Shujin Cai, Hua Ling, Zhixiao Liu, Sen Zhong Ren, Yin Xia Lao Gui, kinsen, Qinwen Li, Mic, Jie Mo, Rui Tang, Mo Ling, Pi Xiu Gang, Yi Ze Xiao K Outsourcing Services: Nekoday UI/PV/Art Designer: Gu Luo (Nekoday) Parts of Sound Effects/Soundtrack: Ka Te Tuan Zhang (Nekoday) Outsourced Project Management: Shi Si (Nekoday) Product Advisor: HD (龍之咲) Game Testers: Ice_935, Jiu Yue, Rachelle
