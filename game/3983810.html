About this gameFull description from Steam+
"The thrill of spiking in volleyball. Why don't you try it out?" Developed by an indie developer who loves volleyball! The sports game made after numerous trial and error to contain the appeal of volleyball! ■ Try out various volleyball strategies! Quicks, pipes, open attacks, and fast tempo attacks in a 3v3 volleyball match! Achieve your greatest spike in diverse scenarios in your screen! ■ Immersive gameplay! Experience the immersion of actually playing on the court yourself by controlling only 1 player on the court from start to finish. ■ Appealing storyline! Meet the attractive characters that appear in the universe of The Spike! Join Siwoo with his journey in both volleyball and inner growth. ■ Diverse volleyball contents! Is normal volleyball boring for you? Enjoy different types of volleyball contents such as tournament, colosseum, beach volleyball, and more.
