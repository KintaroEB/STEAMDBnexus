About this gameFull description from Steam+
Angel Legion is the latest 3D girls idle RPG adventure game. Angel Legion takes place in the magnificent and profound Sheila Galaxy, which was once home to ancient civilizations. However, the shadow of war looms over humanity, making it difficult to ignore the clouds of time and the succession of civilizations. Among the influences shaping this conflict are the Star Alliance, the Oriental Force, and the Rebel Army. These three factions hold the most power and compete for control of the galaxy. Combat intensifies, where advanced technological weaponry has emerged on the battlefield. Among these advances are powerful and beautiful goddesses who have become the most dazzling stars in combat. At the same time, an dark plan that has been long in the making is now unfolding. #Game Features# ▶ Space Exploration & Ladies Assembly Encounter more than 100 uniquely charming angels in the vastness of space! -> All babe support customizable appearances. -> Access a massive collection of costumes for free, which can be shared among all characters. ▶ Sweet Home & Intimate Connections Deepen your bond with the angels, unlocking diverse special storylines! The longer you spend time together, the higher the affection level between Miya and the Commander becomes. By interacting closely with Miya in the in-game home, you will discover new secrets! ▶ 3D Dynamic Scenes & Exquisite Graphics Goddess will interact intimately with Commanders through high-quality 2D/3D animations! Embark on an adventure full of unknowns voyage amongst the stars, as the story unfolds to its ultimate conclusion. ▶ Idle Battles & Strategic Victories Deploy your team of goddesses, who will automatically battle for you! Simply wait to obtain advanced gear and elite character. Enhance your combat power significantly with clever strategies and equipment combinations, embarking on unprecedented and legendary challenges to win rare treasures!
