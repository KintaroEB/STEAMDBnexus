About this gameFull description from Steam+
The world’s most realistic freestyle scooter game is finally here. Ride the skatepark, hit the streets, boost the mega ramp, fully customize your scooter & throw down all of the real life trick combinations you could dream of within this physics based scooter game. Experience total freedom & utilize your imagination in a world where the possibilities are truly endless. REALISTIC PHYSICS Experience the thrill of authentic scooter riding with our cutting-edge physics system. Immerse yourself in the dynamic and true-to-life physics designed to emulate the variations of real-world scooter movements. Our system is meticulously crafted to ensure that every grind, jump, and trick feels natural and responsive, allowing you to seamlessly flow around the skateparks as if you were riding in the physical world. Master the physics, and unlock a world of possibilities for creative expression within the game. PLAY WITH YOUR FRIENDS ScooterFlow’s multiplayer mode brings the social side of scootering to life. Ride together in real time, film lines, and explore parks with your friends or the wider community. Whether you’re linking combos, stomping new tricks, or just cruising through your favorite map, multiplayer captures the same energy and connection found in real sessions at the park. You can drop into public lobbies or host your own sessions, making it easy to ride the way you want. SCOOTER & CLOTHING CUSTOMIZATION Unleash your creativity in crafting the perfect ride! Design your ultimate custom scooter with an extensive array of real-life parts sourced from the world's best brands, ensuring an unparalleled level of personalization. Featuring renowned brands such as Apex, Ethic, Lucky, Aztek, Madd Gear, Envy, and an impressive lineup of 20+ other leading manufacturers. On top of over a trillion custom scooter options, fully customize your character’s clothing and express your style in countless ways. REPLICA SKATEPARKS Ride real-world parks brought to life in ScooterFlow. Each location has been recreated 1:1 from its real counterpart, capturing the layout, flow, and atmosphere that riders know and love. The game features the iconic Madrid Urban Sports competition park in Spain; as well as infamous New Zealand skateparks: Washington Way, Knights Stream, Bay Skate and the renowned X Air skatepark, a virtual version of NZ’s biggest scooter competition park. These authentic replicas celebrate real scooter culture from around the world, letting you ride the same parks that shape the sport today. REPLAY EDITOR Film your lines and create one-of-a-kind videos showcasing your riding. PRO RIDERS Ride as your favorite scootering legends, including the sport’s most iconic names: Ryan Williams, Juzzy Carter, Raymond Warner, Kai Saunders, Dylan Morrison, Dante Hutchinson, Mia Catalano, Cooper Klaar, Cody Flom, and Dakota Schuetz. OFFICIAL SOUNDTRACK Inspired by classic action-sports soundtracks, ScooterFlow’s music blends indie, rock, hip-hop and rap with raw street energy to keep every run feeling alive. CUSTOM MAPS, COMMUNITY, CREATIVITY Dive into an ever-evolving adventure with our custom maps feature. Download maps from our vibrant Discord community for a creativity boost beyond standard skateparks. Explore unique challenges, hidden gems, and landscapes crafted by fellow players. Contribute your own maps and join the collaborative spirit, ensuring the game's adventure is constantly player-driven and evolving. Experience endless exploration and infinite creativity with each session, where player-created content tells unique stories and offers exciting challenges. This game requires a controller to play.
