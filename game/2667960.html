About this gameFull description from Steam+
The latest installment in the GBVS series is taking the genre to new heights! Engage in fast-paced battles with simplified input options in Granblue Fantasy Versus: Rising, the definitive fighting game experience for beginners and veterans alike for free with the Free Edition! Challenge yourself in a variety of single-player modes, engage in battle offline and online, or claim your crown with friends in an all-new party mode: Grand Bruise Legends! Stay Focused on the Battle, Not the Controls The Free Edition offers a weekly rotating roster, featuring Gran and 3 other characters (including DLC fighters) that can be used in ranked and casual matches online! Newcomers can jump right into the action with easy-to-learn controls—no need to memorize complex combos or inputs. Focus on strategy and reading your opponent while discovering the thrill of competitive fighting games. In addition to online matches, you can duke it out against friends offline in Versus Mode or take on a gauntlet of CPU opponents in Survival Mode! Claim Your Crown in Grand Bruise Legends! for Free! Step into cozy and inviting online lobbies inspired by charming fantasy RPG towns, where you and your friends can hang out—or jump right into Grand Bruise! tourneys! Transfer Your Data to the Full Version Upgrade to the full version without losing your progress! Save data from the Free Edition can be carried over—including character level progress up to level 80, main story completion up to the end of Part 1: A Tale of Blue Skies, Battle Pass rewards, and more. The full version unlocks the default roster of 28 playable characters, with even more available as DLC. Explore additional game modes, from advanced training options, a digital figure studio where you can create custom dioramas using in-game 3D models, and the continuation of the main story beyond Part 1. Note: Save data cannot be transferred between different platforms.
