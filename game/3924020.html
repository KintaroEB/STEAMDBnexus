About this gameFull description from Steam+
Until North Ends is an open-world survival crafting adventure with a low-poly stylized Nordic vibe for solo adventurers or groups. Embrace the harsh environments or savor the quiet tranquility of farm-life at your shelter; how you choose to survive is up to you. Sail across the waters to find new islands, dive into caves and dungeons to plunder loot or travel through peaceful meadows. Explore procedurally generated worlds full of unique encounters. Take down dangerous creatures in melee combat while dawning heavy armor, use distance to your advantage while shooting them with a bow, or harness a powerful chain lightning; it's up to you how you approach combat. Construct your home to protect and prepare for your next adventure. Relax, watch your plants grow, and decorate your cozy corner. To survive in this harsh world, you will need to craft the best equipment possible, and wooden swords will not cut it. Refine ores, combine materials, extract plants in the alchemy table, cure leather, and much more to gain access to rare materials. Create your own character that will stand out. From hair color to tattoos, you and your fellow adventurers can customize your look to suit your style.
