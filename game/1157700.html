About this gameFull description from Steam+
Pro Wrestling Sim is a text-based pro-wrestling booking simulator. Control a wrestling promotion, decide your roster, and create storylines, feuds and matches where the winners and losers are in your hands. Features Promises Your workers will request that you make promises to them. This can be asking for a match with a worker, asking for a title shot, or even a title win. Agree to make a promise and you better deliver - you don't want to upset your top star or his friends! Edit Database With the Edit Database functionality, you can design, build and grow a world of your own creation, based on reality or your own imagination, and share it with friends.
