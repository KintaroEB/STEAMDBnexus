About this gameFull description from Steam+
Celebrate 20 Years of Forza Motorsport with the 20th Anniversary Update! Master 144 extreme turns on the legendary Fujimi Kaido track and prove your skills across 16.5 kilometers (10.24 miles) of serpentine Japanese mountain roads. Race and drift your favorite cars, or experiment with new car builds, alongside your friends and other players in Open Track Day and Drift Meetups. Revisit the iconic cover cars from past Forza Motorsport games – each with newly updated audio – and unlock commemorative driver suits in the 20th Anniversary Tour. Get behind the wheel of highly requested new-to-Motorsport cars* including the 2022 Acura NSX Type S, 2024 Nissan Z NISMO, 2005 #1 Sierra Enterprises Lancer Evolution Time Attack, 1984 Toyota #25 Horsepower Techs Starlet Time Attack, and 1995 Formula Drift #34 Toyota Supra MkIV. Join us for the 20th Anniversary celebrations and relax after every thrilling race with a refreshed music playlist, featuring new compositions and a selection of iconic tunes from Forza Motorsport 3-6. Immerse yourself in an expanding world of competition in Forza Motorsport. Race the latest cars and tracks and hone your skills in epic solo tours and online events. Updates are regularly released with new features, gameplay and enhancements. Race over 500 real-world cars including modern race cars and more than 100 cars new to Forza Motorsport. Make every lap count across 30 living tracks with multiple layouts to master and returning fan-favorite locations Fujimi Kaido, Nürburgring Nordschleife and Sunset Peninsula, each featuring live on-track scoring, fully dynamic time-of-day with weather and unique driving conditions that ensure no two laps ever look or play the same. Experience a cutting-edge simulation with incredible photorealistic visuals that deliver real-time ray tracing on-track, new damage and dirt accumulation systems, and massively upgraded physics featuring powerful assists and a 48x improvement in tire fidelity. Out-build the competition by earning over 800 performance upgrades and race our most advanced AI opponents yet in a fun and rewarding single-player campaign, the Builders Cup Career Mode. Install modifications to your favorite cars at any time –new car progression options give you control in how you build and tune cars. Compete for the podium in Featured Multiplayer events with a race weekend inspired structure or create the racing you and your friends desire in Free Play. Online racing is safer, more fun and more competitive with AI-powered Forza Race Regulations, tire and fuel strategy, and new driver and safety ratings. Forza Motorsport takes advantage of cutting-edge PC hardware and technologies, including Ray Traced Global Illumination for enhanced visual fidelity and realism. Maximize your performance with NVIDIA DLSS 2 and AMD FSR 2.2. Experience resolutions up to 4K and beyond with HDR calibration, unlocked framerates in single-player, cross-play and cross-save with Xbox Series X|S players, shader precaching and more. Customize your PC gameplay with adjustable FOV, ultrawide resolutions on-track and dial in your preferred graphics settings using benchmark mode. Plug in your favorite devices with DirectInput API featuring layout remapping, force-feedback on wheels and Xbox controller rumble. **Models redeemable in the Challenge Hub or Showroom using credits earned through play. 2024 Nissan Z NISMO available until 6/25/2025.
