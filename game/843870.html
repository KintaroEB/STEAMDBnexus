Card Games Mega Collection features 150+ card game variations. Includes casino and family style games, plus 5 bonus games for kids. Enjoy the following games: Over 100 Solitaire variations. Texas Hold 'Em and other casino poker games. Ten popular home poker games with wild cards, split pots, and customized betting limits. Casino style Blackjack!
