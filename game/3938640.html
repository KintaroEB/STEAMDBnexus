About this gameFull description from Steam+
🍣 Welcome to Ugoku Sushi Bar, where conveyor belts, cozy vibes, and culinary creativity collide! 🍵 You’ve just taken over a small sushi bar tucked away in a charming Japanese town. With a humble kitchen and a head full of dreams, it’s up to you to turn your little eatery into the talk of the town. As the conveyor belt keeps moving, so does your journey. Slice, roll, and plate an ever-growing variety of delicious Japanese dishes, serve hungry customers, discover over 100 sushi recipes, and experiment with a Minecraft-style crafting system to create new sushi combinations. 🎏 Prepare and Plate: Craft everything from classic sushi to creative Japanese side dishes. Timing, accuracy, and presentation matter! 🍥 Conveyor Belt Chaos: Fulfill orders by placing them on the belt at the right time and in the right order, or face some seriously confused customers. 😺 Quirky Regulars: Meet an eclectic cast of recurring customers, each with unique tastes, stories, and personalities. Some just want sushi, others want a chat. 📚 Learn & Master Recipes: Unlock new recipes as you progress, from simple nigiri to specialty rolls and surprise seasonal dishes. 🛠️ Upgrade Your Kitchen: Invest in new tools, appliances, and ingredients to boost your efficiency and expand your menu. 🌸 Cozy Atmosphere: A warm, relaxing art style paired with ambient sounds and music makes every shift feel satisfying and peaceful. 📈 Story & Progression: As your sushi bar grows, you’ll uncover the backgrounds of your customers, unlock new décor, and even participate in local events and food festivals. 🐟 Fresh Ingredients, Fresh Challenges: Seasonal fish, special requests, and daily challenges keep you on your toes while keeping things fun. Ugoku Sushi Bar is a cozy job simulator full of flavor, charm, and heart. Whether you're a sushi enthusiast, a fan of relaxing sim games, or just love a well-told story in a small package, this game invites you to slow down, serve up joy, and savor the little victories. Slice. Serve. Smile. Your sushi journey starts here.
