About this gameFull description from Steam+
Business Tour is a multiplayer board game that combines simple rules with strategy, competition, and unexpected twists. Every match is different. Buy properties, build your strategy, negotiate with your rivals, make temporary alliances — and break them when the time is right. Your decisions can change the course of the game, but you'll also need to adapt when things don't go according to plan. Easy to learn, challenging to win. Business Tour is simple to pick up, even if you've never played before. But winning against real players takes strategy, smart decisions, and a little bit of luck. Play online with friends or challenge players from around the world. Team up for 2v2 matches, compete in tournaments and climb the leaderboards — or enjoy the game offline with bots and friends. Whether you're playing competitively or just having fun with friends, no two matches play out exactly the same. Key features: Online multiplayer for 2–4 players Online 2v2 team mode Play offline against bots Local multiplayer with friends Invite friends to join your table Global leaderboards Tournament mode
