About this gameFull description from Steam+
Wizard’s Wheel 2 is a retro-inspired idle RPG where your dimension is on the brink of collapse. Recruit from 80+ unique heroes, collect powerful loot with randomized modifiers, and grow stronger through layered progression systems. Play actively by issuing commands and speeding up time with the Wizard’s Wheel, or sit back and let your heroes battle automatically. Defeat bosses to gather Time Mana and construct powerful statues in the Temple of Time, unlocking permanent upgrades. With deep customization, crafting, mini-games, and multiple progression paths, every run brings you closer to saving your dimension. Features Include: Idle or Active Gameplay – Play your way, actively or hands-free Infinite Power Growth – Scale your damage into massive numbers 80+ Heroes – Build your team from a wide variety of unique classes Loot Explosion – Open hundreds of chests with randomized gear Loot Filters – Automatically manage drops to keep things clean Village Upgrades – Expand your town with new buildings and systems Item Crafting – Upgrade, enhance, socket, and ascend gear Alternate Timelines – 20+ variations that change gameplay rules Daily Dungeon – Push your strongest team for unique rewards Pantheon Challenges – Defeat powerful bosses for permanent buffs Dimensional Deck – Play a mini-game for powerful bonuses Skill Gems – Mix and match abilities across heroes Pets System – Hatch, level, and breed companions Seasonal Content – Limited-time events and rewards Wizard’s Wheel 2 is free to play. Optional purchases provide permanent bonuses, but all premium currency can be earned in-game. No heroes, items, or content are locked behind purchases.
