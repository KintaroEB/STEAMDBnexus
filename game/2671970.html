About this gameFull description from Steam+
My Suika: Kyo's Fruit Merge 🍉 Dive into the world of My Suika, a charming and fun fruit merge puzzle game that’s as kawaii as it is addictive! 🍎🍇🍌 Features: Drop & Merge Gameplay: Strategically combine fruits to create bigger ones and maximize your score. Unlockable Rewards: Earn watermelon seeds to unlock unique fruit skins, vibrant backgrounds, and exciting game modes. Power-Up Frenzy: Shake up the game with power-ups like Shake, Surge, and Smash to clear the board and boost your combos. Adorable Visuals: Enjoy colorful, hand-crafted artwork with a touch of kawaii charm. Competitive Leaderboards: Compete with friends and players worldwide to claim the top spot. Relax or Challenge Yourself: Choose between casual play or intense score-chasing action. Whether you're unwinding after a long day or aiming to crush the global leaderboard, My Suika is the perfect mix of relaxation and excitement. 🌟 Join the fruity frenzy today! 🍍✨
