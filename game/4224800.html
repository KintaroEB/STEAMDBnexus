About this gameFull description from Steam+
Police Chief Simulator: Early Days is a free prologue to the full Police Chief Simulator game. Immerse yourself as a young Chief of Police and take charge of a County Police Station. Your mission? Clear Winston Springs from the criminal influence that started in the City. Your role? Help Winston Springs County Police Station "build back better" and swing the odds in the favour of the law! Register crimes, expand cases, hunt criminals - and bring justice back to the County! Build up the Police influence in Winston Springs County! Start from scratch by setting up and developing a Police Station - from taking in Case Registrations through Case Examination to Case Execution - lead your personnel through the process of documenting crimes, collecting leads and solving them. Expand your influence and team to take on more crimes and more criminals and expand the Police Station's reach! Hire additional Police Officers, Detectives and Office Workers to take the burden of work off of your shoulders and observe as the once empty Police Station now thrives. Manage your Officers and expand your Police Station! Strategize and lead your Patrol Unit team to catch criminals, earning rewards that fuel your journey towards station upgrades and advanced tools. Each arrest and Closed Case brings in funds that you can use to enhance your station's capabilities, from better equipment to skilled personnel. Upgrade and expand your Police Station to take on even more challenging cases, gaining access to more dangerous districts in Winston Springs County. Clear Winston Springs County from the Shadow's Gang influence! Being the Chief of Police is not just about managing resources. Your ultimate goal is to cleanse each district of Winston Springs County from the clutches of notorious Gang Leaders. Police Chief Simulator offers a unique blend of strategy and management, experienced in first person perspective. Every decision counts as you work towards creating the safest County. Are you ready to command your Police Station and team to victory and restore peace to Winston Springs County? Key Game Features: Police Station management - from a small outpost to a thriving complex Open World Police Activities - unlock new ways to enforce the law Investigative and Detective minigames - register cases, expand them and work towards catching the suspect. Personel Management - hire additional Police Officers, Detectives and Office Workers to help with tasks around the Station. Winston Springs Map - send Patrol Units to the districts of the County, take hold of them and clear the area of crime! Lots of different Civilians with varying cases to register and solve. Many upgrade options can expand the Police Station’s possibilities and reach.
