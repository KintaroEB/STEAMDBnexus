About this gameFull description from Steam+
World's greatest collection game. Enter the world of ball: Get crate drops every three hours! Open crates and collect unique balls of five different rarity levels. (NEW)Arcade: Equip your favorite skin and play games! Bally Bird (Iconic remake of a very popular game you might recognize) Future plans: Ball is in its early stages. Here's what's on the way: Content updates: not only new balls and crates for you to collect and enjoy, but also some extra features we think you will like... Steam community integration and supporting the artists: we want to see what you can create. submit your work and have a chance to be featured in a "community crate"! More arcade games and ways to utilize your balls: ball is not just another low quality collection game. items will be usable in titles we have planned for the future.
