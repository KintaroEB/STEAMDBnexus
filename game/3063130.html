About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Coconut, every two hours get a free case and spin the mystical Coconut Wheel to receive a random coconut. Will you get a common coconut or will you be the lucky one to snag a super rare coconut, potentially worth a ton on the community market? The excitement of the spin will keep you coming back for more! But that's not all - each week some coconuts will be retired and will no longer be obtainable from the wheel. This means the coconuts you collect now will become even rarer over time, increasing their value and prestige. Don't miss your chance to build the most impressive coconut collection! Coconut is lightweight, taking up less than 0.1 GB of space on your computer, and using under 0.2% of your CPU. Plus, it can run in the background at all times, leaving your desktop free for any other activities! This is a community game, for the community! We welcome any and all suggestions through our Discord. Join the conversation and help shape the future of Coconut. By joining our Discord, you can gain access to exclusive Discord special coconuts and potential boosts to the game in the future for those who are part of our Discord community. Check out our Roadmap for future developments that the community can vote on! If you find and report a bug through Discord, you could receive a free Bug Finder skin, exclusively for those who help us improve the game. Additionally, anyone who submits a skin to be included in the game and has it accepted will receive 10% of the earnings from that coconut. Are you ready to collect them all and spin the rarest skins? Download Coconut and start collecting limited time skins!
