About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dreamlike Love with Seira is a light visual novel, featuring renowned illustrator rubisama as the director and original artist. This visual novel presents a single linear story, with text dialogue, manga-style illustrations, 2D character art, and voice acting. It does not include branching storylines. ❤Hope you could experience the sweet service and warm dreams with Seira within the brief durations❤ Planning, Artist:Rubi-sama (るび様) Scenario: Orisuke (オリスケ) A Story about Sweet Dream The protagonist, Haruto Watari, finishes a stressful day of work. He goes back home, exhausted, to a cluttered room that only makes him all the more fatigued. But unable to find the drive or will to clean up, he collapses into bed and falls asleep. Then, he has an unbelievable dream... A dream where he's the owner of a luxurious Western mansion. And in that dream mansion, he meets a maid named Seira. A maid whose duty is to serve him, her master— Be it brewing a delicious cup of black tea or comforting him with kind swords, she heals his tired soul. A dream of sweet, passionate service is about to begin... A Maid Providing Perfect Service ☕️ Seira ☕️ Voice Actress: Hana Kagehotaru Miss Seira, the maid who appears in Haruto's dream. With proper etiquette and versatile domestic skills, she excels at brewing exquisite black tea. She patiently listens to Haruto's work-related complaints, embodying a truly perfect woman.
