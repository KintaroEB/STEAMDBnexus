About this gameFull description from Steam+
THE ULTIMATE MOBA EXPERIENCE TRUE THIRD-PERSON GAMEPLAY Predecessor isn't just a 3D MOBA - it redefines the genre completely. Vault over enemies and terrain like never before - or leave the battlefield completely, taking to the skies to rain destruction on your opponents from above. Every wall, every structure, every angle is yours to exploit - if you can picture the play, you can break the game with it. EXPERIENCE TRUE DEPTH & SKILL Enjoy a diverse tapestry of over 45 complex playable characters who are expertly designed to be easy to learn but incredibly deep and rewarding to master. Pick from stance-shifters to summoners, gods to soldiers, knowing that your skills will always be rewarded, whether you're a seasoned competitive pro or an up-and-coming prodigy. JOIN A THRIVING COMPETITIVE COMMUNITY Squad up and throw down. Enjoy a packed competitive scene blending community tournaments with official esports events, celebrating some of the best talent in the entire MOBA genre. Do you have what it takes to compete at the highest level? ENJOY A RICH, IMMERSIVE WORLD Immerse yourself in a cinematic rush every time you step onto the battlefield thanks to Predecessor's breath-taking visuals and sound design. Enjoy extraordinary graphical fidelity that pushes the limits of what a MOBA should look like, supported by awe-inspiring audio that lets you feel every explosion. WIN YOUR WAY Unlike other MOBAs, Predecessor puts you in control - literally. Grab a controller and hit the couch, playing effortlessly against friends and enemies on keyboard and mouse thanks to full cross-play on console and PC with Discord-powered voice comms, ensuring you never miss a callout. Squad up and play your way. DEVELOPED BY THE COMMUNITY Predecessor isn't just designed for the MOBA community, it's built by it. Players drive Predecessor's development like no other game. Major decisions are polled, feedback dictates direction and transparency rules communication. From our earliest days of being coded live on Twitch to being a global powerhouse in the MOBA genre, our community is at the heart of everything we do. JOIN OVER 2 MILLION PLAYERS NOW!
