About This Game Meet beautiful anime girls and spend time in their company as you piece together fragments of their memories. 🧩 Get to know charming characters like Mommo (19), Asuka (20), and other lovely girls aged 18 to 23 — each with their own unique atmosphere and artwork. This game is a classic puzzle experience where you complete ten stages across three difficulty levels. As you progress, pleasant surprises and stunning illustrations await you. Relax, enjoy the music, and immerse yourself in a colorful anime world. All characters appearing in the game are 18 years of age or older.
