Williams™ Pinball classics are now available -- and Fish Tales™ is yours for FREE! Zen is proud to bring these amazing games to players around the world. Enjoy! Bring your previous Pinball FX2 purchases with you to Pinball FX Classic at no charge! Not so hot with the flippers?
