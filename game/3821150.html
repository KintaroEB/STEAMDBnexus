About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Prologue is a free intro to “Burger Shift: Team Rush” 🍔 Jump in solo or team up with up to 8 players in co-op. Host your own session or join others through the in-game lobby, using built-in voice and text chat to stay connected as you take on the rush. Run every part of a bustling fast food kitchen, from operating fryers and soda fountains to flipping burgers on the grill. Learn the ins and outs of each station to keep things running smoothly. Expect the unexpected: chaotic events, strange customers, and surprise challenges demand quick thinking and solid teamwork. Communication and coordination are your tools for success. Stay sharp and manage your time wisely. Keep orders flowing and customers happy by assigning roles, juggling priorities, and working as one unstoppable crew. What’s Included in the Prologue? Order up! Here’s what’s on the menu in the Prologue: Single-player and 1–8 player co-op – Run the kitchen solo or with friends. A selection of signature ingredients and recipes – Including baked goods, frying, and burger prep. Soda fountain and coffee machine – Serve cold sodas and hot coffee to keep your customers satisfied. Character customization – Pick your gear and show off your style with early outfit and character options.
