About this gameFull description from Steam+
Cat is a delightfully simple and strangely addictive clicker game where your mission is to collect cats, unlock powerful upgrades, earn achievements, and watch your feline empire grow. Whether you're a casual player or a hardcore cat collector, there's something purr-fectly satisfying for everyone. Features: 33 Achievements to unlock 24 Unique Upgrade Types with nearly 1,000 total levels for massive cat-scaling potential. 37 Collectible Cats, each bringing unique CPS (Cats Per Second) and CPC (Cats Per Click) bonuses that stack as you build your cat collection Auto-save Function so your progress is always safe, even if you get distracted by real cats. Customization options Item drop pool: 70% Common 25% Uncommon 4% Rare 1% Epic 0.01% Legendary Idle Cat Drops Every 8 hours, you can get 2 new cats just by checking in. The more cats you have, the stronger your passive income becomes. It pays to keep coming back! Q: Why Should I Play This Game? A: Cats Q: Isn't that just a copy of the Steam game Banana, but named Cat? A: No. Cat gives you more gameplay choices like upgrades and changing your cat avatar. Q: Is this an NFT game? A: No, cats are not connected to any blockchain. Warning: This game is a clicker, not an RPG with three times the map size, five times the graphics, and ten times more quests than the XYZ game. Most of the time, you will just click the cat button and spend your cats on upgrades.
