About this gameFull description from Steam+
WELCOME TO NYKYLÄ, FINLAND. THE YEAR IS 1994. Step into the boots of Seppo "Zeze" Sahtivaara in a gritty, story-driven action-adventure. Last Drop combines the nostalgic freedom of an open-world sandbox with intense 1v1 combat and a cinematic narrative inspired by real events and authentic Finnish locations. KEY FEATURES: Old-School 1v1 Brawling: Settle your disputes the old-fashioned way. Master a deep, fighting-game-style combat system to crush your rivals. No guns, just pure skill and heavy hits. Learn new moves by watching VHS action movies. Classic Open-World Sandbox: Explore the weird and wonderful streets of Nykylä and surrounding towns. Buy and drive retro cars, ride bikes, find work to earn cash, or just hit the local pub for a drink. You make the rules. Deep Character Progression: Level up Zeze to survive the underworld. Upgrade your stats including HP, Strenght, Agility, Stamina, and manage your inventory, wallet, and even your Drunkenness level. Cinematic Storytelling: Experience a crazy, impactful narrative brought to life with movie-style cutscenes. The golden era of action movies meets interactive drama. A Living, Breathing World: Immerse yourself in a dynamic environment with a full day/night cycle and changing weather. Watch the town react as shops open and close based on the in-game clock. Grab a cold beer, heat up the sauna, and prepare for the ultimate 90s nostalgia trip!
