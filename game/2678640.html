About this gameFull description from Steam+
「Persisting in kindness, some people say it is absurd and dismiss it; But in the Realm where wedge energy and will intertwine, it brought us you and rally you.」 As a kind soul that comes to the realm, begins a journey of redemption in the realm of the raging tide of black beasts. 「When greed and violence destroy the nameless flower, we know why the Dawn Dew comes......」 In the process of continuous progress, discover the history of the realm and uncover the truth of the Black beast tide. Explore box style maps with designs, collect various resources in the scene, and make yourself stronger. Don't worry about boring lost time. You have clear map guidance and can teleport at any time. Free to form your combat style Collect nearly a hundred skills, experiences, props, dozens of postures, and support units to freely explore, form, and integrate combat methods. Use a posture to change the attributes and combat style of Dew, and establish your combat style; Use self assembling skills to strike enemies and enrich your combat strategy; Reasonably matching experiences to maximize their effectiveness, even becoming the core of the battle; Props provide attribute bonuses and can be actively used;   Props will not be consumed, only have a limit on the number of uses (which can be restored) Summon support to assist in combat. Once properly matched, support can also become the main force in combat. Extra combat modes In Counterattacking enemies in traditional turn based systems - as long as the technical level is sufficient, it will be the best weapon for defeating the strong with the weak; (Counterattack exists as a posture mechanism, and even if you never use it, it does not affect the gaming experience. All other turn based elements are waiting for you to explore.(This commitment only applies to normal and simple difficulty levels)) AI Content Description This game is handcrafted by a solo developer, no any AI Content
