About this gameFull description from Steam+
In order to get her hands on a cherished wine collection, rumored to be hidden away by Vampire of Demise Endo, drunken Rogue Lita makes her way to the vampire king's castle. See what story unfolds in this roguelike deck-building game. To make it to the vampire's castle, you must conquer several dungeons, each with its own traps, terrain, and ferocious demons lying in wait. A frontal assault is simply too reckless. Lita takes advatage of her Wind Cloak while Endo posesses the ability to turn into fog, allowing both to evade the perils of their adventure. However, what you evade only comes back stronger on the next floor. Whether you are prepared to face the inevitable trouble is up to you. The best part is that the characters in this game get stronger the drunker they become! The only way to step foot into the all-too-risky dungeons is to do so with intoxicated abandon! To top things off, the drunker you become, the less you can tell what's real and what's not, and you may even hallucinate. Your pet dog Odysseus that appears before you may very well be a fiendish demon. Please drink responsibly.
