About this gameFull description from Steam+
foreverloops BEATS is a playful and easy-to-use rhythm tool. Experiment with all kinds of audio–visual material for beat-making and performances. Freely arrange loops of sound, videos and images on virtual gears to stack and connect them. Evolve simple arrangements to awe-inspiring media machineries that can be tweaked and customized in real-time. foreverloops BEATS is the core version of foreverloops, focusing on rhythms.
