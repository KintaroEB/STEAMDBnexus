About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dokimon Quest is a turn-based monster-taming RPG set in a GameBoy Color-inspired pixel world. Capture, train, and battle alongside 140+ original monsters as you push into a dangerous open world to uncover the mystery behind your missing childhood friend. "Whoa, watch out!" Dokimon may be dangerous, but don't let that stop you! The more you explore, the stronger you'll need to become. Quality of Life Features Speed up battles by holding an action key Edit monster stats and move pools from your main menu (when in towns) Battle helper tips and tools (optional) Quest log, to help you keep track of your journey 8-bit + Piano OST — pick whichever suits your preference World battles let you battle right in your surroundings (enable in options) 20+ retro color palettes, in case full color isn't your style 9 playable characters to choose from 4 save files, in case your friends want to try Features Battle against Rangers who are also on the quest to get stronger, and take down evil corporations that stand in your way and disrupt the peace. Dokimon takes inspiration from the GameBoy Color era of games, without the chunkiness of some of those old systems. (Like, the game's systems... not the consoles — we love those.) On Your Adventure Collect over 140 unique, original monsters Explore an adventure spanning 15+ towns Explore several routes, caves, shrines, seas, and mountains Complete a 15–20 hour story mode, with endless post-game content for completionists Track down rare monsters to unlock the game's post-story Experience 7 alt-palettes for each monster (1 of which is incredibly rare) Go FISHING! Built on the MonMae Engine (aka Luna) Dokimon Quest was made in MonTamer Maker (MonMae, also known as Luna), a platform/engine built specifically for making monster tamer games. The engine keeps growing thanks to its devs and community — so making new Dokimon Quest (and similar) titles is within arm's reach, even for you. Professor Intro "Watch out!"
