About this gameFull description from Steam+
🐾 Meow My Crop! ——Become a cat farmer, and cover the bitterness of work with the sweetness of harvest! Use your keyboard and mouse to spread the fertilizer, let every moment of work accumulate and bear fruit~ Additional device support included: Controllers Graphics Tablets MIDI Keyboards Multiplayer Supported ——Play with friends, double the joy and harvest! Social 'crisis':Be an honest cat farmer or sneakily steal your friend's 'hard work' fruits? Mutual Growth: It's better to enjoy joy together than alone! Online play allows you to share growth points—the more lively it is, the greater the rewards! Remote Item Throw: Send the most genuine 'greetings' to your friend's screen. Meow Farm Insurance: If crops are stolen, there’s a chance to gain bonus items to make them bear fruit faster! Collection Marathon:Collect over 800+ items ——Redeem fruits for cat cans and unlock tons of surprises! Cat skins Headgear Emotes Clothes Stools Fertilizers ——Festival Limited Catch the festive vibe and unlock limited-time rare outfits with special cans~! Focus Assistant ——Max out your office ritual vibe A variety of white noise to satisfy your auditory quirks. Music Records: Have a chance to drop when harvesting crops.
