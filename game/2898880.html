About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mecha Simultactics is a simultaneous-turn-based competitive tactics game on an isometric grid battlefield. Each turn, all players select their actions, which are then executed at the same time. Your available actions depend on the outcome of the previous turn. ALPHA FEATURES Online Multiplayer PvP: Fight online with up to 8 players as teams or free for all. Anime-Inspired Replays: Watch the fight play back in real time. Use the Replay Studio to edit your replays and create epic battle scenes. Map Editor: Create and share custom maps. FUTURE FEATURES More Mechs: Different types of mechs to choose from, each with unique playstyles and abilities. More Equipment: Customize your mech's parts and equipment. More Maps: A variety of new official maps. Join the public alpha! By joining the public alpha, you can play the game early and provide feedback to improve it. This is an early alpha build so you might encounter some bugs. Please report any issues you find. Your feedback can help shape the very foundations of this game. Thank you for your participation and support!
