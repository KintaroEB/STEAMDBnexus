About this gameFull description from Steam+
Work together with up to 8 friends to paddle through cool waters, overcome obstacles and bosses, and reach new islands. Reach the legendary treasure without sinking. PADDLE WITH FRIENDS Teamwork is the key as you and your friends share the same boat, navigating waters filled with unpredictable and hilarious chaos. You have to coordinate your paddles or you'll end up failing! EXPLORE NEW ISLANDS Discover islands with new biomes as you drive through dangerous paths. Each new biome offers a unique experience, and reaching the next island will always be more challenging! FUN WATER PHYSICS Paddling and water physics will challenge you, but that’s not all. Be ready to face new obstacles and bosses along the way. You’ll have to overcome them if you want to reach the treasure! DIFFERENT BOAT TYPES There are several boats: single and two player kayaks, a four player boat and a jet ski. Larger boats are harder to control, so you’ll need your friends. But don’t worry if you’re playing solo, we’ve designed a solo kayak and jet ski for you. You can play the game no matter what! CUSTOMIZE YOUR SKIN Boat Together offers a variety of skins for both your boat and character. With your own unique combinations, you can become the coolest treasure hunter! WE APPRECIATE YOUR SUPPORT If Boat Together caught your interest, you can support us by adding it to your wishlist! This would make us incredibly happy and keep us motivated. Thank you!
