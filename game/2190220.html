About this gameFull description from Steam+
"The collapse of Gensokyo... it was you, Reimu - " 『Touhou Danmaku Kagura Phantasia Lost』is a Touhou Project officially approved derivative rhythm action game. Two modes are available: the Story Mode, in which you can solve the mysteries of the destroyed Gensokyo through the Danmaku Kagura (rhythm game), and the Free Mode, in which you can play the rhythm game to your heart's content. With 68 songs in the base game plus tracks from the announced / released Extra Song Packs Vol. 1–15, you can enjoy over 300 arrangements of Touhou music. Game Experiences Kagura Stage In this mode you hit the notes at the right moment! You can stack up combos and get a high score by hitting the notes consecutively coming through the 4 or 6 lanes. Danmaku Stage A totally new game experience that combines Danmaku shoot 'em up & Rhythm games! Complete missions set for each stages and beat the stage! Customizability to tune your rhythm experience Change the notes' speed, adjust the location of the combo display, change the notes' design, change all you want to give you a stress-free rhythm experience. Characters Featuring over 100 characters from Touhou Project. Characters are animated with Live2D. Beautiful illustrations to color your game Over 300 Mitama cards available to obtain through the progression of the game. Amazing artists such as Yuu Kamiya (No Game No Life), Negi Haruba (The Quintessential Quintuplets), and many more have contributed to producing the fantastic works! The only game where you can play Touhou Project Original Songs Alongside many songs from the Touhou Project games, songs such as "U.N. Owen Was Hero?" by Toby Fox & ZUN were written for this game!
