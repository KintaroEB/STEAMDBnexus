About this gameFull description from Steam+
Need more cats in your life, but you’re afraid the tons of litter sand won’t fit in your monthly budget? Fear not! Chonkers is a cozy idle game for cat lovers. Open the door and welcome the most adorable stray kittens into your rooms! Take a break. Relax and watch them sleep while listening to lo-fi music. Play with them and make them level up faster! Let them chonkify without worrying about health risks! This is digital fun. Always seek professional help for your puffy little friends 😸 If you’re lucky, you might also adopt the rarest galaxy wanderers. Those stray kittens from space with a mesmerizing glittery mane are in need of a new home. Give them a proper welcome ♥ When you think they’re ready for a new family, give them away for their weight in gold! Complete your collection book with more than 100 breeds and 400+ variants. If relaxation and cats are your thing, this is a game for you~
