About this gameFull description from Steam+
Demonheart: The Cursed Trial is an otome visual novel set in a dark fantasy world of demons and magic. Step into the boots of one with a demonic heart and join the twisted game of romance and betrayal. Female protagonist Hundreds of conversation choices - you pick nearly every line the main character says Two human male love interests (Ran and Kaisar), some flirting with a female human (Vita) and a male demonspawn A custom alignment system - you can play as Good, Evil, Tactful, Defiant or in between Customizable name A dark story full of mystery and deceit Some lines of dialogue are voiced over Bad guys and plot twists :) Multiple endings You wake up in a strange place, only to realize you have been abducted with seven other people. A city guard, a royal soldier, a witch, a bard, a librarian and two criminals - all captured and brought together by an Infernal force. A demonspawn appears, delivering a chilling message: there are demonhearts among you - people blessed with regeneration, but cursed to be hunted by everyone else. You are one of them, and you'll do anything to keep your secret safe. As tensions rise and trust erodes, mysterious deaths start to occur. Can you trust anyone? In this chaos, you may find yourself drawn to one of your fellow captives. Will you survive the demonspawn's trial? This game is set in the Demonheart universe, but knowledge of the other Demonheart games is not needed to enjoy the story.
