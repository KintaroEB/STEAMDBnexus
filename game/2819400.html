About this gameFull description from Steam+
Disc golf, built for people who play Disc Golf Masters is a disc golf game made by disc golfers, for disc golfers. The studio builds it in the same factory where Latitude 64 presses its discs. If you play the sport, this is the game you have been waiting for. Real discs Throw officially licensed discs from the brands already in your bag. Latitude 64, Discmania, Westside Discs, and Kastaplast. More brands are on the way. The same disc you bag in real life flies on your screen. Real flight Every disc flies on its own flight characteristics. Hyzer, anhyzer, turnover, fade. Pick your angle, read the wind, and watch the disc curve the way you threw it. Miss it and you are throwing out from behind a tree, just like real life. Play together Take it online against players around the world, or grab friends for multiplayer up to four players. Chase the leaderboards and prove your game. Make it yours Build your player with discs, apparel, and accessories. Your bag, your look, your style on the course. A live game Early Access is the starting line, not the finish. We update Disc Golf Masters constantly. Check the roadmap on this page to see what is coming next. More courses, more discs, new skills, and real-life pro players joining as playable characters. We built the demo with the community all year, listened to real players, and everything we learned is in this version. From the team behind Disc Golf Valley Disc Golf Masters comes from Spin Off Games, the studio behind the mobile hit Disc Golf Valley with over a million players. They are part of the House of Discs family. They play the sport. It shows.
