About this gameFull description from Steam+
Stop wasting paid lesson time on the basics. Virtual Driving School is a serious, skills-first simulator that teaches real-world manoeuvres and traffic systems, so you show up to your first lesson already road-ready. Drill fundamentals in quick 1–3 minute scenarios, then put them to use in practice drives, quizzes, and career modes like delivery driver, and repeat until it sticks. Then test your skills on city streets, rural roads, and motorways, in sun, rain, or fog. VDS is built to get you ahead fast: fewer hours on fundamentals, more time perfecting your driving. Why you’ll learn faster (and spend less) Targeted lessons cover the fundamentals in short bursts (1–3 mins): Gears, clutch control, moving off, lane choice, merges, roundabouts, parking, emergency stops, and more. Practice Drives: Tackle set routes packed with live hazards — pedestrians, parked obstacles, unpredictable traffic — with one goal: get to the end cleanly. Quizzes: Drive and think. Routes pause to test your road knowledge with progressively harder questions and a final score at the end. Career Modes: Step into the shoes of a delivery driver (points-based) or taxi driver (5-star rating). Hit your destinations on time, drive cleanly, and watch your score climb — or tank. Unlimited retries: Master tricky manoeuvres without a meter running. Realistic traffic & signage: Pedestrians, temporary roadworks, box junctions, crossings, level crossings, lane control, motorway incidents, different times of day. Hazard Avoidance: Train for the unexpected — cars pulling out from behind lorries, reckless overtakes, sudden stops, dangerous drivers, and more. Right‑ and Left‑Hand Drive options (mirrors, lane flow, junction priority, etc.). Wheel & control support Steering wheels & pedals: Logitech, Fanatec, Moza, and many more. Drive your way: Pick from preset modes - Arcade, Intermediate and Expert - or fully customise the controls to match exactly how you want to drive. Toggle automatic gears, easy gears, clutch, handbrake, headlights, and more. Whether you're easing in or simulating the full manual experience, the setup is yours. Manual mastery: Clutch control, hill starts, and gear‑shift driving supported. Also supports keyboard & mouse and controller. VR and Multi Display Support Full VR Support – Play the game in Full VR. Multi Display and Surround Display Support – combine up to five displays to create the most immersive gaming environment possible. (Requires NVIDIA Surround). Multiple Cameras – cameras and display cut-ins show the view from multiple sources simultaneously: allowing the player to view a head, behind, wing mirrors, rearview mirror(s) and side windows. Headtracking Support – Headtracking combined with surround screens allows near VR experience with multiple screens. The syllabus Over 30 lessons covering all aspects of driving broken down into several categories. Basics: Setup, controls, gears, moving off, turning, pull up left/right, blind bends, hill starts. Traffic Systems: Traffic lights, zebra/controlled crossings, box junctions, junctions, priority rules, roundabouts. Speed, Stopping & Parking: 20 mph zones, dual carriageways, national limits World & handling HD environments with pedestrians and dense traffic with realistic driving AI. Locations: Launch regions include Conway, Cairnloch, and San Jose, with locations from around the world rolling out (UK, US, Europe, Asia). Dynamic conditions: Day/night, rain/fog, wet/icy grip that changes stopping distances. Vehicle variety: From compact cars and family sedans to vans. Each with distinct visibility, weight, and braking feel. Designed to shortcut paid lessons – responsibly VDS is built to frontload your learning: master core skills, understand road language, and sharpen hazard perception before you meet an instructor. That means fewer paid hours on fundamentals, more on passing the test. Disclaimer: Virtual Driving School is a training‑oriented simulation and a powerful supplement to real‑world instruction. It does not replace certified driver training. Always follow local laws and licensing requirements. P.S DON'T FORGET TO HIT THE WISHLIST BUTTON
