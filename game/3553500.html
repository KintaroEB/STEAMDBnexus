About this gameFull description from Steam+
SIGame is a classical intellectual game (first released in 2002) that lets you test your knowledge in a competitive atmosphere. In this game, you can challenge both experienced players and smart bots, showcasing your expertise across various fields. Key Features: Diverse Thematic Packs: Play quizzes on general knowledge, books, movies, cartoons and anime, music, and video games. Create Your Own Questions: Become a game creator by designing your own question packs. Invite friends to play in your unique setting and prove how well you know your own questions. Multiplayer Mode: Play with friends, compete in PvP mode, and find out who among you is the true master of intellectual games. SIGame offers engaging gameplay, a wide range of topics, and various game modes. Immerse yourself in the world of knowledge, test your intellect, and enjoy the challenge with SIGame!
