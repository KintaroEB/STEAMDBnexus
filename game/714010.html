About this gameFull description from Steam+
Aimlabs - the World’s #1 aim trainer! Train like the Pros. Improve faster. Win more fights. Sharpen your aim, master your fundamentals, and get measurably better at the FPS games you love. Aimlabs is a science-backed training platform trusted by over 50 million players - from complete beginners to professional esports competitors. Aimlabs 2.0 brings a significantly improved training experience with faster and smoother performance, a streamlined and cleaner UI packed with more training content, and a better way to find training for the games you love. Enjoy comprehensive results to track your progress, with more ways to make it yours with new themes and personalization. This update also brings you a brand new way to train - Multiplayer Modes! Whether you're picking up an FPS for the first time or being hard stuck in a rank, Aimlabs meets you where you are. A vast library of training tasks covers every core skill:  tracking, flicking, target switching, and more, with performance analytics that show you exactly where you stand and what to work on next. CORE FEATURES Deep Training Library Thousands of scenarios covering every fundamental FPS skill, from precision clicking to dynamic tracking and everything in between. Game-Specific Training Match your sensitivity and train for over 400 supported titles with tailored routines. Flick Optimizer Find your ideal sensitivity! NEW: Train with Friends in one of these modes Co-op: train any task with friends, plus new tasks built just for playing together. 1v1 Duels: you against one opponent, friend or matchmade. Free-for-All: four-player chaos in one arena, with high-speed movement and mobility. PvP is still evolving. More maps and modes are coming, shaped directly by your feedback. Season 2 of The Official Aimlabs Benchmark Get ranked across Entry, Intermediate, and Elite tiers. Track your progress and see real improvement over time. NEW Profiles & Social View other players' stats, showcase your own progress, and find people to train with. Creator Studio Build your own scenarios or recreate real in-game situations, then practice them on repeat. Playlist Editor Create your own playlists, or copy and remix existing playlists to build your perfect training or warm-up routine. Aim Insights Visualize your performance with heatmaps, trends, and advanced stats that show exactly where you're improving. Ranks & Leaderboards Compete globally, climb the ranks, and prove your skill. Full Customization  Personalize your training with custom environments, weapons, avatars, and more. Mouse & Controller Support Train your way, on your preferred input. The core Aimlabs experience is completely free. Aimlabs Pro is available for players who want to go even further and improve faster, offering deeper insights and additional training tools. AIMLABS PRO - PREMIUM FEATURES Perfect Sensitivity Find your best sensitivity that works for you and your aiming style, no matter what FPS game or task you are playing. Training Programs The Progression System helps you level up faster with daily goals, smart tracking, and skill-based missions. Whether you’re grinding in Valorant , CS2, or Fortnite, you’ll get personalized challenges designed to boost your performance and take your game to the next tier with results you see and feel. VALORANT Training Center: Train Fundamental VALORANT mechanics like optimal crosshair placement, angle and peek efficiency, and movement accuracy - all on 1:1 map recreations straight from VALORANT. Use Run-it back to turn all your VALORANT deaths into infinitely replayable Aimlabs tasks, and practice against realistic bots with Deathmatch. Task Tuning Task Tuning allows you to edit a task in the simplest and fastest way. Adjust target movement, speed, task length, and more, all with just one click. Premium Analysis Unlock a broader range of performance insights. In-depth, actionable feedback as you tackle each task, helping you fine-tune your skills after every play! Adaptive Tasks The most efficient way to train. Adaptive tasks adjust your difficulty in real-time, ensuring you are always training at the optimal difficulty. Coach Take the guesswork out of aim training and FPS improvement. Trained on the world’s leading esports athletes, Coach bring its expertise and guidance to your own gameplay. Use Coach to learn how to improve your raw aim, game sense for any FPS. Make custom tasks, personalized to your needs and skill level. Academy Learn how to apply your aim training to your favorite FPS games with guided, step-by-step masterclasses hand-crafted by the experts. Get unlimited access to an ever-growing collection of premium lessons across a variety of games - New lessons dropping every month! Per-Task Sens Optimizer Aimlabs changes your sensitivity as you play with this enabled, calculating your most optimal sensitivity for a task based on how you perform at different sensitivity level.
