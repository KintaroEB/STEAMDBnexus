About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Ancient Dungeon VR. This is a fast and fun dungeon roguelite game made just for VR. Swing your sword, grab loot, and explore rooms full of traps, secrets, and monsters. The dungeons change every time you play! It’s one of the best-rated VR games out there with Overwhelmingly Positive reviews, and you can play for many hours with your friends! Full Online Multiplayer Team up with friends in multiplayer. Trade blows with dungeon bosses, share loot, and revive each other… or let them get caught while you grab the loot. Your choice. Multiplayer supports up to 4 players, cross-play with Quest native and soon PSVR2. New Dungeons Every Time No two runs are the same! Every time you play, the dungeon looks different, with new monsters and loot. You can unlock secret rooms, new floors, and cool surprises. Real Combat. Real Movement. Real Fun. Slash, block, throw, and dodge, all with your real hands in VR! You can even cut down plants if you’re feeling silly. Unique Powerups, Countless Builds Find special items called Relics that make you stronger or give you new powers. Will you become a glass cannon? A tanky swordsman? Or maybe something... cursed? Permanent Unlocks & Upgrades Earn permanent rewards like new weapons, floors, relics, upgrades, hard mode, and more! Every adventure helps you build power and unlock fresh challenges. Features Procedurally generated dungeons Physics-based VR combat: feel every swing and throw Rich roguelite progression with hundreds of combinations Play online with up to 4 friends Over 70 unique enemies, 11 bosses, 150+ relics Walk, teleport, or even swing like a monkey! Unlock new floors and mini encounters for more variety Keep track of your monster kills, stats, and trophies Permanent progression system via Bestiary, Item Compendiums and Lore Entries Always getting new updates Mods supported too!
