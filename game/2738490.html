About this gameFull description from Steam+
In Sol Cesto, your goal is to descend through the levels of a randomly generated underground world, diving ever deeper in search of the sun, lost for countless years. On the combat grid, choose one of four rows to move into. You'll then land randomly on one of the four tiles in that row: will it be a monster that harms you? A chest full of gold? Or perhaps a deadly poison trap? Anything can happen… It’s up to you to weigh the risks of each tile as you clear the screen. Of course, your character will grow stronger, and magical items, special powers, and sinister curses will help you bend the odds in your favor and keep fate on your side. Features: 5 biomes to explore, each hiding monsters and traps with unique behaviors 7 characters with distinct powers, stats, and abilities Stone teeth that change the game’s rules and probabilities to tip the scales in your favor Metal teeth that trigger devastating curses and combos A wide range of items to eliminate enemies or escape dangerous situations The strange underground inhabitants, some of whom may share their secrets with you And many more surprises…
