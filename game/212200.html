About this gameFull description from Steam+
Enter the mystical land of Erinn, where a century-old battle threatens the balance of the known world. Explore exotic locales, defeat mighty foes and follow the path to destiny. With the unique Rebirth System, your character can be reborn and live their life over and over again, allowing you to try out all the talents, quests and skills you want without losing progress. Welcome to Mabinogi An anime MMORPG filled with fashion, fantasy and unique lifestyle systems built for ultimate self-expression. Deep Adventure Whether facing a kraken, seducing a succubus, or helping the local farmer with their potato harvest, your choices for your next adventure are limitless. Your Fantasy Life Become a commerce mogul or compose your very own music. With an expanding wardrobe of fashion and frequent updates to content, every day in Mabinogi can be different. Unique Combat Imbue weapons with the spirits of fierce warriors. Fight side- by- side with your pets. Take down demigods and master all 12 styles of combat. Community Like No Other Make friends, join a guild, or tag along on a cross-continental journey. No matter the activity, Mabinogi is perfect for a journey solo or with an entire crew. Even decorate your own home for the perfect hangout.
