About this gameFull description from Steam+
Those who control crypto-mining — control the world. And only two real men can stop this madness — BRUTAL JOHN and BRUTAL DOG. Experience BRUTAL JOHN 2, a mind-blowing fusion of the best old-school FPS shooters, like Duke Nukem, Serious Sam, Doom, and Half-Life. Immerse yourself in a heart-stopping, ultraviolent gaming experience that's sure to get your adrenaline pumping. Prepare for a mind-blowing adventure in the year 2090, where the entire world is controlled by the ruthless crypto-mining mutant, Luzilian. With humanity's fate in his merciless and greedy grip, he has brought the global economy to its knees and left the earth teetering on the edge of disaster. Key Features : Crazy Storyline. Save the world from the crypto-mining madness. A true Old-School FPS, featuring retro-inspired graphics and gameplay that pays homage to the legendary FPS classics of the '90s. Play as Brutal John and his loyal companion Brutal Dog, each with their own unique storyline levels. Wield a devastating arsenal of weapons, and experience an ultra-detailed gibbing system where every shot dismembers enemies with gory details. Epic Boss Battles. Face off against giant brain machines, and grotesque alien overlords. Explore the universe! Uncover secrets and hidden Easter Eggs across the entire game. Dive into amazing locations: sci-fi space stations, cities, wild west, caverns, casino, crypto-mining factories, and more. Superior dynamically changing old-school soundtrack.
