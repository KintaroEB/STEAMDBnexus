About this gameFull description from Steam+
Water. Earth. Fire. Air. Master the elements and clash as legendary characters in Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game. Whether you're controlling the battlefield with earth bending, overwhelming opponents with relentless pressure, or out maneuvering them with speed and precision, every fighter offers a distinct fighting style to learn and master. Featuring hand-drawn 2D animation faithful to the series' roots, Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game lets you experience the world of Avatar Legends like never before. Choose from 12 playable characters and go head-to-head across a variety of modes and features, including Local & Online Versus, Story, Arcade, Ranked, Training, and an Art Gallery celebrating the history of the series. ● Hand-Drawn 2D Animation Hand-drawn 2D animation faithfully brings characters and their unique fighting styles from Avatar: The Last Airbender, The Legend of Korra, and the expanded universe to life. ● A Wealth of Game Modes and Features Available Now Story Mode, Arcade Mode, Ranked Play, Local & Online Play, Training Mode, Combo Trials, an Art Gallery featuring never-before-seen concept art and much more! ● Easy to Pick Up, Impossible to Put Down Anyone can start bending with simple, intuitive controls, perfect for quick matches with friends and family. Beneath the surface lies a deep fighting system rewarding technical prowess and creativity. Auto Combos and tutorials get you fighting fast, Combo Trials and Training Mode take you further, and Replay Takeover lets you drop into any saved match and play out the moment differently. ● Seamless Online Play Battle players worldwide with rollback netcode and crossplay across multiple platforms with features that ensure a competitive experience that feels great to play no matter where you are or who you're fighting. Open Lobbies host up to 16 players, with spectating so you can watch the action while you wait your turn. --- Standard Edition Features ● Story and Arcade Mode Experience an all-new, original story outlined by show creators, Michael Dante DiMartino and Tim Hedrick. Shape the fate of time and space in the main story mode, then dive into arcade runs featuring unique storylines for every playable character. Story Mode+ and difficulty options give you new reasons to run it back. ● Ranked Play Put your skills on the line in competitive online play, climb the ranks season by season, and spectate the matches you aren't in. ● Robust Training Mode A powerful Training Mode equips players of all skill levels with advanced tools, including hitboxes, frame data, save states and more. ● Master Flow and Energy Points Control the fight by using Flow to dash, evade, and outmaneuver opponents with high-speed mobility, then build Energy Points through skilled play to unleash enhanced special moves and powerful cinematic Super Arts that can turn the tide of battle. ● Support Characters Choose from three support characters to accompany your fighter, each providing unique abilities that enhance different strengths and open up new strategic possibilities. Experiment with different combinations and discover the partnership that vibes with your style the most. ● Art Gallery Explore never-before-seen original artwork from the show, honoring the history, characters, and world of Avatar. Deluxe Edition The Deluxe Edition expands on the Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game Standard Edition experience, with premium content that pays homage to its art, music, and iconic fighters. The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the full game, plus: ● Year 1 Pass Adds five iconic characters over the course of the season: Iroh, Ty Lee, Lin Beifong, Bolin and the winner of the Year 1 Character Vote. ● Year 1 Character Vote Have a say in who joins the roster. Deluxe includes a voting pass for the Year 1 Character Vote, where you rank the fighters you want most. ● Original Soundtrack Explore the music of Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game with the complete digital soundtrack arriving post-launch. ● Digital Artbook Curated digital collection showcasing the hand-drawn style of Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game through character art, stages, and more to be delivered post-launch. ● Exclusive HUD Styles Platinum and Onyx HUD styles, available only with the Deluxe Edition, to change the look of your battles.
