About this gameFull description from Steam+
Voor de Kroon is an immersive medieval strategy-action game with historically inspired arms and armor. Fight historical battles and command your troops from the front line or the RTS camera. Use the Warforge editor to reshape the entire game and build your own medieval campaigns. Voor de kroon is a medieval wargame. Get ready for immersive historical battles Pull back to the RTS camera to maneuver your formations, time your charges and hold your lines. Then drop into third person and fight in the mud alongside your men while the battle decides itself. Grounded in real medieval warfare All arms and armor are drawn from contemporary sources. Helmets, mail and plate, weapons and equipment all belong to the era they represent and are modeled after manuscript and effigy references. Build anything with the Warforge editor The editors ship inside the game ready to use the moment you launch it. Create units, factions, castles and entire campaigns as easily as laying out a level, then share them on the Steam Workshop or play what the community has already made. The Friso-Hollandic Wars campaign Voor de Kroon's first native campaign is a full historical conquest set in the 13th-century Low Countries. Fight as Floris V to avenge your father's death against the Frisians or play the Frisians in their struggle to survive against the feudal lords. It's the first of more official campaigns to come. Solo developed with ♥ in The Netherlands
