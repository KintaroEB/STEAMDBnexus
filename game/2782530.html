About this gameFull description from Steam+
A shrimple survivor-like where you play as a shrimp, you must kill fishies & other creatures, gather microplastics, and purchase meta upgrades. Current Meta Upgrades: +5% Move Speed - 4 Levels +10% Max Health - 5 Levels +5% Outgoing Damage - 5 Levels -1 Incoming Damage - 3 Levels Free Item Drops are in... Customize your shrimp! The Drop-rates are as follows: 80% - for a shrimp skin (split to 99% common rarity skin, and 1% rare rarity skin) 19% - for a unique rarity hat 1% - for a rare rarity hat. The gameplay is simple, just move, and use the patterns of the auto-firing weapons you have to murder the oncoming swarms of fishies & creatures. if you have issues with the game or suggestions, make sure to let me know. have a good day! :D
