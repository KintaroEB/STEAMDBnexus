Ladies and gentlemen, esteemed guests, and fellow peach enthusiasts, today I stand before you not just as a humble farmer, but as a passionate advocate for the unparalleled joy of the peach. As long as Peach is running, every 30 minutes a perfectly ripe peach, kissed by the sun and nurtured by nature, arrives in your Steam Inventory.
