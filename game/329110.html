About this gameFull description from Steam+
Usher in a new age of puzzle warfare and start your epic odyssey across the world of Krystara in a fantasy Match-3 RPG unlike any other. Create your adventurer and set off on a globe-trotting journey to save kingdoms and conquer regions with a growing roster of unique mythical troops that are yours to command in 4v4 puzzle combat. Embark on the next evolution of legendary match-3 RPGs from the creators of Puzzle Quest! Dive into a world filled with thousands of puzzle battles, deep RPG gameplay, and tactical puzzle strategy. Bring your hero into the fight, or build up to a squad of four troops to take into battle! Dominate the puzzle board by matching gems to earn mana and unleash your troop's abilities. Or match skulls to deal massive direct damage to your foes! Build your army by unlocking over 1,400 troops and upgrading them for even more power! Or equip your hero with a mighty weapon and lead your team of fantastical monsters into battle. Use strategy to build the right team to take on any challenge! Dive into competitive play and go head-to-head with other adventurers on the regional map. Join an Alliance to battle for control over the various Regions of Krystara for total supremacy, legendary rewards, and unique PVP buffs! Explore the world of Gems of War through multiple modes of play! Conquer over 30 kingdoms across Krystara, each with a unique quest line to complete. Or dive into the deep and mysterious Underworld to take on various factions with epic loot to claim! Take your adventure anywhere you go with cross-platform play!
