About this gameFull description from Steam+
Immerse yourself in the art of team management and experience the thrill of leading your team to success in Open Wheel Manager 2. This well-paced, strategic racing management game lets you take control of every aspect of your team's operations, from hiring drivers and engineers to designing and manufacturing cars. Make strategic decisions that will impact your team's performance, and stay ahead of the competition to achieve Formula 1 glory. The Team Is Yours to Manage As the manager, you hold the key to your team's destiny. Seek out sponsorships to develop a top-notch racing car that can conquer the tracks. Make smart decisions, build the perfect team, and strategize your way to victory in each season. Witness the action unfold on the revamped 3D engine: analyze every move, overtake, and event to react with the right decision. Don't worry about micro-management; focus on the crucial choices that will make or break your team. Build Your Racing Legacy Choose your challenge: take your team from the bottom of the pack to champions or continue the winning streak of a top-tier team. Your choices matter, and the future of your team is in your hands. Negotiate contracts with drivers, staff, and sponsors, and watch as some drivers bring valuable sponsorships to boost your team's prospects. Manage your resources wisely, upgrade facilities, and fine-tune your car to excel on different tracks. True Progression Experience the realistic progression of driver abilities as they gain experience, while veterans face new challenges. Witness drivers and staff transition between teams, adding an extra layer of intrigue to your journey. Laser-Focused Performance Engage with the race action in multiple ways! Immerse yourself in the 3D Raceview, get a bird's-eye view with the 2D race view or simulate the race and go straight to the results. While the dynamic commentary will highlight crucial moments in the race. Channel your inner racing guru and set your sights on the path to glory. Are you prepared to make the right decisions, trade-offs and priorities for the ultimate victory? The checkered flag awaits, and the race is about to begin!
