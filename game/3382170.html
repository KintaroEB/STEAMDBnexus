About this gameFull description from Steam+
Build Your Town From a Ruin As your city rivals, the appearance of the buildings will change—from thatched huts to grand mansions, from deserted place to thriving town. Try to use the unique ancient Oriental-style landscape to build up a staggering, prosperous, and lively paradise! Peace Life with Diverse Residents Recruit a variety of classes and different-looking residents! The characters you recruit will live in your city and take up different activities. As your city grows, you can recruit more people to join this city. Every day you'll see them strolling the streets, chatting with each other, or working hard. There are time and weather systems in the game, forming a dynamic cityscape! Craft Equipment, Develop Trade Trade with merchants for resources to upgrade your city! Residents with crafting abilities can create a wide variety of equipment. These armaments can be equipped for your residents to make it easier to defeat enemies when they explore, but also can be traded as commodities. Use earnings to upgrade your building and create a prosperous business center! Vast Map, Free Exploration Seamless maps are available for free exploration! Command your residents to explore the wild, gather resources, or fight for treasure. Train your residents, and lead them deeper into the mists to fight stronger enemies, spreading your name across the entire continent!
