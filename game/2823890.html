About this gameFull description from Steam+
You inherit a shopping center in the middle of the desert, an oasis for your customers crossing this hot road. Renovate it, make your own products in your expandable underground laboratory! Your Shopping Center is autonomous, manage your production of electricity, water, oil, resources. Build your LABORATORY Manufacture numerous industrial machines for your personal and commercial production! Expand your underground laboratory without limits. From the mine, to the ores, to the foundry, to manufacture advanced components essential to your development! Freely place your constructions, and destroy them to better organize your space! Set up your SHOP Thanks to numerous construction plans, arrange your shop to perfection! Sell many products previously manufactured in your laboratory! Automate your payment checkouts. Improvements are available to boost your sales! Expand your PARKING Build a parking lot for your customers using materials previously made in your laboratory! Equip your parking spaces with ticketing to charge your customers! Improve their comfort and earn more money! Industrial autonomy You are cut off from the rest of the world! Thanks to numerous technologies, manage your production of electricity, water, oil and raw materials! Roadmap Next updates to come: Gas station, car wash, laundromat, computer store, car garage, hotel, pets, arcade, clothing section, restaurant!
