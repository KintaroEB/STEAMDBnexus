About this gameFull description from Steam+
By the way, more than 13 million people in total have played the series. Those kinds of questions, these kinds of questions, too. Contains 1,380 questions. There are also stages where you have to do something with iconic characters, like a hero or a moai statues, with your own drawings. Think, get an inspiration, and give it a try. Sometimes you'll fail. Draw freely and solve it however you like! Before you know it, you'll lose track of time and be completely hooked. How would you solve it? --------------------------------------- There's also an 'IQ TEST' mode that measures your IQ (based on Q's unique criteria). *The in-game language can be set to English.
