Space Rock Breaker is a short incremental experience. Mine asteroids using weapons, process the ore using a Plinko machine, and use the rewards to increase your power. Mine asteroids using guns, missiles, and lasers to earn ore Process the ore using a upgradable Plinko machine to earn massive profits Spend your credits to upgrade your guns and ship Open lootboxes to earn bonuses and upgrades Go deeper into the cosmos and discover new and valuable ores.
