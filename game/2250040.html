About this gameFull description from Steam+
Instant Crosshair Overlay for Every Shooter Crosshair V2 gives you an instant competitive edge in any FPS, tactical shooter, or battle royale. Select from pro eSports presets — 3 sizes, 6 shapes, and 7 high-visibility neon colors — and overlay a precision reticle on top of any game in seconds. Lightweight, zero-config, and built to work. No design skills needed — just pick your crosshair and play. Why Gamers Choose Crosshair V2 Pro eSports Presets: Crosshairs modeled after the reticles used by professional competitive players Instant Aim Improvement: Improve your aim, response time, and hip fire accuracy with a clear, always-visible reticle High-Visibility Neon Colors: 7 vibrant neon colors designed to stand out against any game environment — bright or dark Multiple Sizes & Shapes: 3 sizes and 6 shapes to match your play style and screen resolution Zero Interference: Draws a monitor overlay independent of all games — focus on gameplay, not settings Borderless & Windowed Fullscreen: Guaranteed compatibility with borderless and windowed fullscreen modes Simple. Fast. Effective. Not everyone needs a full crosshair designer. Sometimes you just want a clean, visible reticle that works instantly. Crosshair V2 delivers exactly that — no learning curve, no bloat. Pick a preset and dominate. Looking for More Customization? Check out Crosshair X — our full-featured crosshair designer with thousands of community designs, a pixel-level editor, Steam Workshop integration, and Game Bar support for exclusive fullscreen.
