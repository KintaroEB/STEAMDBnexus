About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Digimon Adventure crossover is now live! Taichi and Agumon, along with Yamato and Gabumon, have arrived in Esperia! Log in during the event to claim the crossover heroes! Don't miss the chance to earn 30 exclusive Invite Letters! Enjoy a fun summer alongside the DigiDestined and their Partner Digimon! Set out on an adventure with courage—and with your friends by your side! Welcome to the enchanted land of Esperia! Play as the legendary mage Merlin and explore a beautifully illustrated storybook world. Outsmart your foes in strategic card battles and uncover the secrets of this world alongside other heroes. 1,000 Recruits | Claim Launch Heroes Log in and claim all launch heroes! Begin your journey with your dream formation! Play Smart | Adapt Formations for Different Strategies Power isn't everything! It's possible to defeat stronger opponents with your wit and strategy! Deploy heroes from six classes, position them strategically, and time their ultimates to overcome your enemies! Use faction buffs, terrain, and battle mechanics to your advantage! Shared Levels | Deploy New Heroes Immediately Equipment and levels are shared across heroes. No more grinding! Feel free to try out different formations at zero cost! 100 Heroes | Original and Unique Designs Meet 100 heroes from seven factions! Witness the Lightbearers, who carry the pride of humanity. Watch the Wilders flourish at the heart of their forest. Observe how the Maulers survive against all odds through sheer strength. The Graveborn legions are amassing, and the eternal clash between the Celestials and the Hypogeans rages on. And then there are the Dimensional heroes, visiting from distant worlds. Discover their skills and captivating backstories. Excellent Graphics | Immersive, Vast Magical World Explore the storybook-style magical world, where every detail is meticulously crafted. Roam through forests and mountains, using vines and tree stumps to bridge waterfalls and rivers as you take in the stunning scenery. Along the way, enjoy a range of interactive experiences, such as petting stray cats on the streets, going ice-skating, and having snowball fights... Every moment is full of surprises! Epic Adventures | Uncover Secrets as Magister Merlin A new adventure awaits with every season! Long ago, the seed of life drifted to Esperia from the stars and took root, giving birth to a land of wonder. However, as the gods fell during a chaotic war, six great factions emerged, each vying for power and dominance. Remember, only you can guide heroes to pull "the sword from the stone" and learn the truth about the world. Collect Resources Quickly | Level Up Fast Say goodbye to grinding for resources. Collect rewards effortlessly with our auto-battle and AFK features. Continue gathering resources even while you're sleeping. Enjoy hassle-free resource collection and smooth, rewarding progression!
