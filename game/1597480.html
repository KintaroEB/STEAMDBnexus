About this gameFull description from Steam+
You are the Airman, one of the last surviving people on Earth. Take flight in the AC-130 gunship and protect your troops from the undead. Blast them to pieces and lead your men to victory, soldier. Kill the dead, protect the living. Unleash Hell from Above The dead have mutated, morphed into powerful monstrosities. You’ll need a varied arsenal to deal with them properly, Airman. Collect realistic weapons and increase their power to obliterate your enemies from above. Collect Realistic Weapons Your base of operations is the heart of the survivors’ enclave. If it is to have a chance of standing against the endless waves of zombies, you will need to upgrade your buildings and defenses. Your towers and walls will keep the dead at bay while you rain hellfire from above. Upgrade Your Base Features: Provide air support and protect your troops from hordes of zombies. Plan your mission before deploying your troops to achieve maximum efficiency Choose the right troops for the mission from the available classes: Assault, Scout, Demolitioner, and Sniper. Enjoy accurate infrared night vision and challenging first person shooter combat scenarios from the seat of your AC-130 Gunship. Zombies with a multitude of abilities threaten your squad’s safety. From the massive titans, to the devastating wreckers, these mutated monstrosities will pose a great risk to the survival of your troops. Collect gold and other rewards to build better defenses in your shelter. Upgrade your towers, barricades and weapons to defend your military base against the undead. Compete in weekly events for legendary rewards! Play with a vibrant community. Discuss shooter game strategies with friends.
