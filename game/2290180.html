About this gameFull description from Steam+
Go head-to-head with over 50 players as you fight your way to the finish line! The Republic is always evolving with seasonal events, new sports, and challenges. Jump into pick-up runs, trick competitions, or laid-back crew rides. There's always a new reason to drop in, send it, and shred the massive multiplayer playground! Switch on the fly between bike, skis, snowboard, or wingsuit as you roam an expansive world of urban streets and iconic US national parks: Yosemite, Zion, Bryce Canyon, Yellowstone, Grand Canyon. Unlock a vast array of cosmetic items, from iconic brands to legendary streetwear, and build a look to show off your style as you dominate the slopes and trails.
