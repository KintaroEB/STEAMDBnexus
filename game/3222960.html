About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Eternal Tree" is a turn-based combat role-playing game that describes a journey spanning the Divine Era and the modern age. A grand epic, unknown challenges, and the continent of Titalyera await your exploration. 【Worldview】 ▽A grand epic of a fantasy world The continent "Titalyera" is a land where Divine Era civilization and modern civilization blend. The ruins left by the deities of the Divine Era hide many precious relics. Modern people explore these ruins and use the relics within to rapidly advance civilization. Those who make a living uncovering ruins are collectively referred to as "Seekers." A chance encounter with the mysterious doll Artemisia draws you, an ordinary Seeker, into a cross-era conflict on the continent of "Titalyera." Your adventure will determine the future direction of this world. 【Game Features】 ▽The use of various visual materials in the storyline presents a rich and colorful visual experience. Tense combat scenes are dynamic and vivid, as if part of a beautifully crafted animated series. ▽Under a gorgeous and unique art style, diverse and distinctive characters will protect your adventure! ▽Use powerful Resonance Bursts to decide the outcome of battles! ▽Summon powerful Imaginary Beings to unleash Imaginary Descents 【Game Features】 · Classic turn-based combat—build your powerful team! · Main storyline progresses up to Chapter 11, with a unique visual novel-style narrative for deeper immersion! · Stunning character designs and spectacular mech illustrations offer unique, romantic beauty! · Generous newbie rewards accelerate growth, kicking off an exciting adventure quickly!
