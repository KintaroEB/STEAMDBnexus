About this gameFull description from Steam+
RAZORTRON 2000 is a retro top-down endless racing game. Try to beat your highscore, unlock new cars and climb your way to the top of the leaderboards on this love letter to coin eater arcade machines era! Avoid traffic, side-barriers and running out of fuel on procedurally generated path and traffic. Each car changes the whole palette of the game, creating another variation on the experience. Perfect to play when you want a quick shot of ａｅｓｔｈｅｔｉｃｓ, or are in a queue for another game in a fun small and replayable game. Try to unlock new cars and improve your score few minutes long rounds. Inspired by classic racing game for the 80s and 90s era like Road Fighter, Spy Hunter, Super Sprint. Beautifull retro pixel art with ａｅｓｔｈｅｔｉｃｓ vibe. 10 unlockable cars, each of them with different specs. Endless procedurally generated path and traffic. Original soundtrack, featuring 10 synthwave style music by Derek Volker. Steam Achievements, Leaderboards, Trading Cards and Cloud Save.
