About this gameFull description from Steam+
You have been sent to one of the northernmost regions operated by Company Mining Corp, where a vast wilderness filled with resources, opportunities, and challenges awaits. Starting with limited equipment and experience, it is up to you to prospect for ore, establish mining operations, transport materials, and build a profitable company from the ground up. Every load of ore sold brings you one step closer to expanding your fleet, unlocking new equipment, and taking on larger projects. Dig, blast, build roads, process materials, manufacture products, and develop your own industrial empire across multiple large maps. Establish production facilities, create logistics networks, and shape the landscape around you as your operation continues to grow. Throughout your journey, you'll gain access to a wide range of vehicles and machinery, each of which can be customized with lights, GPS systems, paint jobs, tires, tracks, and other upgrades. From a lone laborer operating basic equipment to the owner of a massive industrial company, every decision and every project contributes to your progression. Work your way from Level 1 all the way to Level 70 and earn the prestigious title of King of Klondike. Whether you choose to focus on mining, construction, logistics, production, exploration, or ambitious community projects, the path is yours to create. Out of Ore supports multiplayer, controllers, joysticks, steering wheels, and head tracking through OpenTrack. The game is a "Work in progress" and still being developed by a small 6 person crew
