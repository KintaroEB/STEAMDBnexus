About this gameFull description from Steam+
Fairground Online – Your Ultimate Ride Simulator! Fairground Online is a realistic fairground and ride simulation in which you take control of impressive attractions and experience a lively amusement park together with other players. Operate rides just like on real fairgrounds, create spectacular light shows, and deliver unforgettable moments for your visitors. From a relaxed family day to a glowing fairground night, every session offers new experiences in a dynamic and atmospheric amusement park world. Take on the role of a real showman and control highly detailed rides directly from the operator’s control panel. Adjust movements and sequences and ensure the perfect ride experience for your guests. Online Multiplayer: Experience the fairground together with other players in a lively online world. Explore the festival grounds together or take control of your favorite rides yourself. Realistic Ride Physics: Experience rides that move and behave realistically. Acceleration, rotation, centrifugal forces, and ride-specific motion patterns have been designed to authentically recreate the feeling of real fairground attractions. Realistic Controls: Each ride features its own detailed control panel. Control speed, direction of rotation, and various functions just like on a real fairground, while maintaining full control over the ride experience at all times. Light Control: Give your attractions their own character. Switch individual lighting elements on or off and create impressive light shows that provide a unique atmosphere, especially in the evening. Music and Jingles: A fairground wouldn’t be complete without music. Choose suitable tracks and jingles to ensure your attractions not only look spectacular but also sound the part. Dynamic Day-Night Cycle: Experience the transition from a busy daytime fairground to a brightly lit nighttime carnival. Different times of day change the atmosphere and make the fairground feel new every time. Dynamic Weather System: Sunshine, cloudy skies, or rain—changing weather conditions add variety and make every session unique. Experience your rides under a wide range of weather scenarios. For Fairground and Theme Park Fans: Fairground Online is designed for everyone who loves rides, engineering, and the special atmosphere of amusement parks. Whether you enjoy relaxing rides or perfectly operating attractions, this game puts the passion for fairs at its core. When the lights turn on, the music starts playing, and the first rides begin to move, your day as a showman begins. Welcome to Fairground Online.
