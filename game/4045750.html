About this gameFull description from Steam+
We are thrilled to introduce Out of the Park Baseball 27 - the 27th edition of our award-winning baseball strategy series. Fans around the world have fallen in love with Out of the Park Baseball, recognizing its unique place in the intersection of video games and sport! If it happens in real baseball, it happens in Out of the Park Baseball! Develop and discover new talent, draft the next superstar, negotiate with free agents, enter trade talks with other teams, build your dynasty and guide a franchise into an infinite future! As a manager, dive into the more hands-on management and set your team's lineups, pitching staffs, in-game strategy and call the shots from the dugout. You can oversee your league at a high level, or play out each game in the gorgeous 3D game mode, managing play by play or even pitch by pitch. OOTP has it all covered! Here are some of the new features coming to Out of the Park Baseball 27: Out of the Park Baseball has always been known as a statistical marvel - and we've taken that to entirely new levels this season! The team has incorporated dozens of new statistics. This is a breakthrough for modern fans, who now have all new tools to understand how their teams are performing - and why! This is the most anticipated World Baseball Classic™ in history, and we are thrilled to deliver the authentic World Baseball Classic™. The real World Baseball Classic™ federations, participants, schedules, and more will be here. This franchise-mode exclusive feature allows everyone to experience the best of international baseball in true OOTP fashion while it's happening and well beyond!! The Out of the Park Baseball team strives to deliver an immersive in-game experience, and this season is loaded with new items. From dynamic weather to updated player models, new animations, and incorporating advanced stats into the action, playing out your games in 3D is the best and most dynamic it's ever been! Customization has always been the calling card of Out of the Park Baseball - and now we've added new Difficulty settings to further flesh out how truly great of an MLB General Manager you are! Plus, there are huge updates to the AI Trade Model, which will present you with unique challenges all season long. There's always a lot to do in Out of the Park Baseball, and this season we've spent an extraordinary amount of time optimizing the user interface to make it as efficient as possible to do everything you'd like. Improved calendars, in-game screens, player pages, and much more await you in OOTP 27! Our incredible partnership with the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum continues this year, with new items and integrations. Plus! All the licensed international leagues you have come to expect from Out of the Park Baseball return this season, including the KBO in Korea, Honkbal Hoofdklasse in the Netherlands, and the British Baseball Federation. As always, Out of the Park Baseball features up-to-the-minute 2026 MLB and MiLB and KBO rosters that get updated all season long, with every franchise represented from its big league club all the way down to the low minors, and everywhere in between! Perfect Team returns this season with a bang! The awesome online competitive multiplayer mode will deliver the most content, rewards, and competitions in its history - with surprises big and small coming all season long! We've got all-new licensed legends making their debut, new Hall of Fame goodies, and so much more. Be sure to follow us on the Road to Release as we unveil the details! If you love Perfect Team, you're going to have a blast this year. If you've never tried it - or have been away for a bit - you'll be blown away at how easy it is to jump right in and have fun, no matter what your play style! We're crazy about baseball here, and as always we have made dozens of improvements that our fans will love! From Quality of Life improvements to an all-new way to play Perfect Team (details soon!) you'll be blown away by how much fun you can have. Out of the Park Baseball 27 runs on PC/Mac and features the American League and National League logos, the World Series trophy, official logos and jerseys for all 30 MLB teams and 10 KBO teams, over 150 Minor League Baseball league and team logos, and historical MLB logos.
