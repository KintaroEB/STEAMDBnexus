About this gameFull description from Steam+
World of Eggs: Idle Adventures is a unique adventure in the style of a global strategy, where you build your own civilization, trade with other players, fight in raids, and all this happens in a fully idle mode. All the routine — resource gathering, crafting, and distribution — is automated, so you can focus on strategy, development, and collection. With a single click, launch processes that continue even while you are offline. Build your economy, plan interactions with other players, and send your armies to hunt for unique rewards. World of Eggs: Idle Adventures combines familiar elements of strategy with an innovative idle progression system. [b>You will find: [/b] [/p] Player-driven economy: build trade chains, rent items, and use the market to grow your wealth. Process automation: crafting, resource gathering, and army development work even without your involvement. Raids and battles: join cooperative events, defeat bosses, and earn exclusive collectible items. Item rental system: new ways to interact with other players. Over 100 unique units: each with its own role and abilities. Over 200 unique items: resources, artifacts, and collectibles. A fully idle game: made for those who value strategy and progression over clicking buttons. World of Eggs: Idle Adventures is an engaging idle adventure that appeals equally to newcomers to idle games and experienced strategists. Every effort influences your progress: investments in the economy strengthen your army, resource gathering unlocks new opportunities, and raids challenge you with unique rewards. Regular updates bring new units, items, and mechanics, so the world constantly grows and evolves. Will you be able to build the greatest civilization in the world of eggs?
