About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dive into the explosive mayhem of Bombergrounds, where every battle is a wild storm of chaos! Whether flying solo or bringing the squad, the battlefield is ripe for the taking. Are you ready to face the pandemonium? Discover a variety of game modes that’ll test your mettle and hone your skills. From the ruthless Battle Royale arenas to the strategic alleys of Duck Grab, every showdown is a step closer to victory. Game Modes: - Battle Royale: Stand tall in a 10-player showdown and seize the Victory Royale! - Duck Grab: Team up in a 3v3 face-off, grab those Golden Ducks, and defend 'em to snag the win! - Team Fight: Dive in, decimate the opposition in a Best-of-Three team clash, and claim your bragging rights. - Duel: A face-off of pure skill. Outplay your opponent and snag that sweet W! Animal Heroes & Powers: Unlock a horde of badass animals armed with killer abilities. Easy to handle, a challenge to master. Level Up Your Arsenal: Harvest and upgrade your animals, harness their unique powers, and carve out your own savage path to victory. Runes: Tailor your animal with unique runes, find your rhythm, and let the battlefield fear your name. Bomber Pass Rewards: Climb the Bomber Pass, reap epic loot. The more you play, the more you score! League Progress: Rack up wins, hoard trophies, and unlock a vault of swag. Leaderboards: Rise through the local and global ranks. Are you the predator or the prey? Skins: Snag the coolest skins for your animal, stand out from the pack, and strut your stuff. - Engage in Battle Royale Online multiplayer mayhem. - Delight in the colorful, cute design. - Unlock a world of cosmetics and emotes. Bombergrounds isn’t just a game, it’s an adrenaline-pumping, laugh-inducing, bomber-blitzing arena of glory. Are you ready to dominate the grounds?
