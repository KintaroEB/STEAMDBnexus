About this gameFull description from Steam+
Become part of an elite special forces unit in Tactical Assault VR! Tactical Assault VR is an intense tactical shooter where you use real-world CQB (Close Quarters Battles) tactics to stack up, breach doors and eliminate threats. Play solo or join an online co-op special forces unit in missions around the world. Features PvE Missions: Solo or Online Multiplayer Co-Op with Up to 8 Players on PCVR PvP: Competitive Online Multiplayer 9 Non-Linear Asymmetrical Missions, 3 Wave Shooter Experiences, Room Randomizer & Sandbox Mode Plan your assault. The choice is yours. Game Mode Variety Bomb Defusal, Hostage Rescue, Enemy Surrendering, High-Value Target (HVT) Eliminations, Mini Games, Killhouse, Hardcore Mode. 60+ Detailed Weapons and Counting Customize your loadout with tactical gear including vests, helmets, weapons, scopes, sights, grips, and breaching equipment.
