About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Just Pizza , a game that turns your love for pizza into an exciting and rewarding experience! This game is all about clicking on a pizza to earn more pizzas. The more you click, the more pizzas you get. It’s as simple and fun as that! In Just Pizza , every click counts. Each click brings you closer to your next achievement, making every moment in the game thrilling. But that’s not all! The game also features in-game drops that reward you with cool skins, adding a layer of customization and personalization to your pizza clicking journey. Features: Click and Earn: Click on a pizza to earn points. The more you click, the more points you get! Item Drops: Receive a random item drop, which can be used as a skin in the game trade or sell on market. Upgrade Items: Upgrade your drop items to get better items. Five Rarities: Items come in five rarities - common, uncommon, rare, epic, and legendary. Unique is for limited items. Endless Fun: With endless clicks and numerous items to collect, the fun never stops in Just Pizza!
