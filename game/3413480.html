About this gameFull description from Steam+
Turn your dream of selling used cars into reality! In Car Dealer Simulator, you'll learn how to run a business from its owner, Little Sam. He’s a strange guy who doesn’t manage his affairs well. Your task is to buy cheap, repair, and sell high. However, as you get the hang of your first tasks, you'll have a chance to take over the business, and then... ...it will all be in your hands! Develop a business that fulfills the automotive fantasies of customers, from budget-friendly cars to flashy rides. Seize opportunities, hire the best, provide excellent customer service, and make a profit. Grow your business day by day. In the demo version, Little Sam will assign you tasks that help you learn many mechanics and introduce you to the ins and outs of the business. The key to achieving high profits lies in negotiating when buying old cars. Look for great deals, and during price haggling, stay firm and professional. Not all purchased cars are in working condition, so you’ll need to transport them using one of two types of tow trucks. Every car requires diagnostics and preparation before being sold. Mechanical repairs, bodywork, painting, and small touch-ups will significantly increase the selling price. Presenting a car to the customer requires a few tricks. Washing, good photos, and an engaging advertisement will be your allies. Highlight the car’s strengths in your listing, but remember—every car has its hidden flaws. Customers buy with their eyes, so use the shine of the paint, a full tank, and quality photos to your advantage. With your profits, you can continuously improve your company. Upgrade your tools and repair areas. Take care of a good sign that will attract customers, and build your reputation. At the same time, stay alert because the competition and even your “friendly neighbors” never rest. Protect what you’ve tirelessly built from potential threats.
