About this gameFull description from Steam+
It's summer. You and your friends are on vacation, when suddenly the sea carries your boat away to an unknown island in another world. This island—Paradisiem Island—is small, with one village. According to the village chief, the island's Guardian Deity foresaw the arrival of you and your friends, and they want you to help the island return to its former glory. Without more tourists, there aren't enough people to worship the Guardian Deity, whose powers you need to return to your home world. Work together with your friends, Sahoto, Mei, and Chiyo, as well as some new friends you make on the island, to help it grow and prosper into a popular tourist destination! Explore Paradisiem Island and all it has to offer! Take a companion of your choice and visit areas like the lush Paradisiem Forest or serene Vienni Quarry to gather materials like wood, stone, ore, and gems to further the development of the island. If you're lucky, you'll find some premium items that you can sell at the Shop! Once the Shop opens up, you'll be able to buy and craft special items that can help you gather more items, improve your bonds with your friends, and help work more efficiently. Gather Materials All Over The Island Along with resources like wood, stones, ore, and gems, there are plenty of materials to gather around the island! You'll explore the island with a companion of your choice and encounter fun events as you search for items. As you develop the island, you'll unlock new locations and be able to get your hands on rare materials! Missions and Rewards Galore! With dozens of different missions, you'll be busy every day! Complete missions and obtain useful items that can aid you in gathering or crafting new resources. Not only do missions help with your work on the island, but some people may give you unique special rewards. Whether you are a completionist or just want some bonus items, there's something for everyone!
