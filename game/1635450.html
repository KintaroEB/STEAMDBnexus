About this gameFull description from Steam+
You have been assigned to travel to Longvinter Island to research everything that makes the island special and uncover what other organizations have desperately tried to hide. You begin your expedition at one of many research camps around the island. Buy yourself tools, sell the resources you gather, and invest in new and more advanced equipment to compete against fellow researchers. Team up, fight, or betray your neighbours as you battle over valuable resources, and establish trade networks to secure what you need next. Key features Hand-crafted open-world - explore, learn and take advantage of the surrounding nature. Learn the best fishing and berry spots and build your camp near them to maximize your income PVP - You're not the only one trying to succeed on the island. Make alliances, defend and conquer new areas you find valuable PVE - Face off against hostile mercenaries and uncover their secrets hidden in bunkers, cave bases, and massive oil rigs Building - Build anywhere you want with anyone you want. Pick a spot for your tent, get yourself a campfire going, and start expanding. Store all your valuables inside the tent, which only you can access. Upgrade your research base along 8 different specialization paths to match your playstyle Crafting - Instantly combine items at a workbench to create new gear from over 500 unique items in the game. Customize your weapons with attachments and paint your furniture to create your perfect base Farming - Grow food for yourself or sell it to make money Cooking - Experiment with countless ingredient combinations and spices to create unique dishes. Master the art of cuisine and become the island's top chef Sandbox - Do whatever you want, wherever you want, with whoever you want. There are no rules or tasks. Make the game what you want it to be Trading - Sell your knowledge and rare items to other players, set up your own shop to specialize in unique goods, or make steady income from camp vendors Travel - Navigate between islands using your own boat or hunt local wildlife to acquire mounts for exploring different regions Dedicated Server tools Free content updates
