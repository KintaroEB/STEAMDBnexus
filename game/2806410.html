About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome, commander, to Project Entropy - the ultimate Sci-Fi and role-playing game with space battles. Immerse yourself in this MMO experience where you'll command alien civilizations, conquer uncharted planets, and build Hero fleets. Command Alien Civilizations: Take control of alien civilizations and lead them to victory. Each civilization comes with its unique abilities, technologies, and units, providing diverse gameplay experiences. Conquer Uncharted Planets: Embark on an adventure across the cosmos and conquer uncharted planets. Explore unknown territories, engage in epic battles, and expand your empire to dominate the galaxy. Build Hero Fleets: Assemble powerful fleets of hero ships, each with its special abilities and attributes. Customize your fleets to suit your playstyle and lead them to victory against formidable foes. Customize Mechs and Weapons: Modify and upgrade your mechs and high-tech weapons to enhance their capabilities on the battlefield. Experiment with different combinations to create the ultimate war machines. Engage in Intense PvP and PvE Battles: Challenge other players in intense PvP battles or team up with friends to take on challenging PvE encounters. Test your skills, strategy, and teamwork to emerge victorious in battle. Explore Vast Maps: Discover vast and diverse maps filled with unique challenges, resources, and secrets. Uncover the mysteries of the universe as you journey through space and conquer new territories. Join a Global Community: Connect with players from around the world, form alliances, and compete in epic interstellar conflicts. Forge friendships, share strategies, and rise to become a legend in the galaxy. Season-Based Gameplay: Experience dynamic gameplay with seasonal updates that introduce new planets, challenges, and rewards. Stay engaged with fresh content and compete for dominance in the ever-changing universe. Epic Storyline: Immerse yourself in an epic storyline filled with intrigue, betrayal, and discovery. Unravel the mysteries of the universe and shape the fate of civilizations as you embark on a thrilling adventure. Stunning Visuals and Sound: Enjoy stunning graphics and immersive sound effects that bring the universe of Project Entropy to life. Immerse yourself in the beauty and chaos of space battles like never before. Embark on an epic journey across the stars and become the ultimate commander in Project Entropy. Download now and join the battle for galactic supremacy!
