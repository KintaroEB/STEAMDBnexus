About this gameFull description from Steam+
Will You Wheel? is an early access car life simulation where you rebuild rusty cars into a race car, and hit the open road to prove it. FIX IT TO START. Make the car run again, then keep it in good condition and upgrade it over time. CUSTOMIZE THE BUILD. EARN CASH. UPGRADE WITH PARTS. Develop the car to match changing goals as the build progresses: parts can be added or replaced, and the setup can be modified over time. Fund each step by selling parts to customers and ordering new components, turning a worn out project into a competitive race car. TOW, HAUL, AND FIND PROJECTS. Use a truck to transport parts and tow scrapyard vehicles out of the junkyard. OPEN MAP. ENDLESS WAYS TO RACE. Drive across a large open map, then enter different race events as your car improves. WIN THE RACE. CLAIM THE CAR. Win races to climb the blacklist. Each victory adds a new reward: the defeated driver’s car becomes part of the garage. KEY FEATURES Semi Detailed  car assembly with lots of parts Buy junk cars, diagnose issues, repair, upgrade, and race Order parts, build at home Missions that keep the project cars funded Multiple race types: drag, drift, street, rally Open-world roads with traffic Property purchases: houses, garages, shops
