About this gameFull description from Steam+
Rooftops & Alleys is more than just a game about Parkour & Freerunning. It’s the adrenaline rush of landing an impossible trick combo, defying gravity, and feeling like you’re flying… until you crash face-first into a dark alley 30 meters below. In solo or with your friends, explore in total freedom massive-parkour-ready maps. Reach every corner of your playground to uncover hidden spots — time trials, trick challenges, and more! In multiplayer, dive into game modes like TAG, Capture the Flag, Trick Battles, or simply freeroam with your friends! Explore huge, unique, vertical parkour environments Discover, learn, and master every trick, move, and their endless combo possibilities Call on your pigeon to discover new areas, fast travel, and plan your routes Tons of challenges and game modes — solo or with friends (TAG, Capture the Flag, Trick Battles, and more) Unlock over 100 customization options for yourself and your pigeon Core Game Features: Parkour & Freerunning Intuitive Controls Tricking & Scoring System Singleplayer & Multiplayer Free Roam Time Trials Trick Challenges Level Editor Session Marker & Pin Dropper System (Pigeon) Multiple Maps Character Customization Third and First-Person Camera Ragdoll/Bail Button Slowmotion Button Dynamic Weather Time Of Day Settings Original in-game Music Replay Editor Photomode Multiplayer Features: Play with Friends (Invitational or Session Browser) TAG Game Mode (Up to 4 Players) Capture The Flag Game Mode (Up to 4 Players) Freeplay Game Mode (Up to 4 Players) Tricks Battle Game Mode (Up to 4 Players)
