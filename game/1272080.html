About this gameFull description from Steam+
New York doesn't give. You take it. PAYDAY 3 is a 1-to-4 player co-op FPS and the ultimate heist fantasy, set in the most iconic city in the world. Gone without a trace or through a hail of bullets. Either way, you're getting out with the prize. Not just any city, it's the city. From the financial fortresses of Wall Street to Manhattan penthouses and the streets in between. The city that never sleeps, a playground for the perfect crime. One moment, a part of the crowd; the next, you are infamous. Build your own heister with custom looks, loadout, and skills, and step into the world's most recognizable crew. The plan is only perfect until it isn't. The job doesn't end when the alarm sounds; it just gets louder. Cops, SWAT, and Special Units converge to shut you down, and your crew has to fight its way out with the loot. Coordinate everything with a tight crew, find one along the way. Every role matters when the law pushes back, and a crew that holds its nerve under pressure is the difference between a clean getaway and a very bad day.
