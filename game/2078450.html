About this gameFull description from Steam+
Are you looking for more speed and more dakka in your life? Look no further YA GITS! Update Speed Freeks is now coming to Playstation and Xbox! With cross platform crossplay support, so you can krump yer mates wherever they play! Join da Speedwaaagh! alone with bots or online with friends as you race through war-torn maps with a variety of heavily-armed, speedy and agile vehicles! Prove you’re the fastest git in DEFF RALLY - where teams of 8 players race and fight through waypoints, battling for position before a final sprint to the finish! Protect your formidable Stompa from getting bombed in KILL KONVOY - where two teams battle to slow down the opposing stompa and speed theirs up by destroying enemies. First to reach the end of the map gets the glory! Play custom maps and racetracks from the community in KUSTOM RALLY. There's all kinds of wacky creations to choose from on the Workshop Master advanced movement techniques and use explosive weaponry to dominate the racetrack! Use different vehicle classes including buggies, tanks and helicopters - each with distinct abilities that target a variety of playstyles. Progress your vehicles to unlock cosmetics and alternative weapons that make enemies go boom! Use the robust Creation Mode LEVEL EDITOR to make your own maps and racetracks! Use 400+ available game assets to create custom races, challenges, stunt parks, tower climbs, and anything else you can think of - the only limit is your imagination! You can even build things with other players in real-time: Jump into the MEK WORKSHOP to share your own creations, or rate and download other players’ crazy kustom maps they’ve made. The Workshop is a place where community creativity can thrive Immerse yourself in the badass Warhammer 40,000 universe by joining Da Kult of Speed! Slap on different kits, rims, tires, accessories, and paint jobs for your war-torn wagons. The game’s accurate Ork vehicles are just like the miniatures! And customize your Ork pit boss for taunting victims you krump! WAAAAAAAAAAAAGGHHHHHHHH!!!!!
