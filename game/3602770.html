About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Shroom and Gloom demo drops you into a weird and moody double-deckbuilding roguelike. Build two distinct decks, battle through 26 handcrafted tunnels, and face off against spore-swarming enemies and biome bosses. Discover game-changing Foresight cards to shape your experience through multiple runs, unlock powerful combos across three playstyles, and explore strange rewards hidden in The Gloom. Our dream version of Shroom and Gloom features hundreds of cards, deep meta-progression and scores of unique enemies, spread across a dense network of caves and tunnels. High fives for being part of the first wave of people picking up our game in this early stage of development! We're so grateful for every player who's joining us on our quest to make Shroom and Gloom the best looking, most fun deckbuilder out there!
