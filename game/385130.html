About this gameFull description from Steam+
RIDE THE RHYTHM! DvDrum, Ultimate Drum Simulator! is Sandbox Drum Simulator that allows to build and customize your Drumkits and play them in a realistic and funny drummer experience with high quality sound! Collect and manage your drumkits and play them with drumless tracks, included, or load your own track. Stream or record your drumming performance! CREATE YOUR DRUMKITS WITH UNLIMITED CUSTOMIZATION! Drumkits, Sounds, Images, Inputs and Drumless Tracks, all aspects are customizable with over internal 800 sounds and 900 images. INFINITE POSSIBILITIES WITH OVER 3000 WORKSHOP ITEMS! It is not enough? Expand your DvDrum with over 3000 Workshop items! You can create your own items easily directly in-game! FUN FOR EVERYONE! LEARN TO PLAY DRUMS AND SONGS! DvDrum allows to learn to control the rhythm in very short time and learn to play drums while having fun! Drumless Tracks inside from easy to hard skill level and a metronome to help you to learn. DvDrum can offers both relaxing and adrenalinic gameplay experience with great satisfaction! Let's Drumming! SUPPORT OF MULTIPLE INPUT DEVICES! Play with your keyboard, gamepad, MIDI controller, game drumkits, USB pedals and much more! You can also combine them to play all devices simultaneously! The input system is fully customizable for all drum parts. If you want you can assemble your drumming station! A NEW DVDRUM: MORE POWER, MORE EXTENSIBILITY, MORE FUN! DvDrum, Ultimate Drum Simulator! is the fourth version of the Drum Simulator. The new DvDrum introduce a new audio engine with panning control, pitching, special sound modes, MIDI velocity dynamics and more. LET'S PLAY DRUMS! \m/
