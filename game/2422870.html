About this gameFull description from Steam+
Bronzebeard's Tavern is a goofy and playful restaurant manager sim where 1-8 players can work as a team with multiplayer co-op. Fulfill a number of different roles, serve a diverse range of customers and cook up delicious dwarven cuisine. 8-Player Multiplayer! Work together (or try) to keep the tavern running for as long as you can with seven other dwarves in online multiplayer. Serve Customers! Properly manage customer seating and orders and deliver them the correct foods to reap rewards! Cook Up and Blend Delicious Food and Drink! Prepare food for the customers upon receiving orders from your teammates, being careful not to burn anything! Customize your Menu! Your daily customers are always different! Design your menu however you like based on the day's forecast and food sales! Many Customers to Discover! There's always an unfamiliar face showing up when you're working in a tavern... can you satisfy them all? Upgrade your Tavern! Build up your tavern with upgrades! These decisions change the way you play and are crucial for progressing into tougher days. Opt for more customer capacity with a shiny new expansion or utilities like more blenders or more stoves to increase your output!
