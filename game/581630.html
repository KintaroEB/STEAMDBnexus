About this gameFull description from Steam+
Sword With Sauce is a fast-paced stealth/action sandbox that puts you in control of how every mission plays out. With an arsenal of weapons and gadgets at your disposal, you can take on enemies however you see fit—go in loud, leave no witnesses, or strike from the shadows, eliminating foes without a sound. Choose from a variety of weapons suited for different playstyles, whether it’s deflecting bullets with a sword, taking out enemies from afar, or unleashing pure chaos with rapid firepower. Complement your arsenal with a range of gadgets, from tactical tools like gas grenades and ninja stars to the more unconventional pocket-sized black holes and explosive robotic crabs. . Each level is a playground of possibilities, giving you total freedom to experiment, adapt, and take down enemies in creative ways. Whether you prefer calculated precision or all-out mayhem, Sword With Sauce lets you become the ultimate assassin on your terms.
