About this gameFull description from Steam+
You and a buddy robbed a mob. You lived a nice life until assassins burst into your house. They found you. You have to find the hidden contact with your friend and together somehow get out of trouble. Go wild in a typical American town. 4 houses are available for theft, all with different loot. Who knows what you'll find? More than 5 missions to complete (from robberies to planting suspicious packages on people, or taking photos for hire). Buy numerous tools of all kinds - use them to help yourself while plunging into others' houses. Crowbars, lockpicks, binoculars, ATM hacking tools etc.
