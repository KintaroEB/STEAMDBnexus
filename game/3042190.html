About this gameFull description from Steam+
Cut down hordes of enemies automatically in Nomad Idle, a spin-off of Nomad Survival. Engage in an expansive storyline, make impactful decisions, and grow stronger through various gameplay features that gradually unlock as you progress. Nomad Idle is an idle game with a defined end and is not designed to be endless. However, that does not mean the game is short or lacks depth. In Nomad Idle, you can expect to have access to the following as you play through the game and unlock more features: Level up and earn Stat Points which may be distributed within 7 unique Stats, allowing you to create a build. Discover, craft, and upgrade Equipment in order to make yourself stronger. Conjure Summons to aid you in battle. Select Passive Skills to be automatically used in battle, further defining your build. Choose a Character, defining your active abilities and special perks for your playthrough. Nomad Idle is an idle game, meaning that it mostly plays on its own. Your character moves and attacks enemies all on their own, but it can be played actively by making use of character-specific skills and abilities. Playing actively in this way is entirely optional and not mandatory, but can be effective in speeding up your progress or help you overcome challenges.
