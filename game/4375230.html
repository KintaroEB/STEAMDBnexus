About this gameFull description from Steam+
■ A Quick Note Up Front About Purchases Three Kingdoms: BOND is a one-time purchase. There is no gacha. All 100+ warlords can be unlocked simply by playing the game. The Time-Saver Packs available on the store do exactly what the name suggests: they only help you unlock warlords faster. Even without purchasing them, you can still unlock every single warlord through normal gameplay. There are no warlords exclusive to the Time-Saver Packs. In PvP battles, every player competes under exactly the same conditions. ■About Three Kingdoms:BOND A competitive auto-battler with matches decided in just 10 minutes. The roguelite element comes from recruiting warlords: each match, you build your army on the fly from a random selection of warlords. Victory is determined by placement and outsmarting your opponent. Battles are fully automated, so it’s your decisions—not your reflexes—that make the difference. Game Features 1. A Full Draft System Where Every Match Creates a New Strategy There are no fixed meta formations. Each match presents you with a challenging selection of warlords. Choose wisely and build the formation best suited to each unique battlefield. 2. Forge the Ultimate BOND Beyond History Warlord affinities and skills create powerful synergies. Build dream teams that transcend historical alliances and turn the tide of battle. 3. Over 100 Illustrated Warlords A passionate roster of warlords, brought to life by a team of talented illustrators. Even more warlords will be added through future updates. 4. Fully Fair Tactical Battles Decided in Short, Decisive Clashes No complex controls. No pay-to-win advantages. Victory is decided purely by how well you read formations, positioning, and skill interactions. 5. Playable with just a mouse! Everything from troop deployment to recruitment can be easily controlled using only a mouse. *Note: A keyboard is required for text input. ■ These modes are playable in Early Access! ① "Board Performance" An intense tactical drill mode where the clear rates for all stages are revealed to the world. ② "Gunshi Chronicle" An asynchronous battle mode where you devour the "past data" of other players to climb the ranks. ③ "Rival Match" A friend match feature that allows you to play against your friends online. Enter a code to enjoy real-time battles together. Forget the “Meta”, Roguelite "Full Draft" System Roguelite Recruitment: Every match in Three Kingdoms:BOND begins with a fresh set of possibilities. Navigate a boardgame-style map to recruit your generals and build a unique army from the hand you are dealt. Dynamic Teambuilding: Players must adapt to randomized pools while managing costs, the only guaranteed path to victory is your strategy. No two battles play out the same, discover powerful formations and outsmart your opponent with a plan that only works in that moment! Strategic Cost Management: Carry over resources for future rounds. Risk for Reward: Pick weaker units early to build late-game power. Unique Tactical Synergies: Build "miracle formations" that only work in the moment. No fixed strategies, no "Meta" sites. Every match sparks a completely new strategy. Ultimate Mind Games: Find the "Perfect Placement“ Victory is decided before the clash begins. Study your opponent’s formation in advance and place your generals on the grid-based battlefield to counter their strategy. Success depends on reading unit counters, skill ranges, and potential for ambushes. Three Pillars Unit Counters Cavalry → Strong against Archers Archers → Strong against Spears Spears → Strong against Cavalry "Hide Cavalry behind Spears for a surprise charge. " "Use high-INT generals to reveal hidden ambushes. " The pre-battle placement is the ultimate highlight of the experience. Once deployment ends, the fight plays out entirely on the accuracy of your strategy and positioning - ultimately your decisions as battlefield commander. Build Your Dream Team: The "Ultimate BOND" Experience Over 100 all-new, originally illustrated generals. Cross-faction combos trigger powerful "BOND" synergies. Combine your deck's strategy with powerful Tactical Decrees to unleash unpredictable combos and experience the pure exhilaration of overturning the entire board. 10-Minute Deep Strategy: Intense Battles, Two Ways to Play Synchronous Battles: The high-stakes tension of real-time mind games. Asynchronous Battles: Challenge elite formations from across the nation at your own pace. Skip the grind and hours of endless gameplay. Make your strategic decisions based on the hand you are dealt, and command your army in intense 10-minute “think first” tactical battles. Designed for a busy lifestyle. Fair and Logical Balance: Every Loss is a Lesson Zero “RNG” Frustration: There are no unfair, luck-based elements. Every win or loss has a clear reason behind it. Intellectual Growth: The more you play, the more your tactics evolve—delivering a deep sense of strategic evolution. Outsmart rivals across the globe with nothing but your wits! Global Matching: Compete with players across Japan, Asia, and the West. The throne is yours to take! Authentic Tactical Battles, Presented by a Legendary Producer. Produced by Yasuhiro Nishiyama (Nishi-P) Former SEGA producer behind massive hits such as “Sangokushi Taisen” and “maimai”. Where Arcade Passion Meets Strategic Depth. A new Three Kingdoms experience, forged on the front lines of arcade gaming.
