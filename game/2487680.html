About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to the Burger Kombat Kitchen! In this virtual kitchen, there is no logic – ingredients randomly appear on your table every couple hours. All you can do is hope that your culinary skills will be enough to figure out what can be prepared from them! Combine various ingredients to create new burgers and amaze everyone with your mastery of cooking from the most absurd and incompatible products. The rarer the product, the cooler the burger. Collect the rarest ingredients to make the most expensive burgers out of them. In the Burger Kombat's kitchen, the main indicator of a chef's mastery is the ability to make burgers that people would want to pay any amount of money for! And remember the main thing – this game will not teach you how to cook delicious burgers, it will teach you how to cook EXPENSIVE burgers.
