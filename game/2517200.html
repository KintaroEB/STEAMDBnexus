About this gameFull description from Steam+
►The game is a funny satire on the topic of privacy, people's fear of losing their data ► It is based on real 20 years of experience in various equipment repair services, and has absorbed many incident situations ►You will have the technical opportunity to interact with the personal data of customers, theoretically you can influence the fate of some inhabitants of this city ►Buy phones from customers and sell excess spare parts, this will help increase income from the workshop ►Getting to know a secret agent can make you an undercover government spy in the form of a master, and of course you will be paid for it ►Every action you take can have consequences. For example, by copying the genome of a valuable plant, you will help the city with the problem of the food crisis. But don't forget that sometimes even the best deeds can have unexpected consequences
