About this gameFull description from Steam+
Explore the World Without Leaving Your Seat GeoGuessr drops you somewhere on Earth and challenges you to figure out where you are. Using visual clues, intuition, and a bit of curiosity, you’ll navigate real world locations captured in street level imagery, turning every guess into a journey. From quiet rural roads to bustling city streets, every round is a chance to discover something new. Study road signs, architecture, bollards, and fauna that reveal where you might be. The closer your guess, the higher your score. Test your skills against other players around the world, where every guess counts and every second matters. Whether you’re competing or exploring at your own pace, each game sharpens your instincts and expands your view of the planet. Whether you’re a geography enthusiast or just love the thrill of discovery, GeoGuessr offers a unique way to experience the world, one location at a time. General notes: Having a GeoGuessr browser subscription does not grant you full access to the GeoGuessr Steam Edition, unless you have an Unlimited or Elite monthly/yearly subscription. Nor does a GeoGuessr Steam Pass grant you access to the GeoGuessr browser version. GeoGuessr Steam Pass holders will be able to cross-play against GeoGuessr browser subscribers and vice versa.
