About this gameFull description from Steam+
Explore the World Without Leaving Your Seat GeoGuessr drops you somewhere on Earth and challenges you to figure out where you are. Using visual clues, intuition, and a bit of curiosity, you’ll navigate real world locations captured in street level imagery, turning every guess into a journey. From quiet rural roads to bustling city streets, every round is a chance to discover something new. Study road signs, architecture, bollards, and fauna that reveal where you might be. The closer your guess, the higher your score. Test your skills against other players around the world, where every guess counts and every second matters. Whether you’re competing or exploring at your own pace, each game sharpens your instincts and expands your view of the planet. Whether you’re a geography enthusiast or just love the thrill of discovery, GeoGuessr offers a unique way to experience the world, one location at a time. General notes: Having a GeoGuessr subscription, an Unlimited or Elite monthly/yearly subscription, grant you full access to the GeoGuessr on Steam, Web, iOS and Android. GeoGuessr Steam Pass (legacy) holders work as Unlimited until it expires.
