About this gameFull description from Steam+
Customize your vehicle, Create your very own race tracks, Share them online and race your friends best times with global online leaderboards and online multiplayer. Created by an RC enthusiast, for RC enthusiasts! High speeds, huge jumps, big drifts, ReCharge RC: High Voltage is a racing game unlike anything you've ever played before! From the creator of the hit indie racer played by millions: ReCharge RC! With a community-driven development approach, get involved and lets make the best RC game ever! Featuring: A fun and rewarding sim-cade handling model that blurs the line between arcade and simulation. Inspired by real life RC but tuned to be an absolute blast to pick up and play. Noobs, racing game fans, RC enthusiasts, and pro racers alike will enjoy the intuitive arcade handling with just enough sim based elements to make those coming from real life RC feel right at home and provide intense competition. With accurately modeled suspension geometry and vehicle mechanics like air control and a detailed damage engine that allows for broken steering links and shocks leading to floppy a-arms, scratched up bodies, parts flying off and more, ReCharge RC brings all the fun of real life RC to your screen! LAN and online multiplayer Supporting up to 12 players for both local LAN connections and full online multiplayer! Play any track in the game, including thousands of user created tracks, with your friends and foes online! With built in chat, lobby leaderboards, and a focus on making sure even poor/slow connections can still have a good experience! Limitless vehicle customization on a wide selection of realistic and detailed RC models, ranging from buggies and short course trucks to micros and solid axle monster trucks. With loads of body shells, wheels, and spoilers and an easy to use but in depth livery and decal editor, you can really make every car your own! An unrivaled track creation system lets you create the track of your dreams with an intuitive and easy to use but extremely powerful editor. Create any shape jump, berm, bump, whoop, curve, hill, dip, or any other feature you can imagine. Freely place objects and play with physics to really bring the creativity to each and every track. With a variety of environments from a suburban backyard or local hobby shop to an abandoned hangar or nationals event location and more. Whether youre wanting realism or fantasy, if you can think of it, you can create it! Online track sharing and global leaderboards with an ever growing catalog of user created tracks of all varieties, from realistic real life track recreations to wild and insane fantasy courses! Plus global online leaderboards for every track, even the ones you create, with asynchronous racing allowing you to race the ghost of every single leaderboard entry to learn their lines! Photo mode A built in photo mode with real camera controls and built in filters lets you capture your custom vehicles and epic action shots at any time. Most of the screenshots featured on this page are in game shots using the built in photo mode! Several camera options Play how you want with drivers stand views, first person mounted cameras, birds eye options to channel your inner retro gamer, and of course standard follow cams. All with extensive adjustability to get it feeling just right. Real RC transmitter support Extensive support for using your real RC transmitter to play with a quick and easy built in setup wizard and tons of in-game input adjustments like expo and EPA. Practice from the drivers stand with your real transmitter and hone your skills for the next time you're out racing for real! Optimized performance Allows the game to run well even on low end devices. ReCharge RC is a game for everyone and that starts with making sure everyone can run it! A planned roadmap 50+ updates have already been released with TONS more content coming, including more campaigns, freestyle and bash modes, road cars, drifting, livery sharing, and much, much more. All backed by a reliable dev that interacts with the community daily and has a 15+ year history of game support. The ReCharge RC community is everything to me, if you guys want something get involved on Discord or reach out and lets make it happen. The RC community has been desperate for a great RC game for a very long time now, one that actually understands the hobby rather than just another Revolt clone, it's time to make that reality!
