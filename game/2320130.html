About this gameFull description from Steam+
Farm Empire is an idle / clicker game where you will be making crazy amounts of money from farming. You start the game with just one chicken, but soon you can expand into tomatoes, tractors, factories and many other assets. Hire managers to do the clicking, upgrade your assets and reach your milestones. You can also expand your empire to other countries like Australia, Madagascar, Denmark and even Antarctica. Farm Empire was originally released in 2016, but has been in active development ever since. Content updates are out every other month, ensuring there will always be ways to expand your empire. Thousands of milestones to play towards. Seven empires to unlock and complete: USA, China, Australia, Denmark, Norway, Madagascar and Antarctica (yes, you can farm there!) Each empire has 36 cool, interesting or funny assets to unlock and upgrade: How about an Australian banjo frog, an arctic sea pig or a Chinese dragon? Discover the best combination of assets for each empire to optimize your earnings - the path you take is important, but you can always restart to try another. Speed up your earnings with the help of friendly workers gained from restarting your empire - restart often, to keep your worker force growing. 15 golden eggs for free each day, and even more if you create your own golden egg farms. Your game data is stored on the mighty Farm Empire server - log on from any device, and your empire will be at where your last left it.
