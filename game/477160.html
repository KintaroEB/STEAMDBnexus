About this gameFull description from Steam+
***NEW LEVEL '10TH ANNIVERSARY' AVAILABLE NOW*** Join the 10th anniversary celebration, step into a brand-new anniversary level packed with nostalgia, hidden secrets such as video interviews of never-before-shared stories from the game's development. Relive iconic moments inspired by classic levels including Mansion, Mountain, Demolition, Castle, Water, Powerplant and Aztec, solve many updated takes on the puzzles you know and love. Join the 10th anniversary celebration now and experience why 60 million players worldwide enjoy the wobbly fun! Join 60 million players across all formats, and explore more than 5,000 unofficial levels from our community creators too! Human Fall Flat is a hilarious, light-hearted physics platformer set in a world of floating dreamscapes. Each dream level provides a new environment to navigate, from mansions, castles and Aztec adventures to snowy mountains, eerie nightscapes and industrial locations. Multiple routes through each level, and perfectly playful puzzles ensure exploration and ingenuity are rewarded. Need a hand getting that boulder on to a catapult, or need someone to break that wall? Online multiplayer for up to 8 players transforms the way Human Fall Flat is played. Your Human is yours to customise. With outfits from builder to chef, skydiver, miner, astronaut and ninja. Choose your head, upper and lower body and get creative with the colours! The Human Fall Flat Workshop, unique to Steam, is a fantastic tool which, used alongside Unity, enables players to build their own Human Fall Flat content and share it with others around the world. Not interested in building levels, lobbies or skins yourself? You can also download more than 5000 different levels and explore other people's creations directly from Steam! Streamers and YouTubers flock to Human Fall Flat for its unique, hilarious gameplay. Fans have watched these videos more than 3 Billion times!
