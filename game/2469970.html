About this gameFull description from Steam+
Overall Introduction · "ShuXian Chronicles: Idle Ascension" is an idle loot-based RPG with turn-based cultivation themes. · Compared to similar games, this title offers significantly expanded strategic depth and breadth in character building. · Systems like equipment forging, affixes, artifacts, immortal beast taming, breeding, and alchemy will deliver lottery-like excitement and surprises. —————————————————————————————————— Party Formation · Collect over 30 unique Immortal Companions, each valued at 1-6 Destiny Points. · Assemble your 2-8 member adventure party within a 10-point Destiny limit. · Whether prioritizing quality or quantity becomes your strategic choice. —————————————————————————————————— Equipment Crafting · Clear chapters and idle-farm materials to forge divine weapons. · Every crafted weapon has randomly generated rarity, stats, and affixes - no two are alike. · Unique affix system: Equipments only activate special effects when reaching 5/10/15 points of the same affix type across all gear. Your build strategy matters. —————————————————————————————————— Artifact Refinement · Unlock faction-exclusive artifacts by completing chapters. · Each artifact develops distinct evolutionary paths during refinement, creating varied combat strategies. · With 2 artifact slots per companion, optimal combinations have no standard solution. —————————————————————————————————— Beast Systems (Capture/Breeding/Fusion) · No cultivation world is complete without mythical beasts! · Each chapter features faction-specific beasts - capture their eggs from defeated foes. · Hatched beasts have randomized aptitudes and growth values. · Train exceptional beasts with powerful skills to enhance combat performance. · Through "Beast Fusion", create superior hybrids with enhanced stats and more skill slots - including rare hidden variants! —————————————————————————————————— Additional Systems · Ore Refining: All immortal gems require raw ore purification. · Alchemy: Convert excess materials into probability-boosting elixirs and stat-enhancing pills. —————————————————————————————————— Discover more wonders in the world of ShuXian Chronicles!
