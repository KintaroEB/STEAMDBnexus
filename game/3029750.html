About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mysterious alien invaders are swarming through unstable wormholes, threatening our great planet for reasons beyond human understanding! But do we sit back and wait? HELL NO! We FIGHT! Join Girl and Raven, members of the Planetary Alien Neutralization & Tactical Strike Unit (P.A.N.T.S.U.), on the frontlines of Earth's survival! Their mission? Defend the power generator—the beating heart of humanity’s last, greatest hope: THE SUPERWEAPON! They stand alone atop their turret, the last line of defense against the alien horde. No backup. No retreat. Just firepower, grit, and the promise of a well-earned plate of 'omurice' awaiting their return. Upgrade your turret! Build frontline generators! Charge the superweapon! Blast alien scum back to whatever hell they crawled out of! Strap in, lock on, and blast away! Our on-rails turret platform provides unparalleled versatility and top-tier alien-annihilating firepower—so even the greenest recruit can rack up confirmed kills! The battlefield is always changing, and so are you! Every mission completed, every alien fried means more resources to upgrade your defenses, power generation, and devastating firepower! Perform well, and you’ll earn the best tech humanity has to offer! Your uniform is battle-tested, stress-resistant, and strategically disposable! Under extreme conditions, it will self-disintegrate to ensure maximum survivability and maneuverability. Who needs dignity when you can have victory? Earn your stripes! High-ranking troopers are rewarded with fresh underwear, elite accessories, and upgraded turret systems! Fight harder, score higher, and earn the best gear—because winners don’t wear rags!
