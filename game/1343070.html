About this gameFull description from Steam+
Command a platoon of WWII tanks in this TPP and FPP game. Engage in tactical, realistic battles across historic battlefields. Play through a full campaign or test your skills in skirmish mode, fighting as either German or US forces. As a German commander in 1944, your mission is to push back the advancing enemy forces in France. Assemble your platoon of tanks, take command, and lead them into battle across a series of intense and tactical engagements. You and your platoon will play a key role in pivotal moments of the Second World War. Relive iconic battles around St. Lô, Mortain, and the Ardennes, and take part in some of the greatest tank engagements in history. Skirmish mode, a fan-favorite feature from our previous title Call to Arms: Gates of Hell, returns with expanded possibilities. Fight on either side of the historical conflict and command powerful weapons like the iconic Tiger tank or the StuH 42. Battle for control of key strategic landmarks and lead your forces to victory. Get to know your crew as they fight by your side. Experience their personal stories, victories, and hardships as they grow and evolve throughout the campaign. Feature List: 5 PvE Campaign Missions: Single-player and Coop 9 PvPvE Skirmish Missions Fully destructible and immersive environment 20 playable war vehicles: 10 historically accurate tanks and armored vehicles for each faction Physics based ballistic and complex damage simulations
