About this gameFull description from Steam+
Kind-hearted person, please save the broken Dark Lord! Our beloved leader, the unholy and terrifying Dark Lord, has been betrayed by the treacherous Death Knights in his campaign against the filthy humans! It is up to us, his loyal servants, to piece back his shattered soul in this abandoned Dungeon. We must rebuild, collect resources, and re-muster our Legions for the glory of our evil master! Observe, analyze, plan, and change the battle! Focus on your legion's combat, analyze key moments and positions. Adjust formations to ensure victory for Dark Lords! A single legion move can change the outcome—this game tests sharp insight and strategic thinking. A game that combines dungeon management with card-based army battles. The developers of this game are fans of simulation and management games. In the past, while playing other simulation management games, We felt a significant regret: after spending a lot of time carefully designing and managing my city or base, the only reward was an increase in in-game economic income, allowing me to build larger and more powerful structures. However, as you can imagine, once my economy is strong, what I need is an army and warfare! By establishing my economic foundation through simulation management, I aim to support the formation of an army to conquer enemies! Therefore, we merged dungeon management with card-based army battles to create this game, and we hope it brings you enjoyable moments.
