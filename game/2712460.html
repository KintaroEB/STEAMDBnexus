About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dungeon Slasher is a side-scrolling roguelike game that combines retro graphics with the fun of picking a combination of character, artifact, and skill to bring down the dungeon, allowing for dozens of unique playthroughs. ◉ ABOUT THE GAME ◉ ▶ Side-scrolling roguelike fun! Combine characters, skills, and artifacts as you wish! Depending on what combinations and development paths players select, methods required for conquering the dungeon will change. ▶A variety of modes to play through! From the classic 'Normal Mode,' where players take down dungeons one at a time, to the 'Super Fast Mode,' which focuses on progress and boss attacks, and 'Challenge Mode,' which will ramp up the difficulty for more challenge, there are tons of modes for players to conquer and enjoy. ▶2D pixel graphic action fun! This game is jam-packed full of characters and bosses rendered in high-quality pixel graphics! In addition to the colorful retro pixel action, players can pick from a number of skins to highlight each character's unique personality! ▶A variety of playable characters! According to which combination of character and weapon players chose, the gameplay experience and movement will differ wildly! Experience all sorts of new gimmicks and gameplay for yourself! ※ Regarding gameplay upload and streaming Any individual or company may stream or upload videos of this game as long as the copyright information and store URLs are included within the video description or the video itself.
