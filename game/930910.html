About this gameFull description from Steam+
A story for every lab member. Discover a new side of each STEINS;GATE character and get a glimpse of their inner thoughts through 10 new stories. While STEINS;GATE unfolded from the perspective of the protagonist, Okabe Rintaro, STEINS;GATE: Linear Bounded Phenogram features stories told by the rest of the main cast. STEINS;GATE: Linear Bounded Phenogram is available only with purchase of STEINS;GATE ELITE
