About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Desktop Pets the ultimate virtual pet experience! In this charming and relaxing game, your desktop comes to life with adorable pets that idle, interact, and entertain you with their delightful antics. Features: Dynamic Pet Interactions: Watch as your pets engage in a variety of random actions, from playful antics to curious explorations. They interact with each other, forming bonds and creating a lively environment right on your desktop. Collectible Pets: Discover a diverse range of pets, each with unique personalities and behaviors. Unlock new pets by hatching mysterious eggs and expand your delightful collection. Personalized Environment: Customize your desktop with toys to create the perfect habitat for your pets. Make it cozy, playful, or exotic – the choice is yours! Achievements and Rewards: Complete fun challenges and earn rewards to enhance your pets' environment and unlock special features.
