About this gameFull description from Steam+
A fast physics-based brawler where you'll get dozens of "holy heck, did I just do that? that was so cool" moments. One-hit death One wrong move and you are out! With 25 weapons as dangerous to you as they are to your enemies, every battle feels like dancing on a razor’s edge. Web swinging & wall crawling Run on ceilings and ramp up wild speed using physics-based web swinging. Wild maps 55 maps to keep your fights fresh with various physics obstacles or hazards, and you can always grab more from the Steam Workshop! Versus Battle friends and randoms in intense acrobatic showdowns or epicly clumsy battles. Co-op Fight off waves of enemies, slowly gaining various upgrades as you try your best to “avoid” friendly fire. Play alone Test your skills by completing a variety of themed combat and parkour challenges. Editor Design your own versus maps, parkour courses, and combat challenges to test yourself or your friends. Customization The spiders are bald - help them unlock cool hats by completing achievements! Disclaimer: this is not Australia simulator.
