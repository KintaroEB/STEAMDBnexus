About this gameFull description from Steam+
Allows you to play animated wallpapers on your desktop. Designed to run efficiently in the background with minimal impact on system performance. COMMUNITY Explore live wallpapers created by other users and set them as your background instantly. IMPORT YOUR OWN Already have something? Use any image or video from your computer. CREATION There are three simple ways to create your own animated wallpaper: Generate from a prompt: Describe a scene. AI creates an image, then animates it into a seamless loop. Animate your own image: Import any photo or artwork and bring it to life instantly. Remix existing wallpapers: Take any wallpaper and generate new variations with AI. PLAYLISTS Tag your wallpapers, then have your desktop rotate through a tag on a timer, from every 5 minutes to once a day. Shuffle optional. ON YOUR DESKTOP A different wallpaper on each monitor. Sits behind your desktop icons. Pauses while a fullscreen game or app covers the screen. Can keep your Windows lock screen matching your wallpaper. Works offline once wallpapers are downloaded. Windows and Linux. WHAT'S INCLUDED Community wallpapers and importing your own files are unlimited and free. AI creation runs on energy, which refills automatically: 100 energy per day, up to 100 saved up. An image costs 15 energy. An animation costs 5 energy. That is up to 20 animations a day, or about 6 images, or any mix of the two. If a generation fails due to an error, the energy is returned to you.
