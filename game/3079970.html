About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into the critical role of a baggage security operative in Airport X-Ray Simulator. As part of the backbone of airport security, you are the last line of defense against prohibited or illegal items making their way onto an aircraft or into the airport. Please be aware before purchasing: Airport X-Ray Simulator has a fairly high learning curve, and as such, it features a text-heavy tutorial that can take 10–15 minutes. The game’s mechanics can be quite complex, and mastering the ins and outs will take time. In this immersive simulation, you handle checked luggage, using x-ray machines to identify potential threats. Bags come through on conveyors, and it’s up to you to spot and handle any suspicious items. X-ray Screening: Inspect bags using an x-ray machine, identifying prohibited or illegal items. Manual Searches: Remove bags from the conveyor and conduct thorough searches, interacting with and examining all objects inside. Item Confiscation and Flagging: Confiscate prohibited items and flag illegal items for further action. Substance Scanning: Utilize a substance scanner for items that are difficult to visually identify. Passenger Information: Use your computer to check passenger details, permits for firearms or blades, and access your email. Performance Summary: Receive daily summaries of your performance, providing detailed feedback and insights. Earnings and Upgrades: Earn money based on your end-of-day summary, which can be used to purchase upgrades to improve your efficiency and capabilities. Fun and Engaging Baggage Screening and Manual Inspection Mechanics. A variety of items to identify, from everyday objects to potential threats. Dynamic challenges and random events to keep gameplay engaging. Detailed performance summaries and feedback. In-depth passenger information system for checking permits and other details. Immerse yourself in the high-stakes world of airport security and ensure the safety of countless passengers. Are you up to the challenge?
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

In Airport X-Ray Simulator, players will inspect and view various items within luggage, which may include mature content. The game includes depictions of tobacco, alcohol, drugs, firearms, large blades, explosives, and sex toys. It is important to note that these items are presented as physical objects within the luggage, and there is no depiction of their actual use. Players have the option to disable the inclusion of explosives and sex toys when starting a new game, providing flexibility for those who may find these items offensive.
