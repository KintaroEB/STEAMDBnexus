About this gameFull description from Steam+
Poke the dungeon and stick around to see what comes out! Every 60 minutes you will receive a tradeable mob in the form of a Steam Item. Grow an impressive collection of unique mobs to show off and trade with other players on the Steam Market! Drop Rate Every 60 minutes the game will drop a mob at the following drop rates: Common Mobs = 95% Rare Mobs = 4.99% Mythical Mobs = 0.01% Rotating Drops Every Monday*, 12 Common Mobs, 6 Rare Mobs, and 3 Mythical Mobs will be randomly selected as the active drops for the week. Releases of new Common Mobs and Rare Mobs will be placed into the item shop, and will only leave the shop until they're randomly selected as an active drop. Once the shop falls below 5 mobs, inactive Rare Mobs will be randomly selected to be placed in the shop, where they will remain until they become active drops again. *For the first month of release, all mobs will be active drops to initialize and populate the market. This period may be reduced or extended depending on the amount of active traders. Feature in Development The ability to view your Steam Inventory in-game and exchange mobs for additional rolls: Exchange 3 common mobs for a rare roll (95% Common / 5% Rare) Exchange 3 rare mobs for a mythic roll (99% Rare / 1% Mythical) More information available on our Discord.
