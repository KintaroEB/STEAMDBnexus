About this gameFull description from Steam+
Explore a long-abandoned land and discover the secrets hidden within. Along the way, you’ll learn a variety of new abilities and use them to unravel one mystery after another. In the end, what will you choose: endless solitude, or eternal slumber? Featuring exquisite cartoon pixel art and visual effects. No violence, no death — everything is focused on puzzles and exploration. Over 100 meticulously designed levels, packed with creative puzzles and collectibles scattered across the world. Rich environmental interactions for an immersive exploration experience. Secrets and easter eggs hidden throughout the game. And of course… chocolate is delicious, meow.
