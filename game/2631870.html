About this gameFull description from Steam+
The most realistic sailing simulation game available. On a global scale, in real weather, ready to be explored. Made by a sailor, helped by the joined effort of sailors from all over the world. ----- Sailaway comes with 2 basic boats (a 38' cruiser and the small Nordic Folkboat). The rest of the boats are made by other sailors like you and are sold separately by them. This also applies to most of the boats you see in these pictures and videos. Compete in races organized by virtual sailing clubs. Sail on boats designed by fellow sailors. Trim your sails for the best performance. Face the biggest storms, circumnavigate the globe or make that journey you always wanted to make. Push yourself to become a better sailor. Not everything is about better, faster. Enjoy a comforting and relaxing experience. Sail at your own pace, in the part of the world you like best. All alone, with friends or with other like minded people. Sailors will relate to each other wherever you go. Learn astronavigation with the build-in sextant. Experiment with finding the best sail trim by using the visualization of air flow along the sails. Learn navigation with the buoys and other seamarks that are all present on the map and in the world. Design your own boat, create your own digital or analog instruments. Or create your own hardware set up with the build-in library of blocks, shackles and winches. Make the world a better place by creating ports and docks. Plant trees. Place 3d models of iconic lighthouses. Make tutorials for other sailors. Sailaway provides the framework, the sailors fill it with content for all to enjoy. Join us and become part of something big! ----- Sailaway comes with 2 basic boats (a 38' cruiser and the small Nordic Folkboat). The rest of the boats are made by other sailors like you and are sold separately by them. This also applies to most of the boats you see in these pictures and videos. ----- Check out the Sailaway.world website for more info. ----- Important: The Russian government seems to have blocked the Sailaway servers. This means that Sailaway is not playable from within Russia
