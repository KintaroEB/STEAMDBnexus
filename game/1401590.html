About this gameFull description from Steam+
Disney Dreamlight Valley is a relaxing life-simulation adventure game rich with quests, exploration, and engaging activities, including fishing, mining, and crafting, featuring Disney and Pixar friends, both old and new. Once an idyllic land, Dreamlight Valley was a place where Disney and Pixar characters lived in harmony, until the Forgetting. Night Thorns grew across the land and severed your wonderful memories tied to this magical place. With nowhere else to go, the inhabitants of Dreamlight Valley retreated behind locked doors in the Dream Castle, awaiting your return. Now it's up to you to discover the stories of this world and bring the magic back to Dreamlight Valley! Forge Friendships with 50+ Disney & Pixar Characters Garden with WALL•E, cook with Remy or kick back and fish with Goofy. Get in touch with your emotions with Joy and Sadness and shrink down to toy-size to meet Woody and Buzz Lightyear! What better way to collect, craft, and rebuild the Valley than with a friend. From courageous princesses such as Cinderella and Jasmine to nefarious villains like Scar or Ursula, every Dreamlight Valley resident brings their own story arc, quests, and rewards. Customize Your Own World and Express Your Style Build the perfect Valley for you, with a fully customizable layout, creative landscaping, and thousands of decorative items inspired by Disney and Pixar. Bring out your inner princess, witch, or embrace a dreamy cottagecore fantasy! Assemble unique outfits and decorate your home with thousands of fantastic items to express your creativity. With the in-game Camera, get ready to snap a sunset selfie with Mirabel, capture a culinary creation, or participate in DreamSnaps, a weekly photo challenge to show off your creativity! Will you settle on the Beach next to Moana, or call Jack Skellington your next-door neighbor in the Plaza? Embark On an Adventure You’ll start your adventure in the Valley, but your journey will take you to infinity… and beyond! Explore what lurks in the Forest of Valor with Pocahontas, brave the deepest caverns on the shores of Dazzle Beach with Ariel, and discover magical Floating Islands high above the Valley. Connect with up to three other players through the Valley Visit Station, which lets you join your friends’ Valley, exchange resources, and more! Discover the Secrets of the Valley Free the Valley from the grip of the Forgetting and unlock the Realms of well-loved Disney and Pixar characters, including revered royalty such as Anna and Simba, iconic duos such as Mike Wazowski and Sulley, and whimsical pals such as Alice and Cheshire Cat. Each Realm contains special challenges with puzzles to solve in the quest to bring friendship back to the Valley. A Game in Constant Evolution New content means that there's always something fresh to explore. Meet new characters in the Valley, check out the latest clothing and furniture collections in Scrooge’s Store, and exciting in-game events. Be sure to dream your way back to Dreamlight Valley regularly to discover new adventures!
