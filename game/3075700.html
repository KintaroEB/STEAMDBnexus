About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to CoinForge, the ultimate clicker game where you generate and collect S-Coins! Click your way to riches and watch your wealth grow!!! Features: S-coins, of course: Click to generate S-Coins and build your fortune! Mint S-Coins to create unique ones. Design submissions via Steam Workshop! : Get involved in shaping the game. Submit your designs for in-game items and see your creations come to life! Why CoinForge? Stable Economy: Enjoy a virtual economy that's more stable than any central bank. Updates: Look forward to new features and items with updates. Events and Competitions: Participate in seasonal events and compete for exclusive rewards. The CoinForge Market: Imagine playing the stock market, where your clicks earn you valuable currency. In CoinForge, the more players join and click, the more S-Coins are circulating, creating a dynamic and stable market. Our goal? To create a completely independent currency exchange system on Steam, where S-Coins become a trusted medium of exchange. Coin Drop System: In CoinForge, coins with different values drop at set chances: 1 million S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 1,000,000 100,000 S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 100,000 10,000 S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 10,000 1,000 S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 1,000 100 S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 100 10 S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 10 1 S-Coin: Chance of 1 in 1 Coin Conversion: You can also convert your S-Coins using a simple exchange rate. For example, 10 coins of value 1 can be exchanged for 1 coin of value 10, etc... Future Plans: In the future, you'll be able to craft coins with community-designed items, minting them at certain values. This means the community can directly contribute to the in-game economy, adding their creativity and designs to the CoinForge world. Join CoinForge today and start your journey towards becoming the ultimate S-coin tycoon. Click, collect, and conquer in a game where your financial dreams can come true! Get ready to click and earn!
