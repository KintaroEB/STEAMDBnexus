About this gameFull description from Steam+
OVER 1,500,000 INSTALLS WORLDWIDE – NOW ON STEAM! After two years of forging, countless battles, and thousands of blades created, your turn has come. Battlesmiths: Blade & Forge is the perfect blend of RPG, deep crafting, strategy, and auto-battler — available now on Steam! Build your town, manage your forge, master the blacksmith trade, create legendary weapons and gear, and lead a squad of heroes into PvP and PvE fights. Prove that you are the ultimate swordsmith and strategist in the medieval world. MASTER THE BLACKSMITH’S CRAFT In Battlesmiths, your gear is not a trophy, but the result of your mastery. Weapons, armor, and runes are created by you — they directly change the appearance and battle style of your heroes. Start forging and upgrading swords and armor, and apply powerful enchantments. Every item crafted by your blacksmith affects the tactics of your heroes in battle. A new smithing master mini-game allows you to personally participate in the creation of every blade. Feel like a true bladesmith! Experiment with skills and gear to create builds that your opponent in the PvP arena will not be ready for. Don't forget about trading: your town residents will gladly buy your handcrafted goods and fill your pockets with gold! LEAD YOUR HEROES TO VICTORY IN TACTICAL BATTLES Assemble a brave squad of powerful warriors and mages. Optimize your builds: equipment and runes directly affect your heroes' damage and movement. Your decisions before the fight determine its outcome. The semi-automatic tactical battles encourage thoughtful preparation and precise tactics. No plan is perfect, but your strategy and the right combination of classes and gear will provide you with the best chance for victory. BUILD AND DEVELOP YOUR TOWN Develop your masters, upgrade buildings, acquire valuable resources, and unlock new crafting and building technologies. Your town is the core of your power. Protect your legacy by crafting the most perfect gear for your heroes. GUILDS, EPIC BOSSES AND EPIC BATTLES Join a clan or create your own. Unite with allies to fight epic bosses, share resources, and receive guild bonuses. All for the sake of victory! EVENTS AND CHALLENGES Participate in challenges with time-limited rewards, complete daily quests, and earn rare resources. Events are regularly updated: a new goal, a new enemy, and new trophies always await you. FULL PC EXPERIENCE Battlesmiths on Steam is the full, uncompromising functionality, optimized for desktop computers. Play and become one of the greatest blacksmiths to forge their legend in the crafty world of Battlesmiths.
