About this gameFull description from Steam+
Sandbox World is a multiplayer action sandbox where you can build your own unique vehicles choosing from over 120 unique blocks and get into exciting multiplayer battles! Game features: Thousands of unique structures created by players: from flying tanks of the future and modern fighters to realistic ships and cars; Exhibition of structures: download and study structures of other players or upload your own; Wide selection of weapons: from saws, flamethrowers and machine guns to ship cannons and guided missiles; Complete freedom of creativity: thanks to a wide selection of blocks, a system of bearings and shock absorbers in the game, you can build anything you want, and an advanced system of connections and logical elements allows you to make complex control and automation systems; Realistic destruction: protect important blocks of your building from different types of shells; Create and develop clans: cooperate with other players, post buildings on a closed clan exhibition and participate in battles with your clan members; Freedom of tactics in battle: Act by any means, the main thing is to destroy enemies!
