About this gameFull description from Steam+
Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition celebrates one of the most beloved real-time strategy franchises in definitive form with enhanced features and modernized gameplay. Take control of empires from Europe, Asia, and the Americas with Home City cards and fresh playstyles. Enjoy new co-op historical battles, a variety of maps, Art of War challenges, refined campaigns, and hero cosmetics in stunning 4K Ultra HD graphics. Command 18 Civilizations Lead a diverse roster of European, Native American, and Asian Civilizations from the original game plus four additions, Swedes, Inca, Mexico, and USA. Leverage the unique bonuses of your civilization and customize your strategy with new, reworked, and fully unlocked Home City cards. Harness the power of overhauled Revolutions like Brazil, Indonesia, or Egypt for the ultimate battlefield pivot, unlocking new nation-specific cards and units. Play History Together in Co-Op Historical Battles Relive iconic moments from history together, where you and a friend take on 9 scenario-driven missions inspired by real events. Coordinate your armies, split responsibilities, and fight back against enhanced AI in objective-based battles designed for teamwork. Experience the stories, stakes, and spectacle built for two. Master the Art of War Challenge Missions Sharpen your strategy in a series of focused scenarios designed to help you master the core mechanics. Take on skill-based challenges that test your economy, tactics, and combat execution as you strive for Gold Medals. Whether you’re learning the fundamentals or refining your play, Challenge Missions are your path to becoming a more formidable commander. Enjoy New Tools & Content • Explore 30 New Maps– Discover a wide range of battlefields that bring variety to every skirmish and match. • Reworked Home Cities– Skip the grind with all cards unlocked by default. Craft your strategy with new and redesigned cards, or jump straight in with pre-built decks. • Reimagined Revolutions & Political Choices– Revolution and Age-Up politicians have been redesigned with new options, units and strategies to explore. • Modernized Gameplay & Tools– Experience refined unit balance, campaign missions, naval formations, and expanded unit variety. Up your game with the built-in Spectator Mode, Photo Mode, and Mod Manager. • Bonus Content- Now includes all the content from the United States, Mexico, and Hero Cosmetic Pack Vol 1DLCs. Jump online to play with friends or test your skills in skirmishes against AI across a variety of maps. However you choose to play, enjoy stunning visuals, a fully remastered soundtrack, and modernized RTS gameplay in this third installment of the award-winning franchise. Complete your collection with the final chapter in the Age of Empires Definitive Edition journey.
