About this gameFull description from Steam+
This is Tyr, a tank shooter where positioning, strategic decision-making, and mastery of unique abilities win. TANKS Tyr features a roster of specialized armored vehicles and pilots. You’ll take control of treaded and hover tanks alike as you angle & position for defense, and rumble and glide across the battlefield. CUSTOMIZATION Each vehicle also offers its own tech tree and ammo & component slots, affording you the ability to upgrade and evolve your ride to suit your playstyle. You may even be able to change up your vehicle’s unique keystone tech to dynamically alter your personal meta. ABILITIES Each vehicle features a unique power that impacts Tyr’s moment-to-moment play on the battlefield. A tank may have the ability to blink several vehicle lengths forward or back, send out a sonar pulse to spot enemies, or overcharge its engine to ram directly into an opponent and deal massive damage. ASYMMETRIC VISION In Tyr, you and your opponents will only be visible when within range or while making enough noise. Deciding when to push forward and when to fire from cover could mean the difference between staying hidden and revealing your position to the entire enemy team, so play carefully. STRATEGY Tyr will test your mental mettle, patience, and teamwork, as deliberate play beats whoever can fire the fastest. FEATURES Multiplayer tank shooter - Engage in strategic team battles with heavy-armored vehicles A roster of heavy armor - Tanks in Tyr each feature a unique armor profile that you can angle and position to deflect enemy shots in the heat of battle Vehicle customization - Individual tech trees, ammo slots, and component upgrades for each tank Keystone tech variations - Max level your tank to unlock meta-changing tech Asymmetric vision - Position yourself correctly, and you’ll see the enemy and spot them for your team, while the enemy can't see you in return Unique vehicle abilities- Each tank has a distinct ability that makes moment-to-moment play more high-stakes and interesting Strategy beats twitch reflexes - Run and gun gameplay gets punished by careful positioning and slow, deliberately placed shots to disable enemies
