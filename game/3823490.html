About this gameFull description from Steam+
Speedway Club Manager 25 is a management game set in the world of speedway racing, designed for the PC/Windows platform. Take on the role of a speedway club manager and lead your team to championship glory! Sign your dream riders, secure sponsorship deals, upgrade your facilities, and develop young speedway talents! The game features a faithfully recreated set of riders, tracks, clubs, and leagues from across the speedway world. Want more? The developers have made the game fully moddable, giving you the freedom to create your own ideal speedway universe. Speedway Club Manager 25 is being developed by two speedway enthusiasts – Marcin Dopierała, whose passion for the sport led him to compete in amateur leagues, and Paweł Krzemiński, a professional programmer from Tarnów. The version marked "25" is the successor to Speedway Club Manager 24, the first full release of the game, which was warmly received by the community. With the goal of continuing the project’s development, we’re expanding our reach by launching the game on Steam. Instead of a revolution, we’re focusing on evolution – fixing bugs reported by the community, adding new features, and improving existing ones. We wish you an exciting gameplay experience and many unforgettable managerial adventures! – The Game Developers
