About this gameFull description from Steam+
Steam group: Official qq group: 292234923 Official qq No.: 211206417 Game background: From the 15th century to the 17th century AD, extensive transoceanic activities and major breakthroughs in geography were initiated around the world, especially in Europe. These ocean-going activities facilitated communication between the continents of the planet and, with it, numerous new trade routes. With the opening of new air routes, cultural and trade exchanges between the East and the West increased greatly, and Europe developed rapidly during this period and laid the foundation for prosperity over Asia. In this era, people not only discovered new continents and gained a lot of geographic knowledge, but also greatly promoted European overseas trade. The new shipping routes also had a long-lasting impact on the development of the world's continents hundreds of years later. Players play as the owner of a fleet in the game, earn silver coins and prestige through trade, obtain crafting materials for various ships through battles, and then continuously upgrade their ships into better ships. Equip yourself with a variety of gear and recruit legendary captains to boost your power. The game faithfully restores the magnificent era of navigation. The map covers the entire waters of Western Europe, Northern Europe, the Mediterranean and North Africa. The unique cities in that era, the distinctive specialties, and the local customs full of rich and exotic customs are displayed through this game. to the majority of players Authentic and regionally specific trade items The goods in the game "Navigation Domination" all reflect the faithful restoration of Europe in the 16th century. In the Mediterranean Sea, the North Sea, and the Baltic Sea coast, there are many ports with developed shipping and trade. These ports produce goods with unique regional characteristics. Some commodities are bought at low prices from the origin, and then shipped to the other side of the sea to be sold, and experience the specialties and customs from all over the world. The original stock trading system, changing rapidly In the game, the price of all goods is determined by the player's behavior. If many players buy the same goods, the price of the goods will soar. The price of goods is refreshed every 30 seconds, and the ever-changing excitement will make you greatly stimulated in the game. Rich social relationships, criss-crossing, emotional sublimation The game has a rich social relationship system, such as friends, enemies, alliances, etc. A true restoration of the historical era After the Renaissance, due to the great geographical discoveries, wealth stimulated the prosperity of commerce and the advancement of technology, which made transoceanic trade that had never been possible before, and thus, an era of great voyages came. In the game "Navigation Domination", the art style faithfully restores the historical features of the era of great sailing. You can see various buildings filled with Mediterranean style in the game, and you can also see the famous sailing ships that once traversed the four seas, and that A navigator with vivid names. The sense of substitution of the game is very strong. Through your growth step by step, you will change from a nameless sailor to a great navigator with footsteps all over the four oceans.
