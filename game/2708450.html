About this gameFull description from Steam+
Oh Deer is a multiplayer hide-and-seek game where one player plays as a hunter and up to four others play as deer. Hunter Role The hunter's role is to use sound, gadgets, and pure skill to locate and eliminate the other players before nightfall. However, knowing if the deer in your sights is a player or not is the true challenge. Deer Role The Deer's role is to hide from the hunter by blending in with a handful of AI deer scattered around the map. Eating food is a priority to keep hunger at bay. But improper timing when eating could mean the difference between life and death. The Map Forests are the perfect place to hide. With lots of trees, bushes, and cute flowers, deer can live happily for years without any worries. That is, until hunting season begins... Tall trees, thick bushes, and plenty of shade sets up a perfect ambush.
