About this gameFull description from Steam+
Overview Such A Guy is a first-person narrative game that blends live-action interactive storytelling with light management gameplay. You play as Zane Zheng, a young man who leaves his city job in search of meaning and, together with two close friends, renovates a lakeside guesthouse by the sea. Featuring cinematic-grade filming, real actors, and on-location shooting, the game offers a deeply immersive experience. It combines branching narratives, relationship development, and exploration to create a lifelike yet open-ended interactive journey. With nearly 1,000 minutes of 4K HD footage at its core, the game incorporates a relationship system and branching events. Players face hundreds of decision points, each influencing the characters' fates and the progression of the story. Over 20 different endings are available, along with side mini-games, hidden surprises, memorable scenes, and an achievement system to encourage exploration and replayability. Key Features 4K cinematic visuals with real actors, natural lighting, and film-style editing—blurring the line between games and film. Dual storylines of relationships and guesthouse management, with hundreds of choices for a branching narrative. Side plots, mini-games, and events add rhythm and depth to the story. Romance six unique heroines—your. Your bond depends on choices and interaction. Achievements and hidden scenes support replays and unlock a whole life experience. Story As summer arrives, Zheng Zai quits his urban job and heads to a seaside town to co-manage an old guesthouse with two friends. Starting from scratch, they face funding shortages, operational challenges, and personality clashes, gradually building a warm and authentic space. What begins as an entrepreneurial journey slowly turns into a journey of personal growth. Meanwhile, six women with diverse personalities and backgrounds enter Zheng Zai’s life. They are travelers, neighbors, colleagues, and possibly destined partners. Every conversation choice, interaction timing, management preference, and even moments of silence influence relationships, shaping emotional outcomes and the narrative's conclusion. Will the business succeed? How will relationships end? Which connection will last? The game hands you full control to experience a complete life story through authentic audiovisual storytelling. Characters Layla Jiang (25, Tour Guide / Surf Coach) Outgoing and cheerful, Jiang Lai is the brightest presence on the beach. A local native who knows every corner of the town, she’s a loyal early supporter of your venture. Passionate about surfing and adventure, her personality is like a sea breeze — free-spirited and unrestrained. Your relationship with her is like shadows running under the sun — pure, direct, yet full of challenges. Ki Gan (30, Bar Owner) Mature, confident, and straightforward, Gan Qi owns the town’s popular bar. She possesses life experience and tolerance, making her a valuable mentor in her career and an unexpected source of support in relationships. She’s not proactive, but she never refuses a genuine connection. Your relationship with her is one of “adult understanding” — if you truly get each other, you can go far. Mina Xu (26, Freelance Graphic Designer) Intellectual and elegant, Xu Manman lives independently with a rich inner world. She expresses emotions through design and often hides her thoughts in silence. You’ll need to understand her “silent language” and slowly gain her trust and companionship. Zoey (23, Resident Singer) Bold and edgy on the outside but soft inside, Zuo Yi sings at the local bar and is passionate about music. She tells stories through melodies, keeps her guard up with the world, but treasures sincerity. If you accompany her for late-night drinks and listen to her wounds, she might sing a song just for you. Ivy Wong (20, Marine University Student) Delicate and gentle, Wang Yutong is an intern in a marine research project. Quiet and kind, she reveals surprising determination at critical moments. She loves aquatic life and has a unique perspective on life. Your bond with her is a steady stream, gradually deepening in calmness and warmth. Erica Luo (22, Travel Blogger) Whimsical and energetic, Luo Yueke is a social media “travel photography queen.” She loves taking photos, being playful, and stirring up trouble — sometimes like a scatterbrain, sometimes unexpectedly reliable. Her world is colorful and lively. Can you leave a lasting impression amid her fast pace? Each character has their own story and branching fate. Will your presence change their paths?
