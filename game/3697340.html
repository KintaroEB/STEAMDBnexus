About this gameFull description from Steam+
Picture this: you're a penguin. Life at the North Pole? Freezing. Dull. Zero prospects (especially with the ladies). Time to break free! Find some actual fun, like in good old YetiSports! Your tools? A best bear buddy... and a bat. What could go wrong? From scorching deserts to a moose butt - this ain't your average penguin vacation. Bounce off bewildered seals, become a snowball of doom, hitch a ride on a whale, and surf on giant mushrooms. Successfully navigate a lake? Bonus points! Moose-back riding? Definitely a thing. Impale yourself on a pine tree? Probably. All for love. Failure is an option! Because.. You. Will. Always. Fail. Hilariously. You'll get eaten by piranhas, become a moose's new hat, get stuck in a tree, and possibly abducted by a creepy green dude. But hey, at least you'll fail in style. The real question is: how far can you get before you fail spectacularly? Who needs fish when you have rocket-powered frying pans and the freakin' Infinity Gauntlet? Pick your bat - from humble frying pan to the Eiffel Tower, a trusty club, or the ultimate power glove. Boost your launch power, flight, bounce, and max speed. Prepare for penguin-launching mayhem! In your quest for better life you'll even reach the freakin' space. So yeah, we say this game IS about space! It's beautiful. (And a great place to launch a penguin.) Your bear buddy's skills and your epic bat collection will get you there. Think you're the ultimate penguin launcher? Prove it. Endless replayability, global leaderboards - compete with friends and foes. Brag about your distance, your style, your collection of ludicrous bats. Dominate the leaderboards. Become a legend. Or at least, a slightly less unsuccessful penguin launcher.
