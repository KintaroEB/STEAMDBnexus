About this gameFull description from Steam+
Take on an epic adventure of cosmic proportions in Out of Orbit. Design, build, and manage your very own spacecraft. Raid, destroy, infiltrate, or steal enemy spaceships. Befriend or jeopardize factions. Build bases on planets to expand your influence and resources. Command your crew. Collect raw ores. Travel through wormholes to unlock new randomly generated universes. The sky is the limit. Literally. Build spaceships, space stations, and planetary bases. Assign a variety of room types. Raid and pirate other spaceships to expand your fleet. A massive selection of stations and objects to choose from. Fully destructible ships. Manage your power and fuel supplies. In-Game Editor with Steam Workshop Support. Harvest resources from asteroids and comets. Keep yourself alive with an advanced health system. Illnesses and Diseases. Manage your oxygen, fuel, thirst, hunger, and radiation levels. More than 500 unique items. Build a sustainable habitat for your crew. Assign your crew different roles and jobs. Set a schedule for your crew for micromanagement. Maintain your crew’s happiness, loyalty, and mental health. Recruit crew members to join you in raiding ships. An infinite star map. An infinite number of multiverses. Explore a variety of landable planets. Different and unique sectors to explore.
