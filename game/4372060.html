About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mahtris 108 is a casual Roguelike game that combines Mahjong mechanics with falling-block gameplay. All blocks are made of Mahjong tiles. As you clear lines, you have the chance to form "Sequences" and "Triplets" Once you assemble a complete hand, you can declare Mahjong and score massive points based on your hand's pattern. Each victory grants random rewards. Choosing and using these rewards wisely will help you handle any situation. The rewards also include "Protocols"—powerful effects that come with risks. Building a synergy of strong Protocols is the key to achieving high scores. The game features 4 difficulty levels and numerous achievements. Conquer these challenges to become an unrivaled master!
