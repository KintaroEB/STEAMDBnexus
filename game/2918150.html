About this gameFull description from Steam+
Take the loot, pirates! - Victory rests on the berry in your hand! Bounty Rush is the first ONE PIECE 4 vs 4 multiplayer team action game. In this game, working with your team is the key to victory! Compete in real-time, with complete synchronization, against an enemy team and aim to steal their berries! - The world of ONE PIECE has been transformed into a 3D battlefield! The stages are filled with items that give you and your team an advantage, as well as obstacles that get in your way. Use whatever you can get your hands on and lead your team to victory! - Emerge victorious using distinctive ONE PIECE characters in Team Battle! In this mode, you can control popular ONE PIECE characters and battle against rival teams. Characters have different strengths too: those who excel at advancing the frontlines and those who are better at taking the defensive in the rear. Pick your playstyle and charge into battle!
