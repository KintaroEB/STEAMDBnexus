About this gameFull description from Steam+
If you like to make puzzles, Super Jigsaw Puzzle: Generations is your game. Enjoy its comfortable and intuitive interface and its relaxing music that will allow you to concentrate on the difficult task of solving these beautiful photos that suppose a real challenge for puzzle lovers. Drag the pieces to their position, form groups with them or use the available boxes to divide them by colors. Configurable snap system to avoid stress. Features 70+ different puzzles!! Autosave, you will not lose your progress 4 levels of difficulty You can form groups with the pieces and combine them together Configurable snap Zoom Relaxing music and atmosphere
