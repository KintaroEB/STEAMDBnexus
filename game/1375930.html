About this gameFull description from Steam+
Tired of boring common square levels? Finally there is a brand new minesweeper with different game modes! Each level is a part of collection. Complete collections to obtain keys and gain access for more episodes. Achieve higher levels to unlock new items, chests, pets and much more! Important! If you’re an experienced player disable "beginner" check mark in options and reach the hardcore episodes. Features: • 22 episodes, 150 collections, 1350 unique levels! • 30 pets. Would you be able to collect all additional evolutions at 180 levels? • Add-on "Puzzles". 42 puzzles and 714 levels available! • Tower of Power - new hard mode! • Two types of game fields: square and hexagon. • Treasure chests and useful items hidden at game field. • And of course coins, which will be handful to buy these items! • Quests - a great opportunity to earn coins and challenge yourself. • Play classic 3 different difficulties of Minesweeper or throw your skills in Custom mode, in which you can design width, height, types and difficulty of the minefield by yourself. • Progress always autosaves wherever you've stopped and also saves in Steam Cloud. • Challenge yourself by getting 50 achievements. • The tutorial's effectivity is proven by the players. • Various skins and animations of the minefield. • Developer worked very hard especially for all Minesweeper fans =)
