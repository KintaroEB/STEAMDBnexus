About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into a fantastic world of streaming! Holoidle is a relaxing idle game where you click and strategize to gain Influence and grow your audience. Purchase assets, collect Collab Cards, and keep your chat engaged for perfect efficiency! Five playable talents for you to choose 🦈 Gawr Gura - The legendary shark idol from Atlantis! ⚓ Houshou Marine - The pirate captain arrives! 💀 Calliope Mori - The reaper is here to collect souls! 🥐 Inugami Korone - The energetic doggo is here for your yubis! 🐙 Ninomae Ina'nis - The comfy priestess of the Ancient Ones awakens! Collect cards from a collection of 100 unique Hololive-themed cards Earn Holocoins by playing and use them to obtain Collab Cards—each one featuring iconic references and talents from Hololive history. ⭐ Rarity System – Cards range from One-Star to Four-Star Legendary, with increasing power and synergy effects. ✨ Foil Cards – Collect 10 copies of a card to unlock a special Foil Version with boosted stats! 🔧 Strategize! – Some cards enhance assets, while others improve the strength of your overall deck. Combine them to create your own unique strategy! Maximize Your Influence Gains! Equip up to six Collab Cards at a time to supercharge your influence generation. Placement matters—combine synergies and position cards strategically to turn a modest 10% bonus into game-changing multipliers! ✨ Foil Cards = Double the Power! Collect 10 copies of a card to unlock its Foil Version, doubling its effectiveness. ⚡ Optimize! – Experiment with different builds to find the most powerful combinations! Keep Your Idol Happy! Your idol needs love and care! Cook meals, clean up, and decorate their home with unlockable furniture to keep them happy and productive. 💎 Holocoins – Your idol generates 1 Holocoin every 40 minutes, but keep them happy and they’ll double their earnings to 1 every 20 minutes! 🎨 Customize Their Space – Unlock and arrange furniture to create the perfect idol retreat. 🍳 Tend To Their Needs – Feed, clean, and interact with your idol to strengthen your bond and boost your progress! Restart, Reinvent, Rise! Encore lets you reset your progress while making your next run even stronger! 🔄 Endless Progression – Each encore doubles your base Influence per Second and stacks infinitely. ✨ Foil Power-Up – A random card in your collection upgrades to a Foil Version, boosting its stats. 🃏 Keep Your Collection – All previously earned Collab Cards remain, letting you optimize your strategy with every cycle.
