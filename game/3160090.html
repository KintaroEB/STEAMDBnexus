About this gameFull description from Steam+
What is "Slot & Dungeons"? Slot & Dungeons is a solo deck-building roguelike where enemy and item placements change based on slot mechanics. Strategically use your attacks and skills to defeat enemies, leveling up to enhance your abilities as you explore deeper into the dungeon! "Symbols" to build slots Enemies along the way and resources needed to conquer the dungeon will be incorporated into the slot draw as "symbols". Symbols can be obtained by stepping on each floor or by various effects in the floor. The symbols are obtained when players step on each floor or by various effects in the dungeon! "Type of Symbols" Spell: Triggered after the slot is drawn, these have various effects on the floor. Items: Placed on floor tiles, these activate when picked up or approached. Enemies: These adversaries are out to get you. Study their characteristics and find the key to defeating them! Characters In Slot & Dungeons, you choose one of several characters to take on the dungeon challenge. Each character has unique abilities and symbols, such as the Warrior, who excels at melee attacks and high durability, and the Mage, who specializes in spell-based extermination.
