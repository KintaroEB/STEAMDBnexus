DAY AND NIGHT CYCLE Experience the dramatic shift in the world, as you change from a hunter to hunted at sundown. Face the coming threat or run away without wasting your time to look behind. 4-PLAYER CO-OP Join forces with other players and raise your chances of survival in an exciting co-op mode.
