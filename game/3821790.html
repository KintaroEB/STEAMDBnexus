About this gameFull description from Steam+
ーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーー ※Save data and bonus content from Ys X: Nordics cannot be carried over. ※Ys X: Proud Nordics includes all bonus content previously released for Ys X: Nordics (excluding consumable items), such as costumes, attachment items, and ship decorations. ※Bonus content included in Ys X: Proud Nordics is not compatible with Ys X: Nordics. ーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーーー Embark on a new adventure with pride in your heart! The action RPG Ys X:Nordics returns to Steam® with a massive upgrade—introducing new story content, new adventure areas, and a host of new features in Ys X: Proud Nordics! ［Game Overview］ The massive island of Öland, located in the northern reaches of Obelia Bay. As the battle against the immortal undead known as the Griegr continues, Adol the red-haired adventurer and Karja the pirate princess encounter a host of ancient ruins deep within the island’s uncharted wilderness. What happened on this mysterious island? A fateful meeting with another Mana user sets Adol and Karja on an all-new adventure into the unknown. In addition to their exhilarating Cross Action combat, this enhanced edition introduces new Mana Actions, an expanded map of Öland Island with new exploration areas, and more! Face off against new bosses, race through Mana Ride challenges, and test your skills in the Arena, where fearsome foes await—now with even more content for players to dive into! ［Featured Characters］ ■Adol Christin He's the main character of the story, a vibrant adventurer with fiery red hair and eyes full of curiosity. Adol left his first adventure land, Esteria, at age 17 and was believed to have set off for Celceta. However, according to one of the adventure logs found in his family home, it's revealed that he had visited Obelia Bay with the former thief Dogi before heading to Celceta. ■Karja Balta The sole daughter of Grimmson, the leader of the Baltan Navy. Karja leads the rough seafarers in piracy with confidence, earning her the moniker 'Pirate Princess' among the people of Karnack who have seen her in action. While she possesses elegant features, she is armed with a hatchet and a round shield, displaying a ruthless, Norman fighting style. ［New Characters］ ■Canute Gamley A sharp-featured young man from the Kingdom of Danmorc, Canute always seems listless and hard to pin down. Despite his laid-back demeanor, he’s a first-rate warrior known as “Canute the Bloodshedder.” He’s also the son of Sver, the ruler of Danmorc, who is currently invading the Seven Kingdoms of Britai. He once fought alongside his cousin Karja during a campaign in Britai. A brilliant tactician, he once saved the outnumbered Norman forces by devising a strategy that turned the tide of battle. Still, Karja has an inexplicable aversion to him. ■Astrid Zayren A female Norman who serves as Canute’s Shield Sister and also his second-in-command. Though she does not possess the power of Mana, she has sharp instincts and excels at logistical support. Cheerful and friendly, she speaks to Adol and Karja with an open heart, but is often curt toward Canute likely due to frequently having to deal with his difficult behavior. ［New Story Content］ ■The Uncharted Island of Öland Still on their mission to hunt down the undead Griegr, Adol and Karja continue exploring Obelia Bay aboard the sailing ship Sandras. Their journey leads them to the largest island in Obelia Bay—Öland Island—where they encounter the Shield Siblings, Canute and Astrid, visitors from the Kingdom of Danmorc. Why have the Normans, who supposedly hold a non-aggression pact with the Balta Navy, chosen this moment to appear in Obelia Bay? And what secrets lie hidden on this island, where Balta Navy leader Grimson once conducted a mysterious investigation? Through the eyes of two pairs of Shield Siblings, this new story arc delves deeper into the origins of the Normans and the mysterious power known as Mana. ［Ultimate Endgame Content］ ■Divine Realm: Muspelheim—The Most Challenging Dungeon Yet! Take on the extreme-difficulty dungeon known as Divine Realm: Muspelheim! Not only is your time in this divine realm strictly limited, but you’ll also face deadly traps and powerful foes. By using a rare item called Deep Crystals, you can lift certain restrictions within the Divine Realm—such as extending your time limit or adjusting enemy abilities—giving you more options as you tackle the toughest dungeon in the game! ［Cross Action］ ■Speed & Combo Attacks: Solo Mode In Solo Mode, you’ll control either Adol or Karja alone in fast-paced, high-action battles. Master quick steps and dashes to chain together blistering combos and take down your enemies! Raid Slash A swift attack that slices through enemies ahead while moving forward. Fast to execute, this skill chains easily into combos following regular attacks. Stellar Array An advanced version of Raid Slash. Adol slashes through enemies while tracing star-like patterns around himself, wiping out any foes in his path. Mana Shoot Fires a wave of Mana energy from the tip of the sword to strike distant enemies. With low SP cost, it’s an excellent choice for keeping enemies at bay. Carmine Blast An advanced version of Mana Shoot. Unleashes an ultra-dense beam of searing Mana energy in a powerful long-range attack. Shield Bash Rushes forward with a round shield, slamming into enemies. While active, the skill allows you to perfect guard against incoming attacks. Skuld Strike An advanced version of Shield Bash. A powerful Norman-style combo attack that combines shield bashes, axe strikes, and kicks in rapid succession. Flip Axe A simple but forceful upward axe strike. Launches enemies into the air, leaving them vulnerable. Lost Cocytus An advanced version of Flip Axe. Harnesses freezing Mana to summon an icy prison around nearby enemies, then shatters it in a devastating attack. ■Power & Coordinated Offense: Combo Mode Control both characters simultaneously in Combo Mode! Build up the Revenge Gauge to unleash massive damage! Double Smasher Both characters leap high in unison, slamming down fiercely upon enemies below. Adamas Saber A balanced offense-defense skill. Blocks enemy attacks with Mana, then instantly counters with a powerful strike. Blazal Cross Adol and Karja dash past one another in a crossing motion, releasing a V-shaped slash that cuts through enemies. Zena Striker Hurls your partner—bound by Mana chains—toward surrounding enemies in a spectacular throw, dealing wide-area damage. ［Mana Action］ ■Spin the Threads of Fate! Nornir's Spindle: Mana String Harness the power of Mana Strings to swing like a pendulum and reach far-off places with this dynamic mana action! Upgrade your Mana Actions to strengthen the strings, enabling you to manipulate objects and activate switches in dungeons. ■Ride with Style! The Grimble Board: Mana Ride Soar and slide over the ground on the Grimble Board in this thrilling Mana Action! Glide over water, race down Mana Roads at high speed, or launch through the air from wind pockets for a fast-paced experience. What's more, you can even take part in a Mana Ride competition, traversing the natural terrain of various parts of Öland! ■Unleash the Power of Mana!: Mana Burst Release elemental Mana attacks with charged strikes! Adol uses fire and Karja ice to not only damage enemies but also to aid in exploration--burn away obstacles or create icy platforms. ■Enhance Your Perception!: Mana Sense Reveal hidden elements of the world with the ancient Druid Monocle. This Mana Action lets you detect enemies, uncover hidden gimmicks, and find buried treasures. As the Druid Monocle gets stronger, your Mana Sense sharpens, allowing you to slow down time and overcome challenges like impassable doors and treacherous walkways. ■Lift and Use Objects: Mana Hold A Mana Action that lets you harness Mana to lift structures or rocks, and launch them toward a target location! Use lifted objects in combat, or create platforms to open up new paths—this versatile ability will prove invaluable when exploring dungeons! ［New Features Exclusive to the Steam Version!］ Supports 21:9 ultra-wide monitors and frame rates exceeding 60fps! Enjoy a wider field of view and smoother gameplay. Preset settings tailored to your gaming environment are also available, ensuring a comfortable experience even on the Steam Deck. ［Engine Powered by DirectX 12］ With improved performance and support for Intel® XeSS, NVIDIA DLSS, and AMD FSR, you can experience the beautiful world of Ys X at higher resolutions and frame rates than ever before.
