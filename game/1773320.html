About this gameFull description from Steam+
Coin Treasures is a Coin Pusher/Coin Dozer on Steroids including incremental progress, realistic physics, numerous upgrades & unlocks and stunning graphics. Unlock artifact spawning. This enables artifacts to drop which will stay in the world after you've won the item. Collect your own treasures in the room filled with the treasure you find. Unlock the Museum to keep track of your valuable Normal, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic Artifacts. Unlock powerful powerups, but be careful this also unlocks powerdowns. Try to get them off the play area to avoid temporary penalties. Collecting the powerups will give you temporary benefits. Use synergies between Powerups to use their combined effects to earn more Treasure. Unlock fun quests, finish them to get awesome rewards to speed up unlocking more! Unlock the Auto Dropper to help you in your clicking journey. Unlock the Power Deck to create your own combination of Powerups and Powerdowns. Unlock The Leaderboard to compete with other player for the highest score. Unlock the Black Flag Jackpot to win high Coin amounts. Play in Marathon Mode to prolong your journey and have a more challenging game and collect a lot more Artifacts in the process. The aim of the game is to keep upgrading everything and have a lot of fun doing so. If you like incremental clicker/idle/upgrade games you will have a lot of fun playing this game.
