About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mars First Logistics is an open world physics simulation game where you design and build mechanized rovers to deliver stuff. Play solo or with up to 3 friends in online co-op! Design and assemble your rover using an intuitive and tactile editor. Combine over 100 parts including springs, hydraulic cylinders, servo motors, rocket engines and many more to tailor your creations to the unique challenges of each job, or just let your imagination run wild. Pilot your creations across the vast, procedurally generated terrain of Mars. Heavy cargo, fragile cargo, bouncy cargo, cargo that’s trying to float away – you never know what new challenges lay around the corner! Help the Martians complete their construction projects by moving their awkwardly shaped cargo from place to place. With each successful delivery you'll earn funds, unlock new parts and blueprints and gain access to new infrusture such as roads, powerlines and monorails to build! Features Intuitive, Flexible Building: Make your rovers as simple or complex as you’d like! Create anything you want, no matter how ridiculous. Sky’s The Limit: No one right answer leaves success up to your creativity and ingenuity. Open World: Traverse the striking, procedurally generated open world, with new challenges over every horizon. Make Mars Home: Watch new buildings spring up as you complete deliveries. Connect buildings using roads, monorails, power lines, water pipes and more! Unlock Parts: Unlock and combine parts like servo motors, hydraulic cylinders and rocket engines. Co-op: Tackle the challenge solo, or recruit up to 3 friends in online co-op. Play your way: Play in standard or creative modes or tweak individual game settings to your liking.
