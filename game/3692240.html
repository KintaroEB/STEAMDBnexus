About this gameFull description from Steam+
What could be more exciting than creating your own private spot to deer hunt? And if you want realism, this is as close as it gets to actually being in the woods! After you have finished creating, managing, and scouting your property you will be challenged with three different stages of hunting season to include; early fall bow hunting, late fall black powder hunting, and winter rifle hunting. And the rewards are huge, create that ideal whitetail deer habitat and the big bucks will come. CREATE YOUR OWN HUNTING MAPS This unique concept allows you to design and create your own custom deer hotspot! Many tree, ground cover and foliage options are available to craft you map to include; 10 species of trees, 6 species of saplings, 9 types of ground cover, 7 types of foliage, and 6 different terrain types. MANAGE & HUNT YOUR PROPERTY Use of one of the four delivered hunting maps, or your custom created maps to create hunting campaigns. By planting food plots, placing trail cameras, and deploying tree stands you can prepare yourself for six hunting days offered in your campaign. Three different stages of fall can be hunted offering different firearms/archery choices and changing deer patterns. Deer sign will also help in patterning and harvesting deer which include; deer trails, deer rubs, deer scrapes, and antler sheds. ACTION MODE & TARGET RANGE Action mode is a faster paced hunt designed for increased shooting and situational awareness. Two hunting areas offer challenging and different topographies. Any firearm/archery tackle offered in the game may be selected which include; 30-06 scoped rifle, 30-30 rifle, 12 gauge pump shotgun, two types of muzzle loading riles, crossbow, and compound bow. The target range offers the same firearm/archery tackle options and provides 3D targets and block targets with shooting from either the ground or elevated in a tree stand.
