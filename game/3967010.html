About this gameFull description from Steam+
DEMO CONTENT In this demo, you have a limited set of questions (300+), and only the online mode. The full set of questions (1000+) and the local mode will be available in the full game. WHO KNOWS YOU BEST? The You Quiz is a multiplayer party game designed for 3 to 8 players. Perfect for game nights with friends or family, it also works great as an icebreaker at social gatherings! ASYMMETRICAL PARTY GAME One player gets to be the King. Everyone else? Courtiers desperate for approval. THE COURTIER'S TASK Guess the King’s opinion on personal, weird, or hilarious prompts: “What's King's unpopular opinion?” “It's the end of the world. What is King's first reaction?” “Name three things King hates:” Better know your friend! Answer with insight, flattery, or playful nonsense. Whatever makes them feel seen, smile, laugh, or glow with pride. THE KING DECIDES As the King, you judge every answer: 👎 Meh? Reject it. 👍 Pretty good? One step forward. ❤️ Nailed it? Two steps up the stairs! Your court, your rules. Reward brilliance. Punish mediocrity. DETHRONE THE KING First to reach the top of the stairs steals the crown. Then they become the new King! FIND YOUR TITLE At the end of each game, players earn titles that match their vibe. Were you the Don Juan? The Emperor? The Jealous? There are 30+ titles! Bragging rights included. DEMO FEATURES LIST Online mode 300+ questions Endless game mode 10 playable characters 30+ titles IN THE FULL GAME ONLY Local mode (with phone as controller) 1000+ questions 20+ playable characters
