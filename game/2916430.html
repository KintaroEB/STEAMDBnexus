About this gameFull description from Steam+
Play solo or co-op with up to 6 players. Open your single-player session to other players and friends to play together. Or you can join a game from the lobby and start serving fast food with others via voice and text chat. Handle everything it takes to keep a fast food joint running smoothly. Experience the realistic operation of authentic drink machines, grills, and fryers. Explore the chaotic nature of fast-food restaurants where unexpected and bizarre events can occur at any moment, challenging you and your restaurant crew to navigate through teamwork. Managing your time wisely is critical as you prioritize tasks to keep customers happy and the line moving smoothly. Team coordination is key, so delegate tasks effectively to ensure seamless operations. Get ready to take on the fast-paced world of fast food.
