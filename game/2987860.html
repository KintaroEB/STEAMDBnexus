About this gameFull description from Steam+
Rock the Islands - a cozy Idle Arcade game with grind elements and 5-player co-op. Team up with friends to explore mysterious islands: mine resources, rescue dwellers from the goblins, gather a gang of powerful bald men to fight back against your enemies! MINE THE ORE You need coins in order to buy anything here! Mine resources and gems, make profit by selling your loot to various traders, so you can afford upgrades, new pickaxe and unexplored lands. RESCUE THE DWELLERS Rescue dwellers from captivity so they can work for you! Make them mine resources in your base, or take them along so they protect you and mine resources with you! UPGRADE YOUR EQUIPMENT Upgrade your pickaxe, unlock new skills and get a better armor. Also don’t forget to upgrade your dwellers so they make profit for you and be well protected! DEFEND THE SETTLEMENT Goblins can raid your village! They use ships and boats to deliver reinforcements! Don’t let them land on your territory, as they can rob you! CO-OP You can play singleplayer, or team up with up to 5 players. It’s more fun to mine resources together! Share responsibilities and distribute the gathered resources for a common goal. MINIGAMES Engage in minigames hosted by strangers. Challenge bosses or be rewarded for killing waves of enemies on arena! FAQ Why is the game free-to-play? This is our first game. As of now our priority is to learn to work with Steam platform and receive feedback from our audience. How will you update the game? We are planning to release new patches to iterate our game and bring the best experience for users. But nothing serious. We are already preparing for our next big game! What does buying chips give? For chips, you can spin the roulette to acquire new skills. Keep in mind your ability to obtain chips for free by completing daily quests and risk it all by each spin on roulette. Purchasing chips or soundtrack is a great way to support us! Are you planning to add cards, items for the Points Shop? We are not sure of the criterias for such a game and it would be great to add trading cards and items for Point Shop in the upcoming future.
