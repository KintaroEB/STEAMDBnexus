About this gameFull description from Steam+
【Bug Fix in Progress】 Set it in the corner of your desktop, and your heroes will continuously battle monsters and find treasure, whether you're working or slacking off! No manual control is needed and interruptions are no issue. The fully automated combat offers a completely relaxing leveling experience. Say goodbye to the fatigue of traditional RPGs with njihovim repetitive map runs, mob grinding, and skill clicking. Simply open the game anytime to harvest your loot, power up, and advance to new dungeons, effortlessly enjoying the thrill of soaring power levels! This is an idle RPG you can really get hooked on—the monsters get stronger on their own, so the grinding never stops! Automated combat plows through everything from trash mobs to bosses, with gear dropping and stats rising constantly, perfectly recreating the satisfying loot loop of RPG dungeons. Slay, loot, and enhance, with the added thrill of random affixes and set effects. You can chase the ultimate god-tier gear, all while idling! The game features over a dozen branching classes, from Warrior and Mage to Assassin, Summoner, Tomb Keeper, and Alchemist. Each hero possesses unique talents and growth paths! The number of skills is staggering, allowing for countless combat styles. You can build a screen-clearing AOE burst team, or a complex tactical squad combining summoning, control, and damage-over-time effects. Freely mix and match builds to create your ultimate dungeon-clearing party! With its retro pixel art side-scrolling style, the game pays homage to the thrilling combat of classic RPGs! It sits neatly in a corner of your screen, taking up minimal space and not interfering with your work or studies. But a quick glance is all it takes to see your adventure squad in the heat of battle! Beneath its casual idle exterior lies a deep RPG progression system. Looting while idling and the satisfying pace of leveling up make for an exhilarating experience that’s sure to boost your mood, even if you’re just checking in during a break. The combat unfolds in a side-scrolling auto-chess format. While the battles are fully automated, every lineup decision is packed with strategic depth! Frontline tanks, backline damage dealers, crowd control combos, main and sub-DPS synergy... with dozens of skills combining in random ways, you can unleash devastating damage combos or intricate control chains. Pulling off an upset with a weaker team or turning the tide with a single brilliant move all comes down to your understanding and strategic deployment of heroes!
