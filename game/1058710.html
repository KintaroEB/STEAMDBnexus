About this gameFull description from Steam+
Introduction to the Current Game & What features can you play with? Play as a lord, general, king, or emperor of Catholicism or Eastern Orthodoxy. Govern vassals, members of the administrative system, and serve superior lords under different administrative systems. Different cultures bring distinct policies, technologies, and unique game mechanics Customize your standing army, ranging from equipment and training to awarding medals and unlocking special battalion skills. Develop your own territory and plan different categories of population Conclude treaties in your own or other lords' territories, and build private estates Characters can gain various relevant experiences through their behaviors to unlock different personal skills. Most interactions between characters depend on conditional exchanges: What do you want from me, and what benefits can I get in return? Expand your family, unlock new bloodlines, or create new branches, family trees, family laws, and bloodline skills. Use intelligence and spies to carry out different missions. Education and management of offspring Balance secular power and religious authority to obtain what you desire from religious leaders. Different eras come with different tasks and unlock era modifiers. Collect collectibles from around the world. The game map spans from the British Isles to Baghdad in the Persian region, encompassing the Mediterranean. Currently, the map includes more than 4,000 fiefs. The tutorial on the Norman invasion does not fully align with historical facts. Versions prior to 2022 have been placed in SteamDev, named the Classic Edition, and you can switch versions at any time.
