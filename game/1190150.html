About this gameFull description from Steam+
6 Minute Games Almost instant respawns and constant action. A casual free game to play solo or with friends with short rounds—and if you get bored just leave—get into a match instantly & no waiting 30 minutes for a game to end. Paint Warfare takes a refreshing turn away from other more tactical FPS games (and even some movement shooters). Equip a combo of 2 unique abilities to get a specific edge over your opponents 10 Abilities including: Bullet shields Dash Jetpack Grapple Hook Invisibility Turret, etc Climb any wall you see holding the [space bar] Fly across the map with the speed strips and boost ramps Double jump Slide or fall to the ground with [CTRL] Maps: 13 unique maps to fight on. Game Modes: 6 gamemodes: Minigame Free for all, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Hunger Games, Point Capture, Hunger Games and private/ custom games! 10 Abilities to unlock, such as: Turret Dash Hang Glider Invisibility Grapple hook Super speed boots Heal Jetpack Enemy Radar Half shield Hats & gun skins: Collect coins through getting XP / completing challenges and unlock awesome skins for your rifles and character! Guns: Paint Warfare has (almost) everything, from railguns and oranges to spoons, teleporters, gas guns and shotgun pistols. I'm always adding suggestions! An awesome free game to play with friends! It's easy to join your friend's lobby using the match codes, or you can create your own private game with your own game rules!
