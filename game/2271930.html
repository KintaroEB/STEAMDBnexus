About this gameFull description from Steam+
IfSunSets is an open-world survival adventure RPG set on the mysterious island of Luminora, brimming with pirate lore and legends of a Kraken. By day, explore the vast, beautiful land to collect resources, hunt, farm, fish, craft weapons, build fortifications, and improve your skills for battles against cursed monsters and powerful bosses from forgotten times. Once the sun sets, the island is shrouded in darkness and transforms into a land of death. Arm yourself and survive the hordes of undead to see another morning! Embark on a mysterious journey to the island, sail across the ocean, and claim islands as your own! Become a pirate and sail the seas! Experience intense naval battles between pirates across the vast ocean. Aim at enemy pirate ships and dominate the battlefield with perfectly timed cannon fire. Your aim will sway with the waves, and strategic positioning will determine victory or defeat. A single shot can turn the tide of battle. Build your own pirate ship! Choose your hull, sails, and cannons to complete your own pirate ship. Whether you prioritize speed, firepower, or durability is entirely up to your strategy. Experiment with countless combinations to build the ultimate ship that can survive any situation. EXPLORE THE MYSTERIOUS ISLAND DURING THE DAY! Stranded on the island with nothing but the clothes on your back, you begin your journey of survival. Travel across the shores, jungles, and deserts of this vast open world. Collect mysterious items like “Cthugha Stones” to endure the dangers of this cursed island. Venture deeper into the island and uncover the ancient secrets it has concealed from the world. BUILD YOUR SURVIVAL STRATEGY AND WARD OFF THE HORRORS OF THE NIGHT! When night falls, a vast horde of undead emerges. They will pursue you no matter where you hide. Build your own custom fortress to prepare for nightfall. Set up traps and obstacles to protect yourself from the night of the dead. Construct your fortifications with defense in mind! EXPERIENCE AN ENGAGING BATTLE SYSTEM! The ancient souls confined to the island are hunting you. Strengthen your stats and sharpen your combat skills for the battles that await. Engage in an action-packed battle system and defeat countless enemies! Experience the underwater ecosystem! The depths of the ocean are an unknown frontier, filled with ancient relics, breathtaking locations, and dangerous marine life. Through underwater exploration, you can obtain valuable resources that aid your survival and growth. TEAM UP AND EXPLORE THE ISLAND TOGETHER! Work together, share resources, and strategize for survival. Join forces with your allies to face the lurking dangers of Luminora!
