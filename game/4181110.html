About this gameFull description from Steam+
Everything you once believed in collapses overnight. You are Xie En, a brilliant architect. Betrayed by your mentor, you are reduced to a prisoner. While incarcerated, you receive the devastating news of your father's resentful death. To avenge them, you endure eight years of patient concealment, then return under the alias Wu Wei, infiltrating the enemy's headquarters—the Yongqiang Group. The law cannot judge his hypocrisy, and hatred consumes your heart. This time, you decide to act on your own terms, walking the fine line of rules to weave an impeccable scheme. Your target is not his life, but the empire he holds dearer than life itself. In the end, can Wu Wei succeed in his revenge and bring the mastermind to justice? How will he survive in this cutthroat corporate world? Everything hinges on your choices. "If one seeks to win, they must neither abide by the rules nor reject them outright—they must walk between the rules." Game Features A Meticulously Crafted Urban Hunt A high-IQ revenge power fantasy.Assume the dual identity of the protagonist, Xie En/Wu Wei, and execute a step-by-step, intricately layered revenge plan. This is no reckless outburst of rage, but a calm hunt where wisdom is your spear, and rules are your shield. Driven by clear objectives, you will never lose sight of your ultimate goal—even amid intertwined romantic subplots. The Workplace Is a Battlefield: Only the Victor Reigns Leave behind the illusion of peaceful coexistence. Use your wits and tactics to fight your way through the labyrinth of office politics and uncover the truth! Say goodbye to monotonous gameplay: the dynamic feedback system delivers the thrill of consequences with every decision. Meticulously designed character interactions will keep your heart racing and your senses on edge. Multiple Branches, Multiple Endings, Parallel Storylines The game boasts nearly 50 distinct endings and hundreds of plot branches. Every dialogue choice, every mission strategy, can steer the story onto a brand-new path. Will you bring total destruction to your enemies? Or escape unscathed? Will you drown in tender affection? Or forge ahead alone into danger? Step into the game, embrace the revenge, and write an ending that is uniquely yours. Immersive & Engaging Game Design Experience a rich variety of interactive gameplay: · In tense, high-stakes QTE sequences, you must stay absolutely calm to ensure your actions leave no trace. · Navigate the perilous corporate environment: choose to sneak in stealthily to gain the upper hand, or step into the fray when necessary. This is a game where choices are tangled in love and hate, right and wrong. On this cold path of revenge, will these passionate emotions become your greatest vulnerability? Or your most formidable strength? Every choice that tests your humanity carries the weight of a thousand jun. Characters Xie En (Wu Wei) Once a wrongfully imprisoned architect, now a ruthless avenger. Under the fake identity "Wu Wei", he infiltrates his former mentor's Yongqiang Group and meticulously plots his revenge—using financial tactics to dismantle everything the mentor holds dear. This high-IQ hunt walks the razor's edge between rules and human nature. Zhang Yun'er The "queen" of Yongqiang Group, Xie En's direct supervisor and "benefactor". She is a paradox of rationality and sensibility. Little does she know that the capable subordinate she personally promoted is a hunter targeting the heart of her family empire. When unyielding business pragmatism collides with uncontrollable emotion, the line between hunter and prey begins to blur. Chen Xiaoman A mysterious corporate accountant at the group. Her meteoric rise from a dancer to a core staff member is shrouded in enigma. Her relationship with Xie En starts as a dangerous game of mutual exploitation. In a world of scheming and temptation, whoever falls in love first will lose everything. Guan Xinling The seemingly innocent "white rabbit" of the group, hiding sharp observation skills and a rebellious heart behind her sketchbook. Desperate to break free from her cage, her transformation will be the biggest wildcard in Xie En's plan. She could be his purest redemption—or his deadliest flaw.
