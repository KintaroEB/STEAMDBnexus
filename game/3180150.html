About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mall Simulator is a chill first-person simulation where you will experience a giant mall tycoon with various stores that you run yourself: 👕 Clothing ⚽ Sports 💻 Tech 🧸 Toys 🛒 Supermarket 💄 Cosmetics 🎬 Cinema 🍔 Burger Shop 🕹️ Arcade 🏠 Home Goods 🎮 Game Store 🥐 Bakery 💍 Jewelry 🌸 Florist 🍣 Sushi Bar 🥤 Vending Machines 💵 ATMs …and so much more to explore in your mega mall! Design matters! Create the best design for your mall and manage your stores. Showcase your best-seller stores and create the perfect mall for your customers. Zero to hero! Open new stores of the most famous brands and sell their products in your mall. Expand your stores in the mall from a grocery to a jewellery. Out of stock? Always supply new products and follow new trends to keep your customers in your mall. Time is money! Your mall may be busy, take payments and hire new workers so you don't keep your customers waiting. Smash the walls! Open up new spaces and make room for more stores and brands in your mall. In Mall Simulator, customer satisfaction is neccesary. You have to keep up with trends, build new stores, set smart prices and grow your mall to avoid going bankrupt.
