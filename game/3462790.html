About this gameFull description from Steam+
Battle For Embolia is a fast-paced indie MMORPG focused on responsive combat, meaningful progression, and constant updates. Developed by a solo creator, the game aims to deliver fun from the very first minute, avoiding unnecessary grind and keeping gameplay clear, dynamic, and rewarding. Choose your path Select from three unique characters, each with distinct abilities and playstyles. Experiment with four weapon types, combine skills, and discover powerful interactions as you explore the world of Embolia. Explore a growing world Travel through ten handcrafted areas, battle the creatures created by the sorcerer Swordon, and take part in group activities and challenging encounters. New zones, systems, and balance improvements are added frequently based on player feedback. Face the first raid boss Challenge the game’s first raid encounter: a powerful being forged by Swordon to control all life in Embolia, only to be ignored by everyone. Team up with other players, learn the mechanics, and overcome one of the strongest enemies currently available. Built with the community Community involvement is central to the development of Battle For Embolia. Players can share suggestions, report issues, and help steer the direction of future updates through email and the Steam Community. The game evolves every week through iterative development and active feedback. Early Access and future plans During Early Access, planned features include new characters, additional PvP and group activities, more areas and bosses, expanded progression systems, and servers for additional regions. Fair free-to-play approach Battle For Embolia is fully free to play. An optional ten-dollar subscription may be offered to support development, focused on cosmetic and non-pay-to-win benefits. All core gameplay features will always remain accessible to every player.
