About this gameFull description from Steam+
Tower! Simulator 3 puts you in control of busy airports around the world, where every departure, arrival, runway crossing, and taxi instruction is your responsibility. Whether you’re managing a quiet regional airport or coordinating traffic at a major international hub, every decision matters. Built for aviation enthusiasts, aspiring controllers, and industry professionals alike, Tower! Simulator 3 combines realistic airport operations, advanced voice communications, a fully interactive 3D tower environment, and extensive customization tools into the ultimate ATC experience. Key Features Voice-Controlled Air Traffic Control Communicate with pilots using advanced voice recognition technology and experience realistic ATC interactions from the tower. Realistic Airport Operations Manage departures, arrivals, taxi movements, runway crossings, gate assignments, and ground traffic using authentic ATC procedures. Immersive 3D Tower View Watch aircraft operate from a fully rendered control tower environment while maintaining complete control of airport operations. Multiplayer Support Work together with other controllers in real-time and divide responsibilities across ground, tower, and departure operations. 11 Airports Included Start your ATC career immediately with eleven detailed airports included in the base game, ranging from regional destinations to major international hubs. TIST (Cyril E. King Airport) KLGA (LaGuardia Airport) KLAX (Los Angeles International Airport) KRDU (Raleigh-Durham International Airport) TXKF (L.F. Wade International Airport) EDDS (Stuttgart Airport) KBUR (Hollywood Burbank Airport) VRMM (Velana International Airport) VIDP (Indira Gandhi International Airport) TNCM (St. Marteen, Princess Juliana Airport) KTLH (Tallahassee International Airport) Community Modding & Customization Customize schedules, airlines, strips, liveries, terminals, and airport operations. Create your own content and expand the simulator through community-driven enhancements. Dynamic Real-World Lighting Experience realistic lighting powered by real-world parameters, delivering stunning day, night, and weather transitions. Multi-Monitor Support Expand your workspace and build the ATC setup that best fits your style. Built for the Community From free airport additions and gameplay improvements to expanded customization and upcoming AI-powered technologies, the simulator is constantly growing alongside its passionate community. Whether you’re issuing your very first takeoff clearance or managing a busy international hub during peak traffic, Tower! Simulator 3 delivers an authentic and rewarding air traffic control experience unlike any other. ATC Mode On.
