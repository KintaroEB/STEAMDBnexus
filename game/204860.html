About this gameFull description from Steam+
Lead the forgotten. Fight the impossible. Men of War: Condemned Heroes is a standalone entry in the acclaimed real-time tactics series, delivering one of the most intense and uncompromising portrayals of World War II. Command a Soviet penal battalion formed under the infamous Order 227 – units sent into the deadliest missions with no room for retreat or mercy. Based on real historical accounts and veterans’ testimonies, the game follows condemned soldiers through four connected campaigns spanning 1942–1945 – from the desperate defenses in Volhynia and Poland to the final assault on Germany. Every mission demands precision, courage, and sacrifice, blending large-scale battlefield tactics with brutal, up-close skirmishes. Built on an enhanced GEM engine, Condemned Heroes introduces improved physics, visuals, and animation, making the chaos and realism of war more visceral than ever. Its unforgiving design and historical authenticity create a raw, cinematic experience that pushes players to lead with strategy and survive against impossible odds. Men of War: Condemned Heroes is a brutal, story-driven strategy experience about redemption, survival, and the human cost of war. Key Features: Standalone campaign experience – no prior Men of War titles required. Four story-driven campaigns (1942–1945) – follow the journey of a Soviet penal unit through the Eastern Front. Based on real events – inspired by true accounts and veterans’ stories of the Red Army’s penal battalions. Enhanced GEM engine – improved physics, destruction, animations, and visual fidelity. Authentic Eastern Front atmosphere – from uniforms and weapons to historically accurate environments. Tactical realism and challenge – every decision, every mistake, and every life matters.
