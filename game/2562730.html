About this gameFull description from Steam+
Jump into a world where monsters are everywhere, and it's up to you to stop them! -A flexible game that you can play as your main activity or enjoy casually while working or playing other games. Create your own builds: There are various upgrading systems in this game, including: unlocking random gears, obtaining powerful passives, and utilizing the skill tree. Key features: Unique Towers: Choose from a different set of towers, each with its special way of attacking and upgrading. Roguelike Elements: With random rewards and various passives and upgrades, no two resets will feel the same. Skill Tree: Spend skill points on new upgrades to boost your gameplay. With six paths filled with skills, you can customize your game experience. Offline Progress: When you exit the game, your towers will keep farming for you. When you come back you will find all the precious rewards they found Two Layers of Prestige: Unlock three new contents, The Champion, Shards of Valor and Books of Powers to significantly improve your power. The Automator: A feature that plays the game for you, unlocks towers and purchases upgrades by itself. And many more
