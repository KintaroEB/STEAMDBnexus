About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Wonderland M" Annual Expansion — "Olympus": The Descent of Divine Punishment! Highlights of this massive update: ◎New Companion: The player-designed partner "Iris" has arrived to face the might of Olympus! ◎New Challenges: Uncover the "Source of Chaos" in the new storyline and survive the "Wrath of the Gods" in the new dungeon! ◎New Feature: The "Wonderland Temple - Star Corridor" system is coming soon—the perfect place to house your Reincarnated Partners! ◎"Wonderland M" New Expansion – Floating Monkey’s Grand Adventure The adorable Floating Monkey debut, with exclusive dungeons, furniture, and mounts! ◎「Venus and Athena」Dual Goddess Rebirth Arrival The brand-new expansion of "Wonderland M - Goddess Rebirth," is officially here! The final storyline and the second part of the reincarnation quests for "Venus" and "Athena" are now fully available. ◎New Dark Attribute Character - Dragon Betrayer Arrives The forbidden power of the Hopeless Black Dragon Nidhogg, they abandoned the innate defense of the dragon race in exchange for immense evil power and forbidden magic. As followers of Nidhogg, the Dragon Betrayers inherit his dark legacy, and both enemies and allies fear their power. With the power of despair bestowed upon the Dragon Betrayers by the Hopeless Black Dragon Nidhogg, they can use Dark Attribute skills. Destroying all creatures, sacrificing defense for high attack, they seem to have an innate suppression effect on all living beings. ◎New Goddess Creation Quest, New Goddess Event CG Story! After the game update, when creating a new character, you can choose the new "Goddess Creation" quest and go to the Raphael Battleship commanded by Captain "Catherine" to begin the Goddess story line. During your adventure, you will encounter classic characters from "Goddess Online" and trigger beautiful Japanese-voiced CG stories. ◎Meet Companions Who Cheat Death! Unravel Vast and Interesting Stories! Encounter "Niss" who just woke up under a tree, rescue the grandmother and granddaughter "Xiao Lan" whose home was invaded by bandits, befriend the brave and adventurous village chief's daughter "Loka", and meet different companions to unlock one interesting and funny story after another. ◎Design Your Unique Home! Housing is one of the most popular systems in the game. In the game, players can transform their initial tiny "shack" into their own "mansion" or "Dream House" through a wide variety of tent styles and furniture arrangements. The official team will also periodically hold housing design events, selecting outstanding works and awarding rich prizes! ◎Capture All Companion Pets! Even Beauties and Beasts Can Be Ridden! "What!? You can ride beauties and beasts!? Am I seeing things?" No need to be surprised, because this is Phasmophobia! In the game, almost all creatures encountered in the wild can be captured. The most interesting part is that all captured pets can become mounts, whether they walk on land, fly in the sky, have human forms, or are animals, everyone can ride them! Unique Transportation to Travel the World! Land, Sea, and Air All Included! Explore the Open Drifting World! Travel to Kyoto, Egypt, Persia, Rome, and more! Most players must have dreamed of traveling the world, but alas, time is not enough, and this goal can only slowly become a fantasy. However, in the game, players will have the opportunity to drive different modes of transportation and personally experience locations across the globe from ancient to modern times, including "Asia, Europe, Antarctica, and even Mayan civilizations"!!
