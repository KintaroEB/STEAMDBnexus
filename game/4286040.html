About this gameFull description from Steam+
Don't Drop the Bomb! Are you ready for a physics-based chaos experience with your group of 2-4 friends? Try to get the bomb to the top of the mountain before it explodes! But there is one small problem... You are all sausages and you can only ride unicycles! Don't drop the bomb or you will explode! Test Your Friendship! Play together with 2-4 players. Will you work as a team or will you drop the bomb in your friend's lap and run away? Physics Chaos Balancing on a single wheel while tossing that bomb to your friend is definitely harder than it looks! Massive Threats Don't just blow up your friends, stand shoulder to shoulder to survive massive boss battles! Precise Balance You must be careful with every move, otherwise the slightest slip, loss of balance, and fall could take you out! Create Your Own Sausage Customize your character from scratch with different costume and accessory options. Create your own sausage with funny hats, wild accessories, and various face styles!
