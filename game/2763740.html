About this gameFull description from Steam+
How far can you go? Revolution Idle is a free idle incremental where numbers explode… and never stop. Multiply. Automate. Accelerate. Reach infinity then go beyond. 2M+ players worldwide. 130,000 on Discord. The revolution is already happening. Prestige. Push further. Speed up circle filling with color spectrums. Trigger prestiges to boost multipliers and increase your exponent. Every run makes you stronger. Reach ~1.79e308 and trigger Infinity. Unlock upgrades, power a chain of Generators, conquer Challenges, and grow Stars that boost every run. Eternity resets everything… so you can go even further. New systems unlock step by step. Deeper. More complex. More powerful. Unity awaits. Absurd numbers. New mechanics. Go beyond. WHY YOU’LL GET HOOKED Highly addictive loop: watch numbers skyrocket at insane speed. Full automation: optimize everything and let it run. Macro builder: create your own automation strategies. 520+ achievements: with real progression bonuses. Offline progress: keep gaining while you're away. Global leaderboard: compete with players worldwide. Cross-platform: PC, Android, iOS synced progress. Daily rewards: log in, stack bonuses. Active development: frequent updates, more content. NEW TO IDLE GAMES? A built-in tutorial gets you started fast. Need more depth? A full wiki is available in-game. THE DEVELOPERS Developed by Nu Games and Oni Gaming. Backed by a community of 130,000 players. The revolution continues.
