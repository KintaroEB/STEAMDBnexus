About this gameFull description from Steam+
Get the newest update first-hand on our Discord server! You can find the access to our Discord server in the links on the right side of this page. EVERY STEP COUNTS, PLAN BEFORE EVERY MOVE Think carefully before you move in this turn-based battle system where enemies move right after you. Be aware of the Erosion. You won't last long when it reaches its limit. Strategize one step ahead and utilize all your belongings to survive. Stay vigilant; you never know when you'll encounter the deadly traps. MORE THAN 100 WEAPONS TO FORGE RIGHT WHERE YOU STAND Create different weapons based on the materials you choose. Each and every weapon possesses an unique Skill, broaden your playstyle. Make shattered weapons materials as well, inheriting their strength to the next masterpiece. Craft and destroy weapons to find your own playstyle. OVERCOME DEATH AND BECOME STRONGER WITH YOUR SOULS Collect Souls for permanent upgrades and items. Stride ahead against a variety of elite enemies. Test your might and knowledge in challenging boss battles. Find Memories to reach out to the truth of "this world." Storytelling via both cutscenes and text-based novels with over 150K word count. All 6 main characters and elite enemies are fully voiced by Japanese voice actresses, with real-time 2D animations. Supports Steam Deck and controllers, with modifyable key configurations. You opened your eyes, but nothing could be seen. Suddenly, one string of dim light shone through the darkness. As you followed the light of hope, it slowly turned from azure to crimson. Once again, your consciousness faded away... You opened your eyes, and found yourself in the middle of nowhere. A mysterious girl, with her monotonous tone, spoke of various confusing matters. Safe House. Purpose. Erosion. Death. The only way to grasp the truth is to hold on the black sword of yours, and fight against "This World," no matter the consequences... "I will stay with you right here. Try all you can, through hell and fire's end..."
