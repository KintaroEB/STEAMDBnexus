About this gameFull description from Steam+
Design and build a factory deep underground in this physics-based automation sandbox. Mine resources, buy machines and master the chaos as you become the ultimate Mine Mogul! Build A Sprawling Factory Enjoy the casual construction of massive, interconnected factories deep underground. Wind conveyors through twisting mineshafts, connect machines, and harness physics to automate your every need. Explore The Unknown Venture into the mysterious, hollowed shell of a forgotten cave system. Discover secrets, unearth hidden resources, and rebuild among the ruins of abandoned mining operations. Every cavern holds new opportunities for the ambitious mogul. Manage The Chaos As your factory grows, so does the madness! Conveyors overflow, parts scatter, and unexpected physics mishaps keep you on your toes. Tweak your designs, optimize machine placement, and find creative solutions to keep the gears turning and the chaos under control.
