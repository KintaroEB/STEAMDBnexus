About this gameFull description from Steam+
Experience the world of professional show jumping with this simulation-based game! Ride through realistic jumping courses with a variety of horses, refine your eye for strides, design your very own courses and share them with friends. Realistic Game Mechanics Developed by riders, for riders, Pro Show Jumping boasts accurate and engaging game mechanics. From stride length and balance dynamics, to take-off distances and trajectory planning, immerse yourself in this outstanding simulation of equestrian jumping. Your riding aids play a pivotal role, but each horse's unique qualities and temperament add a layer of intricacy. Immense Variety Choose from 50 courses authored by world-renowned FEI course designers, ranging from Intermediate to Olympic level classes, or engage in one of ten championships. Play in Career Mode, developing your string of horses all the way to the pinnacle of the sport. Select from a roster of ten top-level horses, adapting your riding style to maximize their potential. Develop your skills with training exercises that target specific techniques. Unlimited Replay Value Unleash your creativity by designing custom courses and exercises using a user-friendly mode featuring distance guides, alignment assistance, and 44 distinct obstacle types. Challenge friends to conquer your course designs and strive for dominance on the Steam Leaderboards by attempting to break world records on each course!
