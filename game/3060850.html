About this gameFull description from Steam+
Note: ChatTDT is not in anyway related or affiliated to "Twitch", the use of Twitch is just for the purpose of clarity. (Requires a Twitch Account and an active stream to play!) Dive into the dynamic world of "ChatTDT: Tower Defense Twitch" where your Twitch chat becomes an integral part of the action! Designed to seamlessly integrate with the Twitch API, ChatTDT offers a unique and interactive tower defense experience that engages both streamers and viewers like never before. Features: Level Up: Progress through challenging levels and enhance your strategies as you go. Prestige: Reset your progress for powerful bonuses and climb the ranks even faster. Viewers Vote Map: Let your audience have a say in the game! Viewers can vote on the next map, adding an extra layer of excitement. Rage Powerbar: Build up rage to unleash devastating powers against your foes. Drops and Inventory: Collect valuable drops and manage your inventory to gain the upper hand. Boss Mobs: Face off against formidable bosses that will test your strategic prowess. Channel Point Rewards Linked: Integrate Twitch channel point rewards directly into the game, making viewer interaction more rewarding. Persistent Saving and Levels for Users: Enjoy a consistent experience with persistent saving, ensuring your progress and user levels are always preserved. Highscore Leaderboards: Compete for glory! Viewers can fight for the top spots on the leaderboards, adding a competitive edge to the game. Discord Webhook Integration: Stay connected with your community through Discord. Game updates and achievements can be shared directly to your server. Stay ahead of the curve with constant updates. If the Twitch API changes, I will strive to update the game as soon as possible to ensure seamless gameplay. Join the ChatTDT community today and transform your Twitch channel into an epic battleground!
