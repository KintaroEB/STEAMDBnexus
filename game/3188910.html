About this gameFull description from Steam+
✨ Features: [*] Unlock 20+ Virtual Cuties with unique personalities and stories [*] Collect, click, and craft Eggs to hatch rare Cuties [*] Unlock full storylines [*] Lightweight idle design — runs minimized [*] Deeper updates coming every few weeks 🍵 Let’s get cozy Love comes to those who click in this comfy, chaotic casual idler. Get a random new cutie every 3 hours — or even sooner if you tap fast and use the right strategy. Some cuties are ultra-rare. Some are… legendary. 💘 Build your dream collection Each cutie has her own personality, dialogue, and now — a full story you can unlock as you bond. Meet tsunderes, yanderes, dommy mommies, goth gfs, bunnygirls, weirdos, hackers, artists, witches, dreamwalkers, disaster lesbians, and more. New cuties and features are added with every update. Whether you’re a casual collector or a long-term simp strategist, there’s always someone new to fall for. 🥚 Crafting, Clicking, Candies & More Not just luck — you can now collect shards and craft Eggs to hatch your favorite cuties. Tap to build affection, reduce cooldowns, and earn higher-tier drops. The deeper your account level, the better your chances at scoring rares. And yes — candies are still here. But they’ve been… rebalanced. 🍬 📖 Storylines & Secrets Each cutie now has a full storyline you can unlock right in their gallery. Learn their pasts, obsessions, heartbreaks, philosophies, and maybe… what they think about you. 😳 Some are funny. Some are spicy. Some are deeply unwell. You’ll love them all. 🌸 Runs on a potato Clicker Cuties runs on just about anything. Minimize it, let it idle in the background, or go full speedclicker — it won’t complain. ~ I can’t wait to meet you, anon ~
