About this gameFull description from Steam+
A charming little shopkeeper accompanies you at all times. Raise fish in your carefully designed aquariums. Once they mature, choose to keep them close for steady gold income or exchange them for instant rewards. Discover multiple themed aquariums filled with hundreds of hidden fish species and decors waiting to be uncovered. Enjoy a collection of over 10 sets of soft music accompanied by ambient white noise designed to help you relax, focus, and recharge while working or studying.
