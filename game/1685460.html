About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into the Role of a Railroad Company President with A-Train: All Aboard! Tourism Embark on a strategic journey in A-Train: All Aboard! Tourism, a business simulation game where you wield the power of the railroad to shape the future of towns. In the A-Train universe, stations are the lifeblood of communities, serving as catalysts for local development. As the president of a burgeoning railroad company, you have the freedom to design stations and lay out rail lines according to your vision. Will you build a sprawling network of efficient trains, or focus on optimizing the existing lines? Make sure to decorate your town by establishing subsidiaries and advertise your company to increase your brand power. The destiny of the town—and your company—lies in your hands. Tourism-based urban planning Each town boasts a unique array of tourist attractions—from tranquil hot spring districts to awe-inspiring historical castles. It's your task to unlock these destinations' full potential. If a tourist spot is hard to access, it will attract fewer visitors, no matter how incredible the view. Use your transportation network, which can include railroads, bus lines, and even ferries, to craft accessible and enticing vacation experiences. With each new surge of tourists to your attraction, you can be assured that your strategies are working! All aboard! Tourism is a one-way ticket to urban planning! Whether you envision a thriving metropolis, a peaceful town nestled next to natural wonders, or a bustling hub with a top-tier transportation network, the canvas is yours to paint. A unique story of your own, told with the help of your friends and associates. Now, it's time to seize this opportunity to dive into the world of tourism planning and take the first steps toward constructing your ideal city!
