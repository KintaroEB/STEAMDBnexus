About this gameFull description from Steam+
<Rune Coliseum> is a complex cold-weapon combat simulation game. Train a gladiator, learn and master a unique Action Lineup system, and use weapons, actions, and magic to kill → loot → grow in deadly elimination battles. Raise a roster of unique warriors and win glory with blood. Action Lineup Arrange actions into a queue and clash with enemies Weapons Choose from many weapon schools: Sword & Shield, Two-Handed, Spear, Dual Wield, Combat, and more Characters Recruit gladiators of every style and unleash their combat strengths and Ultimates Base Buy gladiators → train → fight → retire → legacy; develop your Base with resources earned in battle Ancient God Turn the tide with powerful ancient magic Character Interaction Interact with other gladiators in the tournaments—become friends or enemies Multiple Modes Train gladiators in "Tournament Mode", or try the fast-growth "Sacrifice Mode"
