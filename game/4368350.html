About this gameFull description from Steam+
Gameplay: The general gameplay of SLCSDO (Silly Linguine Cat Simulator Deluxe Online) is that you have to pet Silly Linguine Cat, you will have pet goals, when you get to that amount of pets, you will get a random crate (gambling!!!!). These crates can be opened to reward you with skins for Linguine Cat! However, If gambling isn't your thing, then lucky for you we have one other core gameplay mechanic, the leaderboard!!! The leaderboard will show who has pet silly linguine the most! (We have implemented a top-of-the-line anti cheat system, so don't even think about it.) Ok so that's pretty much everything, crates, pet cat, leaderboard, yep that's everything. oh actually we also have music, that's kinda a gameplay feature. yea. Skins Silly Linguine Cat Simulator Deluxe Online has over 350 skins to collect! Common - these are cool but not that cool Uncommon - very neat Rare - they look cool Ultra-Rare - these are very cool Legendary - these are the coolest they all have unique idle and click animations and took the longest to make Exclusive - these can only be obtained exclusively in seasonal, daily, and gold crates.
