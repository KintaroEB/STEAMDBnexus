About this gameFull description from Steam+
Start from nothing and build your business empire step by step! Overcome the struggles and accomplish great things in the market surge and rise to dominate the scene. Strategize, capitalize on events, manage your staff, and handle crises. Transform your small company piece by piece into a commercial empire! Hire more employees and further expand your corporate scale to gradually become a true E-Shop king! A legendary business simulation experience awaits you! As you continue to grow, many choices will present themselves! Do anything it takes to increase your revenue! Advertise your company, expand and customize your office space, surpass your competitors, and boost your brand visibility! Every choice you make paves the way for your success. Capitalizing on market trends is the key to success! Various holidays are crucial for promoting your empire. Make good use of your marketing tools to build your company's reputation, attract a continuous stream of traffic and orders. Deploy diverse marketing strategies and demonstrate your foresight to ensure your sales figures soar! Unexpected situations will test your responsiveness: sudden interviews and "strange" requests can all become turning points for your company's public image. Handle them cleverly, business reputation is the golden badge that attracts customers! Plan your company's office environment to make employees feel like the office is their home. Here, your goal is not just decoration it's to provide a source of productivity. Design your office to your heart's content! Don't neglect the needs of your employees. A positive work atmosphere boosts work efficiency, while a poor environment will only lower employee morale. Boss, you wouldn't want everyone to go on strike, right? Your team is your most crucial asset! Even with billions of orders in your hand, you can't move an inch without the support of capable assistants. Don't forget, employee satisfaction directly impacts work efficiency and creative output. There is strength in numbers. Use your management wisdom to lead your team to their best!
