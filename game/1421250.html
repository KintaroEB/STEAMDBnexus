About this gameFull description from Steam+
Tiny Bunny Is A Non-linear Horror Visual Novel. When winter comes, everything falls into slumber - everything except this forest. There's something wandering its snow-covered paths, lurking behind black trees, and taking whatever it considers due. It leaves behind strange rumors: voices calling you into the thicket, faces watching through your windows, mysterious prints in the snow, and… a growing number of missing-child posters. Those who have heard the voice of the forest are beyond help. Their only option is to sink deeper and deeper into its cold embrace and face their final choice in that icy darkness: to stay alive… or to stay human? Tiny Bunny is a non-linear horror visual novel with a branching plot, inspired by the short story of the same name by Dmitry Mordas. You’ll play as Anton, a young boy who has recently moved to a small, remote town surrounded by forest. In this isolated place, you find yourself pulled into a deepening mystery as unsettling events occur and local children begin to go missing. Your choices determine which paths open up as the danger around the town escalates. ✔ A fascinating horror story that lingers long after the screen goes dark ✔ Your choices carry weight, making every moment tense and unforgettable ✔ Hand-drawn, black-and-white art style ✔ Bone-piercing original soundtrack ✔ Featuring 20 distinct endings ✔ Disturbing, oppressive atmosphere ✔ Horrifying secrets Warning! This game contains offensive language and scenes of physical and psychological violence. Proceed with caution!
