About this gameFull description from Steam+
Kittens Game is a village simulation text game. You manage a village of kittens as they acquire resources and unlock new technologies. Over 30 different buildings, 50+ resources, hundreds of technologies and upgrades. Build a village of kittens and bring them to the moon Discover the uncountable game mechanics and progression systems on your way to world domination: Collect resources and build structures Manage jobs, skills and happiness of your population Craft, trade and research new technologies Unlock secrets of the Cath planet and reach the outer space And many more Create your pet civilization Kittens Game is designed to be play over a long period of time, in a semi-idle manner. This is your sandbox to enjoy and your personal tamagochi to take care of. Play as actively or hands off as you want, you are the master of your world.
