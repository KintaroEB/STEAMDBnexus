About this gameFull description from Steam+
About This Game This is a roguelike centered around coin-tossing. Navigate a chaotic tavern to collect a variety of unique coins, combine them into synergistic builds, and unleash devastating Combos. Build Your Deck Strategically Manage, add, or remove coins to craft your personalized coin deck—optimize every choice to maximize your in-game gains. Diversify Your Builds The game features coin types tied to multiple factions: Humans, Monsters, Beasts, and Machines—each with its own distinct mechanics. Cross-faction combinations unlock a wealth of creative, powerful playstyles. Synergize with Relics At each stage, choose from a range of effect-driven relics. Pair their abilities with your coin builds to forge diverse, dynamic playstyles.
