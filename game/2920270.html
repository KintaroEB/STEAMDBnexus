About this gameFull description from Steam+
EXPERIENCE INTRIGUE AND STRATEGY - Play as a Spacecrew: Achieve victory by completing all tasks or smartly identifying and voting out the impostors. - Play as an Impostor: Execute covert eliminations and deploy strategic sabotages to create discord among the Spacecrew. - Play as a Neutral: Each neutral role comes with unique objectives. Excel in these roles to claim your victory. - Teamwork is Key: Work closely with teammates to complete critical tasks and ensure the safety of your spaceship. Will you maintain trust, or will betrayal lead to chaos? PARTY FOR FUN - Duo and Squad Modes: Perfect for playing alongside friends and family. Team up, strategize, deduce, and enjoy the game together. - Private Rooms with Custom Rules: Take control by setting your own game rules and inviting friends and family to join. DIVERSE MODES: - Colosseum: Fight to be the last one standing in this ultimate survival challenge. - Hide & Seek: Complete tasks and dodge the hunters in this thrilling evasion showdown. - Lover Mode：Stay alive with your lover and navigate challenges together to secure victory. - More: More modes are waiting for you to explore. CUSTOMIZE YOUR GAMEPLAY: - Upgrade Your Role: Enhance your roles with exclusive Emotes, Action Effects, Fashion outfits, and more. Stand out with your unique style! Ready for a journey filled with trust, deception, and tactical gameplay? Download Super Sus now and step into the vastness of space where every match is a new mystery!
