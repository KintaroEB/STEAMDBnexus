About this gameFull description from Steam+
Imagine the golden age of flash games. Now imagine playing them with a VR headset. BRUTALISTICK VR aims to make that happen with copious amounts of blood, gore, destruction and weapon variety. Realism does not define this game, the rule of cool does. However, that doesn't mean that realism has no place here. Weapons have weight and must be wielded with the intent to kill if you don't want to meet your demise. Firearms require precise aim to fit the bullet between enemy armor plates... Or just buy a weapon with a caliber that will go through anyone and anything that dares stand in its way, the choice is yours. Everything is simulated physically, throwing your enemies through windows and off roofs, launching a stake through their torso and pinning them against the wall, slicing their body in half with a blade that's more of a large hunk of iron than a sword, and even climbing and using grappling hooks. Dismemberment is not limited to limbs or even joints, splitting someone's head in half vertically is not a question of if but rather when. Earn rewards to buy new gear or upgrade your stats and skills Each mission earns you a reward that can then be used to buy new weapons. Or you can upgrade your stats and skills, from simply increasing your health or rewards to lifesteal and the power of your punches. Coat entire levels with the blood of your enemies Blood never disappears so you can marvel at the carnage you've created. Unlock new vendors with an entirely different stock Each vendor has different types of weapons to complement their personality and the levels that they're unlocked from. From farming tools to experimental military armaments, everything is at your fingertips.
