About this gameFull description from Steam+
Isometric insurgent simulator. Fight on foot and in vehicles, build and steal a private arsenal and bring the war home to the businessmen who started it all in this continuation of the Brigador saga. You are a member of Team Anchor, deployed to the SNC homeworld to make them pay for what they did. Get around Mar Nosso on foot, or go loud against local forces with your own armor. Acquire and fire 30+ firearms, from Nossian Corvid zipguns to the latest in man-portable Spacer tech. Upgrade your arsenal by finding different combat rigs. Salvage vehicles for parts to fabricate new tools of destruction in your base. Mount weapons to your new creations and deploy with them. Drive around rich detailed sprite environments in vehicles like clapped-out sedans, tanks or bipedal mechs. Listen to an original soundtrack scored by acclaimed musician Makeup And Vanity Set. Disrupt the corporate control of Mar Nosso by killing Brigadors. Brigador Killers is in active development and coming to a Steam store near you.
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

Players can cause destruction across numerous levels both on foot and in a vehicle. Combat is with both hostile NPCs and vehicles, though both hostile and non-hostile human-scale characters can be attacked and subsequently gibbed by the player, leaving blood spats. In-game text entries may not be fit for those under the age of 16, as some contain descriptions of violent acts, crude humor and horror.
