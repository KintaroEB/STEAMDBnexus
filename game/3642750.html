About this gameFull description from Steam+
Entropia Universe is a unique massively multiplayer online game (MMO) where virtual assets hold real-world value and players can participate in a thriving virtual sandbox economy. The platform has been live since 2003 and continues to push the boundaries of virtual economy design. Set in a persistent, single-shard universe, Entropia Universe offers a deep and evolving sci-fi world spanning multiple planets and vast space environments. Unlike traditional class-based MMOs, players progress through infinite skill development, with the freedom to define their own roles and paths. Players can invest in in-game companies, own land areas, and even acquire shares that pay real dividends, connecting gameplay with genuine economic opportunity. Given its "no-class" system, and real cash economy, Entropia Universe has many professions that players can specialize and participate in such as: Hunting - Hunt creatures, fight bosses, complete dungeons Mining - survey the land and extract resources used in crafting Crafting - Craft items in demand Trading - Buy low, sell high. Become a skilled entrepreneur Taming Creatures - Tame sought-after creatures around the universe and sell them for profit Beauty Services - Skill up and offer avatar modification services Shop Owning - Buy and run your very own shop in a persistent world Land Area Owning - Own a huge slice of virtual land to generate revenue from taxes Investing - Buy and sell shares on the Entropia Exchange PvP - Take on other players in lootable PvP In April 2026, Entropia Universe launched the highly anticipated Fishing and Cooking systems! Discover over 100 different kinds of fish, mix, match and update your rods and attachments - and turn your catch into highly desirable consumable food to buff your avatar. Every body of water in the universe can be fished in, be it Casting, Fly Fishing, Angling or even Deep Sea Fishing from the back of a boat. It's up to you to find the best combination of gear for the fish you want to catch. This update brings a whole new dimension to the in-game economy, offering colonists new careers and specialisations as avid fishermen or gourmet chefs. Entropia Universe has just launched its brand new tutorial and starting experience for the game. This new starter zone: Setesh, provides new colonists with a better start in the universe than ever possible before - so don't miss the opportunity to try Entropia today and be a part of a new Golden Age!
