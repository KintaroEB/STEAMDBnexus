About this gameFull description from Steam+
You are not the hero. You’re in charge. In Acceptable Losses DEMO, you're the officer behind the lines. Make hard calls, assign missions, and push paperwork while your men bleed for inches of mud. Each day brings new orders. Assign squads to patrols, trench repairs, or suicidal assaults. At night, manage the incoming paperwork. Read incident reports, check medical updates, scan intel briefings, and hope for reinforcements. Balance your duty to HQ with the lives of the men under your command. After ten days in the trenches, you will be sent back to HQ for the final review. If you make it that long... 20+ Missions including both mundane and suicidal 15+ Modifiers ranging from morale and fatigue to weather and enemy defense state 40+ Mid-Mission Events impacting mission modifiers and outcomes 15+ Night Events modifying your squads, trench condition, supplies, and more Randomly generated soldiers with millions of combinations including first and last name, birth place and date Permadeath of soldiers within your squads Join your squads on missions for a boost but not without immunity to wounds HQ Evaluations show you how well, or poorly, you are doing
