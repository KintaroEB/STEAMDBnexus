About this gameFull description from Steam+
Play solo or up to 4 players online. Open your own Bakery café and grow your business by serving your customers the delicious dishes you make. Build and furnish your café according to your desires. Place tables for customers, decorate and organize your kitchen to be productive. Manage your orders for raw materials so you always have something to offer your customers. Enjoy a varied and demanding culinary experience. Be careful not to keep customers waiting, they could become unhappy.
