About this gameFull description from Steam+
Hero Zero is one of the most successful free-to-play online multiplayer RPG games, allowing players to create their own superhero character in a funny, exciting, and fascinating universe. With hundreds of villains, criminals, and bosses to fight, players must use their wits and strength to bring justice and peace back to the world. Unlock hilariously powerful gadgets and upgrades to equip your hero for epic battles and challenging single-player missions. Build your own hideout as a secret base, placed under your house in Humpreydale. Upgrade and modify your shelter to get better rewards and increase efficiency, and compete with other players to find out who can build the best superhero hideout. Join or create a team with your friends and build your own headquarters to fight more efficiently against all evil villains, criminals, and bosses. Compete with other players and teams in exciting multiplayer fights and climb the leaderboard on your mission to become the most successful superhero in the world. Experience a whole new dimension to fighting crime with our season feature! Every month, progress through a new season pass that unlocks exclusive armour, weapons, and sidekicks themed around Season Arcs. These narrative and thematic elements tie together three seasons lasting a three-month period, creating a new layer of depth and strategy in Hero Zero. PvE FEATURE: For the toughest of heroes, 'Hard Mode' has arrived in Hero Zero! With this PvE feature, users who have reached the upper ranks of heroism can replay special missions on a tougher mode. Track your progress against other players with our leaderboard, and earn great rewards for defeating the biggest and baddest enemies. - Over 31 million players worldwide! - Regular free updates and new content to keep the game fresh - Free to play - no cost to join the fun! - Create and customize your own hero to suit your playstyle - Build up your own superhero team to take on any challenge! - Challenge other heroes in PvP and team battles for bragging rights - Funny and fascinating storyline to keep you engaged - Easy to learn gameplay for players of all skill levels - Appealing graphics to bring the world to life - Real time villain events with thousands of players for an epic experience Start your epic and hilarious adventure now - for free!
