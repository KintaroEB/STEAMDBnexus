About this gameFull description from Steam+
- Dive into 12 complete story chapters - Choose from 3 difficulty levels, with a minimum of 30 hours experience of main storyline. - Master 4 distinct weapon systems: spears, bows and arrows, swords, and steeds, supplemented by equipment systems like crafting, sets, refinement, and enchantment. - Explore no fewer than 30 expansive maps. - Encounter dozens of well-known Chinese historical figures, who will either fight by your side or become your adversaries. - Enjoy a wide range of visually striking outfits for the protagonist and companions. - Engage in several large-scale, renowned battles that truly capture the essence of the Three Kingdoms atmosphere.
