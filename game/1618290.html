About this gameFull description from Steam+
Train operation is performed by tilting the handle called a master controller back and forth. Push it toward you to accelerate, and push it back to brake. Basically, you can proceed with the game only with this movement. In addition, you can perform various operations such as horns, buzzer to contact the conductor, and switching viewpoints. You can also check your driving rating after driving. The conductor opens and closes doors, makes in-train announcements, and conducts safety checks. The Tatehama Electric Railway main line connects Otebashi Station to Tatehama Station. At present, the line can operate up and down 28 km of this line from Akayamacho Station to Tatehama Station. This game is OK for live video and distribution. It's okay to monetize your videos and distribution.
