About this gameFull description from Steam+
Wedding Witch is a rogue-like game that involves exploring randomly generated maps, fighting against hordes of devils, and finding the Succubus's potions. The Succubus's potions are hidden somewhere in a dark forest filled with devils. Solve various tasks in order to find the potions! Drinking the Succubus's potions will make the witch's body transform. What kind of witch do you desire? The witch gains access to new spells whenever she drinks the Succubus's potions. Create your own spell combinations! Magical flowers containing mysterious energies bloom in the labyrinthine forest. Add them to your bouquet and gain new powers! Find the Succubus's potions, escape the forest, and earn your happy ending!
