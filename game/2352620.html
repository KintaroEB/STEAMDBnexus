About this gameFull description from Steam+
FELLOWSHIP is a multiplayer online dungeon adventure set in an exciting fantasy setting, with endlessly scaling dungeon runs. Players must focus on working together as a team, with an emphasis on skill, cooperation, and strategy to defeat bosses, collect loot, and work together to overcome ever-present challenges. Gather Your Perfect Group Jump in through matchmaking or team up with friends to take on challenging Dungeons. Choose from a growing roster of Heroes spanning Tank, DPS, and Healer roles, and freely swap between them to tackle each Dungeon your way. Cooperation and strategy are key as you face enemies with unique, complex mechanics. Every run rewards you with personal loot and account-wide resources, ensuring each victory pushes you toward greater power. As FELLOWSHIP grows, new Heroes encompassing classic archetypes and bold new designs will expand the roster so every player can find their perfect playstyle. Progress Through the Leagues Unlock new Abilities, Talents, and powerful gear as you climb through Contender, Adept, Champion and Paragon Leagues. Core League Dungeons feature a single Boss and take 10–15 minutes, perfect for fast, focused runs while Capstone Dungeons challenge you with three Bosses and must be conquered before advancing to the next League. Each League introduces fresh twists with new enemy abilities, added Curses, and tougher encounters. The greater the challenge, the greater the reward: higher-level gear, Legendaries, Set items, Gems, and account-wide upgrades that fuel the progression of all your Heroes. Take on the Eternal Challenge For players seeking the ultimate test, Eternal offers endlessly scaling Dungeons where strategy, teamwork, and mastery decide how far you can push. Compete on FELLOWSHIP’s in-game Leaderboards and prove your place among our greatest players. Content-Driven Seasonal Resets Every Season brings major content drops with new Heroes, Dungeons, features, and gameplay. Seasonal resets place everyone on a level playing field; perfect for jumping back in with friends. All cosmetics, mounts, and account flair you've earned across previous Seasons stay with you permanently, letting you showcase your accomplishments throughout the lifetime of FELLOWSHIP.
