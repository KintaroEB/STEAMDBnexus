About this gameFull description from Steam+
Cozy idle cafe management that keeps you company while you work. Grow fresh ingredients, cook, and serve delicious food to adorable animal customers. Build your dream cafe that sits at the bottom of your screen. Grow Fresh Ingredients Plant and harvest crops from your little farm to create the freshest meals. Build your dream cafe Design, decorate, and customize your perfect cafe to match your unique style. Cook & Serve Turn fresh ingredients into delicious meals and earn money to buy decoration or upgrade your cafe. Playing while working Your friendly staff will take care of the cafe, keeping things running smoothly while you work, study, or relax. Twitch & Streaming integration Connect to your stream and turn your stream into cozy cafe where chat join cafe and hangout as customers. Unlock and upgrade more than 300 recipes Cook and serve delicious drinks and dishes. Connect with your friends online with Postal Service Send gifts to your friends. Visit your best friends' cafe.
