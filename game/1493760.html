About this gameFull description from Steam+
Explore NewTube City to discover new trends and build new relationships with different characters to take your channel to new heights every day! Every trend is a story to discover in NewTube City! Explore what's hot all over 3 different neighborhoods among special challenges, seasonal events, famous characters, and many more!
