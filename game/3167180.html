About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Hello Love: 18 Again" is a live-action movie interactive game. In a sudden accident, you traveled back to 18 years old. You still had energy that year; There was still light in your eyes that year; You still had dreams in your heart that year; The most important ... with them: The famous campus goddess "Shen Mengxuan" - played by Sun Qianyu (Sun Xiaoyan), a former BEJ48 girl group member. The powerful cheerleader "Su Xiaoxiao" - played by Tian Bu La, the cheerleader of CBA professional cheerleading team. The mature and intellectual gentle teacher "Lu Yueru". Childhood sweetheart, the girl next door "Chu Yao". The domineering and aloof rich CEO "Xu Jiajia". The first youth funny love interactive movie game "Real and delicate emotional growth", "relaxing and funny Asian sex comedy", "refreshing feeling of fantasy time travel", this is the game experience we hope to give you in our work. Rich gameplay and diverse endings Duration exceeds 500 minutes, more than 20 different endings, and each heroine has more than just intimate contacts and warm confessions of love. There are multiple displays of married life, and the final outcome depends on your choice. The classic movie game options, clue collection, and QTE are packaged in different ways to bring you different fresh experiences. Diverse roles that fit the personality There are five good-looking heroines with different personalities and appearance, and there will always be one that satisfies your "first love memories". There are also a variety of hidden achievements, unlocking wonderful beauty photos, limited public videos, easter egg story lines... Waiting for your exploration. "Later I finally realized that it was not my flower, I just happened to pass by its bloom." "First love" is a precious memory hidden deep in everyone's heart. The first love in school days and the longing and fantasy in youth are pure and passionate. Those "flowers" that are not obtained and regretted when missed, bloom vigorously and wither in the years. Occasionally comes to mind in the silent night. The original intention of creating "Hello Love: 18 Again" was to present these precious memories to players in another form that they miss but have to forget because of time, distance and reality. Indelible Studio is composed of 4 veterans with more than ten years of experience in the game industry. The project was established at the end of 2023. We started very early but walked slowly. This year, the biggest challenge comes from two aspects. Firstly, to resist the impetuous market and concentrate on creating a pure and emotionally resonant movie game - we have experienced the classic era of gal game, and we hope that the work will be new and innovative. The form satisfies both "refreshment" and "resonance" within a limited scale. Secondly, the cross-field of film and television majors, there is a big gap between live-action shooting and game animation production - fortunately, we found like-minded people Film and television production partners, they have created "level-T1" film and game works, and have added a film level production team to the project. As the first part of the "Love Series Trilogy" (First Love, Passionate Love, Broken Love), "Hello!" may be young and imperfect, but we will work hard to explore more possibilities for the future of interactive movie games - compared with those "masterpieces" of the classic era, the potential of interactive movie games has not yet been fully explored, and we are looking forward to it. Every player’s attention, support, criticism and advice. "As long as you live, sorrow will accumulate." But what we want to do is record the things that make people warm.
