About this gameFull description from Steam+
The future of flight simulation is now. X-Plane 11 is the detailed, realistic, and modern simulator you’ve been waiting for. New in X-Plane 11 A completely redesigned, intuitive user interface that makes setting up and editing your flight a breeze. Consistently usable 3-D cockpits and stunningly high-resolution exterior models for all included aircraft. A new effects engine for lighting, sounds, and explosions. Realistic avionics: all planes are IFR-ready right out of the gate. Busy, “living” airports with pushback tugs and roaming fuel trucks, able to service both your aircraft and the simulator’s AI planes. New buildings & roads to better simulate European cities …and more!
