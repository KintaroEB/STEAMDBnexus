About this gameFull description from Steam+
CosmicBreak Universal is an MMO action gamein which players could customize, control, and battle with their very own robots. First released in Japan in 2008 as CosmicBreak, the game has been newly released with improved features and character visuals, significant adjustments to the game balance, and multilingual support (Japanese, English, and traditional Chinese). Grand 30 VS 30 Battles! Enjoy high-speed and exhilarating battles in PvP where a large numbers of people are matched together. Organize your characters according to a wide variety of battlefields and enemies, and aim for victory together with your allies! Freely Customize Your Characters! Combine thousands of different parts and accessories to create your own original character! Players can also take advantage of the skin and color editing features to make characters that are truly unique. Additionally, players can enjoy various game modes such as CHAOS battles, where players cooperate with each other to fight bosses, Missions, and Quests, where they can experience the story of the world's complex characters!
