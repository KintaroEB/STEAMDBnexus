About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Teddy’s Haven. Welcome to a cozy, magical shop simulator set on the enchanting island of Ursa. Run your store your way and sell what you harvest, craft, or discover. And enjoy the freedom to decorate freely across your shop, home, and the wilds beyond. Level up naturally as you play, grow magical crops, and take part in daily events that bring the island to life. With over 250 decorations, 400 items to sell, and no pressure, every day is yours to enjoy at your own pace. Ursa, A World of Calm and Wonder Ursa is a gentle land filled with peace, memories, and mythical spirits. Your shop is a safe haven of supplies for travelers and magical beings alike. The land is rich and vibrant. You can harvest resources, care for its creatures, and meet friendly creatures along the way. In Ursa there is no conflict, only warmth and calm. This is your relaxing world to make your own. Key Features: Manage Your Magical Shop – Create the cozy shop you’ve always wanted. With changing seasons, daily surprises, and rewarding leveling systems, your shop grows alongside you. No rush, no stress, just a magical rhythm all your own. Decorate Across Ursa – Over 250 decorations to decorate freely across your shop, home, and the island’s landscapes. With near-limitless placement freedom, express your style and bring every corner of Ursa to life. A Wide Variety of Goods to Keep Your Shelves Full – Stock your shop with over 400 items gathered, grown, or traded across Ursa. Skill Systems – Your skills grow naturally as you run your shop. Stock shelves faster, harvest more berries, and see your abilities improve with each task. Leveling up rewards you for doing what you love, at your own pace. Crafting – Enjoy simple, stress-free crafting that doesn’t keep you waiting. Place your items and return the next day to find something special created. Always growing, crafting will expand with new features and more complex options in future updates. Farming – Farming has arrived in the land of Ursa! Unlock new contracts that allow you to plant and nurture magical crops, each with unique Aether-infused variations. Grow, harvest, and fill your shop with wondrous goods born from the earth and touched by magic. Unique and Magical Employees – Hire magical stockers and cashiers who keep your shop running smoothly. Whether you’re decorating, relaxing, or out exploring the world, they’ll reliably care for your haven as if it were their own. Simplified Inventory Management – Too busy to sort through a warehouse of items? Open your “bag of everything” and stock shelves directly, hassle-free. Spend less time managing and more time enjoying your shop. Exploration – Wander the ever-expanding world of Ursa at your own pace. Harvest berries, care for animals, or simply enjoy the beautiful scenery and discover the magic hidden in every corner. Daily Events – Your shop is a hub of life and wonder. As the days pass, creatures will stop by with stories, gifts, or even magical blessings for your shop. Everyone you meet is here to help you grow and enjoy your cozy world. Bounds Breaking Joy: Teddy’s Haven celebrates curiosity. And sometimes that means climbing too high, falling too far, or finding yourself under the map. Go ahead: break the bounds. Secret items and small surprises await those who wander a little too far from the intended path. It’s all part of the adventure. Auto Saving – Take the game at your own pace, no more requirements of ending your day for your game to be saved. Ongoing Development: Teddy’s Haven is in Early Access, with plans for new locations, minigames, world events, and more, the journey has only just begun. Created In Loving Dedication To The Memory Of My Best Friend Teddy Bear.
