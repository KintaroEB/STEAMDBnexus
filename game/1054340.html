About this gameFull description from Steam+
Logic World is a first-person sandbox simulation game that's all about digital logic. This is your playground to explore the emergent complexity of binary circuits. Design, build and operate incredible logic machines. Create math circuitry, data storage, displays, control panels, minigames, word processors, communication networks, or anything else you can dream up. You can even invent your own computer! As you play, you'll learn about how different circuits are designed and how you can use them together to perform complex operations. Grow your skills with each completed project. BUILDING Logic World has many advanced building mechanics to help you quickly move a design from your brain into the game world. MULTIPLAYER Logic World is fully multiplayer! Build in a shared world with others, create machines together, and give your friends a tour of your latest invention. CREATION HUB Browse a massive library of user-built creations. Explore others' worlds or use their modules in your own builds. You can easily share your projects on the hub for others to play with, remix, rate and comment on. FEATURES Digital Logic - Logic World is a simulation of digital circuits, running on the same principles as real world computer chips. High Performance - Assemble enormous circuits and simulate them at thousands of updates per second, with no lag! Building - Create at the speed of thought with advanced 3D building mechanics, all from a first person perspective. Multiplayer - Build circuits yourself or collaborate with friends. Demo World - Explore an open-ended introduction to digital logic, with tutorials, challenges, and more. Online Creation Hub - Share your designs online with the click of a button. Explore and expand on the designs of others with the click of a different button! Moddable - We ❤️ modding. Install community-made mods to expand and customize your experience, or even make your own mods! DRM-Free - We believe players should have freedom over their games, which is why Logic World has no copy protection whatsoever. All online features are opt-in, and we've taken great care to make sure multiplayer works without reliance on a central server. Original Soundtrack - Play with circuits against a backdrop of beautiful original music to inspire you.
