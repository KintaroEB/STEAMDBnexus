About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to your new potion shop! As the new owner of the town’s abandoned potion shop, your goal is to build a thriving magical shop from the ground up. Alchemist's Garden is a cozy, incremental crafting game focused on relaxing progression, experimentation, and discovery. Explore diverse locations ranging from lush gardens to frozen peaks and mystical voids. Click to clear obstacles and uncover magical ingredients, recipe scrolls, and unique shop decorations. Take your foraged ingredients to the Crucible to actively experiment and discover new recipes. Once you have unlocked a recipe, use your Grimoire to continually craft potions and enchanted items to stock your shop. Start from humble beginnings and grow your shop. Put your crafts on display and watch your shop flourish. Expand, Upgrade, and Decorate your shop to attract even more customers! Skill Tree: Spend Essence to upgrade your shop and tools Decorate: Customize your space with furniture and decorations Prestige System: Reset your shop's progress to unlock permanent talent abilities that stay with you across playthroughs Relaxing, incremental progression built around foraging, alchemy, and crafting 10 unique locations to explore Experiment with 40 magical ingredients to discover over 64 unique recipes A talent tree and prestige system with over 70+ unique effects and abilities 80+ decorations to collect and furnish your alchemy shop A sweet and low-stress experience that you can play at your own pace
