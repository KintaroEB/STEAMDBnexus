About this gameFull description from Steam+
Unleash your virtual potential with Warudo—the flexible and personalized 3D VTubing software you have always wanted. 100% free for personal use.^ Webcam/iPhone ARKit face tracking and hand tracking included. Full body tracking with mocap suits, VR trackers or any VMC-compatible software. Compatible with VRM, VRChat, or any humanoid-rigged models. Non-VRM models require an one-time export in Unity (check out our handbook!). Fully customizable expressions. Add your own 3D props, environments, particles, etc. Native integration with livestreaming platforms, e.g. Twitch, YouTube, Bilibili. Let your audience interact with your character: throw items, spawn stickers & particles, launch water beams, etc. Blueprint system that lets you customize anything—from tracking to interactions. Control your blueprints with MIDI, Stream Deck, or even WebSocket messages. C# SDK for developers to add features that you desire! ...I don't want to make this a super long list. Explore the rest of our features on your own! Please note that models used in the videos & images are NOT necessarily included in the release. ^ We define "personal use" as: the copyrights of your VTubing persona are exclusively yours, and you possess ownership of your accounts on social/livestreaming platforms; and there are no contractual obligations requiring you to stream for X hours per week, unless such a contract is directly in agreement with a major livestreaming platform (e.g., Twitch, YouTube). If you would like to use Warudo commercially, please contact us at info@warudo.app.
