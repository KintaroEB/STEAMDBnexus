About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into 'Sarah's Adventure: Time Travel', an enchanting game that sends players on a captivating journey through space and time. This game unearths the essence of adventure by combining elements of exploration and puzzle-solving. As players navigate through various locations around the globe, they are tasked with clearing obstacles and unraveling challenges, truly engaging the player's wit and strategy. The game’s appeal lies in its interactive gameplay mechanics, where your choices dictate the progression of the story. Dive into a meticulously crafted world, build and manage resources effectively, and restore historical artifacts to their former glory. 'Sarah's Adventure' is not just a game; it's a chronicle of thrilling escapades and the celebration of human curiosity, designed to intrigue, delight, and immerse audiences in its narrative richness. Immerse yourself in the engaging tale of Sarah, a young professional from a world-class museum, who finds herself suddenly transported to the nineteenth century. Can you survive the sands of time and help Sarah find her way back?
