About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Zone - a rebirth in the best traditions In Renovation the timeless atmosphere of the Anomaly Zone meets updated rules. We've taken the best from the previous Zone and reworked the mechanics to suit the needs of players, making the Zone even more vibrant and unpredictable. A new interface system, redesigned anomalies, and a revamped economy - exploring dangerous territories has now become truly exciting. If you once craved the classic Zone, it's back - only more dangerous, challenging, and engaging. The Zone - new rules In Renovation all has undergone a major rebalancing: weapon, armor, backpack stats, artifact prices, mutant parts and repair costs have been revised. Equipment timers have been removed, magazines no longer break but are reloaded manually, and all equipment and attachments are no longer character-specific - but they also no longer drop upon respawn or finishing blow. A new reputation system with merchants opens access to exclusive goods, and crafting and item restoration have become deeper and more flexible - success now depends on your level. We're already preparing future updates that will expand the possibilities of PvE, PvP, and tech trees - the Zone will change along with the players. The Zone is about discovery If you're just discovering this world, the Zone is a deadly territory, teeming with anomalies, mutants, and valuable artifacts. Anomalies react to the approach of a player or a thrown stone, forcing you to be on guard. The Mysterious Caravan, in addition to rich fields of artifacts and loot, also spawns a new unique artifact, and guides and exits have been redesigned to make travel more engaging. Each artifact has a freshness level and a chance to be "awaken" - a new mechanic that allows you to transform a find into a stronger version with long-term effects. The Zone is about people and opportunities Stalkers now progress along three branches: PvE (crafting materials, chemicals, artifacts), PvP (using weapons and equipment), and Technician (equipment and repairs). By joining the community, you can craft, upgrade, trade equipment, and share experience with other players. Every day factions issue new quests, reputation unlocks new opportunities, and artifact containers preserve loot and prevent it from spoiling. Assemble a team or go alone - stalker friendship and mutual support will help you survive the most dangerous situations. The Zone is about battles Battle-hardened stalkers can master the next stage of the game: arenas, PvP battles, special activities, and unique events. The removal of equipment restrictions and a new economy create fair conditions for battles, while awakened artifacts and rare catalysts offer a chance to surprise your opponents. In Renovation we're laying the foundation for even more activities that will appear as updates progress - the Zone will continue to live and evolve, and it's the players who will help it take on a new identity.
