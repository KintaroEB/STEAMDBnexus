About this gameFull description from Steam+
Join Our Official Discord! (You can access Discord link from the panel on the right.) In the dusty corner of memory, the toy room stirs once more. Awaken forgotten childhood toys and fuse them within the small table to craft your own kaboom legion — from ground collisions to aerial strikes and magical blasts. Through each tabletop battle, fragments of your long-lost childhood return… Arrange, Combine, and Evolve Master the art of toy arrangement on your tabletop! Every toy slot is a strategic decision. Plan your layout, combine rare units, and arrange them wisely to unlock your build’s full potential! Build Your Playstyle Craft your own tactical system from over 400 strategic toys! 100+ summons to clash in tabletop battles 50+ traps and class abilities to outsmart your opponents and shift momentum 250+ magic items and weapons to power up your strategy and unleash devastating combos Collisions & Knockback Summon toys into head-to-head battles where it’s not just about stats—brave charges and knockback strikes can send enemies flying, turning the tide in unpredictable ways. Set Traps Set traps across the table to break enemy momentum and open up opportunities to strike back. Engage in asynchronous PvP where your legion clashes with global builds in tactical showdowns. Analyze, adapt, and optimize your strategy in mirror matches and rise through the ranks. Pick Your Toy Captain Each hero brings a unique toy arsenal and exclusive class abilities. Doll Chef: A kitchen defense expert who controls space and fortifies positions, perfect for defensive-minded players. Dr. Hamster: Commander of Hamster Legion that leads a swarm of tiny units to overwhelm enemies with relentless numbers. Puppet Musician: A rhythm master who speeds up allies and disrupts foes, setting the tempo of every battle. Naughty Andy: Summoner of nightmares — ghosts, skeletons, reapers, and dark curses! Frost Hunter: Rule the frozen land, command wild beasts, and unleash the power of water magic! Turn Your Friends into Toys Transform your Steam friends into fully armed "Game Buddy", customize them with all kinds of weapons and attributes, then rally them into your toy army to charge into battle for friendship! Unveil Toy Memories Unlock achievements, hero skins, and the toys’ hidden memories through a pop-up storybook. Within its dusty pages, the long-lost tales of Fred and his childhood friends quietly come back to life.
