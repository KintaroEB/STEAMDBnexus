About this gameFull description from Steam+
The MINDWAVE Demo contains the first ~25 minutes of the story, along with Endless Mode. Cerebral Microgame Frenzy Play through collections of rapid-fire microgames formed from the minds of multiple colorful opponents in this cerebral story-driven take on games like WarioWare. MAKE YOUR WAY TO THE TOP OF MINDSCAPE TOWER. DELVE INTO THE MINDS OF YOUR OPPONENTS. BE CONSUMED BY THE TRUTH THEY SPEAK TO YOU, IN THIS CEREBRAL BATTLE OF SIGHT AND SOUND HIDDEN WITHIN EACH OF US. Climb the Tower Blackout City— home to Mindscape Co., creators of the world's most played cognitive reality game: MINDWAVE. A game where players link their brains together and go head-to-head in an onslaught of feelings and memories. The time has come for Mindscape Co.'s annual MINDWAVE Tournament, hosted at Mindscape Tower. A young girl named Pandora finds herself with a Platinum Ticket, granting her entry. The only thing standing between her and the large cash prize is several rounds of nonstop mental mayhem. Out of Your Shell In between rounds of the tournament, you can chat with other contestants. Get to know your opponents, analyze the competition, and maybe make a few friends. Mind Over Matter In Endless Mode, you can rematch the minds you've endured in Story Mode. Survive for as long as you can while the speed, difficulty, and weirdness get cranked up. Endless Mode also features leaderboards so you can compare high scores with your buddies and enemies.
