About this gameFull description from Steam+
Pogostuck: Rage With Your Friends is a short and sweet yet very challenging game about climbing a mountain on a pogo stick. Tilt the character, jump, use momentum and 360° powerboosts to reach unimaginable heights. Jump along with friends and strangers from around the globe via automatic matchmaking for up to 16 players. Customize your character with unlockable gear and compete in leaderboards or live rankings - if you want to! Inspired by Getting Over It with Bennett Foddy.
