About this gameFull description from Steam+
Experience one of the most exciting and versatile professions up close. In Emergency Call – The Firefighting Simulation 3, you are part of a German professional fire department and bear responsibility for the safety of your city. When an emergency call comes in at the dispatch center, every firefighter knows their role. Which position you take on is entirely up to you. During your 24-hour shifts, you make purposeful use of the time between calls to prepare for a wide variety of challenges. A daily rotating duty roster gives you the opportunity to take part in training courses, drills, and physical fitness sessions. In doing so, you earn the qualifications needed to be deployed in different roles – from attack squad member to engineer across a range of emergency vehicles. In the training courses, you are taught all the necessary knowledge in both theory and practice to take on new responsibilities on the job. Drills with equipment such as the Halligan tool help you optimize procedures and save valuable time during operations. Working with a thermal imaging camera is also a skill that needs to be practiced. Daily physical training increases your stamina under stress, which makes a real difference out in the field. Strength, endurance, and recovery capacity can all diminish over time if neglected. Your endurance, in particular can be a decisive factor in saving valuable time during an incident. Beyond that, there are many routine tasks at the station that form an important part of daily fire house life – and they all need to get done. Once the alarm sounds, a large operational area with diverse urban districts awaits you. From classic firefighting operations to traffic accidents, you'll face numerous incidents spanning rescue, firefighting, recovery, and hazard protection. A dynamic day-night cycle and changing weather conditions add further challenges and variety. Features Join the ranks of a professional fire department Incidents across four operational areas: Rescue, Firefighting, Recovery, and Protection 24-hour duty shifts with a rotating roster Developed in cooperation with the Berufsfeuerwehr Mülheim an der Ruhr Large open operational area with diverse and varied incidents Dynamic day-night cycle with changing weather conditions Complete training courses and qualify for additional roles in the field Daily drills help save valuable time during operations Train regularly to increase your resilience on the job Keep track of your progress and development at all times There's always something to do at the station between calls
