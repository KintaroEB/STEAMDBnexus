About this gameFull description from Steam+
You signed up for a delivery job in hell. Finish the orders before sunrise - or the underworld will claim you forever. Team up with up to 4 players to deliver & experience the grind and hustle of being a food shipper - one of the most familiar jobs in Vietnam and Southeast Asia. Dodge traps, outsmart monsters, and uncover hidden shortcuts to finish your deliveries before sunrise. No two nights are ever the same - weather, ghosts, and events are procedurally generated for endless replayability. Earn money to unlock new gear, vehicles, and special delivery abilities that stack each night - giving you a fighting chance to survive longer.
