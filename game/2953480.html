About this gameFull description from Steam+
Exploration Explore atmospheric, detailed locations and enjoy realistic graphics with a carefully crafted environment. Overcome obstacles, climb over barriers, crawl through tight spaces, and scale heights - only the most determined will find their way forward. Puzzles You will encounter various puzzles along your path, solving which will be the key to understanding the true nature of these events. Atmosphere Step into the role of a fragile girl and feel the silence of the dark corridors. Explore the shadowy locations hidden around every corner of this once-familiar place. Story You open your eyes - it's a familiar ceiling, but something feels off. The silence is too deep. The place that was your home since childhood now feels empty, lifeless... almost unfamiliar. Where is everyone? Why did they leave you behind? To find the answers, you’ll have to move forward - through the unsettling darkness, through abandoned hallways hiding unknown dangers. But you won’t give up. Because you have a purpose. And if the world has truly fallen… then someone has to save it. Combat A unique close-combat system has been designed to make every encounter intense and dangerous. Even the simplest zombies are now formidable threats. With only a kitchen knife as your main weapon, combat takes place at a dangerously close distance. Experience a classic combat system where every move matters.
