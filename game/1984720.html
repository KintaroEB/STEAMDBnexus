About this gameFull description from Steam+
A stand-alone turn-based war chess game with the theme of Chinese history. The game is a paid download mode. After paying, you will get all historical plots, generals dialogue, dubbing and other functions, and there is no recharge entry in the game. The core gameplay is territorial competition, and diplomacy, construction, combat and other gameplay will also revolve around territorial competition.
