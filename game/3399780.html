About this gameFull description from Steam+
The living beings are sowing chaos, and so are the deceased! A malevolent force, dormant for millennia, now seeks to emerge from the shadows and unleash turmoil upon the mortal realm ravaged by warlords. As a Taoist, you are tasked with leaving your sect to face your destined judgment. You will journey across diverse lands, vanquish monsters, and seek the truth. The distinction between what is right and wrong becomes blurred, with love and hatred intertwined. When the chaos subsides, will you stay resolute in your beliefs and uphold the path of justice? Dive into the newest release of the Hexbreaker series! Step into the epic life of a Taoist! Face formidable foes, perform rituals, wield your bell and recite incantations, and craft talismans with various effects like warding off evil spirits, summoning thunderbolts from heavens, or even commanding corpses to battle on your side. The underworld mirrors the chaos of the mortal realm. Buried beneath the earth are invaluable treasures, but also deadly revenants and traps! Looking to hit the jackpot? Be sure to gear up with the right tools to stay safe from all deadly threats! Feeling overwhelmed by enemies? Rally your allies! Hex Breaker II offers online cooperation for teams of up to 4 players. Each member plays a distinct role: shielding comrades with barriers, unleashing flames to vanquish evil, or deactivating deadly traps. Together, you'll conquer the imminent perils. Stay wary of the paper effigies in desolate tombs and the specters haunting wrecked vessels! Prepare your Taoist skills and talismans for an unparalleled clash between mortals and wraiths. Carve out a path to survival amidst the unending assaults of malevolent entities.
