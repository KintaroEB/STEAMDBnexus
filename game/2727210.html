About this gameFull description from Steam+
In Deathless, you’ll play as four different heroes, each with their own playstyle. VARVARA THE FAIR A cunning and clever treasure hunter who excels at manipulating enemy positions on the battlefield. VASILISA THE WISE A powerful elemental sorceress who can summon and dismiss Nursemaids at will to assist her in combat. DOBRYNYA NIKITICH The famous hero, equipped with high health and solid defense, making him a master of defensive combat. ALYOSHA A warrior from Kitezh. He always carries a sword forged from unique star-steel and a spellbane, a mysterious enchanted wand. Hundreds of card combinations alongside relics provide endless opportunities for creativity — find the most powerful combos to defeat all the bosses and save Belosvet. Complete each hero’s quest chain to learn more about the world and the magical creatures that inhabit it. After each battle, you’ll decide where to go next. A swamp with a weak enemy or a clearing with a serious foe? The harder the path, the more valuable the reward. Each run will offer its own paths.
