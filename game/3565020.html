About this gameFull description from Steam+
In Mad Television Tycoon, you take control of a small, insignificant TV station. Accept the challenge and lead your network to success! Under your leadership, will the station become the country's top channel with millions of viewers, or will the office lights soon go out for good? Beware - your rivals are not resting, and only with smart planning and a bit of skill will you manage to bring the best programming to the screens! Your task is to strategically design your broadcasting schedule, maximize advertising revenue, and outshine the competition. You have a wide range of shows, movies, series, news, and entertainment programs at your disposal - place them wisely to attract viewers to their screens. Increase your reputation and buy new broadcasting towers across the country to expand your reach. Produce your own shows, acquire additional licenses, and use every tool at your disposal to make life difficult for your rivals. FEATURES Create your schedule from thousands of movies, series and shows. Get the most lucrative advertising contracts. Put together a news program with the latest stories. Produce your own movies, series and shows. Upgrade your offices and acquire new licenses to unlock new features. Go to the dark side and sabotage your competition. Multiplayer for up to 3 players. Easy to edit database to add new movies, series, shows.
