About this gameFull description from Steam+
Three heroes, bound by one artifact A wizard, a knight and a thief each reach for a mysterious device called Trine at the same moment, and it binds their fates together. Set in a world of great castles and strange machinery, their quest is to save a kingdom fallen to the undead. There is always another way through The world runs on fully interactive physics, and each hero meets it differently. Amadeus the Wizard summons objects out of thin air to solve puzzles and open new routes. Zoya the Thief uses agility and dead-on accuracy to surprise the monsters and cross the widest gaps by grappling hook. Pontius the Knight unleashes mayhem and physical destruction wherever he goes. Choose whoever suits the challenge ahead, and switch between them whenever you like. Play it alone or with two friends 1–3 players, in online or local co-op. Alone, you hold all three heroes and swap between them freely. Together, each player takes a hero and you combine their skills to get through. A fairytale world, rebuilt The Enchanted Edition remakes the 2009 original on the Trine 2 engine, with new lighting and effects throughout, save-anywhere checkpointing, and online co-op. The original release is included as well, and either can be chosen from the launcher. Features 1–3 player co-op, online and local Play as wizard, knight and thief, switching freely at any time Physics-based puzzles with more than one solution Remade on the Trine 2 engine, with the 2009 original included Save anywhere, for sessions as long or short as you like Soundtrack by Ari Pulkkinen
