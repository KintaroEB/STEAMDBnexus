About this gameFull description from Steam+
Factory Outlet Simulator is a trading game with all the details of a factory and outlet store. FACTORY MANAGEMENT You can try different ways to source raw materials at the cheapest price, buy machines for your factory and increase your production chain. STORE MANAGEMENT You can design and expand your store as you wish. You can determine where products are displayed, manage advertisements and provide your customers with a quality shopping experience. DESIGN You can create original designs using the computer. You can determine the market values yourself and sell your own brand. COMMERCIAL INTELLIGENCE You can use the computer to order raw materials or find other ways to source them cheaply. By balancing customer satisfaction and profit margins, you can sell your products at the prices you want or apply discounts. WHOLESALE AND LEADERSHIP TABLE You can make commercial agreements with famous brands. Depending on the offers, you can export your products all over the world, becoming the best seller in a real-time market! Business acumen is everything in Factory Outlet Simulator! Will you sink or grow in this market?
