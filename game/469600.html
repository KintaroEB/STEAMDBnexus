About this gameFull description from Steam+
Outlast your opponent Waves of enemies attack you and your opponents. Build defenses to survive. Send mercenaries to smash through your opponents' defenses. Scale your economy to become a late game beast. Like Chess, every match is familiar enough to reward game knowledge, but divergent enough to require adaptation. 12 million fighter combinations Each match, you receive a random roll of 6 fighters, from 12 million possible combinations. Every fighter has strengths & weaknesses. Some secure strategic advantages early game, while others have powerful late-game synergies. Your skill lies in your ability to make the most of the hand you're dealt. Stable, balanced meta With 9+ years worth of data-driven, monthly balance patches, Legion TD 2 is perhaps the most balanced competitive game in the world. Rather than introduce disruptive changes every year to shake things up, Legion TD 2 has opted to chase perfecting a healthy meta. If you take a break for a couple years and come back, your same openings will probably still be viable. Core Features Party as 1-8 players. Play 2v2 and 4v4 matchmade games. Single-player/co-op campaign.* ~8-12 hours, plus 10 levels of AI's. Active playerbase. Fast, fair matches since 2017. Still going strong in 2026. One of a kind gameplay. The only standalone team vs. team competitive tower defense. Highlights Fun to learn. Dynamic in-game tips and special new-player matchmaking ease you in. Packed with stats. Winrates, pickrates, match histories, leaderboards, top games, and more. Global servers. All multiplayer is cross-region, hosted on our servers. No pay to win. In-game purchases are 100% cosmetic. Behavior system. AFKers, griefers, and toxic players are dealt with quickly. Monthly tournament. $1,000 in prizes every month, hosted on Twitch. Weekly challenges. A puzzle-like competition to find the best build with a set lineup. *Note: single-player features require an online connection due to technical limitations of the game engine. History Legion TD 2 is the sequel to the 2nd most popular Warcraft 3 mod of all time, Legion TD. Also known as Squadron TD in Starcraft 2. The top 5 most played Warcraft 3 mods are DotA, Legion TD, Wintermaul, Footmen Frenzy, and Life of a Peasant. Legion TD is proud to hold the silver medal. Legion TD 2 launched in early access in 2017, and has received major updates, quality of life, and balance changes every single month since then. After fully launching in October 2021, we believe it has grown to become the most replayable, balanced, competitive strategy experience out there. Unlike other indie multiplayer games, Legion TD 2's playerbase has stood the test of time, having been active & steady for nearly 10 years and counting. Team Lisk (2014 - Present): Game lead, original Legion TD mod creator Jules (2016 - Present): Game design & balance, business management, and marketing Curing (2018 - 2022): Art director Dani (2019 - Present): Web/dev ops, tools, services, and support Trik (2025 - Present): Lead artist Drachir (2025 - Present): Game dev & design But the full team goes way beyond us: artists, community staff, tournament organizers & casters, translators, voice actors, and more.
