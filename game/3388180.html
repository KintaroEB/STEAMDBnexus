About this gameFull description from Steam+
Demo Overview Get a hands-on preview of IT Specialist Simulator! In this demo, you’ll experience: 🔹 Intro Scene – Learn the basics: movement, interactions, hotkeys 🔹 Main Scene – Step into the IT office and face randomized tech tasks involving computers, networks, and printers. Complete tasks until 10:00 PM for a full day-in-the-life experience. 🔹 Network and sharing center: The computer needs a network address to work properly. Set it up via NCPA.cpl 🔹 The Job Market: Explore the in-game job market, finish a short mini-game, and land your first job offer.
