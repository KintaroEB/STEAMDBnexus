About this gameFull description from Steam+
Explore a vast world Explore a secret-filled land in this 3D Action-Adventure, where every level is hidden in plain sight! Travel and fight through a vast, unmarked overworld. The next surprise is only a single search away! The Angel's mothership, Throne, lays dormant behind a fierce storm. To reach it, unlock its secrets, and usher in the future, you must explore the land of Era and survive its trap-filled ruins, primordial forests, treacherous mountains, mines, and more! Overcome intense battles and hilarious hazards in this tale of betrayal, haunting lore, and fruitless yearning. Hello, devs Melos and Marina here. Today, our hero Tets, and his ally Arkas will introduce the game, alongside the villainous Niamh! Fight bosses in thrilling Bumpslash Combat TETS: I’ve spent many years practicing the Art of Bumpslash — just bump me into enemies to automatically attack! It's fast-paced, and you’ll pick it up in no time! PRINCESS NIAMH: HAHAHAHA!! Tets, my dear... aren’t you giving them false hope? After all, the Fae spirit “Melos” has prepared many vicious and hilarious tricks to guard our ancient secrets! My heart swells thinking of the suffering that awaits these unsuspecting players! Uncover secrets around every corner ANGEL ARKAS: Don’t forget the bigger picture: a vast world map you can search to find levels and zones. Follow your instincts to chart a flexible path. Go at your own pace — follow your spirit! Life's not a checklist, it’s an experience!! A moving story, both arcane and emotional TETS: Agreed! I can’t wait to escape into this world’s haunting lore, woven from Irish mythology, early Christian philosophy, and Japanese American history. By poring over ancient texts and researching on-location, Melos and Marina have grounded their world in truth! ANGEL ARKAS: Truth is not limited to dusty details, Tets. You've forgotten about Angeline Era's tales of fruitless yearning, life-shattering betrayal and unsettling beauty! Only the truths of the heart will bring forth the future... PRINCESS NIAMH: What on earth are you two going on about? This is an action game. KILL, KILL!! Features Explore a secret-filled land in this 3D Action-Adventure, where every level is hidden in plain sight! Travel and fight through a vast, unmarked overworld. The next surprise is only a single search away! "Bump the World!" with intuitive Bumpslash Combat. Walk into enemies to attack - no button needed! Fast-paced action levels AND exploration-and-lore focused levels will test your skills. Explore fearsome Fae forests, condemned buildings, trap-filled farms, spiritual mountains sites, mines, towns, and more! Fierce and uniquely designed bosses and enemies that will test your Bumpslash skills Follow wandering ex-soldier, Tets Kinoshta, as he allies with the Angel, Arkas Gemini, attempting to breach the storm guarding Throne: the Angel's crashed mothership. Will they unlock its secrets? Plenty of levels, each offering glimpses into the Fae, Angels and Humans living together in the country of Era. Mix up combat with subweapons and a gun, and visit towns to shop for items. Beat levels to get Scales and level up! On average, a first playthrough takes 20-30 hours, depending on how much of the world you want to explore and how fast you play. (Rushed playthroughs may be less - I've seen as low as 15-16, thorough playthroughs may be over 30) Low-difficulty setting for Beginner Bumpslashers, and nightmarish settings for the deranged! From Anodyne 2 and Sephonie's devs, Melos and Marina.
