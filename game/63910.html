About this gameFull description from Steam+
King’s Bounty: Crossworlds includes the full King’s Bounty: Armored Princess campaign, along with two additional mini-campaigns: Champion of the Arena, centered around gladiatorial boss battles, and Defender of the Crown, offering tactical encounters in unique arenas with randomly selected allies and enemies. The Armored Princess campaign features its own expansion, Orcs on the March, which significantly increases the variety and depth of the game with dozens of new quests, creatures, items, abilities, and spells. New equipment sets and mechanics open up additional strategic options, encouraging experimentation with different army compositions and playstyles. Crossworlds also includes a full-featured world editor, allowing you to create your own maps, campaigns, and tactical challenges. Design custom scenarios, experiment with encounters, and share your creations with other players, extending the life of the game far beyond the included campaigns. Whether you want to experience the complete Armored Princess campaign, test your skills in focused tactical challenges, or build your own King’s Bounty adventures, King’s Bounty: Crossworlds delivers a massive, content-rich tactical experience built for players who enjoy depth, variety, and strategic freedom. FEATURES Standalone expansion with the complete Armored Princess campaign Includes the full King’s Bounty: Armored Princess experience with real-time exploration, deep turn-based tactical battles, hero progression, companions, and the evolving pet dragon. Two additional standalone mini-campaigns Champion of the Arena focuses on intense gladiatorial battles and boss encounters, while Defender of the Crown offers tactical scenarios in unique arenas with randomized allies and enemies. Orcs on the March expansion included The Armored Princess campaign features its own expansion, Orcs on the March, introducing new units, spells, and quests that significantly expand the original experience. Expanded tactical options and progression systems New equipment sets, skills, and mechanics deepen army building and combat strategy, opening up additional ways to approach battles. Arena-focused gameplay modes Experience King’s Bounty combat without long exploration segments, designed for players who want concentrated tactical challenges and faster pacing. Full-featured world editor Create your own maps, campaigns, and tactical scenarios, customize encounters, and share your content with the community.
