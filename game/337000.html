About this gameFull description from Steam+
The year is 2029, and mechanically augmented humans have now been deemed outcasts, living a life of complete and total segregation from the rest of society. Crime and acts of terror serve as a thin veil to cover up an overarching conspiracy aimed at controlling the future of mankind. Now an experienced covert operative, Adam Jensen is forced to operate in a world that has grown to despise his kind. Armed with a new arsenal of state-of-the-art weapons and augmentations, he must choose the right approach, along with who to trust, in order to unravel a vast worldwide conspiracy. HUMAN 2.0 Choose from an array of cutting edge weapons and augmentations, customizing them to fit your playstyle. WORLDWIDE CONSPIRACY Explore diverse locations and discover a near future setting where humanity's fate hangs in the balance. MEANINGFUL CHOICES AND CONSEQUENCES Experience the world of Deus Ex, where the decisions you make and the actions you take play a crucial role in determining the game’s outcome.
