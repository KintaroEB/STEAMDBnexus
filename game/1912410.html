About this gameFull description from Steam+
Minecraft Dungeons II is here with more action, more loot, and more adventure. Step through the rift and fight where no hero has been before: the Sift, an uncharted dimension teeming with threats, mysteries, and breathtaking beauty. Gear up and customize your playstyle to face your toughest enemies yet in explosive battles. Brave the unknown as a lone hero, team up with friends in couch, online and mixed co-op, or find allies through matchmaking. The illagers’ plot is already underway, and their cruelty is only matched by their power. Stop them – or lose the Overworld to their corruption. Fight against a new, challenging threat that puts your combat skills to the test like never before Explore the Sift, a new dimension in the Minecraft universe, for the first time Experience an all-new chapter in the Minecraft Dungeons saga that takes you across the Overworld... and beyond Craft the perfect hero with legendary gear, including a wide range of weapons, armour, and artifacts Team up with new and old friends in a party of up to four players and smash your foes with the power of teamwork
