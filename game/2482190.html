About this gameFull description from Steam+
Power Fantasy is a free-to-play retro survivors roguelike where every run pushes you closer to becoming absurdly overpowered. Unlock new heroes and companions, grind achievements for permanent unlocks, spend talent points, discover broken perk synergies and find strategies to survive deeper and deeper. Push your limits with rising difficulties, combine brutal challenges into new nightmares and keep growing through infinite meta progression. Slaughter endless hordes, break the game and descend through four deadly Depths until you conquer Hell itself.
