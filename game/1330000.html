About this gameFull description from Steam+
TURN‑BASED TACTICS MEETS ACTION RPG LOOTING Stolen Realm is a fast-paced tactical dungeon crawler where explosive builds, meaningful loot, and simultaneous turn-based combat collide. Lead a party of up to six heroes through dangerous expeditions filled with monsters, treasure, and unpredictable events. Play solo or team up with friends in seamless online co‑op as you explore a vibrant, readable low‑poly fantasy world. Every dive into the Realm offers new challenges, new loot, and new ways to break the game in your favour. FAST, SIMULTANEOUS TURN‑BASED COMBAT Both sides act at the same time, keeping battles quick without losing tactical depth. Plan your moves, unleash your abilities, and watch the entire battlefield react at once. No waiting for long enemy turns. No downtime. Just tight, high‑impact encounters where positioning and synergy matter. BUILD ANY HERO YOU CAN IMAGINE There are no fixed classes. Choose from 300+ abilities across 10 flexible skill trees and craft a hero that fits your playstyle. Create classic archetypes or experiment with hybrids like: A spell‑slinging assassin A frost‑born priest A shadow‑infused knight Or something completely unhinged that shouldn’t work but absolutely does LOOT THAT TRANSFORMS YOUR PLAYSTYLE Discover 700+ items that do far more than raise your stats. Mythic weapons and relics unlock new mechanics, new synergies, and new strategies. Wield artifacts like: Abbadon the Soul Crusher - raise slain enemies as skeletal allies Bloodletter - a cursed blade that trades your lifeblood for devastating power Every expedition opens the door to new combinations and new build possibilities. CHOOSE YOUR PATH THROUGH THE REALM Each adventure unfolds across a procedurally generated map where every decision shapes your run. After every battle you choose your next step, whether that’s diving into a treasure room, chasing a story event, visiting a merchant, gathering resources, or risking a dangerous encounter. Every expedition becomes its own story, defined by the choices you make and the luck you carry with you into the Realm. ONLINE CO‑OP FOR UP TO 6 PLAYERS Play solo or bring friends. Stolen Realm is built for co‑op strategy, with encounters that scale automatically and a drop‑in/drop‑out system that keeps the action flowing. Coordinate builds, chain abilities, and unleash devastating team combos. WHAT PLAYERS & PRESS ARE SAYING “The tactical depth of Divinity: Original Sin.” — GamesRadar+ “We’re losing our evenings to Stolen Realm.” — TheXboxHub “A gorgeous looking game.” — PC Gamer “You can do all sorts of weird stuff.” — Sacriel “Straight to the point… no bullsh*t.” — Shroud “It’s rad.” — Sequisha KEY FEATURES Online co‑op for up to 6 players Fast, simultaneous turn‑based combat 300+ abilities across 10 skill trees 700+ lootable items that change how you play Tactical hex‑based battlefields with interactive environments 200+ enemy types with unique modifiers Procedural adventures with branching paths and events Campaign mode, Roguelike mode, and Hardcore permadeath
