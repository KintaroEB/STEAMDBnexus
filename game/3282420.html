About this gameFull description from Steam+
Zoominoes is the ultimate tile deck builder all about combining cards on a board to score infinite points. Crazy Combos Upgrade animals using snacks and souvenirs to create crazy combos. Transform your simple starting deck into the perfect masterpiece. Manipulate the board with space modifiers to boost your score even more. Focus on different animal types or color build arounds to create the ultimate scoring army. Become The Ultimate Zookeeper Play as a variety of 20 different Zookeepers. Be anyone from a Rich Evil Zoo Ceo, a Diva Herpetologist, a shop-a-holic, to an extreme adventure Survivalist. Collect Everything 218 Animals each with a fun fact to unlock. 35 Snacks can help you customize your deck exactly how you want. 112 Souvenirs to double, multiply, and transform how everything plays. 20 Zookeepers all featuring their own unique starting decks, abilities, and personalities. 26 Special Events that place unique modifiers to shake up the average day. 8 Difficulty levels to work your way up the Zoo ladder from Volunteer to Director. Features Endless Mode: Keep your run going after beating the full 28 Day month and try to reach Day 56 and score infinite points! Daily Run: Compete against the world everyday with a new seeded run. Will you become king of the jungle? Challenges Mode: 24 extra challenges await with Chally. Attempt to earn all 192 points and complete his varying wild missions!
