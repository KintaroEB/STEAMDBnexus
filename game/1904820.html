About this gameFull description from Steam+
Play together with 3-11 other players, each player gets a random role and will need to work with his role mates to win. Prisoners have 4 days to escape prison while the guards will need to prevent them from escaping. Core Features 3 Maps - Bricksfield, Lost Island, Space. Many ways to escape - Underground, Entrance Door, Bomb, Helicopter, Jetski, Raft, Ship, Spaceship, Escape Pods. Voice Chat Customizable Game Settings Many Areas - Rooms, Yard, Vents, Underground, Roof, Ship docks, Abandoned City. Crafting System. Many Items for Prisoners to Use. Cool Guard Items - Placeable Trap, Remote Camera, Radio, Sound Detector. Tasks for Guards Find loot inside the prison's restricted and unrestricted areas. Avoid being witnessed carrying illegal items or committing illegal actions. Dig underground to escape and get rare resources. Craft different items that will help you escape like a fence cutter and a bomb! Search up players for illegal items. Witness players doing illegal activities like being in restricted areas, going trough metal detectors with metals and more. Arrest wanted players and send them to solitary. Repair broken walls/fences outside the prison.
