About this gameFull description from Steam+
*Special Note: The Three Kingdoms: Rebirth is an interactive live-action adventure game set in a parallel version of the Three Kingdoms. It is an original story and does not involve historical facts or the original classic. "Romance fulfilled, ambition achieved"—this time, I will have it all. You are Liu Xiaoshan, an ordinary modern young man who unexpectedly travels to a parallel Three Kingdoms era, encountering renowned generals and remarkable women by chance. Every choice you make will rewrite history—or decide whether you live or die. This is no painful dilemma between "finding love" and "conquering the world." Zhen Fu’s wealth, Cai Zhaoji’s strategy, Da Qiao’s foresight... the game’s refined systems ensure that every emotional investment yields rewards. Meanwhile, your rise to power becomes the strength to protect those you love. Mutual support, mutual success! As a time traveler, your knowledge, humor, and worldview are your strongest tools. A proposal about "shareholding" might convince Zhen Fu to invest, but a modern meme could just as easily catch someone off guard—reshape the fate of ancient figures with a future perspective. This is the true romance of the time traveler, brought to life here. Your decisions affect more than just affection meters—they can alter the course of history, shape the fates of characters, and unlock over thirty distinct endings. Someone may wait for you for ten years, someone may die for love, and someone may smile as they hand you a poisoned cup—all of this will be written by your hand. You won’t need to command armies, but you will need to earn Cao Cao’s trust, gain Liu Bei’s support, and make Sun Quan think twice. Forge alliances and navigate power struggles—will you side with someone at the Battle of Red Cliffs? Whose side will you take at Guandu? Every allegiance could keep you alive for another episode—or end your journey early. You’ll meet not only peerlessly stunning "god-tier" beauties, but also independent partners with their own minds, ambitions and desires; they may grow jealous, form alliances, betray you, or grow alongside you, every character has her own temper, and romantic rivalries here can truly turn deadly. Dozens of endings, vast branching paths, hidden stories, and rare achievements—every new playthrough is a fresh adventure. Discover countless possibilities until you find the ending that feels right for you. Romance fulfilled, ambition achieved. This is your story, your Three Kingdoms.
