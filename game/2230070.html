About this gameFull description from Steam+
You are the Great Ancient Knight, go undercover in the Lair of the Lizards. Get Intelligence for your Knight Brothers, return the Ancient Manuscripts to the base, take the Baikal Water and Destroy the Lizard Base! Unique Characteristics Call the Great Knights to your aid - Billyslav Herringtonov, Ivan Darkholmov, the God Perun Collect 5 Ancient Manuscripts Drink 18 cans of Baikal Water You have access to 4 Main and 2 Bonus Locations - Destroy all Lizard Bases 15+ Cutscenes - Main Cutscenes To All Locations & Bonus Cutscenes 15+ Species of Lizards Five Great Knights - Choose your Hero Ancient Slavic Spaceships assembled on the basis of technologies stolen from the Lizards Great Atmospheric Music and Sound Effects Main Locations Lizard Camp - there is a Lizard Camp in The Forest under the walls of the Ancient Great Capital - Hyperborea Lake Baikal - the Great Battle took place here, but now this place is under the power of the Lizards, help the Knights to return Baikal to the Native Harbor. Mars - Diversion to the Lizard Military Base on Mars Nibiru - Explore the Home Planet and the Main Lizard Haven Bonus Locations Saint-Petersburg - Lizards have taken over the city, walk the main streets of the city and hunt down each Lizard New Year's Winter Forest - Celebrate the New Year by chasing away the Lizards from the Great Christmas Forest
