※All characters involved in sexual content are over the age of 21. ＜About＞ Another unforgettable night is about to begin... Bunny Garden's brand-new title finally arrives! Reunite with familiar faces while welcoming new additions, delivering an even more immersive experience than its predecessor. The “gentleman's games” have evolved further; how you enjoy them is entirely up to you. Forget the hardships of daily life and indulge in sweet, heart-pounding nights. ＜Story＞ Haito Kanda (name changeable) lost his job due to a major mistake at work, and had fallen into despair over everything. Just as he was heading to his usual cheap pub to drown his sorrows... A woman suddenly spoke to him, noticing his dejected expression. That was the fateful encounter... Guided by her, he stepped into a sanctuary for gentlemen, where women as adorable as rabbits welcomed him... “BUNNY GARDEN.” What changes will this encounter bring to their lives, and yours? It's all up to you... A new BUNNY GARDEN, set in a different world line from the previous title. A brand-new story and a cast you have yet to meet await you. ＜System＞ ◆With a drink in hand, enjoy sweet and delightful moments with the cast. Bunny Garden is a social gathering place brimming with relaxation and excitement for gentlemen. Savor an extensive selection of drinks and snacks while enjoying conversation with the uniquely charming cast.
