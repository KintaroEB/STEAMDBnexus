About this gameFull description from Steam+
For the first time ever, you are able to immerse yourself in a world of milling. Use a conventional milling machine to drill, mill, grind and cut. Import your own blueprints, to prototype unique products in Free milling mode. Measure with precision and complete challenging tasks in Career mode. Customize workpieces to fit your needs, by choosing materials and size. Get everything done with realistic physics and movement. And don't forget your cutting tools can break!
