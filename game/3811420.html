About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into the ultimate truck simulator with Transit King! Build a thriving trucking empire as the manager of a growing logistics company in this exciting tycoon game. From small deliveries to large-scale operations, create a logistics network that connects cities, ports, and production hubs. Manage a diverse fleet of trucks, semi-trucks, buses, and even ships as you transport goods and expand your truck simulator empire! MANAGE YOUR TRUCKING EMPIRE Start with a small fleet and expand your garage with a variety of trucks and cargo vehicles. Grow your routes, meet rising demand, and unlock strategic opportunities to become a successful tycoon. Are you ready to lead the ultimate truck simulator and grow your business? TRUCK SIMULATOR & TYCOON FEATURES Deliver Goods Across Cities: Move cargo to cities, businesses, and factories to keep supplies moving. Upgrade Your Fleet: Unlock, buy, and upgrade an extensive selection of trucks to boost capacity and efficiency. Build Facilities & Increase Demand: Create warehouses and production sites to generate new orders and revenue. Streamline Your Road Network: Build and upgrade roads to optimize routes and speed up deliveries. Assign Trucks to Jobs: Keep your fleet busy with jobs to ensure that cargo is delivered smoothly. Unlock Seaports for More Reach: Go beyond land – use seaports to expand and reach new markets. Join Alliances & Earn Rewards: Collaborate with other players and tackle greater challenges. Idle & Active Gameplay: Your trucks keep earning income while you’re idle, providing passive progress for your business. EXPAND AND BECOME TRANSIT KING From busy cities to remote production sites, your network grows as your company succeeds. Optimize routes, build facilities, and make key upgrades to streamline operations and deliver cargo faster. Invest in new vehicles and strategic upgrades to become a top truck simulator tycoon. IDLE TRUCKING MADE EASY Even when you’re offline, your fleet of trucks works tirelessly, earning cash and expanding your empire. Return to manage contracts, make upgrades, and guide your company forward in this immersive truck simulator. BECOME A TRUE TYCOON Transit King combines the excitement of a truck simulator with the strategic depth of a tycoon game. Grow your small trucking company into a powerful logistics empire that reaches cities across the map. Can you build the largest truck business and become a true truck tycoon? Transit King is a free-to-play truck simulator game with optional in-app purchases. Join the world of trucking and start your journey to becoming a top tycoon today! 🚛🏆
