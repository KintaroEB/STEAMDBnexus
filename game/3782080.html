About this gameFull description from Steam+
“Talent won’t carve numbers into your bank account!” “To ensure every employee’s potential is fully realized in their account balance, company policy now dictates: no employee shall pay a bride price exceeding 100,000 RMB. Violators will be publicly shamed across the company and severely punished!” Xu Kuan, CEO of Domi Network and multi-billionaire, barked these orders during the monthly wrap-up meeting. “My god... it’s the ‘Kuan-Kuan New Deal’!” Beside me, Xiao Zhang from the programming team let out a tortured groan. “There goes my rating on the matchmaking platforms. It’s gonna tank again.” I felt the exact same way. Tell me, is it even possible? Could a corporate drone like me, working at a game company, ever meet a beautiful 2D-loving girl and enjoy a sweet, heart-fluttering romance? Just as the thought crossed my mind, I instinctively glanced at my female colleague sitting silently in the corner— Yin Zhaozhao from the Art Department. In private, she’s a "Landmine Girl" who wraps her arms in runed bandages and spends her nights grinding BALOLANT. A hidden-edition "Landmine" right here at Domi Network? How refreshing! And just recently, I discovered that my childhood friend Xiuhua—the girl I’ve had a crush on forever—is actually a yandere Landmine Girl herself! Rumor has it her wardrobe is stuffed with chocolates engraved with my name and notebooks filled with "essays" about me written since we were kids... Ding! The choices before you are: A. You’re a love-starved Landmine, I’m a 9am-9pm*6days corporate slave—let’s just lock ourselves together for eternity! B. Do everything possible to escape the minefield. C. Abandon everything, seal your heart away, and follow CEO Xu to the end. The sweetest Chinese-style "Dating Minefield" story begins now! At Domi Network, a company notorious for soul-crushing "involution" and constant overtime, I lived the monotonous life of a corporate drone. Everything changed when a sudden accident revealed a shocking secret: three "Jirai-kei" (Landmine) girls were hiding in plain sight right beside me! YUNA – A sharp and efficient colleague from the Art Department by day, but a total "Landmine Girl" in her private life? XIUHUA – My childhood friend who hides a closet yandere side beneath her gentle exterior? AIKA – A struggling underground idol rookie, a pure-hearted girl desperate for recognition! With over 300,000 words of rich narrative and three emotionally intense "subculture" girls, come experience the most authentic Chinese-style "dating minefield" journey! Xia Zhai was her only psychological pillar during her darkest times, leading her to develop an intense, morbid attachment and possessiveness toward him. Her "landmine" doesn't explode directly; instead, she uses passive-aggression and emotional manipulation—feigning weakness, self-blame, and guilt-tripping—to solidify her status and sense of security. Pure-hearted, stubborn, and sometimes lacking common sense, her entire logic revolves around the desperate need to "be seen" and "be remembered."
