About this gameFull description from Steam+
Emberward is a roguelite tower defense game focusing on maze building and interesting tower mechanics. You are a guardian who is tasked with protecting the fire essence to illuminate the darkness. Embark on an adventurous journey, selecting your favorite combinations from a variety of distinctive towers and create mazes using differently shaped blocks to fend off monster invasions. Continuously build, strengthen, and adjust your strategies until you reach the goal! in This Game You Can... 👑 Have a roguelite tower defense adventure. 🚩 Plan and create massive maze with tetris-like blocks! 🏹 Collect and build uniquely designed towers. 💡 Use buff cards, talents, and level mechanics to enhance your defense. 🐲 BOSS LEVELS!! Massive 2x2 Towers: Building powerful 2x2 towers requires not only coins but also careful planning. Poison Towers that Span Monster Paths: Instead of shooting, we unleash poison directly on the enemies' heads! Drone Towers that Track Enemies: Who says towers have to wait for enemies to come? Proactive defense is the way to go! (...and more!!) Construct mazes before battles, quickly assess the terrain and routes, and find the most suitable configurations. Make the most of your limited wall blocks! Should you make monsters take detours or place towers in better positions? Are you on the verge of being overrun by enemies? Utilize enhancement cards to strengthen your towers within a short period! Each adventure is unique with different maps, tower compositions, and mazes. Upgrade your towers during your journey, acquire rare maze walls, or obtain special enhancement abilities. Every expedition accumulates experience, granting you richer knowledge for your next departure. Add to wishlist now!!
