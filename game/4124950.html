About this gameFull description from Steam+
A World Reborn from the Ashes Ashes of Creation is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) set in a high-fantasy world where player actions shape the world itself. Cities rise and fall, populations shift, and entire regions transform as players rebuild civilization on living, reactive continents. Every decision, individual or collective, leaves a lasting mark on the world of Verra. Whether you’re leading armies into battle, building empires through trade and craftsmanship, or uncovering ancient secrets long buried beneath the sands, Ashes of Creation offers an evolving journey defined by the choices of its players. Key Features A Living, Breathing World Experience a dynamic world that evolves with player activity. Build and defend player-driven cities known as Settlements - each unlocking new content, quests, and political structures as they grow. Risk and Reward PvP & PvE Engage in large-scale battles, dungeon raids, and open-world conflicts. Wage war and siege against your enemies, defend trade caravans, or venture into dangerous dungeons where glory and peril go hand in hand. Player-Driven Civilization Every realm develops differently. Players influence the growth of settlements, elect mayors, form alliances, and wage wars that reshape the political landscape of Verra. Integral Crafting and Economy Systems Gather resources, trade goods, and craft powerful items across a player-driven economy. Control resource routes, establish trade hubs, and profit from your influence, or sabotage your rivals’ Caravan supply lines. Epic Exploration and Adventure: Discover a vast, hand-crafted world filled with unique biomes, ancient ruins, and hidden treasures. Mount up and explore the wild frontier- every journey holds the promise of discovery and danger. Boundless Archetype Combinations Choose from eight primary archetypes to create your character, customize your skills and abilities. Whether you’re a stealthy Rogue, a commanding Summoner, or a protective Tank, your playstyle will be entirely your own. Massive Warfare Join hundreds of players in epic sieges and realm conflicts that decide the fate of nations. Gather resources, and command armies in battles that alter the course of the world. Your Story, Written by Many In Ashes of Creation, no two realms or stories are ever the same. The world evolves as players build, explore, and destroy, forging a tapestry of triumph and tragedy that belongs to its community. Will you be a builder of empires, a bringer of chaos, or a legend remembered in song? The choice and the world is yours to shape.
