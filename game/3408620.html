About this gameFull description from Steam+
Build, decorate, and manage your own cozy café, where guests can relax in a warm and welcoming atmosphere while enjoying delicious pastries, freshly brewed coffee, and a variety of treats. At the same time, develop a supermarket filled with hundreds of detailed and appealing items. Organize the space by placing shelves, refrigerators, and freezers. Set optimal prices and create a comfortable shopping experience for customers by providing baskets, carts, and efficient checkout service. Play solo or team up with friends in cooperative multiplayer! An intuitive building menu, grid placement system, the ability to paint floors and walls, and a flexible table customization system in the cafe allow you to design your establishment with maximum precision and create your own unique style. You can also customize your character's appearance to highlight your personal style in cooperative mode. It gets even more exciting with the option to switch between first-person and third-person views to see your character and their style from every angle. Choose from several unique locations to open your business, each inspired by different corners of the world. Currently, you can enjoy the charm of a North American city with mid-rise buildings or feel the nostalgia of an Eastern European city with its recognizable panel buildings, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the desired theme. You can create your own sign for your establishment by uploading an image from your computer or using a web link to place any image from the internet. For example, you can upload the logo of your local cafe or store and role-play as its owner. You can also select one of several real currencies to see familiar banknotes, prices, and symbols, adding realism and helping you dive deeper into the atmosphere. Dynamic day-night cycles, realistic weather effects, and beautifully rendered night cityscapes with lit streets and bustling traffic create an atmospheric environment. You can feel the coziness of your cafe while rain falls outside in the nighttime city, illuminated by passing cars. For even greater immersion, the game features a music player with original compositions in various styles, perfectly complementing the atmosphere. Demo Version Limitations The demo version offers full, unlimited co-op multiplayer and access to both starting regions. However, store development is capped at a few initial levels, limiting the available selection of products, dishes, and furniture, as well as the maximum store size.
