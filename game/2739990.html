About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mahjong Soul is an online game that adopted the classic Japanese Mahjong gameplay. Here, you can hone your mahjong skills with players from worldwide or spend leisure time with friends, bond with various charismatic characters, and experience an abundance of unique stories. Classic Japanese Mahjong Mahjong Soul preserves the features of classic Japanese Mahjong (also known as Riichi Mahjong). You can play against opponents at similar skill levels to hone your skills. Easy tutorial for beginners Beginners are welcomed with easy-to-understand tutorials, which cover not just the basics but also teach you how to handle various game scenarios. With step-by-step guides, even beginners can become proficient in no time. Enjoy yourself with pals, or engage in global matches You can not only create private rooms to play with friends in Friend Room, but also battle players from around the world. Test each other's skills, and enjoy a variety of special and amusing mahjong modes. Meet an abundance of charismatic characters Interact with a range of charming characters that bring unique stories to life, enhancing your gaming experience as you form bonds and explore the world of Mahjong Soul with them. Fancy-up matches with decorations Immerse yourself in dynamic effects that make every mahjong match a thrilling game. From a tiny tile to an entire tablecloth, every detail can be customized to suit your taste. Expressive character emotes Make use of the emotes to liven up your matches. Engage in mischief and mayhem with hilarious character emotes!
