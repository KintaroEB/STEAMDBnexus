About this gameFull description from Steam+
With TRYP FPV, immerse yourself in the most realistic FPV flying experience ever created. Designed for all levels of pilots, this simulator pushes the boundaries of realism with stunning graphics and ultra-accurate flight sensations. Fully experience the adrenaline of FPV drone flying with no limits! IMMERSIVE FLIGHT SENSATIONS Feel the thrill of controlling an FPV drone like never before, thanks to ultra-realistic flight simulation manage with the PID loop : Inertia and gravity management: Every turn and speed change has been fine-tuned so your drone responds as it would in real-life conditions. Optimized aerodynamics: Wind effects, air resistance, and acceleration are carefully calibrated using digital twin technology to replicate the authentic sensation of flying outdoors. Realistic sound design: Every engine sound and gust of wind is crafted to enhance the feeling of immersion. REALISTIC ENVIRONMENTS Discover a wide range of spots scanned from real-world locations flown by pilots from around the globe. Explore 5 high-quality maps. Venture through the pyramids, cross urban bandos, weave between palm trees, ski down slopes, or slalom between skyscrapers. VARIED GAME MODES Explore environments in long-range with the TRYP Mode. Challenge your skills in freestyle with CHALLENGE Mode. Take control of the camera in SCENARIO Mode. NEW: Compete against the best pilots in RACE Mode and climb to the top of the global leaderboard. EXTREME CUSTOMIZATION TRYP FPV offers advanced drone customization for complete control over your flying experience: Adjust your rates to refine the responsiveness and fluidity of your controls. Build your dream drone by selecting from a range of frames, propellers, batteries (LiPo), and cameras, creating a setup that fits you. Customize the angle and settings of your camera for optimal immersion, whether for freestyle or racing.
