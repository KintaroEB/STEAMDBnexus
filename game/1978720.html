About this gameFull description from Steam+
Ultimate Ragdoll Game is a sandbox game without any limitations. The game has no specific way to play or end, your goal is to have a good time! GAMEPLAY The game is divided into several modes that allow you to build your dream sandbox. Build with the blocks available in the game, freely change their properties, and have fun with ragdolls. STEAM WORKSHOP The game has full Steam Workshop support. Download various ragdolls, blocks, weapons, maps, and much more! You can also create your own content using the included SDK and simple step-by-step instructions. FREE DLCs The game comes with 2 free expansions: Goofy Ahh Sounds and Blood and Gore, which significantly enhance the gaming experience (actually, just fart sounds and a bit of blood, but in my opinion, it's still a cool feature). JOE BIDEN Despite Joe Biden appearing on every third screenshot, this is NOT a game about Joe Biden... BUT (!) you can download his model from the Steam Workshop, and THEN it CAN be a game about Joe Biden... or someone else... do whatever you want, after all, that's what this game is all about.
