About this gameFull description from Steam+
Backpack Jianghu is now open for free DEMO! Featuring four major sects—Huashan, Shaolin, Wudang, and Wudu—12 sub-classes, 10 talent skills, and nearly 200 items! Discord： Backpack jianghu is a inventory management and battle royale game. Choose a starting sect, enter the world with dozens of players, pick your path, battle and grow, and compete in the final showdown at Mount Hua's peak. Inspired by Backpack Battles, but with big innovations. Use inner energy to boost skills, upgrade weapons, combine low-tier skills into high-tier ones, and use skill fragments to permanently level up abilities. Get creative with 200+ items. Make overpowered builds like: Lonely Nine Swords – attack 20 times in a row, each hit applying armor break and bleed. Taichi Mastery – counter-attack automatically when hit. Dragon Strike – deal 99,999 damage in one blow, ignoring armor. Qiankun Dodge – evade and reflect the next attack. Every strategy has a counter. The possibilities are endless. You share the world with 99 others. Pick your path: fight, shop, find chests, rest, or risk random events. Note: Other players are saved data, not live people. Take your time to manage your pack. But always remember: your goal is to reach Mount Hua’s peak and defeat everyone else. On the map, you can spy on enemy packs. See what items they carry. Learn their strategy and what loot they’ll drop if defeated. Oh, and when you beat someone, you loot some items from their pack. Each sect starts with a different pack, items, and location. The shops, chests, events, and enemies you meet also change based on your sect. Planned sects (may change): Shaolin Wudang Huashan Wudu Xuedao Gaibang Xiayao Tangmen Emei Liushanmen After a game, share your lineup code to Discord and social media platforms to directly battle against other players' best teams.
