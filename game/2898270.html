About this gameFull description from Steam+
You start with an empty room and a dream. You end with the biggest casino empire in the city — if your friends don't gamble it all away first. 🤝 ONLINE CO-OP (1-4 PLAYERS) Run everything together over Steam invites: tables, customers, money, vehicles — fully synced. Split the jobs: one deals cards, one runs the cashier, one serves drinks… and one quietly tests their luck with the shared vault. 🃏 EVERY TABLE IS REALLY PLAYABLE Blackjack, Poker and Roulette across three tiers — realistic dealing animations and unique interactions at every table. Deal the cards yourself as the croupier, or hire staff and watch the chips flow. Slot machines run real games on their screens, with spins, free games and big wins. 🌆 A LIVING CITY Step outside your casino: city traffic, a car dealership with tuning and drift, a tractor with digger attachments, houses and a full mansion to buy and live in. 🏗️ BUILD & MANAGE Build mode: lay floors, raise walls, decorate, expand the building itself Cashier system: sell and cash out chips yourself, or hire a cashier Full staff roster: croupiers, cashiers, bartenders, cleaners, security Bar & food service and customer requests Deep economy: vault, bills, prestige, oBay second-hand market ✨ AND MORE Character creator — design your own look 28 Steam Achievements Missions & tutorial, minimap, emote menu, reaction voices 22 supported languages In the shadow of the bustling city there is a small, modest casino. Your gateway from rags to riches. Starting out as a miserable with nothing but greed, you're ready to turn this humble casino into a thriving casino empire. Every decision can lead to luck or madness and step into a world where your entrepreneurial spirit is the key to rising from zero to hero. "Increase Your Casino's Prestige" is an engaging simulation game where you manage your own casino. Each item placed in the casino contributes to its prestige value, and customer reviews also affect this prestige. More customers and more money are the ultimate goals! "Handling everything can be challenging, hire employees to ease your workload." "A wide area is crucial for becoming wealthy. Expand your space by placing more gambling tables, slot machines, and decorations." "There's no place for sleeplessness, with you, more fun and profit await!" "The lucky can make a lot of money." "A beautifully decorated interior encourages people to spend more time there. Ensure uniqueness with over 300 decorations for the interior."
