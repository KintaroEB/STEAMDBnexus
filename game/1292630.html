About this gameFull description from Steam+
Game Features 3v3 Online Multiplayer Team up with players around the world and compete in fast-paced, high-energy street basketball matches. 1v1 Competitive Mode Test your skills in intense one-on-one matchups and prove your playstyle and individual performance. Career Mode (New) Experience a solo PVE mode featuring stage-based progression, roguelike elements, and long-term character growth. Practice & Training Mode Improve your skills through various training systems and prepare for real matches. Single Player & PVE Experience Enjoy a fully developed single-player experience designed to help you grow at your own pace. Stage-based progression with character-specific content Dynamic difficulty that adapts to your skill level Roguelike elements that provide a fresh experience every run Free-to-Play Basketball Experience A free-to-play basketball game where your time and effort directly translate into meaningful progression. Enjoy both solo and competitive modes with a deep and rewarding experience. Real-Time Online Community Play with and against users across multiple platforms in real time. Form teams, compete against rivals, and enjoy seamless matchmaking anytime. Dynamic Street Basketball Gameplay Master intuitive controls and pull off stylish street basketball moves with ease. Choose from a diverse roster of unique characters, each with their own skills and playstyles, and create your own strategy on the court. Unreal Engine Lumen Graphics With the latest update, 3on3 FreeStyle now features Unreal Engine Lumen-based graphics, delivering a more immersive and realistic visual experience. Real-time lighting that enhances the atmosphere of each match Improved reflections and indirect lighting for greater immersion Dynamic environments that bring the court to life Experience sharper, more vibrant street basketball like never before.
