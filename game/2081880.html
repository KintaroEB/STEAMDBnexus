About this gameFull description from Steam+
Team up with friends and meet adventurers from around the world in Kathana, a classic MMORPG built around shared adventures. Take on quests together, face formidable enemies, and explore a world filled with monsters, skills, crafting, dungeons, and challenges. Develop your character, improve your equipment, and continue your journey across Kathana. Rich, Immersive Storyline: Uncover the mysteries of Kathana through a deep and engaging narrative. Solo and Party Quests: Tackle quests alone or team up with others for greater rewards. Epic Boss Hunts: Face off against powerful bosses in thrilling encounters. Dangerous Dungeons: Explore perilous dungeons filled with secrets and treasures. Guild Wars: Battle for dominance and glory in large-scale guild conflicts. Ranked PvP Arenas: Test your skills and climb the leaderboards in competitive PvP. Duels and Open PK: Challenge others in one-on-one duels or engage in open player combat. Choose from eight distinct character classes, each with its own abilities and approach to combat. Shape your build by distributing Spirit Points, developing skills that support your strategy, and equipping weapons and armor that influence both your capabilities and appearance. Equipment-Based Customization: Personalize your character with a wide selection of weapons and armor. Visible Equipment: The gear you equip is reflected in your character’s appearance. Flexible Stat Allocation: Distribute Spirit Points to strengthen the attributes that suit your build. Distinct Class Identities: Each of the eight classes offers different strengths, abilities, and combat styles. Adaptable Skill Progression: Develop your class skills according to your preferred role and playstyle. Turn the rewards of your adventures into lasting progress. Collect weapons, armor, accessories, and materials, then improve your equipment as you prepare for increasingly difficult encounters. Equipment Refining: Upgrade weapons and armor through a multi-stage refining system. Additional Enhancements: Apply secondary refinements to further develop selected pieces of equipment. Refining Materials: Choose from different stones and additives that can influence cost, success rates, and the risks involved. Crafting and Quest Rewards: Gather materials, complete questlines, and work with specialized NPCs to obtain valuable equipment. Buy, sell, repair, and trade as you prepare for your next adventure. Multiple Ways to Trade: Exchange items directly, operate a personal shop, or use the marketplace. Specialized NPC Services: Visit merchants for equipment, supplies, repairs, storage, and selected crafting services. A Varied Item System: Discover weapons, armor, accessories, consumables, refining materials, and special rewards throughout your journey. Ready to forge your legend in Kathana? Your adventure begins now!
