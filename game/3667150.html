About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Mech Builder 3D — your perfect escape into the Gunpla-inspired world of model building. Experience the relaxing process of building a model kit with complete freedom. Effortlessly assemble, paint, and pose all your models. Build mechs, monsters, armors, and so much more. Building all the kits will take around 7-9 hours of your time. All 3D models and pieces have been carefully crafted for a relaxing experience. Features 61 model kits to build. A pressure-free experience: build your way, at your own pace. Pose the models kits in any position you want. Unlock kitbash mode to make your own models with all the pieces.
