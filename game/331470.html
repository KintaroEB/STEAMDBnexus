About this gameFull description from Steam+
Meeting Semyon, the game's main character, you would've never paid attention to him. Just an ordinary young man with thousands, even hundreds of thousands of those like him in every ordinary city. But one day something completely unusual happens to him: he falls asleep in a bus in winter and wakes up... in the middle of a hot summer. In front of him is "Sovyonok" - a pioneer camp, behind him is his former life. To understand what happened to him, Semyon will have to get to know the local inhabitants (and maybe even find love), find his way in the complex labyrinth of human relationships and his own problems and solve the camp's mysteries. And answer the main question - how to come back? Should he come back?
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

The game contains no explicit depictions of nudity or sex, but several characters appear in a partially clothed state and sex is mentioned on a few occasions. There are no graphic depictions of violence, but character death occurs on a couple occasions and is partially described in a fade-to-black manner. There is no graphic depiction of alcohol consumption/abuse, but it and its negative consequences are described in a couple places (don't do drugs, kids!) The only way to access this content is to progress down the routes/read the story as it almost exclusively appears in text form. Almost all content that can be described as mature/risque appears in later parts of the story and cannot be easily viewed bar fast-forwarding through the game.
