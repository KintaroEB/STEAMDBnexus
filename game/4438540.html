About this gameFull description from Steam+
Limitless Survivor is a incremental roguelite action game with infinite progression. Treasure Hunting Rarely dropped Artifacts grant permanent boosts to your stats and skill performance. Collect more to stack their effects and grow stronger. If farming starts to feel like a chore, you can leave it to auto-play and let it handle the grind for you. Stat Upgrades Use crystals brought back from dungeons to enhance your core stats and extend your exploration time. The deeper you go, the more efficiently you earn crystals. Skill Progression Level up by defeating enemies to learn and upgrade skills. You can learn up to five skills. Max out your favorites or build a balanced loadout — the choice is yours. Randomized Item Affixes Loot powerful items from chests dropped by enemies in the dungeon. Items can roll random affixes, unlocking effects like on-kill explosions, chaining lightning on crit, and massive boosts to attack count and area. Prestige Spend special crystals to perform a Prestige reset. Each Prestige greatly increases artifact drop rates and crystal gains. Repeat it enough, and the numbers go absolutely wild. Achievements Complete achievements to unlock new weapons, skills, and items as you progress. Progress made in the demo will not carry over to the full game.
