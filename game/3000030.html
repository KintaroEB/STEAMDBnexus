About this gameFull description from Steam+
Gaiadon: Eternal Quest is an incremental/idle RPG. Embark on a thrilling journey through the realm of Gaiadon and its continents. Battle monsters, collect loot, upgrade your gear, ascend, find skill books, complete fame trials, add heroes to your arsenal, take part in timed battles, craft, send free heroes on expeditions. Increase world tiers and see how high you can get. Features: Equipment - Level and upgrade your equipment through various rarity tiers amassing synergy bonuses. Craft unique set gems to equip on your Gear. Daily Quests - Complete daily quests to be rewarded with coins, XP, quest tokens, essences. Gain quest XP to reach higher quest level thereby increasing your rewards. Ascension - Ascend to gain more stats and unlock further levels. Transcend to gain even more until you can awaken Apotheosis. There are 3 layers of prestige. Offline Progress - Keep progressing even when you're offline. Pets - Collect and level up over 150 pets dropped from defeated enemies. Upgrade pet rarity to boost your stats. create loadouts for specific battles. Achievements (In-Game and Steam) - Over 175 achievements with each having over 1000 ranks in-game which reward with stats. Also, 60 steam achievements to collect. Continents - Unlock and progress through the various continents of Gaiadon. Incursions - Battle against rotating factions every day to gain points, loots and ranks, collect all of them boss pets. Incursion has its own prestige system. Boss hunt and Onslaughts - Defeat Demons and the mighty Titans. Climb endless onslaught tiers for loot. Fame - Complete fame trials and increase your renown through Gaiadon unlocking hero slots and heroes. Heroes can be equipped to give you more stats. Corrupted Domains: Corrupt your pet and heroes, produce loot of your choice. Skills - Skill Trainer produces skill points for you to learn new active and passive skills. Crafting - Craft superior gears, medallions, skill books, heroes. Expeditions - Send your inactive heroes on expeditions to various locations to fight for you, they collect loot, work towards your achievements, collect unique essences. Seasonal Events - Every other month features a month-long festival, each with its own currency and shop with medal, pet, hero and cosmetics. Craft Elixirs to start any festival. In-Game Time - Moon Phases and seasons, Collect Celestial Tokens to level up mystic moon phases which gives bonuses. This idle game is in early access, with exciting new features on the horizon.
