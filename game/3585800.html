About this gameFull description from Steam+
What is Keep Digging? Keep Digging is a multiplayer digging survival craft game where up to 8 players dig through an underground world, bring materials back home, craft equipment, and dive even deeper. Version 2.0 greatly overhauls the game systems and map. It adds a procedurally generated 5000m underground world, more than 350 items, inventory management, the smelter, forging, enchantments, and more, turning everything you dig up into progress for your character. Dig, Bring It Back, Build Gather ores and materials underground, bring them back to base, and use the smelter to turn them into ingots. Then forge your own equipment using blueprints found while exploring. More than 350 items to discover An inventory system for managing materials and equipment Use the smelter to turn mined ores into ingots Forge equipment from blueprints found underground Equipment, blueprints, and orbs have quality levels, so the same item can perform differently Search for better materials, craft better equipment, and dig even deeper. What you bring back from each dig changes what you can do on the next expedition. Build Your Gear With Enchantments Create mysterious orbs with random effects from Gemstones. By combining orbs and stacking multiple effects, you can build equipment that fits your play style. Random stats are added when creating or combining orbs Enhance digging efficiency, movement, exploration, and more Effect combinations can greatly change equipment performance Keep refining your setup to create your own equipment set Aim for the ultimate gear so you can dig deeper, faster, and stronger. A Procedurally Generated 5000m Underground World In Version 2.0, the underground world has been completely renewed. The 5000m-deep map is procedurally generated, so each playthrough brings different terrain, treasure chests, fossils, and exploration routes. A completely new 5000m-deep map A different underground world every time through procedural generation Many more treasure chests, fossils, and discoveries Digging deeper matters, but securing a route back is just as important This is not just about digging straight down. Explore your surroundings, search for materials and blueprints, and build a route that lets you return safely. Multiplayer for Up to 8 Players Online multiplayer supports up to 8 players. Explore the underground world together with friends, or enjoy the tension of competing for items through PvP. Online multiplayer for up to 8 players Explore the underground world in co-op with friends PvP elements create item-stealing encounters between players Bring equipment and money earned in single-player with you Items found in other players' worlds can also be taken back to your own world Costumes such as samurai and gorilla outfits, along with emotes that have special effects, let you customize your character. Change your look and make underground exploration even more fun. Version 1.0 Legacy Mode Version 1.0 remains playable. Select Legacy Mode from the title screen to enjoy the previous version of Keep Digging. Version 2.0 and Version 1.0 use separate save data, so you can play either version whenever you like.
