About this gameFull description from Steam+
Winds of Valen is a fantasy MMORPG inspired by the golden age of old school MMOs like Old School RuneScape. Explore an open world with meaningful, non-linear progression, where your progression is driven by what you choose to pursue. Non-Linear Open World Step into a living world with no set path. Choose your own adventures, travel in any direction, and play at your own pace. Challenging Bosses Take on challenging, mechanics-driven boss encounters and earn powerful gear. Skill-Based Progression Train and master your skills: Combat Skills: Attack, Archery, Magic, Defence, Evasion, Warding and Health Professions: Mining, Smithing, Fishing and Potion making, deeply tied into your equipment, upgrades, and overall progression. Player-Driven Crafting & Progression Gather resources, smelt ore, and craft powerful gear. Every tool and weapon has purpose. Loot & Reward System Every monster in Winds of Valen drops its own rare, unique item. Rare drop chances are displayed openly and dynamically change to prevent getting too unlucky Every item is balanced, useful, and designed to have its place in the world. Membership Winds of Valen is free to play with many hours of free content to enjoy. However, If you want to expand your journey, purchasing a membership pass grants account-wide access to members-only content, expanded bank storage, and a vote on future game updates. as shown below. Winds of Valen is built for players who enjoy the open-ended, sandbox design of old-school MMOs.
