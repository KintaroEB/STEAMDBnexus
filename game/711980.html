About this gameFull description from Steam+
A crash left you stranded on a planet. Mine and craft your way back into space, build your own starship and explore a vast, dangerous galaxy alongside your crew. For players who love a meaningful survival progression, a unique and elegant building system, and epic space-opera scale. Craft it. Build it. Fly it. Rule the stars! Survive And Reach The Stars - Mine and harvest resources, craft better gear, and build a hovercraft to explore the surface. - Scale up production with an assembler, arc-furnace, and H2 farm. Meet the growing power demands and design an efficient inventory network. - Build your first spaceship and reach orbit! Explore the starting system to acquire a faster drive and better weapons. Manage fuel and oxygen. - Defeat the pirates blocking the Stargate. The galaxy is now yours to explore! Build Anything, from Fighters to Capital Ships - Stretch bricks to the perfect shape and blend grid sizes from 0.125m to 32m to create anything from small starfighters to massive dreadnoughts, mechs to space stations. - Paint everything and apply decals to make your builds truly personal. Share on the workshop or explore the thousands of creations made by the community. - Engineer intricate cockpits, landing gear, and turrets using sliders, rotors, hinges, and pistons. Design the perfect interior for you and your crew down to every detail. - Use systems like artificial gravity, life support, mag-lock, and shipyard. Wire everything together and trigger logic with event gates and sensors. Make your Mark in a Dangerous Galaxy - Pick your fights: liberate sectors from pirates in the outer rim, or defeat factions for control toward the core. Come prepared! - Visit space stations to trade goods and take on missions. Build the right ship for the job. - Be the captain: hire crewmembers from several colorful alien races. - Fly seamlessly from orbit to surface, explore planets and asteroid rings. Find your own place in the galaxy!
