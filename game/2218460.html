About this gameFull description from Steam+
Climb, tumble, and rage — the ruthless obstacle race for bunnies with nerves of steel. Play alone or online against up to 8 players: whoever reaches the top wins. Everyone else… falls down in spectacular fashion. Master dangerous parkour routes, dodge nasty traps, and jump with the precision of a bunny on espresso. One wrong step, one jump too early and boom, back to the start. Challenge your friends, chase each other through chaotic levels, and fight for a spot on the global leaderboard. Only the fastest earn the title “Golden Bunny” complete with an epic fireworks show. Features Challenging climbing and parkour levels with fair checkpoints Online multiplayer for up to 8 players Global leaderboard with time comparison Traps, checkpoints, and wonderfully frustrating “One more try!” moments included Average playtime: 60–120 minutes… or hours? (highly dependent on fall rate) How far will YOU get — and how many times will you have to start all over again?
