About this gameFull description from Steam+
ALTF4? The Way You Play Games Is Simple!! Become a somewhat clumsy Knight and overcome Trap!! But the way to get there won't be simple. Will you be able to overcome the various traps and irritating terrains in your way? Test your reflexes! There is no SAVE in this game!! It's full of exciting background music and annoying traps! Don't you feel like you can play better next time every time your character dies? The various traps will keep driving you to death! Imagine how good it will feel to overcome those traps and finally clear the game! Once you get your first clear, why don't you aim for clearing the game at one go? You can also play together with Remote Play Together.
