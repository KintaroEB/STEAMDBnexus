About this gameFull description from Steam+
Start your path as a simple human — and build your own story of becoming a true mage. Plan your daily routines, manage carefully crafted lists of actions, and steadily improve every aspect of your magical life. Explore a rich and expanding world full of opportunities. Unlock new actions, resources, and systems at your own pace — and discover how much there is to unfold. Every hour brings something new: a potion to brew, an upgrade to earn, a choice that shapes your next step. Design your workflow by combining manual tasks and automated sequences. Experiment with dozens of magical materials, learn powerful spells, and customize your home with furniture that provides meaningful bonuses. Whether you play actively or prefer passive growth, your journey continues — even while you're away. 🔹 Core Features Smart Progression Start with just a handful of basic actions — and gradually unlock a vast network of upgrades, abilities, systems, and tools to manage them. Action & List System Plan, combine and automate your daily routines through a modular system of reusable action lists. Min-max or just let it flow. Crafting & Alchemy Craft dozens of materials, potions and unique accessories to improve your long-term growth. Procedural Map Exploration Explore an ever-changing map to discover rare herbs, treasure fragments and one-of-a-kind items with permanent effects. Magic Learn and upgrade your own arsenal of spells — from passive effects to direct magical actions. No Prestige System Your progress is permanent. No forced resets or repetitive loops — only forward momentum. Flexible Skill Tree Specialize your mage with a vast skill tree that adapts to your current needs — you can reassign skills during your journey to suit different strategies. Customizable Home Build and upgrade your private space with furniture that boosts your actions, automation, and progression efficiency. This is not just a game about numbers going up — it’s about unfolding systems, planning, discovery, and magical self-growth. And there's always more waiting to be revealed. Your journey awaits!
