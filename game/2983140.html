About this gameFull description from Steam+
Trials of Innocence is an indie courtroom detective game developed by C&D Games in China. This Game is a remake of a mobile game originally released in China in 2015. Enjoy a fully localized experience crafted entirely by a talented Chinese team! ◆In a modern, fictional courtroom, players will defend clients, delve into mysteries, uncover the truth behind murder cases, and exonerate their clients. Play as rookie lawyer Luna Ray or seasoned attorney Charlie Drake, exploring and cracking cases occurring in various unique locations: ◆Chinese-style Haunted House ◆Serene Mountain Village ◆High-Tech Research Center ◆Mine Park ◆World Games Venue ◆Skyscraper ◆With a script exceeding 500,000 words, the game offers a deeply immersive and satisfying experience. ◆◆Over 100 unique dialogues can be triggered by showing the lawyer's license! “Anyway, just present the lawyer's license!” Gather information through testimonies in court and gradually uncover the truth! ◆Use evidence to expose the lies of witnesses! Beyond traditional trials, there are several other exciting gameplay elements: ◆Special Performance: Brainstorm! When you're at a dead end, review all known facts. The key clue might already be within your grasp! ◆Special Mode: Confrontation! Engage in direct confrontations with the prosecutor. It's thrilling to debunk the prosecutor in one go, but it's also fun to see the imaginative wrong answers! ◆Special Mode: Mind Link! Connect keywords related to the case to piece together the truth. The game adopts an anime-inspired art style iterated on the basis of the original artwork. Characters are animated using frame-by-frame techniques instead of keyframe animation, making their movements more fluid and expressive while staying true to the original style. We hope you enjoy our art style! ◆Full anime immersion ◆Nearly 50 fully remixed original tracks! ◆Engaging sound effects ◆Voice acting in Chinese, Japanese, and English Producer, Director:话龙（韩子彧） Executive Producer, Head Writer:黑猫（王欣） Art Director:发面儿 Associate Head Writer:影子/雨宮影 Programmer:杀某 Character Concept Artist and Animator:发面儿、灰野龟、冷不丁、空言、冰绛、JiuNotte、藤 Environment Concept Artist:良/Leung、魔都大湿 Prop Concept Artist:周醒 CG Concept Artist:山茶、一百字、起床、周醒 UI Designer:zizi、战斗妖精雪疯 Marketing Artist:发面儿、良/Leung Directing Team:盘子、Lysandre、姜山、启人、网络尚未中断 Japanese Localization Lead and Supervisor:影子/雨宮影 Deputy Japanese Localization Lead:Minana37 Japanese Localization Team:黑羽白夜、ホラボ、咔咔、ひっか English Localization Supervisor:黑猫（王欣） English Localization Lead:程孟巧 English Localization Team:Ester、韩晓珍、金多银、邬丽洁 Music Designer:ino-Studio Sound Effect Designer:宇成 Voice Acting Team:北斗企鹅（北京）文化传播有限公司 Voice Director:山新 Voice Production Coordinator:吉吉 Recordist:郝敏、刘慧敏、刘乐鑫 Voice Actors:山新、皇贞季、兰酒、宋文凯、糖饴、关云月、蔺婵婵、真心、李铫、来晓风、杨凝、李绍刚、孙一鸣、陌上、周健、逝枫、云安、归什、粟大伟、言灵
