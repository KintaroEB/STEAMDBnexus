About this gameFull description from Steam+
What happens after Plague Inc. wipes out the world? Decades after the Necroa Virus ravaged humanity, a few survivors emerge. Build a settlement, explore, scavenge resources and expand as you shape your post-apocalyptic society. The world is green and beautiful, but danger lurks in the ruins! After Inc: Revival is the next game from the creator of Plague Inc. - one of the most popular games ever with over 190 million players. Brilliantly executed with beautiful graphics and critically acclaimed gameplay – After Inc: Revival is engaging and easy to learn. Build multiple settlements and gain abilities across multiple campaigns to lead humanity out of the darkness. Public Service Announcement: Unlike our other games, I’m pleased to say that After Inc: Revival is not based on any real-world situation. No need to start worrying about a real-life zombie apocalypse yet… Emerge into a broken world and build a thriving society in each new level Discover, scavenge, and harvest new resources Build firepits, kitchens, workshops and more to appease your settlers’ needs Make difficult decisions - Are children an unaffordable luxury? Are dogs pets or a food source? Democracy or Authoritarianism? Train and upgrade Fighters. Exterminate the zombie infestations. Defend humanity! Build multiple settlements in a persistent world and develop a civilization Find the truth of what happened to the world across 3 campaigns spanning 28 levels Take on new content every day in Expeditions to unique, dangerous regions Evolve your civilization with 11 Settlement Leaders, 16 Population Traits, and over 700 rewards Test yourself with community leaderboards, as well as 9 difficulty levels, from Casual all the way to Mega-Brutal 6 Sandbox mode: Build endlessly without Goals or Impatience Escape Hatch mode: Replay remixed versions of each level Custom Scenarios: Use the powerful Scenario Creator and share your scenarios with the world Steam Workshop support: Browse and play scenarios made by other players Explore a beautiful post-apocalyptic United Kingdom Abandoned Kingdom
