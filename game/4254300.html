About this gameFull description from Steam+
Swords And Magic: Path Of Conquest provides a unique experience of Real-Time Auto Combat with Team RPG elements set in a fantasy world, where you are the leader of your Faction. Recruit Adventurers, choose their path of Talent and Ability to work best with your combat strategy, challenge Dungeons and Bosses, grow more powerful and reveal the mystery of the world. Fantasy World In Realistic Graphics Team RPG In Swords And Magic: Path Of Conquest, you are able to assemble a team of powerful characters and command them into battle! You can progress characters in the direction you like, equip them with Gears and Weapons, choose different paths for each character's Talent and Combat Ability, and develop your own strategy! Roguelite Dungeon Aassign your characters to a Dungeon Raid, entering different dungeons, each with unique design and monsters to fight! Dungeon combat is separated into different waves of enemies. After claiming victory on each wave, you can choose to take powerful Collections (which work only in dungeons) and progress further, or withdraw with what you have obtained. Before each wave, unlike pre-designed wave monsters, you can choose who you want to fight next! Monster Codex Know yourself, know your enemies. Collect information on those whom you have defeated, plan your strategy, set your goal, and fight on! Crafting Craft your own equipment, harvest resources from dungeons and the wild, then forge your path to glory!
