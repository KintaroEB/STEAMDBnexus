About this gameFull description from Steam+
Explore 'Wonderia', a unique open-world game set on an alien planet. Your journey combines survival, creativity, and exploration in a dynamic, procedurally generated environment. Discover a vast, ever-changing sandbox world. Uncover resources and secrets hidden across diverse landscapes, each offering unique challenges and opportunities. Build and customize homes for space travelers, influencing them to make this world their new home. Your constructions directly affect the planet's landscape and ecosystem. Delve into mysterious dungeons scattered across the planet. Navigate through complex labyrinths and uncover hidden treasures in these subterranean realms. Cultivate an alien garden, planting seeds collected on your travels. Delve into the art of fishing, capturing exotic aquatic species in hidden nooks of the planet. Experiment with your harvests and catches in the kitchen, concocting culinary creations that offer surprising benefits. Enjoy 'Wonderia' solo or with up to 7 others in multiplayer mode. Work together or compete in constructing, exploring, and surviving on this alien planet. 'Wonderia' offers a blend of adventure and creativity, where your decisions shape the world and your experience in this alien landscape.
