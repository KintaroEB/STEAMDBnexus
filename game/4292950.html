About this gameFull description from Steam+
特别说明： Shao Song I is a live-action interactive narrative game adapted from Shao song, the literary work penned by Liudan Paishui, an author on Qidian Chinese Network under Yuewen Group. All storylines, historical timelines and plot developments are fictionalized interpretations. About the Game： You take on the role of Zhao Jiu, a college student who accidentally travels back to the Southern Song Dynasty and becomes Zhao Gou, the controversial Emperor Gaozong of Song. You inherit a crumbling realm in turmoil: the humiliating Jingkang Incident has fallen, the Central Plains lie occupied, foreign invaders loom on all borders, and most court officials only advocate fleeing south for self-preservation. A treacherous path lies ahead. Will you drive out the invaders and launch a northern campaign to restore the glory of the Song Dynasty, or yield to fate and scrape by for the rest of your days? This pivotal turning point in history rests entirely in your hands to shape! Win Ministers Over with a Jade Belt, Win Hearts with a Single Duck The imperial court is split into cliques vying for power. As the reigning emperor, how will you wield your wit to rally supporters, balance rival officials, and forge an imperial legacy all your own? The Eternal Regret of Jingkang When you cross paths with loyal, valiant generals including Yue Fei and Han Shizhong, will you stand side by side with them to defy the odds and march north? Can you fulfill their unwavering loyalty and erase this age-old national sorrow? Immersive Imperial Experience Step into the emperor’s shoes to govern the court, lead armies into battle and reclaim lost lands. Jurchen troops press at the borders, and wars rage endlessly. Will you draw your sword to purge treacherous courtiers, or devote all your strength to protect loyal patriots? The fate of the dynasty hinges on every single choice you make. Live-Action Scenes & Star-Studded Cast All scenes are shot on custom-built real-life sets. Renowned actors Xu Yue, Zhang Xiaochen, Zuo Xiaoqing, Zhang Qi and more join the cast, delivering vivid portrayals of court intrigue and battlefield heroism. They stand beside you through this chaotic era, marching forth toward a blood-soaked homeland together. Over 10 Hours of Live-Action Story Content Countless branching paths unlock diverse endings, with secret conclusions and surprise plot twists waiting to be uncovered. The game blends QTE interactions and SLG, pulling you straight into the action rather than letting you merely observe from afar. Every decision you make alters the course of the northern campaign, shifts the fate of the Song Dynasty, and forges your one-of-a-kind Shaosong legend! About Us Greetings, fellow lords! We are the development team behind Shao Song I. We are a group of IP fans deeply moved by the core belief from the original novel: The Song may fall, but the realm shall not perish. This shared passion for the story brought us together to explore new possibilities blending history with interactive storytelling. We aim for Shao Song I to be far more than a live-action interactive title. We hope it serves as a window for global players to experience the unique devotion to nation and unyielding integrity found within Eastern history, delivering an unforgettable journey through the Song Dynasty for all interactive narrative enthusiasts. As loyal fans of the original novel, we have refined this game with utmost reverence. Liudan Paishui, the original author, has also fully supported our creative process to preserve the authentic soul of the story. Here, you will embody Emperor Gaozong Zhao Gou, live through the chaotic age of shattered homelands, uphold the oath that The Song may fall, but the realm shall not perish, and personally avenge the eternal grievances of history. The game is still undergoing continuous optimization. Add it to your Wishlist to show your support. We look forward to meeting you soon to ride the tempest of the Shao Song I era side by side.
