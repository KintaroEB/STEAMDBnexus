About this gameFull description from Steam+
Embark on a heartwarming adventure with Sushi Bot! Decorate your restaurant with game-changing furniture, serve customers quickly to earn a top rating, and complete quests to learn more about your neighbors and discover the mysterious spirits that watch over Rolling Hills. DECORATE AND DELIVER Customize your restaurant with furniture that grant practical bonuses and express your style, then serve customers quickly to earn coins and complete quests. LEARN FROM YOUR FRIENDS Build relationships and complete quests to gain new upgrades and abilities, like a helpful mini-robot or the power to stop time. A SPIRITED ADVENTURE As you meet the spirits that guard Rolling Hills, you'll lift the town out of obscurity and gain access to otherworldly challenges with unique rewards, like an underwater restaurant!
