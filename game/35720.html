About this gameFull description from Steam+
Three heroes, one artifact Amadeus the Wizard conjures boxes and planks out of thin air. Pontius the Knight charges through what stands in the way. Zoya the Thief slips past it with rope and arrow. Play together, or on your own 1–3 players, in online or local co-op, and the whole campaign works either way. Play alone and switch heroes freely, or bring two friends and watch each of them reach for a different answer. Puzzles with more than one answer Twenty levels of physics-based puzzles, hazards, enemies and contraptions. Turn goblins to ice with frost arrows, levitate a monster to a great height and then trap it in a box, fly across a chasm on the Kitesail Shield, or slow the world down with the time-bending gravity bubble. A fairytale world worth the trip Travel through a castle by the treacherous sea, a burning desert, snowy ice mountains, and all the way to the insides of a giant worm. Features 1–3 player co-op, online and local Play as wizard, knight and thief, switching freely at any time Twenty levels of puzzles, platforming and combat, with more than one way through most of them Award-winning soundtrack by Ari Pulkkinen
