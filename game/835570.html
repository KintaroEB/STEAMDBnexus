The Three Kingdoms Season Is Now Live! [Newcomer Perks · Massive Rewards] Free Starting Units: Create a character to instantly receive 13 core units. Start at zero cost. Zero-Cost Respecs: Infinite free resets for attributes and skill points before Lv. 200. Carry Mode: The Knight & Squire system is live! Squires can freely borrow max-level, fully equipped units from their Knights, or even use the Knight's Shared Lineup. Graduate to receive a 5-Star Unit Selection Box. Weekly Free Rotation: Experience a top-tier, max-level, fully mastered unit for free every week. Conqueror’s Blade – A Real-Time Tactical Warfare Game on Multi-Civilization Battlefields Step into the boots of a legendary Warlord and lead your troops into cinematic PvP siege warfare on ancient battlefields inspired by world civilizations. Whether you’re a fan of grand-scale sieges, fierce ACT games, or in-depth real-time strategy, Conqueror’s Blade delivers an unmatched battlefield experience that challenges your tactical mind and rewards your leadership skills. Multi-Civilization Battlefields: A Journey Through Time and War Explore ancient battlegrounds inspired by iconic civilizations. From the cliffs of Thermopylae in Sparta to sandstorm-swept deserts along the Silk Road, and the blazing bamboo fortresses of Sengoku-era Japan – every map offers a distinct atmosphere and thrilling historical flavor. Dynamic environments and historically inspired terrain ensure that every fight feels immersive, strategic, and unforgettable Highly Diverse Siege Battles: The Ultimate Test of Strategy and Tactics Take part in truly massive siege battles with thousands of troops clashing on-screen. Use trebuchets to shatter walls, deploy siege towers to breach ramparts, and lead armored infantry through kill zones in fierce close-quarters combat. Each battle unfolds in three tactical phases–siege preparation, front-line assault, and point conquest–mirroring authentic military strategy and testing your command at every turn. Troops Command: A Tactical Symphony of History’s Finest Choose from over 100 historical troops from legendary cultures, including Spartan warriors, Viking raiders, medieval knights, Chinese dynastic warriors, samurai, winged hussars, royal guards, and deadly assassins. Coordinate troop abilities like healing, charging, shield walls, buffs, and explosive attacks to dominate the field. With new troops and maps added regularly, you’ll constantly need to evolve your strategies to stay ahead. Global Esports Arena: The Battlefield of the Brave Step into the Conqueror’s Blade League (CBL) – the ultimate annual esports competition where warlords from around the world clash for glory. With over 1,000 participants and a prize pool worth tens of thousands of dollars, the CBL is where legends are forged! Performance: Optimized for All Warlords With advanced game engine optimization, Conqueror’s Blade delivers stable frame rates – even in massive thousand-troops battles – on low-spec machines. Immerse yourself in the chaos of war without compromising performance.
