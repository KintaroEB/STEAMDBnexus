About this gameFull description from Steam+
Last Pirates: Die Together is an online co-op horror experience filled with chaos, monsters, and LOTS OF BOOTY! You and your crew will use your unusually stretchy arms to gather as much loot as you can for your captain. Be mindful of your surroundings though. There are others who mean to keep the treasure for themselves. You land lubbers aren’t that tough! You and up to three of your closest mates will work together, communicate, and survive as you try to make your ol’ captain rich and happy! You are quite an unusual sailor! I bet you can grab just about anything with those pairs of hands. Dismantle doors, grab furniture, or destroy the island for all I care! Just bring that money back aboard or the captain will have our head! Remember, scallywags, you are here for gold! Collect all the booties (and protect your own!), shake things down, and don’t come out empty-handed or no-legged. Feel the adventurous drive push you deeper into the mysterious island, but make sure that at least one of you lives to carry out the gold. Make it back to the ship in one piece, would ya? Avoid all the dangers that the island has in store for you. Play the hero or hide somewhere if you find yourself in a pickle. Or maybe one of your crewmembers can provide a distraction… I’m sure they’ll be fine, trust us! Don’t be afraid to look at Davey Jones’ Locker. We all die anyway, so why not create an adventure worth remembering? Grab your crew, grab some booty, and make a story worth telling those bilge rats outside!
