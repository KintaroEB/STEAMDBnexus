About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dark Hours is a co-op survival horror game for up to 4 players. You and your crew were ready for the perfect heist, until something went terribly wrong. Right after breaking in, the power cuts out and you realize something isn’t right. You’re trapped inside the building you came to rob… and you’re not alone. A monster has taken over the place and you’re its next prey. Will you manage to escape? Will you survive this apocalyptic night? PLAN. BREAK. STEAL. Alone or with friends: Explore 5 environments: an auction house, a museum, a casino, a power plant and a cruise ship Use 50+ heist items: hacking tools, EMPs, scanners, cameras, teleporters, tasers and more. Complete 12+ mission objectives: steal precious artifacts, destroy evidence, blow up the building, escape with the jackpot and more. Steal valuable loot, complete your contracts, and score big! COOPERATE. SURVIVE. ESCAPE. 8 unique monsters, each with their own abilities, strengths and weaknesses. Dynamic co-op gameplay: solve puzzles and minigames, deactivate lasers and security cameras, share (or steal) the loot. Be stealthy and aware of your surroundings: every bad step can attract the monsters. Survive together… or use your friends to get out alive. PLAY. EARN. REPEAT. 3 mafia factions to build your reputation and unlock rare, useful, and fun gear among 50+ items. 100+ customization items to define your own style. 4 difficulty levels, including a Nightmare Mode. Warning: This game contains flashing lights that may make it unsuitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or other photosensitive conditions. Player discretion is advised.
