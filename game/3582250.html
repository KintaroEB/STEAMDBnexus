About this gameFull description from Steam+
Farm to Table is a farming and restaurant management sim where you build a restaurant from scratch and grow food in the backyard. Explore a colourful island where you can grow and gather food, raise animals, go fishing, produce better food with machines and cook delicious recipes for your hungry guests! Build and customise your dream place, hire staff and grow your organic business into a 5-star restaurant! Build From Scratch Build your restaurant from scratch on a coastal hill overlooking a colorful island! Customize each corner of your kitchen and farm, indoors or outdoors. Choose from a wide variety of wall, flooring, window, decoration, and furniture styles. Grow, Raise & Harvest Grow fresh crops and raise animals right by your kitchen! Collect fresh produce and adopt pet companions. One harvest at a time! Cook & Serve Cook and Serve or Sell at your Farmers’ Market. Discover new recipes by exploring ingredients on the island! Create diverse menus, cook, bake, grill and serve flavoursome dishes to raise your reputation and attract even more customers! Gather & Fish Gather all kinds of wild flavours like mushrooms, berries and much more or go fishing in the open seas for rare catches! Produce Get useful machines to turn harvest into more advanced ingredients and unlock new recipes. Manage Manage resources, hire chefs, waiters and farmers, upgrade and grow the reputation of your place.Take it slow on the farm, then pick up the pace in the kitchen, turn your cozy humble spot into a 5-star restaurant!
