About this gameFull description from Steam+
This is a flash-dodging training game specifically designed for VALORANT, featuring nearly all in-game flash effects. It innovatively combines a dot-clicking mechanic, challenging players to precisely hit targets while swiftly avoiding sudden flashes. This dual-tasking gameplay enhances reaction speed and multitasking skills, making it an effective training tool for players.
