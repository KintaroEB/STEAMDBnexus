About this gameFull description from Steam+
MediBang Paint is the definitive illustration and painting app. With over 100 million downloads worldwide, this drawing app is popular with beginners and professionals alike. Its intuitive controls make it easy for anyone to create illustrations and pictures. Enjoy more fun and freedom with this digital painting tool. It offers a professional drawing environment. From sketching and line art to coloring and finishing, you can complete your work in one app. It also comes with a host of professional-grade features, including over 180 brushes, layers, tones, gradients, and fonts. The UI is designed for beginners, making it ideal for those who want to get started with drawing or try out a drawing app. Tutorials, templates, and coloring materials are also available to help even first-time artists feel at ease. The guided drawing process makes it perfect for practice. Practice coloring with this simple coloring book. Learn how to draw characters and backgrounds using templates. Edit and recolor your drawings as many times as you like. Even if you've always wanted to try drawing illustrations but thought it might be difficult, you can start having fun drawing right away. With pressure sensitivity, you can create delicate strokes and nuanced lines with ease. The smooth movement of the pen and brush creates a natural drawing experience, just like drawing on paper.
