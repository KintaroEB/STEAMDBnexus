About this gameFull description from Steam+
Hello, we're Nordic Games Lab, and this is a biathlon simulator game – NGL Biathlon. Race as your favorite athlete, level them up in their career, and compete for the Big Crystal Globe! • Large number of athletes, many real World Cup tracks, all biathlon disciplines • Career mode: play multiple seasons as your athlete, progress from junior to top-level • Customizable tournaments • Create your own athletes: lots of skins for faces, skis, rifles, and other equipment • Biathlon atmosphere: finish with a flag, awards ceremony, spectators on the tracks • Broadcast mode: you can simulate races and entire tournaments
