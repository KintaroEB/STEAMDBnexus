About this gameFull description from Steam+
Run your dream bakery, make friends with adorable animals, collect friendship photos and unique scrapbook elements. Forge ahead, using the power of baking to solve the town’s challenges. Let every animal enjoy its favorite bread—and open up about the secrets they’ve kept hidden. Box Bakery is a cozy and heartwarming simulation game where you step into the shoes of a newcomer baker, running your very own dream bakery. In the charming town of Greenfield, you'll create delightful and inventive breads to meet the tastes of the local residents, all while unlocking new, unique recipes and building the bakery of your dreams. The gameplay is rich and diverse: 🥐 Simulation Management: Greet customers every day, listen to their stories and worries, bake each loaf by hand, and stock your shelves with care. Expand your shop, unlock new equipment, and decorate to your heart’s content. 📖 Collection System: Collect friendship photos and journal elements that capture the small yet meaningful moments you share with the townsfolk. Each conversation deepens your bond, unlocking new stories and surprises. 🗝️ Puzzles & Quests: Help solve the worries and problems of the townspeople, from sourcing mysterious ingredients to discovering hidden recipes. Every quest brings new inspiration and challenges to your bakery. When it comes to the story atmosphere, Box Bakery emphasizes warmth, comfort, and a strong sense of community. Every customer has a story—perhaps a little fox searching for the taste of their childhood, or a bear longing to reconcile with their family. Amidst the comforting aroma of bread, you’ll become the “heart baker” for the town’s residents, helping them open up and rediscover the small joys of life. There’s no intense competition or stressful time crunch here—just a peaceful, cozy bakery and a heart that’s always ready to listen. Whether you prefer a leisurely business simulation or heartwarming stories, you’ll find your own slice of “bread time” here at Box Bakery.
