About this gameFull description from Steam+
A bullet-building roguelite where victory comes before you pull the trigger Defend your home from shadowspawn invaders in this bullet-building roguelike! When a mystical rift unleashes waves of monsters, arm your Arcane Rifle and craft the ultimate magic ammunition to vanish them. From the Creators of "Endgame of Devil" comes their latest masterpiece! A gunslinger who crafts magic into every bullet! Unleash the Storm & Gather Power! Every enemy you destroy releases the energy you’ll need to forge new bullets. Clear waves, harvest power, and keep your finger on the trigger. The real battle begins when the smoke settles. Forge New Bullets Use the energy you’ve gathered to craft over 220 unique bullets, each with distinct effects and elemental powers. Your next bullet could redefine your entire build. Mix & Fuse for Maximum Impact Rearrange your bullets in the Arcane Magazine to trigger powerful synergies. Chain effects, amplify damage, build wild elemental combos and triple merge three identical bullets to get a new and more powerful version. Strategy starts long before you pull the trigger. Seal Your Fate At the end of every wave, pick from 150+ magical Seals to modify your bullets and abilities. Each run is a new experiment. Mix effects, evolve your loadout, and craft your build to become an unstoppable force. -Game Features •3 Unique Playable Characters with distinct mechanics •220+ Bullet Types across 6 elemental attributes •150+ Magical Seals with diverse enhancement effects •Bullet Fusion System merging three identical bullets for empowered versions •13 Ancient Runes for strategic permanent upgrades •8 Magic Carpets offering unique gameplay styles •15 Waves of Escalating Challenges with Omens & Megastructures •Multiple Difficulty Levels catering to both casual and hardcore players •Dimension Events to encounter mysterious visitors between battles •Endless Synergy Possibilities between bullets, seals, and runes •Exquisite Pixel Art with impactful visual effects -AI Content Disclosure All the programs, designs and artworks of the game are all created manually and contain no AI elements.
