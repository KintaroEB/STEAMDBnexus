About this gameFull description from Steam+
IGRS 13+ The realm of Teleria trembles beneath the shadow of the Dark Lord Siroth. You have been called upon by the Arbiter, the immortal guardian of Teleria, to stop him. Heed the call! Dive into a deep turn-based RPG with 8 exciting game modes and extensive character customization. Enjoy a story-driven campaign, competitive PvP clashes with players from across the world, and constant new challenges with our stream of updates. KEY GAME FEATURES SUMMON 800+ CHAMPIONS Collect hundreds of unique Champions from 16 playable factions and combine them into unstoppable teams to take down your enemies. Dark elves, Ogryn warlords, Lizardmen, and more can all join your ranks – if you earn their loyalty. MASTER TACTICAL TURN-BASED GAMEPLAY Kit out your Champions with epic gear you loot along the way and customize their masteries for millions of possible builds. Level up your team and play them your way, unleashing a barrage of powerful skills and game-changing support abilities. PROVE YOURSELF IN 3 PvP MODES Enter the Arena and go head-to-head with other players to unlock special gear and climb up the leaderboards. Then, really put your skills to the test in real-time PvP battles with Live Arena. EXPLORE ENGAGING PvE CONTENT Tear your way through a visually stunning campaign accompanied by a rich, fully-voiced dark fantasy narrative. Conquer the forces of Darkness as well as 13 challenging Dungeon bosses, fast-paced Faction Wars, and the notorious Doom Tower to earn valuable loot. TEAM UP WITH FRIENDS Join forces with your fellow players to take on Clan Bosses for exclusive gear, Shards to summon new Champions, and more. Once you’re strong enough, you can also compete against 4 other Clans in the epic Hydra Clash. GET LOST IN THE LORE Learn about the inhabitants of Teleria through their unique backstories as well as the world itself. Immerse yourself even further by checking out the animated limited series RAID: Call of the Arbiter on YouTube.
