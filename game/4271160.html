About this gameFull description from Steam+
Enemies constantly run toward your walls. You buy defenders, upgrade production, and watch the waves grow. But they can’t reach you. There is a river of lava at the end. Every enemy falls into it. Every time. Your conquest map is your skill tree Zero stress gameplay. No losing.  No pressure.  No "restart the level" moments. Just numbers going up and enemies running. Be the Zero Stress King This is a tower defense where you already won. Sit back, relax, and enjoy the lava.
