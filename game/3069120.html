About this gameFull description from Steam+
Story You’re entering your junior year of college. Your first two years went by quickly— You were an average college student, living an average college life. However, after almost getting hit by a car, you meet a mysterious woman who says you’ll die if you don’t find your soulmate soon! Now you have to juggle school, work, and your impending doom?! Fate works in mysterious ways. You bump into your childhood best friend who you haven’t seen in years. Your friend’s sister takes a keen interest in you. The senior you occasionally bump into helps you get an internship. And even the CEO at your new job seems to have her eyes on you… Who will be your soulmate, and who holds a hidden vendetta against you? In this twisted, passionate game of emotions, what will come to light? Love, possessiveness, manipulation, and obsession swirl together… Will you discover the truth behind it all? Characters Selene Yan (*First name can be customized) A junior at Ocean City University. You’re a soft and sweet person with a big heart who’s always willing to stand up for what they believe in. Kind and empathetic, your caring nature is adored by those around you. Nyx Xia Your childhood best friend. You two previously had an arranged marriage, but that fell through pretty quickly. You ended up moving away and not seeing her for years. She’s changed— She’s bubbly and energetic, but sometimes grim… Maybe there’s more to her than the eye can see. Iris Wen The student council president. She’s a year above you and about to graduate. Iris helps you with an internship opportunity. She’s nice and friendly, but don’t be fooled— her gentle nature is simply a façade.  She struggles to let anyone in, keeping her heart locked up in a cage. Perhaps there’s a hidden side to her she’s keeping a secret… Eleanor Si She’s your classmate from another university, and the twin sister of your friend. Eleanor’s always eager to learn more, so she’s agreed to attending lectures in place of her sister. She’s been interested in you since the day you two met. Victoria Qi The CEO of Huanyu. She’s a genius who graduated with dual degrees. Victoria pays extra close attention to you, making sure you’re thriving at work. She’s strict and dedicated to her job, often appearing impersonal to others. Details Love Curse is a lesbian visual novel spanning across a variety of genres, including romance, fantasy, and historical elements. The final version of the game will be paid. The storyline will span over approximately 200,000 words, with over 40 exclusive CGs (chibi versions included), 4 romanceable characters, and 7 supporting characters. More Information 1. The romanceable characters are not perfect. They each have obvious negative personality traits which will be revealed during the game. 2. The game may contain a few bugs. These can be reported to the production team via “Option.”
