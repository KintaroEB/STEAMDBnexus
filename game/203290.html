About this gameFull description from Steam+
Do you have what it takes to train like a U.S. Army Soldier? Take on the role of an 11B Infantryman practicing combat maneuvers at JTC Griffin, a fabricated training MOUT (military operations on urban terrain) environment. This training is crucial to your success as part of a Long Range Combined Arms – Recon (LRCA-R) unit, a full spectrum capable team that embarks on special operations missions behind enemy lines. Key Features You are a U.S. Army Soldier Two Game Modes with 4 objective types Character and Weapon customization Teamwork is Critical Train in Shoot Houses Mission Editor and Steam Workshop
