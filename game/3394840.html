About this gameFull description from Steam+
Embark on a covert mission to the dinosaur theme park Prehistoria, where you must fight to survive in this Action-Adventure Survival Horror game. Navigate diverse biomes, solve intricate puzzles, and uncover the secrets hidden within the park. With danger lurking at every turn, shoot, strategize, and escape before the park becomes your grave. Play Classic Mode or Survival Mode Explore a fully realized dinosaur theme park with two distinct gameplay experiences: Classic Mode: The original Clawed survival horror experience. Survival Mode: Survive against hunger, thirst, dynamic weather, a full day/night cycle, and random dinosaur encounters as you complete your mission. EXPLORE THE PARK'S GROUNDS Explore Prehistoria, a rundown dinosaur park that has seen better days. Brave deadly dinosaur enclosures, delve into the underground tunnels, and aquatic environments. The entire park is yours to uncover as you make your way through this action-adventure, survival-horror. FIGHT DEADLY PREDATORS Face off against terrifying dinosaurs, from the ruthless Deinosuchus to the swamp dwelling Spinosaurus, Prehistoria is home to many prehistoric creatures. SOLVE INTRICATE PUZZLES In traditional survival-horror fashion, your journey through the park will test your mind and wits as you work to solve challenging puzzles and difficult challenges Co-Op Play! Fight dinos, explore the park and solve puzzles with your teammates! Invite your friends to join you on your Journey through Prehistoria.

Content notice

This is a co-op survival horror game in which players combat various dinosaur species using firearms and other weapons. Impacts result in a mild level of blood appearing on the dinosaurs and surrounding environment. Some areas of the game feature human remain, skeletal remains, and dinosaur carcasses, adding to the intensity of the atmosphere
