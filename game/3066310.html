About this gameFull description from Steam+
Final Outpost is a survival strategy base-builder. You lead the last survivors of the apocalypse. Assign roles, craft tools, and manage resources carefully. Construct buildings, level up your citizens, and fortify your town against hordes of zombies. How many days can you survive? Choose from 10+ job roles for each of your citizens, and watch them get to work! Construct and upgrade 12+ building types and expand your sprawling settlement. Build vital resource storage, production buildings and housing for new survivors. Deck out your defences to keep 5+ zombie types away from your walls. Start out with a single knife and progress to mowing down hordes with machine guns, crossbows and snipers. Feed your hungry citizens as your Outpost expands. Begin farming wheat in your fields to overcome the dwindling deer population outside your walls. Survive harsh droughts, cold winters, and unforgiving thunderstorms with simulated weather and seasons. Upgrade your citizens’ skills to gather, defend and repair more efficiently.
