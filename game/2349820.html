About this gameFull description from Steam+
Game Info Class is in session! Hero's Land's brand-new map, "Brawl Legends", is officially open! Engines are revving, fists are flying, and the schoolyard is your new battleground. In this arena, no victory is completely safe, because a bitter defeat is just the setup for an epic comeback! Well kid, are you ready to rule the school? Endless resources and mobs await outside the safety zone. Equip your finest build from stash and inventory, selling loot to level up fast. Hack 'n' Slash your way out! Survive and thrive with the hero of your choice. Take on mobs and bosses to loot epic weapons and gear. Always be prepared for PvP situations that may break out at any time. Heroes will face substantial losses of levels upon death, with the peril of losing their gear and weapons on treacherous maps. Yet, the riskiest zones yield the most incredible rewards—the longer you last, the greater your potential gains. Stake it all to secure the best gear unlock heroes, pets, and talents, making you even more formidable in your next fight! Dive into the action with our game's rich lineup of PVP events, from battle royales to guild wars. Tailor your inventory on the fly with various weapons and gear to suit every twist and turn of battles. Leverage unique buildings like Boost Pads, Sentries, Traps, and more to outmaneuver opponents. Create your combination of survival and destruction! Team up in dungeon raids and other engaging game modes, or ally with friends to build a guild and reign supreme. Plus, bring up to three pets, each with unique skills, to bolster your strength and strategy on every adventure.
