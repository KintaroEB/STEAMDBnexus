About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Full Casino and Resort Tycoon Experience 3D graphics. Deep systems. Massive customization and creativity. Extensive mods. AND full soundtrack, from world-renowned composer Jerry Martin! You are the Casino. Does the House Always Win? You set the odds! Thieves, cheaters, and criminals can tilt the odds against you! Deter them with cameras and interrogation rooms! If you want to manage every last chip in the casino's vault, micro-manage the games & odds on slot machines, keep tabs on every deck of cards, or even design the ultimate high-efficiency staff schedule & security patrol scheme -- you can do all of that, and a lot more! Workshop Support Everything from custom pictures, floors, walls, slot machine games, TV videos, and even code! Share your casinos and mods on the workshop! Get Creative - Customize & Design Every Aspect This isn't a one size fits all kind of game. Build luxury hotels or cheap no-frills casinos. Choose every finish, from the walls & flooring styles, the lighting, the counter-top & bar-top materials, even the restroom stalls. After building your huge resort, walk around it with our first person mode! Huge Number of Building Options! - Slot Machines - Table Games - Sports Betting - Jackpot Displays - Bingo - Theatres - Dressing Rooms - Dance Floors - Gyms - Single & Multi-Guest Bedrooms - Luxury Rooms with Wet Bars & Hot Tubs - Buffets - Bars - Vending Machines - Lounges - Plants - Signs - Wall Decor - Lights & Neons - Themed Decor - Computer Rooms - Meeting Rooms - Conference Halls - ATMs & Cashier Cages - Security Cameras - Interrogation Rooms
