About this gameFull description from Steam+
A visual novel about a brother and sister who return to an island after losing their parents and confront a huge force that lurks in the sun. FEATURES 2+ hours of story Full Japanese voice acting by seasoned professionals SUMMARY Seiran Island. Even here in paradise, there are those who work in the shadows to bring it down... United, these misfits stand side by side as the secret society NLNS. As their founder and head honcho, I, Junnosuke Tachibana, won’t stop until we’ve crushed this crazy law once and for all! I’ve even found a benefactor in this crusty old rich guy, name unknown... He’s hooked us up with a secret base and a buttload of cash already. In return, he wants us to find someone and “protect her at all costs.” Apparently, she holds the key to our mission’s success...? In which case, there’s only one thing to do: locate this mystery girl. And all while keeping the SHO off our scent, too... Make no mistake! I’ll stop that law in its tracks, just wait and see! CHARACTERS Nanase Katagiri (Voice: Hitomi Yanagi) The campus’s resident baddie, rumored to be up to constant miscellaneous mischief by the student body at large. The reality is that she’s still very much a good girl at heart, but uses these rumors to her and Junnosuke’s advantage to get out of unwanted sticky situations. Hinami Watarai (Voice: Shino Amekawa) Petite upperclassman who’s actually in her third and final year. Despite appearances, her short stature belies a big heart and a motherly knack for doting on others. Asane Tachibana (Voice: Soramame) Junnosuke’s younger sister and most sympathetic supporter. Although somewhat of a recluse, she has a wild side to her, too. Junnosuke Tachibana The game’s protagonist.
