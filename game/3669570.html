About this gameFull description from Steam+
Alchemy Factory is a medieval-themed automation game. Start with a small shop and team up with friends. Use conveyor belts, pipes, and magical devices to build automated production lines, then stock your shelves with potions, jewelry, and relics. Fulfill commissions, amass wealth, build your own medieval alchemy factory, and become a renowned alchemy tycoon! Let Conveyor Belts Drive Everything In Alchemy Factory, conveyor belts connect every corner of your factory, carrying materials and products around the clock. Grind, extract, distill, smelt, and process materials. Link devices into complete production chains and automate every tedious step of alchemy. Balance inputs and outputs, tune production, clear bottlenecks, and keep your factory running efficiently. Build a Multi-Level Alchemy Factory Your factory does not have to stay on a single floor. Freely arrange conveyor belts, pipes, devices, and your shop. Build production chains at different heights and in every direction, keeping resources flowing efficiently through a three-dimensional layout. As your factory expands, extend production upward or downward and integrate scattered functional zones into a tightly connected automation network. Ultimately, you can create a vast multi-level factory with every floor working together. Produce, Sell, and Expand Production is only the beginning. Stock your shelves with potions, jewelry, and relics, serve customers, and complete commissions. As sales become automated, you can focus more on planning your factory, researching recipes, and scaling up. Use the coins you earn to buy land, research equipment, and upgrade technology, further boosting efficiency and capacity, driving sales, and setting your factory on a cycle of continuous growth. Build with Friends Plan production lines, build your shop, and manage logistics with friends as you create a sprawling alchemy factory together. Whether you excel at optimizing production lines, mastering management sims, or designing efficient spaces, you will find a role that suits you in co-op. For long-term play with friends, you can also host a dedicated server to keep the factory running, ready for everyone to return and continue building at any time. Get Started Fast with Blueprints Alchemy Factory supports Steam Workshop! Subscribe to blueprints shared by other players, from beginner-friendly starter layouts to complex production plans for veteran players. These blueprints can help you learn Alchemy Factory's production systems faster and draw inspiration from other players' designs. You can also upload your own blueprints and share your factory layouts with alchemists around the world. V1.0 Available Now Alchemy Factory v1.0 is out now! The full release delivers the Early Access roadmap's major features: the Grand Portal, Wagon Logistics System, rebuilt Reputation System, and revamped shopkeeping! Production, transport, and sales are now more tightly connected. Expand your factory, grow your shop, build your reputation, and forge your own path through alchemy. Create a truly thriving alchemical empire and become a master alchemist whose ventures in potion-making, metallurgy, jewelry, and more bring wealth to rival a kingdom!
