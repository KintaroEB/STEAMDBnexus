About this gameFull description from Steam+
Journey to the West: Idle RPG - Pure Offline Adventure No complex operations required! Auto-battle through the Underworld, traverse the Dragon Palace, and retrieve the True Scriptures with full idle gameplay. Cultivate steadily across the Three Realms, choose from 8 original classes, raise divine gear and pets freely, and refine magical artifacts to break through realms—transform from a mortal to the Fighting Buddha! Experience the most laid-back idle grind while living out your passionate offline Journey to the West dream! 1. 8 Original Classes, 4 Core Roles Covered Abandon classic MMO faction stereotypes and embrace a unique Journey to the West class system: Physical AoE, Magical AoE, Healer/Support, and Seal/Control. With clear role positioning and skills inspired by mythic lore, new players can start with brute force, while veterans can strategize for different idle scenarios. 2. Full Auto Idle Combat - Hands-Free Progression Deeply optimized turn-based idle mechanics let your team fight automatically—level up and farm nonstop without manual input. Maximize idle efficiency: progress effortlessly during work breaks or fragmented time, no need to stay up late grinding. 3. Level-Less Equipment Crafting - Divine Gear Grows With You Say goodbye to the "farm-discard-refarm" gear cycle! Your level 10 starter equipment can evolve into level 130 divine weapons. Unlock full crafting options: enhancement, refinement, socketing, and recasting—freely reforge attribute affixes. In an offline environment, your hardcrafted gear never becomes obsolete—witness every step of your divine gear's growth. 4. Free Pet Cultivation - Mythical Beasts for All Three pet tiers: Common Pets, Mutated Spirit Beasts, and Ancient Divine Beasts—all obtainable through open collection methods. Every player can grind for divine beasts in offline mode. Stable skill slot unlocking ensures even common pets can rival divine beasts with smart skill combinations. 5. Pure Offline, Non-P2W Ecosystem - Grind for Everything No online trading or paywalls—all gear, pets, artifacts, and cultivation resources are earned through idling. Save progress locally and via cloud sync for data security. No server shutdown risks—your cultivation journey is permanently preserved. Grind intensity matches sense of achievement! 6. Limitless Strategic Combinations - Unique Idle Builds Customize your playstyle across 5 dimensions: class talents, gear affixes, cultivation paths, artifact synergies, and pet skills. Create builds like "Burst Damage," "Sustain," or "Control"—add strategic depth to idle gameplay. Every combination carves a unique Journey to the West path—no repetitive gameplay!
