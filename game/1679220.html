About this gameFull description from Steam+
Supports up to four players in online play Over a dozen controllable characters to choose from Over a hundred types of items available for players to choose from Create your own unique build! Side-scrolling roguelite adventure game There are more than a dozen controllable characters in the game each with 1 special attack and 4 skills. Each skill can evolve into 3 variants! You can personalize each character until you find the one that suits you best! Portal Dungeon is a side-scrolling Roguelite game. There are currently eight levels in total, with each level having very different maps, monsters, and bosses. How will you take on these different enemies with their different styles of fighting? There are hundreds of items in the game with no limit to the number of items you can take, so there's no need to worry about carrying to many of any one item. You can equip items as you wish and use them as main items in conjunction with character talents to form your own unique build as you bravely venture forth into the world of Portal Dungeon! Supports single player and up to 4 players connected online. It's more fun to take on the dungeon with friends!
