About this gameFull description from Steam+
A Relaxing Idle Creature Collector Tiny Monster Haven sits cozily at the bottom of your screen, letting you raise your monsters at your own pace. Become the caretaker of your own Monster Haven and raise a variety of monsters to help run things smoothly. Improve monster stats by feeding them crops and delicious meals. Let them battle or play minigames to earn further rewards such as coins and Monster Essence, with which you can improve your Haven and monsters! Each monster contributes in different ways: some will water and pollinate your crops while others help out with the harvest. Certain monsters even cook meals for your team! Build up Your Monster Team Choose your Starter to take on your role as monster caretaker. Plant many types of crops and watch them grow while you play with your monsters or focus on other things. Once they're ready you can harvest and feed them to your monsters to improve their stats! Cook delicious meals for your monsters by choosing from a wide variety of tasty recipes! These delightful treats will further boost and level-up your monsters, making them more effective in combat and minigames. Harness Monster Essence from defeated wild monsters. Use it to power-up and evolve your monsters or to expand your team with new monster eggs! Embark on a Campaign to become the Best Monster Battler Challenge the Elemental Gurus and other tamers in an expansive mini-campaign. Make the most of your monster team in the challenging 2v2 and 3v3 battles. Become the regional champion by winning the grand tournament! Put your monsters to the test in tactical turn-based combat! Make clever use of varied stamina-based moves with tons of status effects. If your monsters are in a tough spot, perhaps their Overdrive ability can make the difference? Hatch and evolve them all Complete your monster compendium by hatching and evolving over 50 unique monsters. Make your compendium truely shine with 2 Astral color variants per monster! Defeat a wild Astral to pick up an Uncommon or Rare Astral Gem. Give it to an Egg and hatch your own Astral variants. Chill and Customize your Haven Decorate your Haven with tons of unlockable scenery. Scenery isn't just for show, every item you place will improve the efficiency of your monsters and your Haven. Enter your monsters in various minigames such as the Monster Race. Each minigame further enhances a specific skill. Enjoy a Living, Breathing World Experience a seemless day/night cycle and the passing of the 4 seasons. Each season has its own weather patterns such as rain or snow. Be on the lookout for certain wild monsters that only appear at specific seasons or at certain times of the day!
