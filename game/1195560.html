About this gameFull description from Steam+
🛡️ Rise to the destiny of a forgotten kingdom Alek The Lost Kingdom is an open-world role-playing game that immerses you in the universe of Zarabon. You play as Alek, a hero you can embody as both a teenager and an adult, whose choices will shape the kingdom’s future. This world, scarred by war and chaos, awaits you to rise again from its ashes. 🏰 Rebuild, manage... and dominate Build your villages, restore lost cities, and develop your own production chain with the help of your villagers. Exploit the kingdom’s resources: wood, ores, hides, wheat... and transform them to forge armor, weapons, and feed your people. Not to forget special armors and weapons, unique to certain factions or obtained under special conditions. 🍲 Manage needs and professions Your villagers are hungry, work hard, and rely on you. Assign them to specific roles such as woodcutter, hunter, miner, baker, or blacksmith to ensure the survival and growth of your people. Grow your own vegetables, raise animals, cook nourishing meals, and maintain a vital balance between production and well-being. ⚔️ Fight or negotiate with factions Zarabon is home to several races, including humans and goblins. Other peoples will enrich this world in future updates. Forge alliances… or declare war. Each faction has its own equipment, strategies, and ambitions. Your decisions will influence the balance of power and redefine the kingdom’s history. 🧱 Build your empire, defend your people Erect walls, build towers, and raise an army. Recruit warriors in your barracks — strong and effective in close combat — train archers for ranged attacks, and repel invaders. But beware: your villages can be attacked at any time by outside enemies. To turn the tide, use the war table to declare conflicts, plan your offensives, and gain the upper hand over neighboring villages. Your survival will depend as much on your strategy as on your bravery. 💰 Trade, a strategic lever Trade your surpluses, buy what you lack, and strengthen your economy. Trade is dynamic: convoys of horse-drawn carts travel across the kingdom, guided by your drivers to connect your cities or engage in commerce. Each transaction can affect your relations with neighbors. A good deal can spark a powerful alliance... or provoke the wrath of a rival faction. The creatures of Zarabon The world of Zarabon is filled with fearsome creatures: • The Yetis, masters of snowy peaks • The Werewolves, prowling deep forests • The Cave Trolls, guardians of forgotten passages • The Mammoths, titans of frozen plains But some beasts can also become your allies: tame creatures such as the legendary Buckdjeuves, and roam the world atop formidable companions. 🗡️ Evolving hero, exceptional enemies Your character progresses throughout the adventure: upgrade their equipment, unlock new abilities, and grow stronger with every battle. But beware: the world is also filled with formidable bosses that will put your tactical skills to the test. 🌟 A living world, constantly evolving The world of Zarabon evolves with your choices. Nothing is set in stone. Your adventure will have a real impact on the environment, factions, and the balance of power. Alek The Lost Kingdom is a game in constant evolution, fueled by the passion of its community. Join us, forge your legend, and leave your mark on the kingdom of Zarabon.
