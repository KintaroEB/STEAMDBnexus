About this gameFull description from Steam+
Whisper Mountain Outbreak - Friend’s Pass Survive together, even if only one of you owns the game. Whisper Mountain Outbreak - Friend’s Pass lets anyone join a full co-op session without owning the main game. All you need is a Room Code from a friend who owns the full game, then prepare to dive into the horror together. Why This Friend’s Pass is a Game Changer Play the entire isometric PvE co‑op survival horror experience together, without owning the full version. Jump straight into your friend’s co-op lobby, no barriers, just scare-filled teamwork. Sample the solo demo offline to feel the tension and mechanics on your own before diving into co‑op. How the Experience Unfolds with Friend’s Pass Here's how it works: 1. Grab a Room Code from a friend who owns the full game. 2. Download Friend's Pass for free. 3. Play the entire game together! A Friend’s Pass player can: ✅ Join a friend’s lobby ✅ Play solo demo offline (limited levels) A Friend’s Pass player can not: ❌ Host multiplayer lobby ❌ Unlock achievements ❌ Unlock new content ❌ Recorded in the leaderboard ❌ Earn Steam Trading Cards Enter Mt. Bisik's Horror, Co‑op Only Gets Stronger Set in 1998, a mining operation awakens an ancient curse on Mt. Bisik. A mysterious fog brings whispers and monsters, trapping you and your team inside. Together with up to four players, you’ll: Fight off waves of terrifying creatures. Armed with melee and ranged weapons, herbs, and teamwork. Solve environmental puzzles. Unlock doors, find keys, and piece together the curse’s secrets. Scale your abilities. Choose from six agent classes each run, progress skills, craft items, and adapt to mounting threats. Explore tense and claustrophobic terrain. Fog shrouded rooms, limited visibility, and eerie pixel art visuals mixed with 3D environments build constant tension. Download Whisper Mountain Outbreak - Friend’s Pass now, get a Room Code, and join the outbreak for free.
