About this gameFull description from Steam+
THE COZY MMO Villagers & Heroes is a free-to-play fantasy MMORPG where heroic adventure and a welcoming home share the same world. Explore vast realms, battle monsters and bosses, master crafting and gathering, design your homestead, and become part of a thriving village community. Play at your own pace—solo or with friends old and new. EXPLORE A VAST FANTASY WORLD Journey through a wide variety of handcrafted regions filled with quests, hidden corners, memorable characters, dangerous enemies, and powerful bosses. Follow the story across the Seven Realms, explore freely, or pursue the activities you enjoy most. Venture out alone or join other players for Elder bosses, raids, and seasonal event challenges. Villagers & Heroes is focused entirely on cooperative PvE, with no player-versus-player combat. BUILD A PLACE TO CALL HOME Design and furnish your own homestead, cultivate a garden, and raise animals through ranching. Collect recipes and decorations to create a home that reflects your character and your accomplishments. You can also join a shared village where players work together to develop useful facilities and build a welcoming community. Whether you arrive with friends or meet them along the way, there is always a place for you. GATHER, CRAFT, AND TRADE Fish, mine, forage, collect bugs, and gather resources throughout the world. Turn what you find into weapons, armor, meals, furnishings, and other useful creations through smithing, tailoring, cooking, and carpentry. Trade directly with other players or sell your creations through the Auction House. Use the unique Gnogmenting system to combine equipment and runes, preserve your favorite abilities, and shape gear around the way you want to play. CREATE YOUR HERO Choose from ten combat specializations: • Lightning or Fury Warrior • Fire or Ice Wizard • Holy or Shadow Priest • Marksman or Nature Hunter • Earth or Water Shaman Customize your appearance, abilities, equipment, and play style as your hero grows. Discover distinctive outfits and collect more than one hundred mounts ranging from noble horses and magical creatures to fearsome beasts and quirky companions. ONE COMMUNITY ACROSS PLATFORMS Villagers & Heroes brings Windows, iOS, and Android players together in the same live worlds. Adventure with a worldwide community wherever its members choose to play. Created and continually updated by an independent development team, Villagers & Heroes combines the scale and progression of an MMORPG with the warmth of a place you can call home.
