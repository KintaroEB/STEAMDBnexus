About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Lich Queen Valindra has declared war on the people of Neverwinter and it’s up to you and your fellow heroes to push back her army of darkness. Create your very own adventurer and experience the Forgotten Realms like never before. Explore both classic Dungeons & Dragons locations, battle iconic enemies, and uncover the many treasures and secrets these lands hold, whether solo or with friends. Start your adventure now for free! Completely Free-to-Play Explore and defend Neverwinter, one of the most beloved cities in the Forgotten Realms from Dungeons & Dragons, completely free! Dungeons & Dragons Experience Whether you play table-top or immerse yourself in Dungeons & Dragons novels, experience iconic locations, classes and encounters - with both heroes and villains - on your journey through Faerûn. Experience Fast Paced Action Combat Move swiftly and boldly through encounters to maximize your effectiveness. Every attack, ability, and dodge will require planning, reflexes, and precise execution.
