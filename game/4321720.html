About this gameFull description from Steam+
Speedway Challenge 2026 is the next version of the realistic speedway racing series, aimed at both dedicated speedway fans and players seeking dynamic, competitive racing. Take on the role of a rider or manager and experience the thrill of a speedway season. Authentic Speedway Experience Feel the power of brakeless motorcycles, fight for every inch of the track, and make split-second decisions. The driving model combines realism with accessibility, providing satisfaction for both beginners and experienced players. Extensive Gameplay Modes Career Mode (available after in-app purchase) – Develop your rider, sign contracts, improve your skills, and build your position in the world of speedway League Mode (available after in-app purchase) – Select your favorite team, take care of finances and build your dream team Quick Races and Challenges – Instant action without unnecessary preparation Multiplayer Mode – Compete in ranking game with other players and prove who rules the track Play with friends (after in-app purchase) - Improve your online skills by playing with friends Official Content and Realism Participate in competitions inspired by the real world of speedway – featuring authentic tracks, leagues, and riders. Each facility has been meticulously recreated to perfectly capture the atmosphere of speedway arenas. Control, Tactics, and Development Adjust your bike settings, analyze the track surface, and choose the optimal driving path. Success depends not only on reflexes, but also on strategy and skillful financial management. For Fans and New Players Speedway Challenge 2026 offers a clear interface and extensive configuration options, ensuring everyone can find a playstyle that suits them.
