About this gameFull description from Steam+
Summer's Gone is a high-quality, Interactive Visual Novel. This game is about healing, hope, and love. A young man plagued by depression and isolation. The game explores numerous essential topics, including mental health, ambition & love. Depression, shame, loneliness, and not fitting into a social template are some of the primary topics. Much emphasis is placed on worldbuilding, interpersonal connection, and, especially, the characters. Dating, love, and overcoming obstacles together are the premises of Summer's Gone. SG Season 1 features a wide variety of characters who accompany the main character through a new Chapter of his life. The MC has isolated himself for a long time, but is now faced with something that falls outside his comfort zone, challenging him like nothing has before. **College & Sports** Once upon a time, he was the star player on the high school basketball team, and now the college tryouts are just a few weeks away. But there's something in the back of your mind... Why couldn't you choose a college? Why did you have to go to the ZPR college? Mysteries are about to unfold, and comfort zones are to be shattered. Summer's Gone - Season 1 is interactive! Your choices matter! *"Homesickness for a place I've never been."*
