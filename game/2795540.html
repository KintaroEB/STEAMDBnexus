About this gameFull description from Steam+
<SEARCH, FIGHT, AND ESCAPE> The Midnight Walkers is a hardcore PvE zombie extraction FPS. Play in Solo or Trio Mode as you search for valuable loot, gear up, fight off zombies, and make your escape. Escape successfully and you can use the materials you find to craft items or buy and sell more powerful equipment. Die, however, and you will lose everything you collected. <LIBERTY GRAND CENTER> Liberty Grand Center, a massive complex in Golden County, features a different theme and unique loot on every floor, including a hospital, shopping mall, casino, and more. Zombies are not the only threat lurking inside. As time passes, toxic gas spreads through randomly selected floors, forcing you to move between levels using elevators and stairwells. <CHOOSE YOUR CLASS> Brick, a steadfast warrior armed with a massive hammer and heavy armor, Crow, an agile assassin who catches enemies off guard with swift movements and deadly daggers, Lockdown, a marksman who uses a bow to strike enemies from a safe distance, Bartender, a support specialist who mixes cocktails to heal and empower allies, Choose the class that best suits your playstyle. <FACE YOUR ENEMIES> Every zombie in this place is deadly. Even the Walker, the weakest of them all, can become a serious threat when it surrounds you with the horde. Beyond the relentless Runners that pursue survivors without mercy, even deadlier mutations await. But no matter what you encounter, the greatest threat will always be the other survivors. Fight alone or alongside your teammates, defeat your rivals, and claim even more loot. Golden County’s greatest show—and its only show! We look forward to seeing what you can do in The Midnight Walkers.
