About this gameFull description from Steam+
In 2030, the Beilan Island Incident erupted, triggering environmental deterioration that threatened humanity's survival and intensified global conflicts, ultimately leading to a worldwide war. Now, after years of relentless warfare, you take on the role of a Commander under the employment of Griffin & Kryuger Private Military Contractor. With a squad of Tactical Dolls, you will navigate the power struggles between factions, strategically deploy your forces, and restore order and stability to a war-torn world. Girls' Frontline is a turn-based strategy RPG that offers: Hundreds of Unique Tactical Dolls Recruit and command Tactical Dolls, each representing different firearms. With stunning artwork, full professional voice-overs, and distinct personalities, these girls will stand by your side in battle! An Expansive Storyline & Immersive Worldbuilding Set in the latter half of the 21st century, the game takes place in a war-ravaged world where "Autonomous Doll" technology has risen to prominence in both military and civilian sectors. As various factions vie for survival, conflicts driven by technological rivalry, ideological clashes, and political strife unfold. As a Commander, you will lead Tactical Doll combat squads through intense battles, unravel deep conspiracies, and reshape the post-war world while seeking a path toward humanity's future. Intricate and Strategic Combat Mechanics Experience a fusion of real-time tactical deployment and pre-battle strategic planning, where quick decision-making and careful positioning determine the tide of battle. Different firearm classes—Assault Rifles, Submachine Guns, Shotguns, and more—each come with unique combat roles and abilities, offering a rich tactical experience. Every Tactical Doll possesses distinct skills and combat styles, allowing for endless strategic combinations. Adapt to your enemies and adjust your squad formation to master diverse battle scenarios! A Heartwarming & Romantic OATH System Strengthen your bonds with your favorite Tactical Dolls and unlock exclusive voice lines and special stories through the OATH System! Forming an Oath also grants combat bonuses, making your squad even stronger. Find your perfect partner and forge an unbreakable bond!
