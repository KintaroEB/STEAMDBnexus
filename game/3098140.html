About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to a vast and fantasy-style medieval world full of danger and excitement. Over 50 alignments are contending in this land, and various content and epic drama await your exploration. A thousand soldiers are fighting on a battlefield with a classical top-down view. Deploy different soldiers, command squad attack in real-time, release skills, and use items. Use your tactic to influence the war! Over 50 characters for players to recruit, try to improve relationships and recruit them for your adventure. 8 classes combine with vast equipment builds, try to develop your favorite heroes by anyways. Based on the fantasy medieval design, players can experience different races and civilizations on a vast map, meet partners with various abilities from around the world, take on diverse missions and adventures, and challenge powerful bosses everywhere! After gaining territory, players can manage cities, build constructions, expand faction, and conquer the world. Face the crisis, create a legend! Embrace the call of destiny and embark on an epic journey!
