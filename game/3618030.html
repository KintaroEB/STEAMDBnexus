About this gameFull description from Steam+
Flock Around is a co-op birdwatching game where you explore a nature sanctuary. Solo or with friends, snap pictures of funny birds, and fill out the park guide book! Snap them all! Goose Lake Sanctuary is home to numerous bird species, and if you can see it, you can take a picture of it. Fill out the guide book with pictures of each bird in its various poses, from the common Robin, to more rare and exotic birds. Explore a variety of biomes, perform actions and use items to lure in species, and mess with your friends! Flock Together! Flock Around is designed to be a shared social space where you and your friends can work together to get pictures of birds or each other! Birding is best with others, so you can play with up to 10 friends! Customize your birder! Collect cosmetics and make your birder unique, expressing your personality and eagle eyes to your friends. After taking your pictures, develop them for money to spend on outfits so you can go birding in style!
