About this gameFull description from Steam+
Lyca is a small, relaxing, incremental game about a magical wolf restoring a barren wasteland into a lush meadow. Run freely through the grass, gather resources, and explore a vast upgrade tree. Unlock incredibly powerful upgrades, helpful abilities and valuable resources. Bring life and rain back into a desolate wasteland. Enjoy: Incremental progression through resource gathering A vast upgrade tree to explore Unlockable "idle" and "endless" modes Approximately 3-4 hours of gameplay to complete the campaign Simple and relaxing experience Accessible controls: Keyboard, Mouse-Only, and Controller support Lofi royalty-free beats to chill to by Purrple Cat
