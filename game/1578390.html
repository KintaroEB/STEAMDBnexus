About this gameFull description from Steam+
MY GARAGE is a car simulation game with main focus being simulating real car repair, maintenance and physical behavior. CARS - Damage system so you can fix and replace any of body panels - Painting system - Suspension where every part has a usage, wear which affects the handling - Engine with realistic behavior and components ( down to oil condition affecting wear) Environment - Your own garage that you will have to buy parts and tools for - Tools - for everything there is a special tool - there is even a tire pump lying about somewhere ( you will have to buy a new one if you lose this one) - Shops - that sell supplies, car parts, paint and your angle grinder will need new discs, etc. - Junkyards - where to find car parts or even a whole car if you think it is worth saving - Barn finds - Delivery jobs - More features coming
