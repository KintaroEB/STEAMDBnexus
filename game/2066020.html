About this gameFull description from Steam+
Unleash Chaos. Become a God. Soulstone Survivors is a fast-paced action roguelite where you obliterate hordes of enemies, battle colossal bosses, and forge your path to godlike power. As a Void Hunter, harness devastating skills, craft unique weapons, and unlock game-breaking synergies in your quest for power. Master the Void 350+ Skills, Endless Builds – Customize your playstyle with hundreds of active and passive abilities. Face the Titans – Overcome massive bosses that test your strategy and reflexes. Deep Progression – Unlock characters, upgrade a vast skill tree, and shape your perfect build. Craft & Conquer – Forge over 100 unique weapons to push your power even further. Ever-Changing Challenges – Take on curses, explore diverse maps, and master multiple game modes. Become a Void Hunter – Learn about the order of the Void Hunters and face the Void King himself. Join over a million players in the ultimate roguelite experience and carve your legend in the void! Special THANKS to the Community We are a tiny team that chose to stay independent and continue developing the game we love, and would like to thank each and every one of you that has helped us along the way! Soulstone Survivors would not be the same without your feedback!
