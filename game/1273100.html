About this gameFull description from Steam+
Put your management skills to the demanding test and prove yourself as a fine ruler of the ancient Greek city. Make world-shaping decisions, command your subjects, erect magnificent structures, and make your Polis the most glorious place in the whole ancient world! Be sure that throughout your journey, you will face many challenges, unexpected events, and promising opportunities. Stay vigilant, however, as hostile forces will not remain indifferent to your success. Will you be able to secure the well-being and safety of your people? Features: Dozens of varied historically accurate buildings Extensive management and decision-making system Expanded production and resource gathering networks Random events and weather conditions Engaging trading, research, and war systems Old-school City Builder gameplay with a modern twist Plan out your Polis with great precision - work out your own meticulously detailed city layout and establish unabated production chains. Choose from dozens of varied buildings at your disposal and decide which of them will best serve your loyal subjects. Maintain a balance between residential zones, administrative institutions, workshops, service buildings, and military facilities to provide your city with stable, dynamic development. Make fateful decisions and manage all the aspects of your city's life. Set your taxes, establish new trade routes, occupy resource-rich terrains, increase the population of your empire, react to unpredictable events, or become a local military power. The way you rule is entirely up to you! Take good care of your citizens' needs, guarantee their safety and secure their well-being. If you fail, your Polis will soon be hit by riots, plagues, or rapid emigration. Only a good, wise politics will encourage the local population to settle in your city. Prove as an efficient ruler and turn your small provincial town into a huge metropolis known throughout the whole glorious Greece! If you succeed, your city will become famous for its elite, top class society, astonishing wealth, magnificent buildings, and superb military power. Be careful, though... your rivals will not remain idle! Be sure to join our Discord server, where we regularly hold engaging events and competitions, and talk about the future of Builders of Greece with our beloved community. Know, that we are always happy to hear your thoughts, feedback and suggestions! Stay up to date, follow our Social Media channels, subscribe the YouTube channel and become part of our ancient society!
