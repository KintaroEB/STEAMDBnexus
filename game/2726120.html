About this gameFull description from Steam+
Aura Wallpaper enables animated desktop && lockscreen wallpapers on Windows 10 and 11. It enables the display of any HTML page, for example Websites or YouTube videos on your Windows desktop or lockscreen. How to use Aura: Start Aura and right click the Aura logo in your task bar Click on Select Wallpaper Browse for your favorite video Fast forward to the point you want your wallpaper to start Click Set LockScreen Wallpaper The video is now your lockscreen wallpaper starting at the same timepoint Performance and resource usage is optimized: For lockscreen wallpapers Aura Wallpaper will not consume any resources unless your PC is locked.
