About this gameFull description from Steam+
Zumbi Blocks 2 is a zombie survival game. It's going to be available for free while it's in early development. Kill: fight zombies with melee and ranged weapons, both in first person or third person mode. Loot: explore the procedurally generated city, to find resources. Overcome: defeat bosses that live in interest points along the island. Upgrade: unlock loadout tiers, that you can bring to the match with you.
