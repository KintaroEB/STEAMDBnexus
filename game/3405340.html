About this gameFull description from Steam+
HOW LONG CAN YOU SURVIVE? Megabonk is a roguelike survival game where you must fight your way through hordes of enemies and bosses in randomly generated maps. Grab loot on the way, level up your character, upgrade your weapons and survive for as long as you can! How far can you make it? UPGRADE & GET OVERPOWERED Defeat enemies and pick up XP to level up! The more enemies you defeat, the more XP you get. Each upgrade offer is random and will vary in rarity, which makes each run unique!
