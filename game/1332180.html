About this gameFull description from Steam+
CIPHER ZERO is a minimalist logic game. You have two actions: Toggle a tile on or off. Check your solution. Experiment with curious glyphs to infer their rules. Delve into over 300 hand-crafted puzzles to solve at your own pace. Explore several locations that evolve and grow with your understanding. Vibe with an immersive, chill soundtrack that reacts to your actions and experiences in the game.
