About this gameFull description from Steam+
This is a straightforward game that combines "tower defense battles, base building, and idle gameplay." The game simulates the development process of an ancient mountain bandit stronghold. With tower defense (Tower Defense) as its core combat mechanic, it pays tribute to (Fenghe) several classic masterpieces (such as Warcraft RPG - Thief TD, Element TD, and Cultivation Simulator) while ultimately simplifying the gameplay. By integrating idle mechanics, it balances "addictive excitement" with "simple and relaxed" enjoyment. Each character functions as a stationary tower—they do not move on their own. Whether engaged in combat, gathering resources, or crafting, all actions are carried out in the form of tower defense (TD). Plunder residents, merchants, and troops from various cities to obtain currency, resources, and experience. This is all about attack—no defense. Engage in battles with zero loss, allowing you to acquire resources effortlessly and worry-free, with no fear of stagnation from defeat. In a reimagined world of Huaxia, legendary figures from various dynasties converge. Recruit, upgrade, assign professions, and equip them—multifaceted cultivation systems allow these characters to grow and evolve in every dimension. Characters can be deployed not only for combat but also for gathering resources, forging equipment, researching technologies, and refining elixirs. Leverage their unique strengths to continuously develop and expand your mountain stronghold. A map of alternate history awaits your exploration—cities, wilderness, and special locations are scattered across the land. Plunder resources, hunt beasts in the wild, delve into hidden realms, and in the later stages, embark on campaigns to conquer the world and claim dominion over all. Featuring extensive advancement paths, this chart only outlines the initial tier of professions. By the mid to late stages, a rich and intricate professional system will take shape, offering specialized directions for both combat and production roles.
