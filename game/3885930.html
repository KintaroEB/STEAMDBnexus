About this gameFull description from Steam+
The free DLC scenario "Campaign against Dong Zhuo" is now officially available! Friendly reminder: This game features pure sandbox simulation gameplay, similar to a tabletop wargame. There are no combat cutscenes or battle scenes. If this matters to you, we recommend trying the free demo before making a purchase. "SandTable War: Three Kingdoms" is a strategic simulation game set during the Three Kingdoms period. Here you can play as various factions from Three Kingdoms history to conquer cities and lands, unify the realm. With abundant historical officers and scenarios, plus a boardgame-like experience, come restore the Han Dynasty! Game Features 160+ regions and cities on a grand strategic map for your conquest 400+ historical officers with hundreds of diverse traits; recruit and appoint them to accelerate city development 100+ items and equipment to enhance officer attributes and abilities 20+ regional buildings to construct and develop productivity 10+ playable factions with distinct characteristics and varied gameplay Multiple historical scenarios for immersive experience Manage regional civil and military affairs, adjust taxes and conscription, balance output and public order Earn administrative decrees through development to continuously strengthen your faction Execute secret schemes at opportune moments to turn the tide of war
