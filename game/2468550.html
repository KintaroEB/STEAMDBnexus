About this gameFull description from Steam+
Vivid World is a completely new party-building roguelike auto-battler, where you assemble a team of characters that have transformed into jewels to engage in combat! Transverse a new world with quirky characters who've all been redesigned from Vivid Knight! Create your own unique and powerful party to clear every dungeon in your way! More in-depth party building than before! Do you know the proverb: "Three heads are better than one"? Gather several of the same jewels, enhance them, and see your buffs and synergy explode with power! And now, introducing "Orbs." You can equip Orbs to each character in your party, which means your ability to create and change your party has now expanded infinitely! Strategic battles. In this game, the battles advance automatically while the player utilizes "Gems" to support their party during battle phases. With our new and improved battle system, the player can combine several Gems together or unleash the power of individual Gems! Enjoy strategic battles with greater freedom than ever before! Resource management that will test your thinking. The characters who battle for you are, after all, only jewels. That means you can "buy and sell" them. You can buy different types of Jewels or strengthen a single Jewel by merging several of the same Jewel with it. The choice is yours. Feel free to play around with your resources however you see fit. But be careful. If you're too greedy, you might find yourself being sucked into a dungeon here in the Underworld that you can't get out of. A world shrouded in mystery. The Underworld's deep, dark secrets. Those secrets will be revealed as you conquer dungeons and interact with the denizens of the Underworld. Just who are these characters who have transformed into Jewels? By strengthening your bonds with them and unleashing their hidden powers, you might find that out as well.
