About this gameFull description from Steam+
New Drawing Game Mode Grab your friends, pick up your pens, and get ready for this chaotic new mode where 4-8 players join the ultimate drawing showdown. A random category and secret word appear - everyone starts sketching… except one player. That’s right - one of you is the Impostor, drawing without knowing the real world! Your mission: Draw your clue without giving too much away. Spot the faker hiding among you. Debate, accuse, and vote for who you think is bluffing! If the group catches the impostor, the honest artists score points. But if the impostor pulls off their sneaky masterpiece and stays hidden, they take the win! With thousands of words and endless laughs, the New Drawing Mode will test your creativity, deception, and detective skills all at once! Can you outsmart your friends - or fool them all? Feign invites you into a charmingly twisted town full of suspicion, strategy, and playful deception. In this role-based game for up to 12 players, you'll take on the role of an Innocent, Impostor, or Neutral. Use your unique abilities and instincts during the night, then help vote someone out in the morning. What Makes Feign Stand Out: Unique roles, with surprising twists Mysterious nights and deceptive mornings Customizable characters Easy to learn Endlessly replayable Deeply satisfying Ready to join the village? An Innocent role, but a dangerous one! You'll believe that you're an Innocent, but your actions won't have any effect. You won't know that you've become Insane until the very end. You could be going around completely unaware that you're the one responsible for the Innocents' fall. Could you be... Insane? Either collect information and try to survive the night to uncover the impostor in the morning. Eliminate impostors using your role, and avoid suspicion at all costs. Or have your own agenda to manipulate the situation. Eliminate Innocents using your role at night, or gather information to avoid suspicion. Try not to get caught by the Innocents in the morning. You have your own agenda. Your role is different from both the Imposters and the Innocents, which can make it harder to gather information. That's why you need to stay alert and pay close attention to how the game unfolds. Depending on how things play out, you can fulfill your objective by working against either the Innocents or the Impostors. This might mean eliminating players from either side, convincing others that you're Innocent based on the info you've collected, or shifting suspicion toward someone else. The choice is yours... Customizable Characters: Want to stand out? Use the in-game currency, Feign Cookies, to unlock stylish costumes and accessorize your avatar.
