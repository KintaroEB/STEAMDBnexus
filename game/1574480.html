About this gameFull description from Steam+
Step into an adrenaline-fueled, retro first-person shooter paying homage to the beloved console shooters of the ‘90s. Engage in pulse-pounding shootouts against cutting-edge enemy AI straight out of the futuristic realm of 1997. Story Mode (Solo or Co-Op!): Embark on 14 daring missions as Secret Agent John Walter, an elite operative tasked with saving the world from his arch-nemesis, Dominic Pulp. Protect the future against the phantoms of the past. Traverse skyscrapers, nightclubs, museums, catacombs, and snow bases in a heart-pumping journey of espionage. Play the entire campaign as a one-man army or alongside friends via split-screen and online cooperative play! Each story mission is a self-contained environment where you have to accomplish varied objectives, such as hacking terminals, stealing secret plans, freeing civilian hostages, and much more… all while fighting armed guards in epic shootouts, of course. 3 Difficulty Levels Every mission can be completed in 3 distinct difficulty modes (Agent, Special Agent, and 64 Agent). With higher difficulty comes more objectives, and more open areas to discover. (Tip: Start with Agent to familiarize yourself with the missions, then ramp up the difficulty to discover what comes next.) Paradox Mode: By beating par times and other trials, you can unlock Paradox Modes, which contain 70+ modifiers and cheats that enable various effects on regular gameplay, including: Big Heads Melee Disarms Slow Bullet Speeds No Reload …and many more! Ready for an unorthodox challenge? Complete Contracts: remixed missions with different objectives and Paradox mode pre-sets for additional Paradox unlocks. Arena: Fight against friends in local split-screen multiplayer combat, or take the gunfight online with up to 8 players and 8 additional bots. Enjoy full lobby customization including teams, weapon set, rule sets and paradox modifiers, with up to 10 saved pre-sets to your liking. Deathmatch: Classic deathmatch, score the more frags to win! Briefcase: Capture the briefcase and hold it for long enough to score a point. But don't get too comfortable, after each point won, the briefcase position is reset to a random point on the map. Zone: You have to capture the zone on the map to score points. Don't let any enemy contest it as it will stall the countdown! Challenges: special matches against pre-set bots where players must reach a precise score under a certain amount of time. The faster you win, the better the prizes. Unlock maps, characters, music tracks, and more!
