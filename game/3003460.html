About this gameFull description from Steam+
You've awakened the Unnamable, and now hordes of enemy minions encircle you! Take aim, shoot them down, and use the recoil to dodge for your life! Your power grows over time, yet hallucinations and visions never cease. Will you navigate your way out of these treacherous waters? Caution Survival Tips: Fire at anything that looks suspicious (even if it appears friendly). Your guns won't misfire, but their recoil might backfire. Avoid unnecessary physical contact. Embrace moments of unexpected wonders. Swapping outfits might cause illusions (stay calm if this happens). Team up with your allies (or "drag-ons") for thrilling adventures! Facing off against Cthulhu? Well, there's a slim chance of success... fingers crossed! P.S.: A kiss from your friend can bring you back to life (in-game only).
