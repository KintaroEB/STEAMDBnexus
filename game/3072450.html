About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dive into the virtual world of social media in this point-and-click mystery adventure! Uncover secrets hidden by families, friends, corporations, and idols on a virtual social media platform where millions share their daily lives. “Fakebook" is a social impact game designed to explore both the bright and dark sides of modern social media culture. Within its vast world, millions of virtual users form intricate relationships, posting their lives for all to see. But beneath these posts lie hidden truths, waiting for you to discover. stimulating stories you find and post, the more followers you have on your account! THE STORY Your sister, accused of defaming a beloved national idol, became the target of relentless online harassment. Unable to bear all the pressure, she chose to take her own life. Determined to reveal the injustice that led to her tragic death, you set out to grow your own social network account, gather followers, and expose the truth to the world. Craft posts by uncovering intimate and provocative stories and watch your follower count soar. The more secrets you reveal, the closer you get to justice. GAMEPLAY FEATURES Interact with Virtual Accounts Exchange DMs to receive requests, and choose from over 30 intriguing requests. Explore and Investigate Examine profiles, timestamps, and even ad banners to uncover crucial clues. Grow Your Influence Solve requests, write posts, and attract more followers. Every secret uncovered can boost your account. Open-World Freedom Dive into the Fakebook universe without restrictions. Investigate whatever sparks your curiosity. IMMERSIVE OPEN-WORLD Fakebook is an open-world social media experience filled with hidden elements and surprises, including cameos by real-life influencers reimagined as virtual personas. New stories and updates keep the world of Fakebook ever-evolving. Log in now and uncover the secrets of Fakebook!
