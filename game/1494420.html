About this gameFull description from Steam+
Silica is a crossover of FPS and RTS, where you take part in epic photorealistic battles either as humans or aliens. Lead as the commander, or join the assault from the ground - the choice is yours! Take Part in Epic Combined Arms Warfare Immerse yourself in epic battles where humans and aliens fight for ultimate domination. Dominate Land and Sky Take control of both land and air vehicles to assert power over the sandy dunes of planet Baltarus Lead as RTS Commander! Take charge as RTS commander over battles of epic scale. Use your tactical prowess to dominate the battlefield, coordinating complex operations from a top-down perspective. Experience Realistic Unit Scale Witness the impressive scale of units, from small infantry squads to colossal harvesters and massive buildings. Each unit type is meticulously designed to reflect its size, providing an immersive and realistic experience on the battlefield.
