About this gameFull description from Steam+
Are you interested in MMORTS fantasy oldschool wargame? Take part in the strong cooperative features, trade with other kingdoms, conquer and defend your provinces and lead your alliance to victory! YOUR CITY choose one of four different races (Orc, Elf, Dwarf, Undead) create powerful economy research new units or spells STRATEGY conquer new territories defend your fortresses setup alliance, create pacts, destroy enemies PVP (strong teamplay element) PVE (regular events) HERO 90+ unique artifacts 90+ hero skills to choose from 20+ spells WORLDS new realm starts every two weeks 97-day long server ending with a challenging bossfight many special events with unique artifacts and units possible to play on web / iOS / Android
