About this gameFull description from Steam+
Fueled by love but driven by rage, Cherry embarks on a blood-soaked quest for revenge, explores the massive Megastructure, and eviscerates the armies of Infonet in a best-in-class action-adventure that breaks tropes and bones. Cookie Cutter, which IGN calls a “GORY, GORGEOUS, AND BLOODY GOOD METROIDVANIA,” features lovingly crafted hand-drawn 2D visuals, challenging and satisfying combat, a wild dystopian world full of irreverent characters, buckets of violence, and underneath it all a sweet, beautiful story. As Cherry, a chainsaw-wielding, uppercut-dealing, barrier-smashing, gore-splattering android with a mean streak, players will journey into the high-tech underbelly of a dystopian society, meet allies, slay enemies, battle epic bosses, discover new regions and unlock new abilities. What secrets does the Megastructure promise? Cherry and her fearless friends must find out if she has any hope of saving her creator, Shinji Fallon, from a ruthless villain determined to rule at any cost. Play this techno-pop-punk post-kawaii Metroidvania for yourself and learn why Den of Geek raves that Cookie Cutter is “EASILY ONE OF THE BEST METROIDVANIAS OF THE YEAR.” Features: An epic, one-of-a-kind action-adventure / Metroidvania spanning 15-plus hours Experience an emotional story full of quirky, lovable characters Explore a gargantuan far-future world and uncover its countless secrets Equip Cherry with a variety of new weapons like a chainsaw and unlock her ultra powerful mecha form Meet an insane cast of allies and enemies and gain access to gated areas of the expansive world Engage in fast, responsive, satisfying combat, time counters perfectly and execute violent brutals on enemies Battle huge, deadly, unforgettable bosses Gorgeous hand-drawn 2D visuals look like an animated feature brought to life This is the DEFINITIVE Cookie Cutter experience. Cookie Cutter Overkill Edition adds a ton of content to the original game. If you've played it before, experience it again with all new features, including: Fully voiced dialogue for every character in the game New map features like the ability to gauge completion status Brand new special moves and upgrades for Cherry Never-before-seen cinematics add even more style and background to the game Tons of gameplay tweaks, progression balancing, and more
