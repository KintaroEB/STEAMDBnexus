About this gameFull description from Steam+
eBaseball™: PRO SPIRIT Powered by the next generation eBaseball™ Engine, the game delivers lifelike motion and sound throughout the stadium. From the flight of the ball and the players giving their all to the explosive cheers of the crowd, every moment comes alive with immersive detail. Combined with smooth, live-style play-by-play commentary*, the experience brings you closer than ever to real baseball. *Color commentary is unavailable in English. The eBaseball™ Engine takes baseball games to a new dimension This next-generation visual experience is powered by Unreal® Engine, enabling highly realistic details. Hair and skin textures appear almost tangible, and even the rain that falls onto the field is rendered with impressive authenticity. 【WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP】Challenge Rivals from Around the World in Online Matches Build your own original team and compete online against players from across the globe. Sharpen your skills through intense matches and aim for the top of the rankings. 【EXHIBITION】Enjoy Next-Generation Baseball to the Fullest In Exhibition mode, you can take your time and enjoy next-generation baseball with your favorite teams. Customize match settings and rules, hone your skills, or experience realistic baseball together with friends. 【TEAM EDIT】Build Your Ideal Team with Carefully Selected Players In WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP, each tournament features regulations such as cost limits. Use TEAM EDIT to carefully choose from a wide roster of players and build a team capable of advancing through the competition. About the Professional Baseball Spirits Series eBaseball™: PRO SPIRIT is a title in the Professional Baseball Spirits series. The Professional Baseball Spirits series is a professional baseball game franchise dedicated to realism. The first home console title, Professional Baseball Spirits 2004, was released on March 25, 2004. With Professional Baseball Spirits 2015, the series introduced 3D scanning technology, capturing real professional baseball players from every angle to recreate them in three dimensions. Laser scanning technology was also adopted to precisely measure and reproduce stadium layouts, delivering an exceptionally realistic baseball experience. The mobile game Professional Baseball Spirits A, released in 2015, has celebrated its 10th anniversary and surpassed 65 million total downloads as of February 2026.
