About this gameFull description from Steam+
Device Doctor Simulator 2024 offers an interactive and detailed simulation experience of becoming a technology repair expert. In this unique simulation, dive into the complex world of various digital devices, including smartphones, tablets, and smartwatches. Each device awaits with its unique problems and repair needs. Filled with realistic repair processes and challenges, this game will develop your skills as a device doctor, saving your customers' digital lives. Features Realistic Repair Scenarios: From screen replacements to battery changes, software updates to diagnosing hardware issues, a wide range of repair tasks awaits. Various Devices: Repair a variety of devices, including smartphones, tablets, and smartwatches. Knowledge Base and Learning Module: Each repair task is accompanied by information on how the devices work, allowing you to learn while playing. Career Mode: Manage your own repair workshop, increase customer satisfaction, and expand your business. Visual and Sound Effects: Enhance your repair experience with high-quality graphics and realistic sound effects. 🛠️ Want to become a master phone repair technician? Set up your own repair workshop with the wide range of equipment found in our game! 🔧📱 Discover all the tools needed to solve various malfunctions. ⚙️🔩 In our realistic simulation, you'll feel like a professional as you repair all types of phones! 🎮✨ 🎨 Create your own space! Personalize your area with the customizable decorations available in our game. 🛋️✨ Decorate your repair shop or home in your own style with furniture, wall colors, and accessories that suit every player's taste. 🖼️🌿 Build your dream environment and unleash your creativity! 🏡💡 Why Device Doctor Simulator 2024? "Device Doctor Simulator 2024" is more than just a simulation game; it's a way to explore and enhance your passion for technology. As you gain in-depth knowledge about device repair, you'll also improve your critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Experience the excitement of setting up and managing your own repair workshop. Earn your customers' trust and become a master of technology repair. Are you ready to become a hero in the digital world with Device Doctor Simulator 2024? Get ready to test your tech repair skills and embark on a journey to become a true device doctor!
