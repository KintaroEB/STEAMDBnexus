About this gameFull description from Steam+
Grim Clicker is an IDLE/RPG clicker where you will find your own strategy to explore the lost worlds. Inspired by games such as Diablo, Grim Dawn, Dark Souls, etc., the game features a complex character development, many unique items, active and passive playstyle. Key Features: Skills - A large and clever skill tree provides a lot of ways for character development. Inventory management - You can improve your weapons, enchant them, disassemble them and create new ones. In addition to weapons, there are plenty of other items to help you on your journey. Ritual - If you feel your combination no longer works, perform the ritual and start over. Every time you will become stronger! Idle/Clicker - The character is immortal, so you can safely let him fight on his own, and only manage the general trend. Or, if you like to click, come up with your own strategy in this manner and help the hero on his way to knowledge. Shop - The shop of shards and black wax will offer you improvements that will sharpen your own style and increase the power of your hero. Chests - The rich chests contain masters' weapons, materials for crafting, potions, pets - in short, everything that the old masters put there. This is your support in the dark world of Grim Clicker! Cosmic Scale - The further you go through the game, the more possibilities you have. The new content implies new game strategies. Sound - Beautiful soundtrack and attention to sound. Mystery - Who are you? Why are you here? Look for the remains of ancient civilizations, travel through the desecrated shards, find allies!
