About this gameFull description from Steam+
SCHiM is a game about jumping from shadow to shadow, while interacting with a relaxing and lively environment. What is a schim? A schim is the soul and spirit of an object, thing, or living being. Everything in the world has one. A schim should never be separated from their thing! This does happen to your schim, this schim who is attached to a human being is separated from them early on in the game. Can you get back to them before it's too late? Features Unique 3D platforming through shadows. Animated shadow platforms and environments. Each level has its own scenery with small stories. A story of a person losing their shadow. A beautiful abstract world.
