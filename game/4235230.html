About this gameFull description from Steam+
Werewolf: The Inner Beast is a 3D third person survival horror game with a female protagonist. Story driven linear gameplay in a dark, claustrophobic atmosphere. The heroine has been kidnapped and imprisoned in a cruel station far away from civilization, stalked by a werewolf bringing hell with him. Can she escape this nightmare station? List of features: Storyline with dark and claustrophobic atmoshpere. Voiced, cinematic cutscenes Interaction with friendly and hostile NPC's. Dress up Challenging but fair gameplay Find out why you were kidnapped: Explore the station, complete missions: Interact with your environment: Escape and hide from the Alpha Lycan who is hunting you: Make friends: Fight: And hunt down your enemies:
