About this gameFull description from Steam+
Ever wondered what it’s like to work airport security... without the stress? In Nothing To Declare, you step into a cozy, hand-drawn world where your job is to keep things safe, with just a sprinkle of suspicion. From weighing luggage to scanning x-rays and questioning quirky travelers, every shift brings surprises (and the occasional questionable electronic device). Use your judgment and intuition to decide: do you let it through… or unzip and investigate? 🧳 Your Daily Routine: Start Your Shift: Begin your day at the cozy airport checkpoint or behind-the-scenes baggage station. Serve Passengers: Greet travelers, check passports, weigh bags, and scan them for suspicious items. Investigate Further: If your gut says something’s off, dig in, literally. Unzip luggage and search with care in mini-game form. Grade Your Performance: Make smart calls, earn money, and avoid penalties for mistakes. Or don't, chaos is also an option. 🔍 Mini-Games You’ll Master: Bag Weighing: Check if a suitcase is pushing the limits… and guess what might be inside. X-Ray Scanning: Layered, noisy bag visuals hide secrets, can you spot the danger? Passenger Interviews: Choose from dialogue options to assess each traveler’s intent and honesty. Luggage Searching: Unzip, poke around, and carefully examine every odd-looking item. ...and more as the airport grows! 🎨 Features & Feel: Hand-drawn 2D visuals full of charm and character. Mini-games inspired by real security tasks, but make it cozy. Light narrative elements with funny or suspicious passengers. Endless replayability as each shift brings new challenges. Packed (literally) with items from the Lost But Found universe, and more! ✈️ Ready to start your shift? There’s nothing to declare… except a good time.
