About this gameFull description from Steam+
With the Free Starter Access: ENJOY THRILLING RACING ACTION No matter the surface – asphalt, dirt, grass, wood, plastic, ice, or even underwater – control your speed and drift with precision as you take on jumps, loops, wall rides, and sharp turns. Perfect your trajectory and race to victory! RACE ON THE BEST PLAYER-CREATED TRACKS Every week, take on five new Shorts tracks in one of the five environments: Red Island, Blue Bay, Green Coast, White Shore, and the iconic Stadium. Every season, discover the easiest tracks from the official campaign. Every hour, race online on top creative tracks in the Arcade Channel. COLLECT PRESTIGE CAR SKINS Climb global and regional leaderboards, collect medals and trophies. Unlock Trackmania’s most prestigious car skins through your driving talents and in-game achievements – and show off your skill in a unique way! EXPRESS YOUR CREATIVITY Create custom tracks using the track editors (simple and advanced) and over 3,000 building blocks and items. Design your own car skins with the skin editor, record and share videos and images of your best races with the replay editor. With the 1-Year Club Access: UNLOCK THE INFINITE WORLD OF TRACKMANIA Enjoy all full seasonal campaigns, top-notch community creations, exclusive activities, and premium car skins. Host your online rooms, create tracks, in-game events, and invite all players to join!
