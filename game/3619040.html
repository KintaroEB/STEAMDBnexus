Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

Though the graphics are dated, Half-Life 1 depicts violence in a somewhat realistic [for the time] but not in an over-the-top way. Players are able to shoot down hordes of alien species alongside villainous Government soldiers; essentially, it is a "shoot or be shot" scenario. However, through ways of cheat commands, players are able to, in the opening levels as well as throughout the game, shoot or beat down their allies. Human and alien blood is depicted as red and yellowish respectively and with enough damage, enemies can be "gibbed," which is when a subject essentially explodes resulting in debris consisting of bodily parts. As aforementioned, as the visuals have aged, they may seem rather tame compared to modern standards.
