About this gameFull description from Steam+
KUBOOM - hurricane new cross-platform online 3D shooter game available on these platforms: Steam, Android and iOS. Features: - good optimization and decent FPS rates even on slower devices! - more than 20 carefully designed and balanced maps! - more than 50 units of different types of weapons! about 100 of the skins for weapons! - over 45 clothes for character customization! - dynamic battles 5x5 team mode, with up to 10 players in the "Deathmatch"! - beautiful graphics, not occupying much space on your device! - clans with the possibility of doing clan wars!
