About this gameFull description from Steam+
Joker Show - Horror Escape - 3D FPS Hide&Seek Game Your parents left for the night, and you stayed at home alone, you were bored and you decided to call the Jester (Joker), but something went wrong... It looks like you succeeded, now the jester wants to play with you, hide and don't let him catch you, find a way to call the jester away. Description of the gameplay: You play as a teenager who is left alone at home and decides to try summoning spirits. Your task is to first find the necessary items for the ceremony, and follow the instructions to call the jester (joker). When you call the jester, he will trap you and lock you in the basement, try to find a way out and escape before the clock strikes 12, because at 12 o'clock at night, the jester will start a show where you will be the main participant! You have 5 hours to escape, or find a way to get rid of the jester, hide, run away, solve puzzles to find a way to escape. Features: Atmospheric graphics Interesting puzzles Excellent optimization
