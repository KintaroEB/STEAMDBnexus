About this gameFull description from Steam+
Turn the dial, match the parts, and listen to their stories. D1AL-ogue is a cyberpunk repair puzzle game where you fix androids in a city under an eternal gray night. Welcome to your workshop. This is Chroma City, a place where the sun never rises, and only the artificial light of 'Neon-Shine' illuminates the streets. With 'Polar Night Day,' the longest night of the year, just ahead, the city is shimmering with festive lights and shadows. You become the manager of a repair clinic tucked away in a corner of the shopping district, welcoming customers who emerge from the dark. They are not ordinary humans, but androids known as Electronic Vital Entities (E.V.E). Your mission is to operate the old-school diagnostic console [D1AL] to fix their broken parts and return them to their daily lives. Circular Puzzle Action Forget simple repairs. You must manually rotate and manipulate a circular grid that visualizes the android's internal core. Rotate and Merge: Within a limited battery capacity, move and rotate modules to merge three of the same kind into a higher-tier unit. Strategic Repair: Precisely replace units while avoiding spreading electronic narcotics and overloaded units on the verge of exploding. Stories of those living daily lives where the abnormal is the norm Androids with unique charms and backstories will visit your workshop. Instead of a warm cup of tea or a cocktail, serve them a perfect repair. The stories they share will change depending on your skills. Key Features Circular Puzzle: A unique puzzle mechanism based on a circular grid. Light Narrative: Trivial yet deep daily stories within a cyberpunk universe. Retro Aesthetics: Pixel art visuals combining 98-era PC style with Neon Noir. Now, turn the D1AL and connect with them. ------------------------------------------------------------- All resources used in the game are either 100% hand-crafted or licensed assets. Log collection is strictly limited to gameplay information for game improvement purposes.
