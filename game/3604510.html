About this gameFull description from Steam+
Moxiangshan Girls' High School—a private school in the suburbs, has been crowned the "aristocratic academy" for its lavish amenities and prohibitive tuition since its founding. The "Problem Child" Yun Xinyao—who enrolled at her overbearing mother's insistence—gradually grows closer to Jiang Xiyue—a "special admissions student"—through a series of coincidences. Cold and socially awkward, the habitually distant Yun Xinyao finds herself gradually influenced by the bright outsider. She begins to harbor quiet admiration for the girl. But as campus life progresses, Xiyue attracts more companions, leaving ever-fewer moments for just the two of them. ﻿"If I could be her only one..." The thought festers in Yun Xinyao's mind, an obsession taking root. Then comes that fateful encounter behind the school—where her once-pure admiration begins to twist into a vicious curse. Thus, the story unfolds. 1v1 yuri story - A slightly "twisted" mutual relationship 200k Chinese chars plot with 5 original characters - Their intertwined stories Multi-perspective narration - Unfolding the "whole truth" gradually 15+ CGs with full voice acting - Visual & audio emotional immersion
