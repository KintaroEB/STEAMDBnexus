About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dive into the Musical World of My Singing Monsters🎵Breed them, feed them, listen to them sing! Harness your creativity by assembling a squad of wacky, wild, and weirdly loveable Monsters! Every creature is a living instrument, and together you will rise to the top of the charts to compose the next big hit! Create the ultimate Squad by discovering hundreds of unique Monster characters who are all ready to pump up the volume! Each Island has a unique combination of Monsters, making sure your Monster gang is just as diverse as your playlist! Create vibrant landscapes with dapper décor, alluring artifacts, and charming chattels! Express yourself with Crafty Costumes that will turn your Monsters from drab to fab! Even the Monster Life can be glamorous, after all. The Monster World isn’t the only place you can crank up the bass! Share your musical manifestos on Composer Island, achieve celebrity status by climbing the ranks of the Top Islands, and explore your artistic ambitions through community-driven Monsterpieces. Join millions of players from around the world and showcase your talent! The Monster-Handlers are giving you a backstage pass to the latest and greatest trends! We’ve got you covered, with regular content updates, Seasonal Events, and community collaborations! You’ll never have a dull moment in the Monster World! Get ready to drop the beat and create the Ultimate Monster Mash Up! Download My Singing Monsters today and unleash your Inner Maestro. FEATURES: • Squad up with more than 650 wacky and wild Monster friends! • Create vibrant landscapes on over 40 Islands to make sure your Monsters are living the high life! • Dress your Monsters with the latest drip, with over 450 dapper costumes! • Take your Monsters all over the globe by collabing with friends! • Game updated regularly, so there’s never a dull moment! HELP & SUPPORT Get in touch with the Monster-Handlers by contacting us in-game by going to Options > Support. My Singing Monsters is completely free to play. Some in-game items can also be purchased for real money. If you do not want to use this feature, please disable in-app purchases in your device's settings.
