About this gameFull description from Steam+
Get prepared for the FIRST EVER shooter game based on Rage Comics ! Rage Wars is a well-made action game with a variety of weapons, worlds and enemies to challenge with. A game where you have either a storyline or an Arena mode to challenge your friends over Game Center! Simple controls let you take over the battlefield easily. Features: - Storyline with 30 levels, lots of enemies and cut-scenes to catch up with dramatic story. - Arena mode - Kill as much zombies as you can. - Dynamic main character facial rage expressions. - Most of Rage Faces used just in one game for your joy. - Lots of Y U NO, FUUUUU, and Trolling moments you will never forget. - Collect potatoes to unlock different weapons! Tips: - Use your RAGE button whenever you're in trouble! So Y U NO TRY IT OUT? True Story ;)
