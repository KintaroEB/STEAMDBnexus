About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Story Embark on a treacherous adventure as you track down the villains who killed your master. Uncover hidden secrets as you meet dozens of complex characters. Develop your skills and reveal your true potential as you become a master in your own right. The World Immerse yourself in the dense forests, breathtaking vistas, expansive deserts, and savage wildlands of the Earthen Realm. Freely explore alluring regions, meet friends in bustling villages, and delve into dark corners where dangerous foes lie in wait. Infinite Windwalk With no stamina bar explore the far reaches of the Earthen Realm with unprecedented freedom. Scale walls, soar over mountains, and run on water in style with new custom Windwalk animations. Martial Arts Enjoy Blade & Soul's iconic action-based combat with upgraded animation and graphics. Switch seamlessly between Classic and "B&S" combat modes as you spar with powerful opponents. The new skill extraction system allows for an unprecedented amount of customization as you master the unique skills of your class. Dungeons Powerful opponents threaten the balance. To defeat them you must master your skills, learn their attack strategies, and work closely with your allies. With each victory, you'll earn powerful weapons, valuable accessories, and unique portraits. Explore dungeons solo or use the robust party matching system to team up with up to 5 other players.
