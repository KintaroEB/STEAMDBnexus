About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Ranchers is a cozy country-life sim for 1-4 players set in an open world sandbox. Try The Ranchers today in early access  and join our incredible community to build the future of The Ranchers. Join us on Discord to follow updates, forward feedback and much more. CREATE YOUR RANCH After many years of living the hectic city life, you finally decide to leave everything behind to buy your first lot of land in the country and start a new life, in the quiet, close to nature. However, your skills as a farmer will be put to the test to make your ranch a flourishing business and earn the villagers' respect and esteem. Take care of animals, grow crops, claim resources, buy new lots of land, and much more. You'll have plenty of space to create the ranch of your dreams. The Ranchers is for you if you: Are looking for a farming simulator with sandbox freedom to play your way. You want a cozy space to unwind, but with enough action to keep things exciting. Love farm simulator games. Appreciate a big open world waiting to be explored. Want to build alone or invite up to three friends to help out. Find joy in the creative freedom of base-building and home decoration. Fancy the charm of indie games built on passion. DEVELOP YOUR RANCH Grab your trusty hammer and head over to your workbench! Gather basic resources and turn them into useful items such as fences, sprinklers, greenhouses, and much more. Transform your humble home into a thriving ranch! MANAGE YOUR RANCH & TRADE WITH THE LOCALS Managing a ranch can be expensive. Optimize your Ranch's resource consumption, sign water, electricity contracts, or build your water wells, wind turbines, and solar panels and sell your surplus to the village. A well-tended ranch will produce the high quality crops you need to catch the attention of the villagers, fulfill daily orders, and sell your goods at the local markets for a decent profit to keep things running smoothly. TAKE CARE OF ANIMALS It wouldn't be a proper ranch without animals! Build barns and coops of all sizes, and invite little (and big) friends to join you. From chickens and bunnies to goats and cows, you can pick your favorites or try to collect at least one of each. A BIG OPEN WORLD Whether on foot, by bike, in a pickup truck, or in a fancy sports car, there is so much to explore. From markets and fashion boutiques to car dealerships, parks, or even a casino, it’s all waiting for you. But be mindful of the clock! Just like in real life, The Ranchers features both shifting seasons and a daily cycle, meaning you’ll need to plan your errands carefully while the stores are open. BUILD THE HOUSE OF YOUR DREAMS A simple studio-apartment or a multi-story house? It's really up to you. Build your home from scratch and decorate it as you please. Rancher, home builder AND designer? Not too shabby. PLAY WITH FRIENDS Invite up to 3 friends to visit your ranch and enjoy country life together! Farm, build, raise animals, explore the open world, and help each other grow. Each player has their own ranch and world progression. When you join a friend, you are visiting the host’s world, so ranch and quest progression remain tied to the host’s save. However, playing as a guest still rewards your own progression: you earn money and Skill Points (XP), and you can bring any items and blueprints in your inventory back to your own ranch. Play entirely solo, open your ranch to friends, or visit theirs whenever they’re online. PLAY YOUR WAY Are you a good citizen or living on the edge? In The Ranchers, actions can have consequences. Destroying, stealing or reckless driving may fall under the police’s radar, so be careful - or simply enjoy the rush of an action-packed getaway! And once again - remember to join our incredible community on Discord.
