About this gameFull description from Steam+
holoVillage: Our Cozy Days is a game where you can enjoy a slow and carefree rural life together with your favorite VTuber. Join the cute chibi versions of VTuber talents from hololive Production as you develop your village, explore dungeons, build houses, and enjoy a relaxing slow life together. Relaxing slow life with farming, mining, and fishing In the village, you can till the fields, go fishing, craft items with collected materials, open a shop and do business... Enjoy your time freely and leisurely with hololive members in your own way. Exciting dungeon exploration Outside the village, enemies appear and dungeons with even more powerful bosses await, offering thrilling adventures for those who seek action. Make the most of different weapon types, enhance and customize your equipment through smithing, and combine them with job ability trees to aim for even greater heights! The Summer 2026 major update added Step Dodge and Guard actions to melee weapons, along with exhilarating bonus effects — greatly enhancing the appeal of adventure gameplay. hololive talents visiting the village Familiar hololive talents can be welcomed into your village as residents, allowing you to live together. Have them help with farm work, dress them in your favorite outfits, or take photos together... As you build Affection with them, you may receive various gifts — like beautiful Profile Cards unique to each character, furniture, and more. Build precious memories together in a relaxing and peaceful life. Rich housing features In your home or shop, you can freely decorate with furniture and goods inspired by hololive to create your ideal interior. At launch, there are over 1,800 types of furniture available! You can stack furniture in multiple layers or place items on walls, allowing for a high degree of freedom in placement. Upload your proudly designed room to the server, share it with others, or visit your friends’ rooms! Maybe your oshi might even come visit!? A wide variety of costumes You can customize your character and enjoy dressing up. Create a character that reflects yourself, or become your favorite hololive talent... There are over 1,300 costumes and character customization parts in total!
