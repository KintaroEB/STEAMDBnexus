About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Liar's Bar: The Ultimate Deception Playground! Step into Liar's Bar, the most twisted and thrilling hangout in town. This isn't your average pub – it's a den of lies, deception, and mind games. Grab a seat at a table of four and immerse yourself in the ultimate first-person multiplayer online experience where cunning and trickery are the name of the game. Four Game Modes with Early Access Players take turns playing a card face down and declaring its value. The value should match the table’s value. (e.g., "King" or "Queen"). When a player lays down a card, they might be lying about its value. If you suspect a lie, you can call their bluff. If someone’s bluff is called and they were lying, they must play a round of Russian Roulette. Surviving one bullet out of 6 rounds of shells means you stay in the game; failing means game over for you. After Russian Roulette is played, the cards are reset, and the game continues. Roll your dice. No one can see your roll, but everyone’s watching your next move. On your turn, make a bid predicting the total number of dice on the table, showing a specific face (e.g., "There are five 3’s"). The next player must either raise the bid by increasing the number of dice or the face value (e.g., "There are six 4’s") or challenge the previous bid. Think someone’s lying? Challenge their bid! The loser drinks a bottle of poison. Drink two bottles, and you’re out! The last one alive wins! This is Texas Hold’em- but you bet bullets, not chips. On your turn, Call to add a bullet or Fold and pull the trigger with what you’ve already bet. Every round raises the risk. Once per game, Coward’s Fold allows you to escape in your first turn by pulling the trigger with only one bullet. As bullets are added, shared cards are revealed one by one until the final showdown. Once per game, you may Switch, swapping one of your cards before the table is fully revealed. You can also go All-In, forcing everyone to bet everything or fold and face the gun. Players take turns spinning the slots and declaring how many Hearts they’ve got (1, 2, 3, or JACKPOT if all four match). One slot is always visible to everyone, bluff wisely. Think someone’s lying? Call LIAR. If the claim is false, the player faces Death Spin. No Heart means instant death. If they survive, their last claim’s points are removed and the game continues. Reach 5 points to unlock EXECUTE. Only the single highest-scoring player can use it to instantly kill the lowest-scoring player (chosen at random if there is a tie). Each player has a one-time opportunity to use DOUBLE SPIN, allowing them to spin twice in a single turn. Timing it right can change everything. Why You'll Love Liar's Bar Engaging multiplayer online experience Thrilling bar games focused on lying and deception Unique characters with captivating backstories Regular updates with new games and characters Immersive bar simulation Meet the Cast Every night, nine regulars bet their lives at this table — their pasts are murmured at the next. Discover their whispered stories in Table Stories, available from the main menu. Follow Us Stay updated with the latest news and sneak peeks by following us on social media. Don't miss out on the action! Grab a seat, order a drink, and get ready to lie!
