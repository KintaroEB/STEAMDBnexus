About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dive into the thrilling world of Aquapark Tycoon! Create the ultimate pool paradise from the ground up in this classic tycoon simulation game that offers endless creativity and challenges, whether you're a seasoned waterpark magnate or a newbie pool start-up. Combine creativity, strategy, and management, and make a splash in the world of waterparks to become the ultimate Aquapark Tycoon! Slide Building Use your creativity and engineer the wildest water slides the world has ever seen! From towering spirals to adrenaline-pumping speed chutes, you design every twist, turn, and splash. Customize slides to cater to all thrill levels, ensuring guests of all ages have the time of their lives. But make sure your guests won't fly off into the stratosphere. Pool Building Design beautiful pools that serve as the heart of your waterpark. From action-packed wave pools to luxurious wellness oases, your pools will be the place where memories are made. Tailor each pool's shape, depth, temperature, water, and attractions to create a diverse environment that satisfies all your guests' needs. Sauna Building Make your park a place for cozy relaxation and luxury with special sauna facilities. Customize temperature settings, scents, aesthetics, and amenities to provide a top-tier wellness experience, and satisfy guests seeking a peaceful retreat from the excitement. Define your park's own infusion schedule, using a wide range of essential oils and aromas. Each infusion & aroma offers different benefits and attracts guests with different tastes. Co-op Gameplay Enjoy the fun with friends in Aquapark Tycoon’s co-op gameplay mode! Join forces with other tycoons to build and manage the most extravagant waterpark the world has ever seen. Strategize together and divide management duties to optimize your park's success. Share your builds on the Steam Workshop for everyone else to enjoy, or use Workshop builds in your park. Guests' Needs & Preferences Keep an eye on the desires and needs of your guests. Whether they're looking for thrill, sports, relaxation, or wellness options, your ability to meet their expectations determines your park's success. Take in their feedback and adapt your park to build an enjoyable park for a wide range of guests, from thrill-seeking kids to adventurous seniors. Make sure everyone finds their favorite spot, whether it's the kiddie pool, the extreme slides, or the pool bar in a calming thermal pool. Park Management Oversee every aspect of your park's operation, and shape the future of your aquatic wonderland. Use indoor and outdoor areas to your advantage to be prepared for different weather conditions and seasons. Define different areas to optimize your park layout for maximum enjoyment and efficiency, ensuring every guest leaves with a smile. Staff Management Hire and manage a team of professionals to keep your park running smoothly. From lifeguards who oversee the safety of your guests, entertaining sauna masters performing fun infusions, to maintenance workers taking care of the behind-the-scenes stuff, each staff member plays an important role in creating a safe and enjoyable environment for all. Diverse Scenarios Embark on a journey through a variety of scenarios that test your creativity and management skills. Each scenario offers unique objectives and constraints. Be it an easy outdoor park in a summer land where it's always sunny and beautiful, or challenging swinging seasons that sway your guests' preferences, where summer's heat drives guests outside into cool refreshing pools, and low temperatures in winter turn the cozy warmth of your saunas into your guests' favorite retreat. You’ll need to be on top of your game to keep your park afloat and your guests happy.
