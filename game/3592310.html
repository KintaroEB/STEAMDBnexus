Desktop Pals 2 is the most advanced desktop companion game out there. Featuring full Steam inventory integration, custom user-created Desktop Pals, game overlay, and much more. Steam Inventory Integration 25+ Desktop Pals with different rarities to collect as Steam items! Collect daily rewards & unlock new and rare pals! Browse, Create & Share Pals Create custom Workshop Pals using animation sprite sheets! Share them on the Steam Workshop for everyone to try! Game Overlay Overlay your Desktop Pal over other games! Play with it in those pesky downtimes! Full Customization Change the Desktop Pals location, size and much more! Customize the settings to your liking!
