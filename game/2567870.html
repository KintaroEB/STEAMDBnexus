About this gameFull description from Steam+
Climb or die! Take on the challenge of the infamous hardcore platformer either alone or chained together with up to three friends. Climb as high as possible to escape Hell. Coordinate your clambering and perfect your jumps to scale punishing platforms and outrun the scorching heat. Every layer threatens your ascension with unique challenges, each tougher than the last. Will you make it to the summit or perish before glory? Features Choose your difficulty: Brave the punishing Normal Mode, play with checkpoints in Beginner Mode, or race against constantly rising flames in Lava Mode. It's masocore heaven! Local and online co-op: Play together in person or online Realistic chain physics: The chain is fully simulated and wraps around platforms. Use this power to your advantage Leaderboards: Compare your climbs with the world Meaningful rewards: Leaderboards and Achievements are only valid in Normal and Lava Modes
