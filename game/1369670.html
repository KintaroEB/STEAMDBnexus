About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Sometimes the best therapy is a long drive with music" Open World Drive day & night, pick up hitchhikers, refuel, check engine temperature. Watch out for the cops! Driving jobs Taxi, Bus, Trucks and much more! Realistic Tire Physics With sophisticated vehicle and tire physics, you can enjoy realistic and challenging driving. Feel the tire grip and burn some tires by drifting! If you are a keyboard player, don't forget to use 'Burnout' when you want to let the rear go sideways. Driving Assist is available for keyboard players and controllers are fully supported. Singleplayer and Multiplayer Mode Enjoy solitude in single-player or have fun with friends in multiplayer mode. Time attack The online leaderboard shows you how fast you are. Autocross is a good way to improve your driving skills. Race You can compete with other players on the on-road and off-road tracks, or you can set waypoints and open a street race event. Company Create a company and make your own bus/truck route. Hire an AI driver or other player to work for you. Key Features Open world (24 x 16 Km) with Day & Night cycle 50+ Cars, Trucks and Trailers Driving with Keyboard, Mouse, Gamepad, Steering wheel Realistic, yet easy (with the assist feature) driving Single-player and Multiplayer Mode Passenger Transportation (Hitchhikers, Taxi, Bus) Cargo Transportation (Pickup, Trucks, Semi-Trailer) Special Vehicle Jobs (Police, Tow Truck, Ambulance and more will be added) Refueling Replacing car parts Buying/Renting cars Blinker, Headlight, Horn, Driving Mode, and Auto-Pilot!
