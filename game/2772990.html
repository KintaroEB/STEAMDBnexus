About this gameFull description from Steam+
Ghost Janitors is a 1-4 player co-op horror game where you and your friends clean haunted locations. What starts as a simple job quickly turns into a terrifying fight against eerie ghosts and dark secrets. Stay brave, work together, and scrub away the supernatural—if you dare. DO YOUR JOB Clean up the house with your team, get out and get rewarded for each task you complete. STAY IN THE LIGHT Keep the generator and lights running, and stay in the light. Be careful not to turn on too many lights, or the generator will shut down. RANDOMIZED GAMEPLAY Every playthrough has a unique experience; Ghost Janitors introduces randomized ghosts, tasks, sounds, jumpscares, and more, delivering dynamic and unpredictable gameplay PROGRESSION Dedication pays off, as the game rewards your progress with unlockable content. Discover 5+ maps with captivating backstories, perks to enhance your gameplay and a range of cosmetics to personalize your character.. FEATURES Ghost Janitors boasts impressive graphics, heart-stopping jumpscares, and immersive 3D audio, creating a truly chilling atmosphere. Engage in proximity voice chat to share the terror with your friends in real time. If you're seeking a horror game that combines thrills and entertainment, Ghost Janitors is a must-play. Gather your team, equip yourselves, and let's conquer the haunted challenge together!
