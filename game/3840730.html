About this gameFull description from Steam+
A Roguelite Puzzle Game That Reinvents a Timeless Formula What if falling blocks met a jump button and a rebellion? Gunny Ascend transforms the classic block-stacking puzzle formula into a fast-paced, genre-bending experience. Blending roguelite progression, adaptive platforming, and a dimension-hopping story full of chaos, charm, and surprise. ABOUT THIS DEMO: Jump straight into the action with this special demo of Gunny Ascend and get a hands-on preview of its chaotic roguelite gameplay. What’s Included Play a condensed version of World 1 from the full game. Battle through 5 frantic levels packed with hazards, tricky jumps, and relentless enemies. Face the first boss, Miasmo, in an intense showdown. Try the starting character and experiment with a small set of core abilities. Unlock the first 6 guest characters. Experienced our online multiplayer PVP mode. What’s in the Full Game Multiple themed worlds, each with unique enemies, hazards, and bosses. A full roster of playable characters, each with their own skills and upgrades. Expanded progression systems, unlockable abilities, and power-ups. More secrets, challenges, and replayability than ever.
