About this gameFull description from Steam+
Frontline 1942 World War 2 is an exciting online shooter that takes you into the thick of the battles of the Second World War. In this epic game, you will become a real hero, being on the front line as the best soldier. Accept the challenge and embark on a journey through destroyed cities, burning fields and dangerous jungles. You will fight in first-person mode, feeling the adrenaline and tension of each battle. The graphic execution of the online game will simply amaze you with its quality. Every texture in this shooter, every effect is created with love of detail so that you can fully immerse yourself in the atmosphere of the Second World War. The soundtrack recreates the sounds of shots, explosions and commands, making the game even more realistic. You will have to fight in various theaters of military operations, including city streets, trenches, snow-covered plateaus and other dangerous locations. Each place has its own characteristics and strategic advantages, which you should skillfully use in your actions. Weapons will be your faithful companion in battles. You will be able to choose from a diverse arsenal, including rifles, pistols, machine guns and grenades. Each weapon has its own characteristics and capabilities, and you have to use them according to the current situation on the battlefield. Your teammates will be your friends or other players who also strive to win. Cooperation and coordination with the team will be important elements of success. Team tactics, communication and interaction - all this will help you overcome obstacles and win. Fight in exciting game modes, including team battles, assaults and holding positions. In each mode, you will have to use your skills and strategic thinking to achieve goals and win. The game features the intersection of several game genres such as: shooter, action, strategy, world war, race and pvp. You won't be bored! Frontline 1942 World War 2 will allow you to plunge into the historical atmosphere of the Second World War, feel a real spiritual connection with the heroes of the past and experience large-scale battles full of tension and dynamics. Be brave, be heroes and prove your strength on the battlefield!
