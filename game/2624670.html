About this gameFull description from Steam+
Go on a scavenger-hunt adventure that spans 84 hand-crafted levels, while getting to know the four friends who struggle to keep their video game studio together. You may find much more than just cats on this journey... Find Matt's Cats features: Thousands of items to find, and 20+ hours of gameplay. 3 difficulty modes, with different goals in each, allowing for multiple play-throughs. Spot-the-difference challenges, and other secret game modes. A meta narrative about game development and friendship. Occasional dark humour. (disabled by default for a family-friendly experience) Lots of accessibility options. (play at your own pace) 100% DRM-free, so you can make a copy for your grandma!
