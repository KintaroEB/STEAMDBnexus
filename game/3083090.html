What is Coins? Coins is a completely Free To Play Idler Clicker Coin collecting game How to get Coins? Click the Coin on screen to get a random Coin every 2 ingame hours How to trade Coins? You can jump into the Steam Community Market at any time to trade, buy and sell Coins. You just need a regular Steam account, no other setup required! Collect Coins and complete your collections or show them off in your Steam profile! Start your collection today for free!
