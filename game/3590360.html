About this gameFull description from Steam+
Your kingdom awaits. Will you rise… or be forgotten? Your adventure begins with nothing more than a small keep and a handful of loyal villagers. From there, it’s up to you to create a thriving, self-sufficient kingdom that can withstand the test of time and the threat of your rivals. Construct and expand everything from sawmills and stone quarries to blacksmiths and barracks. Design your city to be both efficient and defensible—because enemies lurk around every corner. As your settlement grows, so does your influence. Unlock new technologies, train specialized troops, and manage your economy with care. Every structure, upgrade, and layout decision matters. Will you become a benevolent ruler focused on prosperity, or a ruthless warlord driven by conquest? Forge Unbreakable Alliances and Rise Together No ruler can conquer the world alone. In Goodgame Empire, cooperation isn’t just encouraged—it’s essential. Band together with other players to form powerful alliances and expand your collective reach across the continent. Allies can support each other during attacks, rally behind joint offensives, and share valuable resources during times of need. What begins as a simple pact of mutual benefit can evolve into deep strategic relationships that shape the fate of entire kingdoms. Coordinate large-scale operations through alliance chats and shared planning tools, and experience what it means to build not just an empire, but a legacy—together. The bonds you form may one day determine your survival in the game’s most epic conflicts. Plan Ruthlessly and Dominate in PvE & PvP Battles In this world, cunning is just as important as strength. From the very beginning, you’ll be faced with threats, and only those who plan ahead will emerge victorious. Engage in challenging PvE battles against fierce bandits, rogue warlords, and monstrous event bosses to earn rare resources and gear. Every encounter is an opportunity to grow stronger and sharpen your tactics. But the real thrill lies in PvP warfare. Scout your enemies, analyze their defenses, and strike when they least expect it. Whether you’re defending your walls from a siege or launching an all-out offensive across enemy lines, each battle demands precision, timing, and nerve. Customize your troops, equip your commanders with powerful relics, and execute devastating formations to outwit your opponents and claim your rightful place among the elite. Compete with the Best on a Global Scale Goodgame Empire is more than a game—it’s a living, breathing world populated by millions of players around the globe. From weekly events to grand seasonal campaigns, there’s always a new challenge waiting. Compete in cross-server wars, battle for dominion over entire regions, and climb the global leaderboards to earn exclusive rewards and eternal glory. Fame and fortune aren’t handed out—they must be fought for. Participate in epic events like the Kingdoms League, the War of the Realms, or the legendary Battle for Berimond, where the stakes are high and only the most organized, skilled alliances can prevail. Your reputation will echo across kingdoms. Will it be one of honor, power, or ruthless ambition? Strengthen Your Army with Legendary Generals Even the best armies are only as strong as the leaders who guide them. That’s why Goodgame Empire introduces a thrilling twist to traditional strategy: a constant rotation of brand-new, powerful generals who can turn the tide of war. These elite heroes aren’t just cosmetic—they’re game-changers. Some inspire your troops with unbreakable morale, others decimate enemy walls with siege mastery, and a select few offer rare synergies that can only be unlocked through smart gameplay and dedication. Collect them, upgrade them, and build your perfect lineup to match your style—whether that’s iron-clad defense, overwhelming offense, or balanced domination.
