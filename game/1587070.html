About this gameFull description from Steam+
Fruits is a clicker game where you click a basket of fruits and it counts how much have you clicked. You get a fruit every fixed hours of game time, each fruit is different it ranges from rarity, fruit and a multiplier to your basket clicking. You can get a fruit every hour, 5 hours, 10, hours and each day. Each chance are different and you get a higher rarity on the longer drops. You can also chop any unwanted fruit into fruit bits and use those to craft another fruit with the same chance as the 5 hour chance. Each fruits and bits can be traded and sold. Send your design via discord and if the community likes it we will put it into the game. Feedback is always welcome! Come have fun!
