About this gameFull description from Steam+
Fisher Online - online wild fishing simulator. Forget about licenses and catch rates. Choose bait or lures and catch fish in Europe and Russia. You can fish anywhere on open water at any time of the day using a float, spinning or bottom rod. Many species of fish, from small bleak to huge sturgeon, are waiting for you.
