About this gameFull description from Steam+
SEGA Football Club Champions (FC Soccer) delivers the most authentic soccer manager simulation game, powered by Football Manager™ and featuring real-world player data from FIFPRO™. With official partnerships including Major League Soccer (MLS), Manchester City, J League, and K League, this is your gateway to managing a global soccer franchise. Play for free anywhere, anytime on mobile, desktop, and console with seamless cross-platform play. 🆕 The MLS Update Is Here! Major League Soccer has arrived! Lead iconic MLS clubs to glory with exclusive cover athletes representing South Korea, USA, and Germany. Compete across American cities, win the MLS Cup, and rise through the ranks in this soccer league manager! 🏴Manchester City Partnership Take the reins of England's most dominant football club with exclusive access to Manchester City players representing Norway, Egypt, and the Netherlands. Build a squad worthy of Premier League dominance, lift the cup, and lead your club to greatness. ⚽ Manage, Compete, Dominate Build your team of soccer superstars from nations across the globe from Spain, Argentina, France, Germany, USA, Norway, Egypt, the Netherlands, Brazil, Japan, South Korea and beyond. Whether you're chasing glory on the international stage or dominating global football leagues, master the art of club management towards lifting the world's biggest cup in SEGA Football Club Champions (FC Soccer). 🌟 Lead Your Team to Greatness • Create and manage your own soccer club, recruiting elite players from MLS, Manchester City, FIFPRO™, J League, K League, and more. • Play through official leagues and compete in cup tournaments as you aim for international glory. • Scout talent from around the world, and shape them into world-class superstars. ⚽ Realistic Soccer Manager Simulation • Dive into advanced club management simulators to create winning strategies and tactics. • Set up world-class training programs and upgrade your stadium for peak performance. • Scout the transfer market for top international recruits and grow your soccer team into a powerhouse ready to compete for the cup. 🏆 Competitive Football Gameplay • Challenge players worldwide in PvP modes. • Compete in league matches, cup battles, and international tournaments featuring the world's best nations. • Aim for the top of the Leaderboards and claim your title as the best soccer manager! 🔥 Authentic Global Football Experience • Real-world player data powered by Football Manager™ and FIFPRO™ for authentic gameplay. • Control licensed players representing top footballing nations and recreate iconic cup-winning moments on the world stage. • Every decision shapes your club's future and your path to lifting the ultimate cup. 🎮 Play Anytime, Anywhere • Seamless cross-platform play on mobile, desktop, and console. • Completely free to play! Rebuild your hometown club into a global franchise and chase cup glory on the world stage! SEGA Football Club Champions (FC Soccer) combines passion, strategy, and competition. Step up, build your dynasty, win every cup, and transform your local team into the ultimate soccer franchise known across the world. #SFCC
