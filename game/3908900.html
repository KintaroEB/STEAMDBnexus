About this gameFull description from Steam+
REMAINS is a mix of genres with its core as a platformer: side view, familiar game mechanics (jumping, climbing stairs). A rich arsenal of weapons plus fights being a crucial part of the game give you vibes of 2D-shooter. Finally, you can take it as a roguelike game with exploring procedurally generated locations, picking locks, gathering loot, leveling up and learning new skills/perks, crafting, trading, etc. The amount of content right now is huge: several large locations with numerous kinds of rooms, almost a hundred types of enemies, about two hundred types of weapons and explosives, dozens of spells, more than sixty perks and plenty of other things. The development is complete, but new versions may appear in the future. Extra content Reforged - A mod that changes many aspects of the game and fixes bugs, made by Kostr184. Extra bunker Rooms - a mod that adds new rooms in military base locations, made by convetangent.
