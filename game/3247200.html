About this gameFull description from Steam+
Engage in authentic medieval warfare, loot weapons, and lead your army to battle! At the dawn of medieval history, build your castle and evolve your army using real weapons and armor from ages past. March against hostile civilizations as you re-trace the steps of history. Engage your rivals and crush their empires in battle! 🗡️No Dragons, No Magic, Not Now, Not Ever. Dawn of Ages is a game that’s grounded in historical accuracy. No fantasy creatures, no mystical spells, just real, historical empires and war strategy games like no other. Every weapon, armor, and tactic you use is based on the reality of ancient battlefields. This isn’t just another castle game — it’s your chance to relive history as it truly unfolded! ⚔️Epic Battles Await! Command your forces in brutal, large-scale battles. Plan and execute strategies that mirror those of real historical conflicts. Customize your troops with historical armor and weapons, and deploy your strategy in war strategy games that will test your tactical skills to the limit. Victory depends on your ability to outthink and outmaneuver your enemies on the battlefield! 🏰Build and Expand Your Civilization! As you progress through Dawn of Ages, you’ll need to manage and expand your empire. Construct mighty castles, recruit workers, and transform your modest village into a thriving medieval city. Each structure you build directly impacts your ability to wage war, train soldiers, and gather resources. Whether you’re looking for the next great civilization game or a castle game that tests your building skills, Dawn of Ages offers it all. 🛡️Customize Your Army and Gear! No two armies are alike in Dawn of Ages. Choose from over 100 unique historical weapons, shields, and armor to equip your forces. Each piece of gear offers different advantages and can turn the tide of battle in your favor. Strategic thinkers will love the depth of customization and the ability to craft the perfect army for any situation. 🔥Engaging Gameplay with Easy Controls Experience the thrill of medieval combat as Dawn of Ages offers immersive, fast-paced battles with an easy-to-use interface. Whether you’re a fan of strategy games or just looking for quick, satisfying battle games, Dawn of Ages puts the power of command in your hands. Build your empire, conquer your enemies, and outlast rival civilizations in epic war strategy games! 🌐 Play Anywhere with Cross-Platform Support! Dawn of Ages offers seamless cross-platform play, allowing you to enjoy your gaming experience on both PC and mobile devices. Whether you’re at home or on the go, you can access your account and continue your journey without missing a beat. Team up with friends or compete against players on the opposite platform. Experience the freedom to engage in epic warfare whenever and wherever you choose!
