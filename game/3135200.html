About this gameFull description from Steam+
Game Description: Become the hero of a fantasy world and stop the monster invasion! Legacy of Defense combines innovative side-scrolling action with deck-building elements. Embark on an adventure, recruit allies, and defend yourself from dangerous foes! Key Features: Side-Scrolling Action: Experience intense battles with stunning 2D graphics and intuitive controls. Feel the thrill of defending against swarming monsters. Deck-Building Strategy: Collect various cards to create your unique deck. Each card includes powerful skills, requiring strategic gameplay. Fantasy Monsters: Face various fantasy monsters that you can only imagine. Battle against monsters with different abilities. Skill Upgrades: Gain experience through combat and upgrade your hero's skills. Use powerful skills to effectively clear out monsters. Gameplay: Defend against incoming monsters. Choose cards to use skills and defeat enemies. Strategic deck-building is the key to victory. Clear each stage and face increasingly powerful monsters, ultimately defeating the final boss! Test your strategy and skills. Are you ready to become a brave hero and save the fantasy world?
