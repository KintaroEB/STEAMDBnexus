About this gameFull description from Steam+
About the Game Deeply integrated with the classic features of dark fantasy games, this title delivers a rich gear-building experience. Tailor-made for dark fantasy fans who are tired of intense mechanical operations, it lets you fully enjoy the fun of matching top-tier gear without tedious controls. Game Background • You will serve as a servant of the Old God, building a town upon the sealed grounds of Blackreef Isle.   Grow your cult, expand your influence, and choose those with hidden potential from exiled criminals.   Awaken their power with the blood of the Old God, transforming them into Godsworn under your command.   Send the Godsworn to wage war across the world, growing stronger through slaughter and trials.   At last, step into the Void, defeat the mighty Outer Gods, and reclaim the sealed power.   When every seal is broken, the sleeping Old God shall rise from the depths of Blackreef Isle. Game Features Idle Auto-Battle Full-auto real-time combat frees your hands completely—sit back and watch valuable loot fill your inventory effortlessly! Six Unique Classes Each class boasts an exclusive and one-of-a-kind skill system. Smartly combine skills to unleash devastating combat power. Robust Gear System Inheriting the essence of multiple dark fantasy games, gears of different rarities each serve a distinct purpose, enabling countless build possibilities. Town Construction & Upgrade A variety of town buildings offer rich functional services. Customize upgrades at will to greatly enhance building efficiency. Progressive Gameplay Modes Five adventure modes—Expedition, Exploration, Divine Tower, Alternate Dimension, and Finality—unfold in a layered progression, bringing diverse challenge experiences. Single-Player Seasonal Challenges (Seasonal content will be gradually added after the launch) Each season features unique new content with independent save files. You can embark on any seasonal journey at any time.
