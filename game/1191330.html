About this gameFull description from Steam+
Inferna – Classic Open-World MMORPG with Real Progression (Level 1–100) Inferna is a classic free-to-play open-world MMORPG with long-term progression, grind, and real risk – set in an oriental-inspired fantasy world. No resets, no seasonal wipes – your character stays and grows permanently. Reach level 100, farm rare drops, close hellish rifts, defeat powerful bosses, and upgrade your equipment with real stakes: gear can be destroyed on failed upgrades at the blacksmith. Progress comes from playtime, decisions, and perseverance. Characters & Builds Four playable classes: Warrior, Wizard, Assassin, and Half-Demon – each with a unique playstyle. Custom builds: Enchantments, bonuses, and equipment define your combat style. Level 1–100 with no reset system: Long-term character investment instead of short-lived seasons. Classic Progression: Grind, Drops & Risk Upgrade system up to +10: Risky upgrades with possible destruction – success feels earned. Enchantment system: Additional bonuses for PvE or PvP. Rift events: Dynamic rifts with monster waves and valuable materials. Massive content: 8,000+ items, 400+ monsters, 500+ quests, and 33 maps – from desert regions to highland areas. Factions, PvP & Endgame Two kingdoms: Jerro or Caratas. PvP zones: Fight for resources, spots, and glory. Endgame up to level 100: High-level bosses, rifts, and PvP content – expansion to level 120 planned. One World for All – Cross-Platform PC and mobile players share the same world and can play together. The Android version is currently in beta. Fair Free-to-Play & Supporter System Inferna is free-to-play with an optional item shop and supporter system. Convenience options, boosts, and cosmetic content. Content and systems are achievable in-game. Supporter benefits speed up progress but don't replace long-term development or good equipment. The World of Inferna Pangaeus was once a united realm. After the Night of Infernumantis, hellish rifts opened, monsters overran the land, and the kingdoms fell into hostility. Architecture, desert regions, and cities are inspired by oriental fantasy, giving the world its own distinctive style. Independently Developed in Europe Inferna has been developed since 2016 by a small, passionate indie team in Europe. Over 250,000 registered accounts have tried the game since then. Community feedback flows directly into updates, balance adjustments, and new content. For Players Who Miss Classic MMORPGs If you're looking for grind, drops, risky upgrades, and long-term character commitment, Inferna is your home.
