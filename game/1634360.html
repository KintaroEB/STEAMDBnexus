About this gameFull description from Steam+
Discover rules, gather information, and use the gained knowledge to find new paths forward. There are layers of secrets hidden in plain sight! Bit by bit, the lost world of the EMUUROM creatures begins to unfold. Study creatures and reveal hidden pathways with your trusty scanner beam. Traverse an interconnected world full of mysteries and use your scanner to solve puzzles ranging from obvious to obscure. Your every scan is stored in the EMUUDEX. Connect the dots as you gather more information about the world and its denizens. No hand-holding! It's up to your wits to figure out the workings of the world. Unique retro look and feel arising from the TIC-80 fantasy console restrictions.
