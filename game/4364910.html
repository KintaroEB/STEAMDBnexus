About this gameFull description from Steam+
A loot-driven action role-playing game (ARPG) crafted by a solo loot enthusiast. Step into a demon-infested, monster-ridden continent and embark on a challenging, surprise-filled journey. Core Gameplay: This is an isometric-view ARPG. you start your adventure in a humble starting village. Defeat demons and demon-corrupted monsters, level up your character, and gear up with collected loot. Challenge elite enemies and conquer all boss foes. Classes: Choose from 3 classes—Warrior, Mage, Warlock—and switch anytime to use their unique skills in combat. Gear: Hundreds of base gear pieces are available in the game, spanning multiple rarity tiers with random extra affixes. Powerful legendary affixes and gear deliver massive stat boosts. Forging: Unique forging lets you add random affixes/runes, upgrade tier, and reforge gear—tailor your character’s progression. Bosses & Elites: Bosses lurk deep in maps—their defeat is the main source of god-tier gear. Elites are plentiful, dropping gear and forging materials. Skills: All skills are learned via skill books. Skill books drop randomly, with the most powerful ones only obtainable by defeating bosses. Rifts: Enemies may spawn invasion rifts on death, spawning tough bosses/elites for better rewards. Trials: Find Rift Keys from looting or boss kills—enter trials, navigate traps, beat the gatekeeper, and claim rich rewards. Quests: Grab bounties from the bounty board. Random high-tier scrolls unlock elite challenges with better rewards than bounties. Ruins: Use Ruin Gems to access challenging monster ruins packed with bosses. Supports multiplayer co-op. Created to relive the joy of exploring and looting with friends years ago. It features classic class/skill divisions—ranged sword auras from melee, screen-filling fire walls, mighty skeleton armies, charmed followers—and over 100 affixes for infinite, easy-to-customize gear builds. Gameplay modes including Rift Trials, Ruin Exploration, Rift Invasions and Monster-Guarded Chests keep combat from feeling monotonous. Step into this demon-infested, monster-ridden continent and embark on a challenging, surprise-filled journey.
