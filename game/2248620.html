About this gameFull description from Steam+
This is a game that stretches beyond your typical tycoon and economy simulator, in the Economy Bros you will start your epic journey by customizing your character's face and skin color. Please note, this is currently being developed by a solo developer, I am continuously striving to make the game as good as it possibly can be with my limited time. I have implemented a few incentives for giving me feedback and will be updating the game well after release. Thank you for your consideration and feel free to join our community discord in the links section of this page!! Character Customization Once you're ready to enter the world as your new beautiful self you have two options to begin with, option number one, you can find a business that is open and that is in need of you're help! There is a variety of different jobs, including being a cashier, fry cook, burger builder, pizza maker, and even a floor plan designer, each with varying pay, and for each individual job, for every few orders you do, you will get a raise that increases the amount of money you make per order completed, the sky is the limit for your opportunities! Work Numerous Jobs and get raises Option number two! If you decide you don't want to walk that mediocre 9 to 5 lifestyle get your hands dirty by punching down trees to get logs, use those logs to craft a log splitter to make planks, use those planks to craft a hand miner, use that miner to dig up rock and iron ore, take rock and planks to make a brick press, that presses rock into bricks, use those bricks to make a furnace and smelt the iron ore into ingots! You can sell all of these resources at the market for profit! Gather and Sell Resources Once you've made enough money you can buy blueprints at any blueprint store or on the in-game computer, and place this building where ever you think looks best, or if you don't want the hassle you can just buy one that is for sale! You can check the value of each business type on the computer as well to make sure you are not losing money on your trade! Though buildings are quite expensive so you may want to get a loan at the bank to give you a bit of a head start. Get a place to stay Every building has an interior in this game, even all of the homes! You can buy or build any home you like, and decorate it and even change the materials of the walls and floors! You can also rent these homes out to make even more money! Customize your homes Gamble If you are a real risk taker, take your funds to the casino and gamble your life savings away or hit big and take your winnings to a more responsible investment! With games like Craps, Roulette, Ball Drop, Blackjack, Poker, Slots, Mines, and even horse racing, you have plenty of options to decide! Manufacture, Customize or Sell Cars There is also an entire vehicle industry where you can manufacture cars at the Vehicle Factory, and sell them at a dealership! You can also keep them and customize them at any body shop, and wash them when they get dirty at any carwash. Don't run out of gas in the middle of nowhere! Keep your tank full at any gas station as well! Once you've made it big, buy more islands and create cities from scratch to fit your needs. The opportunities for success are endless, and play with your friends for more competition! The Economy Bros is the ultimate life simulator, work hard, build your wealth at your own pace, and live large by splurging on fast cars, luxury homes, and everything in between.
