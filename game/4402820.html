About this gameFull description from Steam+
Become the Peerless One A turn-based combat game infused with deck-building. Step into the world of Wuxia, challenge its myriad masters, and embark on your legend to become the peerless warrior. Master new martial Arts through combat and build your unique deck of moves. Defeat powerful champions, claim their prized relics, and evolve your combat style into an unstoppable force. Game Features Simple controls suitable for all ages! Play effortlessly with mouse, keyboard, or gamepad. Counter every move! From your own attacks to the enemy's actions, everything is governed by your strategy. A wide variety of enemies! Duel against all manner of martial arts masters and become the peerless one. Multiple character choices! Play as different characters, each with unique moves, offering high replayability. Uniquely Chinese pixel art style! Enter the world of Chinese Wuxia and experience the clash of blades within its poetic visuals.
