About this gameFull description from Steam+
LUGH WORLD – A Massive Multiplayer Creature-Taming Adventure Lugh World is a massive online adventure where every step opens up new possibilities. Discover the City of Miranda, capture and evolve Lughs — unique creatures full of personality — and begin your journey in a living, dynamic world. It's not just about battles: it's about friendships, rivalries, and the freedom to create your own story. In Lugh World, you connect with players from all over the world, share stories, and build memories in a universe that's constantly transforming. Step into Lugh World, a universe brimming with magic, diverse cultures, and environments with unique biomes. From floating islands to frozen canyons, every setting brings new discoveries and challenges. At the heart of this world lies Miranda, the great city that pulses as the gathering point for players — a place of trade, socializing, and mysteries waiting to be uncovered in this new world. And of course, the true protagonists: the Lughs. Fascinating creatures, full of personality, elements, magic, and special abilities that turn every encounter into a new story. It all began with an accident. Manu was transported from Earth to Lugh World and never returned. Now, you are one of the explorers who has accepted the mission to find her. Your reasons are your own, but the outcome of this journey could change far more than just a single life. Explore, capture, evolve, prove your worth in this new world, and become a true Lugher. Every capture is an achievement. Defeat a wild Lugh to create the perfect opportunity to capture it — but remember: you'll only get one attempt. On top of that, each species has its own capture difficulty, demanding strategy, persistence, and the use of special crystals to boost your chances of success. Lughs are unique beings of mystical nature that emerged from the Light of Creation! Because of this special origin, they possess special powers and abilities! They can be found on the islands and across other maps in the game. As you explore the world alongside your Lughs, you gain experience and grow stronger together! Lughs can take on new, more powerful forms as they gain experience. There are several forms of evolution: through stones, through levels, through the time of day, or a combination of these methods. Some Lughs evolve into new forms in a linear way: While other Lughs have a non-linear form of evolution: In addition, you can use special stones capable of awakening new evolutions and completely transforming your Lughs' potential. You can obtain them as loot from wild Lughs and from evostone lootboxes in other content. Take on the world of Lugh World itself alongside your friends. Wild creatures, colossal bosses, and new adventures await those who explore the unknown lands. Every battle is a chance to collect loot, gain experience, and unlock new challenges. You choose how to fight: you can control your Lugh in third person, giving commands through your Lugher, or you can control your Lugh directly, allowing for greater strategic precision. In Lugh World, there are forces, powers, and organizations that seek to control the Lughs and their powers, solely to satisfy their own ambitions. The 3 main known sources of threat are: ▪ The Blight: The origin of the Blight is a mystery as ancient as the Light of Creation itself. When a Lugh — or any other creature — is covered by the Shadow of Ruin, that creature takes on the Blight. And in extreme cases, beings that contract the Blight transform into monstrous, violent versions of themselves. Through this, Lughs can become corrupted. ▪ The Heralds of Ruin: They can be considered a group of cultists who attempt to channel the Shadow of Ruin to destroy the world. There are rumors claiming that elite members of the Heralds can control Corrupted Lughs, in the same way a Lugher controls a normal Lugh. ▪ The Masked Wanderer: Many rumors tell of a man wearing a white mask and a black cloak. He appears near ruins or where unstable crystals erupt with raw energy. He avoids direct contact, observing from afar, but leaves enigmatic marks: symbols carved into stone, runes that seem to contain fragments of earthly writing. Enter a fully online MMORPG where everything happens in real time. Here, battles, encounters, and events are experienced side by side with thousands of players from all over the world. Form alliances, explore cities and regions with friends and rivals, take part in dynamic events, and dive into living marketplaces that reflect the strength of the community. Every session is unique, because the world of Lugh World never stops evolving. This is a persistent, vibrant, and social universe, shaped not only by the developers but by everyone who ventures into it. What's more, the same character can join any server without losing progress, making the social side even more appealing. In Lugh World, every encounter can become an opportunity. Trade rare Lughs and valuable items in real time with other players, expanding your collection and creating new strategic possibilities. Our servers are integrated, so with your character you can trade with players from all over the world. Trading goes beyond simple exchange: it's a social and economic bond within the world, bringing trainers closer together and strengthening the community. Every trade tells a story and can change the course of your journey. Complete special challenges from Miranda's great guilds, increase your influence, earn prestige, acquire tokens, complete daily, weekly, and monthly missions, and unlock new exclusive rewards in the shops. Every season brings different themed challenges. Progress through the season to earn exclusive rewards while you explore the world and complete objectives to become an ever-better Lugher. Advance your progress by carrying out activities, completing daily missions, defeating wild Lughs, and taking on Bosses, and unlock cosmetic items, resources, and special rewards. Customize your character with clothing, accessories, and looks to create a unique identity. Beyond just basic colors, you can choose textures, color combinations, wear the outfits of important characters, or even complete looks inspired by Lughs and unique themes. Manage your Lughs, cosmetics, and resources with an intuitive interface. On top of that, you can use the storage system, with sections and customizable tabs, to better organize your collection. Choose your team, equip your best items, and get ready for any challenge. The Lughpad is a technological device similar to a tablet, where the Lugher records the Lughs they encounter along the way. This instrument is a source of valuable information, both for the player and for the Lugh Research Center! Record general information about Lughs, Prismatics, and Variants, and expand your knowledge of these incredible creatures. And beyond Lughs, this device can also interact with the environment! Lugh World is made up of continents and islands with the most varied biomes and terrains. Each region of the world has its own socio-cultural dynamics and local history. And the past seems determined to make itself known to everyone — after all, throughout the world there are mysterious structures (some in ruins), statues, and monuments that seem to have been built by no one. Every corner of the world holds part of Lugh World's secrets. Lughs that reach their final form can be used as mounts! And this isn't limited to travel by land. Whether running, flying, or swimming, your Lughs can carry you to areas in a much faster and more fun way. Experience the freedom of gliding through the skies with your paraglider! Explore new areas and soar across the heavens in a thrilling and fun way! Find extremely rare Lughs with exclusive appearances that stand out during your adventure. Not only are they hard to find, they're also hard to capture — having a Prismatic in the game means dedication and consistency throughout your journey across the many maps and islands. A single Lugh can have more than one form! Discover all the variations — not only in appearance, but also in elements, abilities, and stats. Here, your collection has real strategic value. Lugh World will keep growing with new regions, creatures, systems, events, and content throughout development, together with the community. Keep an eye on the roadmap and our official social media to stay up to date on all the news. Note: the texts may contain translation errors. If you find any, please get in touch so we can correct them.
