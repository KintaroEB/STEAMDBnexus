About this gameFull description from Steam+
JUMP INTO THE CHAOS – PLAY FOR FREE! Grab your friends and dive into the ultimate multiplayer party battle royale! Race, stumble, and battle it out in hilarious obstacle courses where only one player can become the winner - will it be you? 32-PLAYER MULTIPLAYER ONLINE MADNESS! Race against up to 32 players in fast-paced, chaotic matches and tournaments. Can you dodge, jump, and stumble your way to victory? PLAY TOURNAMENTS & CLIMB THE RANKED LEADERBOARD Run, clash, kick and fall to survive in tournaments and collect points to climb the Ranked leaderboard and unlock cool rewards! 60+ COOL MAPS, ENDLESS FUN! Speed through insane obstacle courses, dodge crazy traps, and outplay your rivals! With over 60 maps packed with challenges, every match is a new adventure. CUSTOMIZE YOUR STUMBLER – THOUSANDS OF COSMETICS! Unlock epic skins, hilarious emotes, and more to stand out from the crowd. Become a ninja, superhero, or even a chicken, there’s a style for everyone. Collect them all and show them off! LIMITED-TIME EVENTS & AWESOME CROSSOVERS! Jump into special events playing the best game modes like Floor is Lava and Banana Bonanza or expand your collection with legendary stumblers from SpongeBob, My Hero Academia, and more! CROSS-PLAY ON STEAM & MOBILE! Play anywhere, anytime! Stumble Guys is free to play and supports cross-play between Steam and mobile, so you can battle your friends no matter where they are! Ready to stumble? Download now and join the fun!
