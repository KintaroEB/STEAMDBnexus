About this gameFull description from Steam+
As a famous worm once said: “C'mon, there's crime to cause- I mean, err, fight.” The iconic intrepid invertebrates return with a vengeance in the fan-favourite classic Worms™ Armageddon. Relive their glory days once again as you blast your way into enemy territory, sweep the field and remain the last worm standing. A Blast From the Past Rediscover the classic with over 55 iconically weird weapons and tools including Earthquake, Freeze and French Sheep Strike. But it’s not all fun and games: combine absurd artillery with serious strategy to dominate the field, blast your enemies off the map and win the war! A Classic That Never Gets Old Blast your way through single-player Deathmatch plus an exciting 40+ mission campaign. Master tactics using five special training modes including Sheep Racing, Crazy Crates and Advanced Weapon Training. And if you’ve not had enough, send your friends to the drink in online multi-player mode.
