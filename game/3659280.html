About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Off The Grid, the next evolution of the shooter genre from visionary director Neill Blomkamp (District 9, Elysium). Set in a near-future world where contestants are cybernetically augmented and dropped into a televised bloodsport where death is temporary, but fame is forever. In Off The Grid, YOU are a modular death machine. Switch your cyberlimbs at any time, and adapt your playstyle on the fly. Loot the body parts of those that have perished around you, and assemble your dream loadout. With more than 15 limbs available to loot, steal or drop in with, the possibilities for mayhem are endless. Off The Grid takes movement to the next level with a dexterous aerial movement system driven by advanced jetpacks. With strong utilization of the verticality of the maps players are able to both enter and exit fights rapidly, rewarding aggression and a fast-paced gameplay. Drop onto Teardrop Island, a living, breathing paradise built with cutting-edge technologies. Every corner of the map is designed to deliver a truly cinematic experience, setting the stage for an ambitious 60-hour narrative campaign coming later this year. Finetune weaponry to fit your play style: with thousands of combinations there is a loadout for any kind of killer. Tune your guns to perfection with a wide variety of cosmetics and attachments, or lean heavy into Teardrop Island’s latest cyberlimbs in order to dominate the battlefield. Call in your customized loadout at any time and make the battlefield your playground. Extract high-value loot off the island amidst a brutal battle for survival. Adapt, outsmart and work your way closer to victory. Off The Grid isn’t just about being the last one standing, it’s about looting and extracting the most valuable gear before becoming the next casualty. Every item you extract or earn can be traded on the in-game marketplace. We've build a truly open game economy, whether you're snagging that weapon you’ve been coveting, or flipping unused gear, every deal is driven by real supply and demand. List, trade and build up your perfect loadout. When Regen technology is invented, it's bought out exclusively by a consortium of billion-dollar gaming companies to stage a Real Battle Royale. It's a Super Bowl-style televised bloodbath where everyday people are augmented with cyberlimbs and dropped onto an abandoned island to fight to the death.
