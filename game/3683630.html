About this gameFull description from Steam+
This Demo is a limited version that prevents experience gain past level 10. Certain items, pets, and customizations are locked, and the demo is only playable in the France location. Save data does not persist to the full game. Wishlist now to enjoy the full experience! Fulfill your dream of owning and operating your own bookshop. Buy and sell inventory and use the profits to decorate and expand your shop. Build your business from a small boutique shop into a retail empire! Buy and place bookshelves, then fill them with new books, comics, and other items to sell. Run the cash register to earn revenue. As your business grows, hire employees to be cashiers and item stockers so you can focus on expansion. This sneak peek is limited to 2 employees and certain items are locked. Expand your reach to new locations around the world. Use your income from established shops to help set up new shops. Travel between shops to manage them and optimize your profits. Early access will launch with the France and Japan locations. Use your profits to customize your store. Choose walls and floors. Decorate with rugs, plants, wall art, and more! Create specialized sections for your favorite genre, or build cozy book nooks. Shop expansions are limited to 2 sections for the demo. Get boxes of used books that may contain rare mythical books. These books have powerful abilities to improve your profits and capabilities. Adopt a cat to keep you and your customers company every day in the shop. Buy food, a bed, and other items to keep the cat active and happy in the store.
