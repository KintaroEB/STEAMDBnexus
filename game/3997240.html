About this gameFull description from Steam+
MILITARIA COLLECTING In this game you are a militaria collector starting in Europe, looking for items by metal detecting, auctions or going to markets. You can also travel across the world to find relics from different locations from various conflicts in history. 1000+ DIFFERENT ITEMS With the amount of items in this game noone is expected to find and keep everything. There are items for almost every niche be it Soviet, German, American, WW1, WW2, Cold War, Yugoslav and many more. RESTORING ITEMS When you find a a gun by digging it up in the woods, it is obviously rusted and may be missing parts. You need to clean up the rust, clean the wood parts and find parts to fully restore it. EXPAND YOUR COLLECTION, CUSTOMIZE YOUR HOME With 1000+ different items to collect, no collection will be the same. Collect and display items that fit your interests, sell items you do not want to keep.
