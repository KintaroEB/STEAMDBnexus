About this gameFull description from Steam+
《Late Ming: Reborn in Flames》is a 2D open-world visual novel game. In the game, you play as Wei Xie, a young man who travels back to the tumultuous final years of the Ming Dynasty. Navigate between powerful factions, complete quests, and build relationships with characters to unlock their storylines—ultimately deepening your bonds with them and uncovering their secrets Immerse yourself in this era of chaos and intrigue as you experience Wei Xie’s intertwined fate with each character. Build your stronghold to face the challenges of the chaotic late Ming era; cultivate the land and gather confidantes representing diverse regional styles and charms. Upgrade your facilities and build your own commercial empire. Assemble your own troops to defend your homeland and challenge rival factions. Unlock various story chapters through your efforts.
