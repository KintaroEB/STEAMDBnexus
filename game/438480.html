About this gameFull description from Steam+
Rocked by an explosive soundtrack, choose your band, place your equipments and defend the Rock 'N' Roll from everyone and everything who try to pull it down, on a totally hardcore tower defense! Rock 'N' Roll Defense, is a tower defense game, where you should protect the rock concert from minions who are trying to stop the party! Think you strategy, to get 3 skulls in every one of the 40 stages. Pop, country, dance and even the hell minions are tryin to stop Rock from the spotlight and it's your job to stop them. Use strategy to get coins to place and upgrade the best speakers. 40 stages, 20 enemy types (4 bosses), 8 different speaker (tower) types. 10 music soundtrack by amazing artists, across multiple Rock 'N' Roll Styles (You can download it for free!)
