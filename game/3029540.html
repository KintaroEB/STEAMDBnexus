About this gameFull description from Steam+
Gunship Battle: Total Warfare, a hit modern military strategy game with over 10 million downloads, offers the perfect blend of strategy, intense combat, and an immersive, unique environment. Reclaim and fortify your base, build and upgrade iconic modern military units—ships, jets, and tanks—each designed with real-world precision and power. Immerse yourself in a gripping narrative set in a drowned world where the last survivors fight for dominance and survival. Key Features: - Legendary Military Units:  Strengthen, upgrade, and deploy real-world inspired ships, jets, and tanks, to enhance their unique abilities, firepower, and capabilities in combat. - PvE Features: Battle powerful enemy fleets and squadrons to conquer new territories. - PvP Features: Enjoy competitive and casual PvP battles, offering challenges for both casual players and hardcore military enthusiasts. - Alliance Power: Form powerful alliances with other Admirals, coordinating strikes, defending territory, and pushing back against the tide of invasion. - Mini-Games for Extra Action: Enjoy action-packed mini-games that offer a change of pace:    - Fortress: Test your strategic mind and tactical skills in this tower defense mini-game.    - Sky Ace: Strap into the cockpit and take to the skies in this arcade-inspired jet mini-game.
