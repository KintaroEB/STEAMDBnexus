About this gameFull description from Steam+
●Background A few days ago, a bright meteor streak across of the sky, the golden light lit up the whole sky. The color of golden symbolizes wealth, power, strength, sacredness and happiness, has attracted countless adventurers, including you, on the adventurous journey for this reason... ◆Chase the golden meteors and uncover the secrets of the golden legend, with abundant mainline chapters waiting for you to play! ◆ Explore a brand new world, experience different customs, and learn more about this world through more than a hundred side quests! ●Highlights of the Game 1. Equipment System ◆Thousands of well-designed unique equipment, hundreds of dark entries and randomly dropped enhanced spoils. ◆Surprises in dropping equipment, chances to upgrade to ancient equipment, even the legendary Titan creation!!!! ◆Rune words, collect the lost runes in all parts of the world to build the elite weapon!!!! 2.Character Development ◆ 50+ characters can be freely made to your own favorite team. Each character has an independent skill tree, for example, you can let Aikun (character) use Chicken Claw, HardStrike for ADC, you can also let him use the of serial earth of Basketball or Egg-laying to attack the enemies! ◆Ten talent trees with a large number of skills, free DIY. You can strengthen the basic attributes through the talent, the lightning damage into physical damage. If you don't want to be a mage and want to play as a tank, you can even swap the magic attack with your physical attack 3. Gameplay of Pet ◆Capture and train your own pets, becoming a master pet trainer! You can train pets with violent output, backup healing, and aura passive to provide bonus for characters. You can also meet mutant pets and flash pets during the adventure and hope you are lucky enough to find out the mutant flash pets! 4.Secondary Profession ◆Practice secondary professions, find design drawings, recipes, etc. to make potions, food and equipment to help you in your adventures! 5. Recruit NPC to build your own towns ◆Recruit NPC from all over the world to make your town more prosperous 6. Abyss Treasure ◆There are many fantastic secret treasures buried in the Abyss within extremely dangerous monsters... 7. Gameplay of Adventure ◆Adventures will happen during the game from time to time, including unique and rare monsters like treasure chests and so on! 8.Rich Branch Content ◆The game has more than a hundred side quests, hidden events and hidden treasure chests for you to explore! The exciting gameplay process will take you deep into the RPG story, to a whole new world! 9. Endless PlayAgain ◆With a wealth of customizable professions and skills, in-depth gameplay systems, randomized bonus, and follow-up development, PlayAgain 2: The Golden Legend is a game that can play again for hundreds or even thousands of hours. 10. Away From Keyboard ◆The game has built-in perfect AFK system, whenever you are, whatever you do, you can just leave it there, after work or sleep, come back with plenty bonus! 11. Easy to Start with, Difficult to Master ◆New and existing players can easily learn the game and use strategies to defeat the enemies. The game not only provide free mode, there is also a challenge mode including Normal, Nightmare, Hell three major levels, each pass will be extremely generous rewards! 12.Follow-up Content ◆We will continue to update and improve the game for a period of time after the game is launched, and the new gameplay will be updated in 1-2 months!
