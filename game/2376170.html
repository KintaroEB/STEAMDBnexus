About this gameFull description from Steam+
Hamster Clicker is a clicker game about collecting hamsters! Every 3 and 18 hours, you get dropped a chest with hamsters! Details You will get 1 common chest drop every 3 hours and 1 rare chest every 18 hours You can freely sell chests on community market, trade them or open in game. Most of them are free to open, but rarest one requires keys which you can get from items shop or buying DLC You can trade up 9 items of the same rarity for 1 with increased rarity You can use hamsters in game as models Game has leaderboards for best collectors with maximum amount of hamsters You can easily get achievements just for clicking
