About this gameFull description from Steam+
War Commander is a large-scale MMO RTS set in a post-apocalyptic world. Players take the role of the Commander in an effort to push back the forces of chaos and destruction and attempt to rebuild. Welcome to the apocalypse, Commander. The air is thick with the fog of war, and the horizon crawls with hostile factions hungry for control. Yet this small plot of land is ours to defend and expand. Daily Targets, Weekly Missions, and Monthly Events ensure there’s always a new challenge to tackle, rewarding quick thinking and decisive action. In this world, hesitation is fatal. The weak are eaten; the strong rewrite history and forge empires from ruin. From humble beginnings, watch your base evolve from a modest military outpost into a heavily fortified citadel capable of withstanding the most brutal of assaults. Manage resource production to keep your war machine running, invest in research institutions to unlock cutting‑edge technology, and expand your military infrastructure to support larger, deadlier forces. Your base is more than a cluster of buildings, it’s your lifeline in a wasteland that wants you dead. Customize your base and make it your own while protecting it from all who would challenge you. Study the World Map to survey all the hostile targets scattered across your Sector. Each one represents a rogue base, enemy encampment, or fortified threat waiting to be dismantled. Clearing these targets grants valuable rewards, resources, and opportunities to strengthen your forces. Deploy your platoons strategically and strike fast. In this world, if you don’t take the shot, someone else will. Daily and Weekly Missions provide a steady stream of objectives designed to test your skills and reward your efforts. Whether you’re destroying enemy bases, upgrading your forces, or gathering resources, each completed task brings you closer to major payouts and long‑term progression. These missions keep your war effort moving forward and offer a clear path toward building a stronger, more capable army. Each month introduces new units to recruit, experiment with, and master. Upgrading your troops unlocks their full combat potential, transforming basic soldiers and vehicles into elite battlefield assets. Equip them with powerful tech components to modify their behavior, enhance their strengths, and tailor them to your preferred playstyle. With the right upgrades, even a modest platoon can become a devastating force capable of turning the tide of battle. The Sector is never safe for long. New threats emerge every month, bringing fresh Operations packed with multi‑stage challenges and high‑stakes encounters. These events demand strategic unit selection, tactical precision, and careful planning to overcome. Completing Operations rewards you with the latest units, tech, and upgrade materials.  All are essential tools for staying competitive in an ever‑evolving warzone. Dive into each monthly event and prove your worth on the battlefield. If mere survival isn’t enough, take the fight directly to other Commanders. Launch assaults on rival platoons, raid their bases, and assert your dominance across the Sector. Every victory strengthens your reputation and secures your place among the elite. But remember, every attack invites retaliation. Other Commanders are watching, waiting, and eager to strike back. Only the most cunning and ruthless will rise to the top.
