About this gameFull description from Steam+
Enter Pawnbroker Simulator, where every deal, appraisal, and choice forges your path to pawn shop greatness. Sharpen your instincts, unlock new talents, craft a storefront that turns heads, offer high-stakes loans, and stay one step ahead of cunning thieves. Dive into the core of the game: examine glittering treasures up close, negotiate deals, accept pawned goods, close sales, and guard your shop with vigilance. Put your eye for value to the test, outsmart shrewd customers, ride the waves of market demand, and protect your haul from thieves lurking by day and prowling by night. Negotiation & Evaluation Negotiation is your engine for wealth. Read the room, strike at the perfect moment, and keep your margins fat without letting a deal slip away. Train your eye to spot fakes, uncover treasures among hundreds of unique items, and let your growing expertise lead you to clever pricing and foolproof trades. Bring in specialists for rare finds and build mastery in Fine Art, Arms, Gear, and Collectibles to boost your accuracy and repair prowess. Customers & Progression Each customer is a puzzle with quirks and secrets. Uncover who is trustworthy, who is impatient, who drives a hard bargain, and who hides traits like Hoarder, Reseller, or Aggressor. Build relationships with regulars to access secret deals and golden opportunities. Level up your skills in appraisal, negotiation, prediction, and management, and earn qualifications that open doors to new perks and clever strategies. Store & Security Turn your shop into a showstopper with vibrant colors, chic furniture, dazzling décor, and a signboard that demands attention. Personalize every tile, wall, and corner to your vision. Grow your space, craft displays that lure in the right buyers, and juggle storage with limited slots. Then, defend your turf: set up cameras, alarms, locked cases, and reinforced doors, and face down thieves when all else fails. Every day is a fresh challenge, making security a battle of brains. Money & Management Feel the pressure as bills pile up and expenses mount. Pay rent, utilities, upkeep, and maintenance for items while balancing loan risks and changing item values in a dynamic economy. Monitor your cash flow, plan upgrades carefully, and protect your reputation to unlock rarer items and more demanding customers. Every decision affects your survival. Key Features 250+ items with dynamic market values and condition-based pricing Interactive, confidence-based appraisal system Advanced negotiation shaped by personality traits and patience Full pawn system with loans, interest rates, and reclaim verification Item restoration mini-game tied to skills and qualifications Store expansion and tile-by-tile customization Physical slot-based storage management Real security gameplay: cameras, alarms, locks, and thief confrontation A dynamic demand system reacting to economic conditions Reputation tiers unlock rare items and tougher customers
