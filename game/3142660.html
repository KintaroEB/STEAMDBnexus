About this gameFull description from Steam+
ABOUT THIS DEMO In this demo you get to play through the first part of the game and explore a section of your home area Middlewood. You can meet the nomadic hedgehog Moss and play through the 3 first quests they provide. You can encounter the insect wildlife and you get to craft, knit and cook. The demo offer around 30-45 minutes of gameplay. ABOUT WINTER BURROW Winter Burrow is a cozy woodland survival game. Explore, gather resources, craft tools, knit warm sweaters, bake pies and meet the locals! In Winter Burrow, you play a mouse who returns from the big city to find their childhood home in ruins. To make matters worse, your Aunt (who was supposed to be looking after it) has gone missing. Restore your broken-down childhood burrow to its former glory so you can relax in front of the fireplace. Brave the elements and journey out into the frozen wilderness to collect vital resources for both yourself and your new home. Beware of the insect wildlife and remember that nobody survives on their own; find new friends, expand your survival handbook and solve the mystery of what happened to your Aunt. Winter Burrow is a survival game you play at your own pace. Customize your character, decorate your burrow and choose a path that fits your play style. This story is yours to tell.
