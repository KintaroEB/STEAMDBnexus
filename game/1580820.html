About this gameFull description from Steam+
Sky Realm Idle is a Xianxia RPG that blends deep strategic theory-crafting with laid-back idle progression. Cultivation here is more than just leveling up; it's about mastering the "Spirit Array" and the laws of the Five Elements. Gather resources, manage your estate, forge artifacts, and refine pills. Ascend from a mere mortal to an immortal legend. The Part 2 update roadmap has been updated! Completed roadmap items: Strategic Idle Combat: Switch Freely Between Auto & Manual Master the elemental cycle (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth) and the opposing forces of Light and Dark. Combat features an automatic turn-based system where characters automatically unleash Skills and Charge Attacks. Optimize your team composition to exploit elemental weaknesses for massive damage, or withstand the enemy's Overdrive onslaught. Facing a tough challenge? Switch to Manual Mode to time your skills perfectly and secure victory! Core Gameplay: The Spirit Array Say goodbye to mindless power creep. Your strength comes from the "Spirit Array" (Grid) System. Collect and upgrade hundreds of unique artifacts to fill your 12 grid slots. Each artifact carries unique skills (e.g., Might, Critical, Enmity). Building the perfect grid to counter your enemy's element is the key to victory. Estate Management & Life Skills Cultivation isn't just about fighting; it's about resource management. Gathering: Dispatch labor to forests and mines to gather Spirit Wood and Star Iron. Spirit Garden: Plant rare herbs, the essential ingredients for Tribulation pills. Alchemy & Forging: Refine pills personally or forge legendary weapons. Manager System: Recruited companions fight for you, but they can also manage your buildings to boost production efficiency. Defy the Heavens: Tribulation In Sky Realm Idle, even a common R-rarity character can defy destiny and ascend to SSR or even UR quality through the "Tribulation" system! Gather materials, refine pills, and help your companions break through their limits to unlock powerful skills. There are no useless cards, only untapped potential. Infinite Possibilities with Modding Built-in powerful Modding Tools. Easily create your own characters, artifacts, enemies, or even write new story events. Share your creations via Steam Workshop or download mods from other cultivators to expand your world infinitely. Enter the Sky Realm and start your idle cultivation journey now! Have suggestions or found a bug? Join us on Discord and help shape the future of the game!
