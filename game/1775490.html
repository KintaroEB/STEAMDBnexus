About this gameFull description from Steam+
Slice & Dice is a tactical dice battle roguelike. Take control of 5 heroes, each with their own unique dice. Fight your way through 20 levels of monsters and try to take on the final boss. If you lose a single fight you have to start over so be careful/lucky! Gameplay - 3D dice physics, choose which dice to reroll - Simple turn-based combat - Level up a hero or gain an item after each fight - Randomly-generated encounters - Ridiculous combos - Undo actions as much as you like - Each turn is like a mini-puzzle Features - 128 hero classes (+99999?) - 73 monsters - 473 items - Too many modes - ∞ curses - Weird modding - Online leaderboards - Steam achievements Credits - Created by tann (it took ~7 years) - Pixel art by a3um - Music by Ziggurath, Aleksander Zabłocki, Louigi Verona, Cold Sanctum, Ro'sø, Andrew Goodwin - Trailer by Everdraed I plan to not participate in any sales/deals/bundles. No AI was used to make this game.
