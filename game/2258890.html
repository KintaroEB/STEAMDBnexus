About this gameFull description from Steam+
Key Features Xanadu land is a fast-paced massively multiplayer bullet hell game that brings you to the arcade gameplay. In the nonstop BOSS battle, your death is just the beginning，accumulate experience, acquire equipment, and fight side by side with other players to knockdown all the BOSSes! Arcade style gameplay A fast-paced, arcade-like gameplay, you can jump in and out of the game anytime you want, easy to learn but challenging. Together with dozens of players, you will face hundreds of bosses and elite monsters with unique combat methods, defeat them, collect legendary loot, or be defeated... Diversification Progression and cross-platform The heroes of Xanadu Land will grow with you, their death will make you stronger, up to hundreds kinds of equipments, as well as a wealth of skills, talents, fellows, can be formed into different builds, PC players and even mobile players together to achieve legend. In-app purchase value-added services Paying will not make you stronger, but it will give you a more dignified death, like a bigger tombstone...
