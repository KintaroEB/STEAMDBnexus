About this gameFull description from Steam+
Enter ATLYSS, a strange world thriving with creatures and critters! With the outside realm and dungeons deep ahead, there are plenty of locations to explore and discover. - Character Creation - Make your character with five playable races to choose from, with color, head, and body customization! - Class Advancement - Start off as a witty Novice, destined to choose between three primary classes: Fighter: The melee class that rends and staggers foes, dealing devastating damage and threat. Mystic: The caster class that evokes powerful offensive and supportive arcane magic to their advantage. Bandit: The acrobatic class that are experts of ranged weapons, traps, and quick melee attacks. - Play alone, or with Friends - Experience ATLYSS with friends online, or play solo-- no internet connection required! - Your character, saved locally - All of your character progression data is saved locally! Play a character solo, and join in later to a net-play session where you left off, and vice versa. - Questing Abound - Party up with friends and take on quests to slay creatures, discover new locations, and uncover the mystique of the shattered world.
