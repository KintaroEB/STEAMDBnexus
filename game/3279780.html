About this gameFull description from Steam+
Horizon Walker is a turn-based RPG with a unique storyline and captivating graphics. ​ Join forces with stunning characters from beyond the dimensions and fight against the gods.​ Story Overview Mankind’s thriving civilization was brought to a halt by the sudden appearance of dimensional “Rifts.”​ From these Rifts, divine beings emerged, ruthlessly destroying and pillaging civilizations. Mankind called them the "Forsaken Gods."​ Despite resistance, mankind could not find a way to counter the barriers created by these "Forsaken Gods" and the Oblivia phenomenon, which turned humans into abominations.​ Fear and despair engulfed the world, and hope vanished.​ As mankind hopelessly awaited its destruction, a miraculous rumor spread—someone had killed an Abandoned God and stolen their power.​ People honored him as the Human God and offered their devotion.​ ​This is the tale of a remarkable Human God born in the East Asian Federation.​ Key Features ▶ Unique and Distinctive Characters​ Join forces with stunning characters from beyond the dimensions and fight against the Forsaken Gods.​ ​ ▶ Tactical Real-Time Strategy System​ Dominate time and space as both the commander of the battlefield and a Human God to save humanity!​ ​ ▶ Rich and Deep Romance Events​ Experience complex romantic events as you grow and interact with various characters!​ ​ ▶ Secret Content that Reflects Inner Desires​ Discover the secret chambers that reveal the deepest affections of the characters!​ ​ ▶ Gripping and Unique Worldview and Storyline​ Experience a grotesque and dark world where you, as a Human God, save humanity from its fate.
