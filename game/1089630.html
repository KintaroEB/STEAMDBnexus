奇迹一刻 Surmount

Surmount is a simulation game which is involved with moderate strategy on the subject of E-sports. You'll play as a college student who choose to return back to the league and win the competition as a coach. Those members can not only be your teammate, but also close friends or lovers.

About this gameFull description from Steam+
Story What could happen in one short instant? Enough to take a nobody to the top, or beat down to the hell. In my last year of high school, I was just one step away from my dream to become an e-sports player. That game -- what became known as the "miracle moment" -- was supposed to be the beginning of everything, but it ended my dream. I had already accepted this fact and even started to give up on myself. That day, the team sent me an offer for a coaching position. This is the story that I create the miracle of e-sports one more time together with them... Interview, then sign up! Meet with the favorite player offline, see their true image, then sign up. Choose your favorite players from the 15 players with unique characteristics to form your own e-sports team. Multiple identities and daily life: coach, student, and online game player. School attendance: Check attendance, do not skip class! Urban daily life: Internet cafe, board game hall, food court... Meet new friends. Online game world: login, travel the online game world, do the tasks. Start as friends, acquaintance, familiar, ambiguous, step by step to deepen the fetters of both sides, harvest the love finally. There would be 18 romance available characters, 12 of them can begin romance relationships so far, and the remaining 6 will update in the EA period. Love regardless of where they belong to, the enemy characters also could become romance object! Love drama: all the characters could be chased at the same time, who would be your final decision. Shuttle between real life and the online game world, explore the inner sides of players. Games, reality, social network, which side is the true self? In addition to cultivating players' basic attributes, players can also develop new classes to unlock different tactical line-up. The coach's self-cultivation is also very important! Improve coaching skills, can make the players into twice the result with half the effort. Build up diversified tactical strategies for the team, use superior tactics to defeat the opponent, win easily! Unique intelligence war: Conditions on the game are varied. Leading the intelligence war to develop the best strategy before the start of the game. Intense schedule: from tournament to tournament, efforts to improve the team in every round of the regular season rank, competing for the finals round ticket. Random game experience: luck is part of ability! Randomly generated game elements make each game experience different.
