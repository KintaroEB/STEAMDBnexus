About this gameFull description from Steam+
POLYGON is a tactical multiplayer team 1st person shooter. Using various weapons, you will battle with up to 32 players on large-scale maps. Properly managing your arsenal and skills, you will lead your team to victory! Team up with up to 16 players to fight in furious PvP matches. Group up with your friends in the squad to bring the teamplay to another level! A large arsenal is available – each weapon has a real prototype! Choose between assault rifles, PDWs, LMGs, DMRs and Sniper rifles to find your play style. A customization system will help you to adjust your weapon to your needs. Change sights, handles, muzzle devices and accessories. Also, you can unlock many skins to show off on a battlefield! Jump into battle in any location: from spacious valley to narrow subway. Different maps – different situations! The game is constantly being improved. Change the outcome of the battle using new weapons and skills. Team up with friends and explore new locations. DEVELOPMENT Intuitive controls and obvious gameplay make the game simple and fun. POLYGON can be run on outdated PCs and show all its all capabilities on modern computers – thanks to the Unreal Engine 5. Launched by cutting-edge innovations, the Unreal engine gives the POLYGON game amazing graphics! At the moment, the game is being developed by only one person. Hi, my name is Nick! Meet me and our friendly Moderators on Discord where we can help you and tell you more about the game :) We also often hold various events where you can get in-game rewards. Dive into battle with POLYGON!
