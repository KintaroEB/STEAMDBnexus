About this gameFull description from Steam+
The game has been fully developed and will be converted to the official version at the appropriate time, please feel free to buy! Automatic production easy placement This is a game based on industrial construction games such as Alien Star Factory and Dyson Ball. It turns the three-dimensional, complex industrial assembly line into an easy and simple flat conveyor belt. Minerals, smelting, power generation, storage, scientific research, manufacturing, all the elements of the game, can be easily clicked, easily placed to achieve! Space battleship Planet Plunder In addition to traditional manufacturing elements, it can also produce and assemble various types of space warships. Build a massive starfleet to attack the rest of the universe. Is to plunder the planet resources for their own development, or occupy the planet to increase the empire territory, or ruthlessly destroy the instantaneous destruction of hundreds of millions of lives, are up to your choice! Roles Develop multiple careers No need for krypton! Recruit the leader! 8 military skills, 8 production skills, 31 different career transfers, hundreds of different roles (ship girls)! Let the role become a production management expert, or the commander of the aircraft carrier fleet, all-round free training! Science and technology R&D giant construction Huge technology tree, as of the current version has 158 different technologies! In addition to the basic technology tree, there are complex fleet strengthening technology, military technology, advanced technology and other science and technology trees. In addition, space giant structures can also be built, although the cost is huge, but when completed, it will bring permanent benefits to the Empire! Simple operation and rich content The game just simplifies the original complex building gameplay, but the content and gameplay are richer! More than 110 kinds of various resource items, more than 40 kinds of production buildings, make the game more hardcore! In addition, the trade order system, production capacity control system, power management system, make the gameplay more convenient and free! Full game, game sincerity The game is now fully completed! The reason why it is not immediately converted to the official version is to find a competitive time point for survival! Please feel free to buy! We are open to any creative ideas and suggestions for improvement. Interstellar industry Genesis QQ group: 147928341
