About this gameFull description from Steam+
DemonCrawl Is Bursting With Content 1000+ unique items to discover and collect: whenever you find something new, we'll record it in your Codex for future reference. 5 Quests & multiple difficulties for a long-lasting challenge. 60+ exciting stages to explore: think you've seen everything? Then it's time to dive into the Endless Multiverse, where stages are blended together and randomized for virtually limitless possibilities. 100+ stage mods to keep things unexpected: from "Abstract Burning Forest" to "Double Dry Ocean" and everything in between - DemonCrawl stage mods can have a dramatic effect on how you approach the game board! Open-ended player progression, the roguelite way: find Tokens while playing and spend them on Talents that complement your playstyle. DemonCrawl lets you create multiple profiles in case you'd like to experiment with different builds! Gamemodes galore: in addition to standard Quests, we've got Endless Multiverse, a daily Hero Trial, an extra-tough 3-stage mode called Beyond, a simple Tutorial, and yes, even a Classic mode for the purists. Compete in the online PvP Arena: match up against 7 other players from around the world and prove your skills. DemonCrawl also includes the autobattler-inspired "Autosweeper Division" that sweeps for you, allowing you to concentrate on your item strategy and economy. 40 unlockable Masteries: enhance your runs with passive effects and charged abilities! Are you more of a KNIGHT or a SURVIVOR? A perilous HUNTER or a penny-pinching BANKER? A glorious FIREFLY or a special SNOWFLAKE? Customize your profile with cosmetics: build up your collection of Avatars and Inventories, then get wild with super-rare Paint drops... assuming you're lucky enough to find one! Online leaderboards for DemonCrawl savants: there's a leaderboard for every quest in Hard mode and above - including the daily Hero Trial. All scores are cross-platform. There's a crafting system too: solve blueprints, then craft your favorite items at will. Available in Hard Mode. Over 100 achievements and yes they are tough.
