About this gameFull description from Steam+
Zomcats Invasion Originally created in 2021 as "Zombie Kittens" its coming back in 2024! Completely remade with better graphics, animations and smoother gameplay! Its made by ONE person... me! With musical help from DJ Flow'n Zomcats Invasion is a 2D birdview zombie shooter. Play with up to 10 friends online and fight endless hordes of Zomcats! (Zombie + Cat) Play alone or play with randoms or play with your friends, the choice is yours! Earn cash(not real cash) by killing Zomcats to buy weapons and ammo or to buy a door open. Barricade the doors to keep the Zomcats out or to give yourself some time. Fight different Zomcats who have different abilities and who get stronger after each wave you complete. Have fun in the random mutator waves that can be good or bad for you. Buy new weapons to fight the dead. Choose between a variety of maps and difficulties. Choose a class to play and collect xp to unlock new buffs. Find cool cosmetics to customize your cat with. Create your own cosmetics and upload them to the Steam Workshop. People can upvote your cosmetics and if your cosmetics will receive enough votes it will get added into Axe's Shop! How much will this experience cost? NOTHING! ITS FREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE! (This game contains flashing lights!)
