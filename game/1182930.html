About this gameFull description from Steam+
Movavi Video Suite 2020 Steam Edition has everything you need for video editing in a single program. Turn your gameplay videos into engaging Let’s Play clips. Convert videos to any format, record program usage, watch movies and TV series, transfer data to optical disks, and more. Highlights Brand new design: navigate through the program and create your videos with ease Online subtitle search: search for movie and TV series subtitles right from the program Change subtitle font, color, and size Make video editing your favorite hangout Use your creativity to make the most engaging vlog ever – with this software, you have everything you need to make it happen. Use the media bin to store photos, videos, and music. Review your files before adding them to your project and delete the ones you don’t need. Add pictures, videos, and music on a convenient multi-track timeline and don’t worry about oversized files – the program perfectly works with even Full HD and 4K. Trim, rotate and enhance clips, use special effects like Chroma Key, Pan and Zoom, and Object Animation, add stickers and titles. Make your own animated intro with the help of ready-made presets or use backgrounds, titles, stickers, and even music from the program. Convert files in no time Convert files for mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and TVs. Now you can upload subtitles from within the program: search for the name of the movie or series number. Compress HD and Full HD movies so you can save space on your hard drive or upload to YouTube, Vimeo, or other sites. Before converting a video, you can enhance the audio- and video files using the built-in editor. Compress large files to save disk space and prepare them for sharing online on YouTube, Vimeo, and other video hosting sites. The SuperSpeed mode guarantees conversion in no time. You can also take advantage of other types of hardware acceleration: Intel HD Graphics, AMD, NVIDIA NVENC and CUDA. Record live streaming at the highest quality Capture any activity from your desktop: live streaming, Skype calls, or program usage. Record comments with the mic or capture them on webcam. The Scheduler mode helps you record TV series, webinars, or football games – create multiple recording tasks and the program will record the screen whenever you need. You can send your computer to sleep or turn it off after the recording. More tools Easily share captured or edited videos via YouTube or Google Drive Automate video-making with the Montage Wizard: just add your clips and enjoy the result Record full screen, part of the screen, or a single window Convert videos for iPhone 11, Samsung S10e, Huawei Mate 20, and another new smartphones Use the frame-by-frame preview to review your project files
