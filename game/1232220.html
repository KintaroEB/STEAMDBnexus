About this gameFull description from Steam+
Professional Fishing 2 is a realistic open-world simulator, for playing solo or with friends, that will take you through different regions where you can fish a wide variety of species while being enveloped by its calmness and natural surroundings. Start your adventure as a novice and progress to become a legendary angler while catching special fish to place in your trophy lodge! Fishing experience in a true open world. Discover different regions of the world represented on large maps where you can fish anywhere you want. Even the most remote and rugged corner of a lake can hide numerous secrets that you can discover without restrictions or predefined fishing spots. Nature as the main thread. Experience the thrill of fishing thanks to the realistic representation of the environments, a set of elements that will make you feel like you're actually there fishing. Day and night cycle, changing weather, a myriad of small details like insects, frogs, birds, and other animals, along with a soundtrack that aims to faithfully represent nature, achieve total immersion. Two game modes for you to choose the one that best suits your tastes. Choose between normal mode and hard mode so that your games are more satisfying and suit your playing style. If you prefer a more dynamic fishing experience, select normal mode, and if you prefer a more challenging and changing fishing experience, choose hard mode. Play solo or with your friends, you decide. If you enjoy solo gaming sessions and getting lost in natural environments to catch trophy fish, this is your game. If you enjoy gaming sessions with friends and seeing who can catch the biggest fish, this is also your game. Realism as the central axis. Dive into the world of fishing and experience the satisfaction of catching a big fish. Use the Fishpedia to learn about their preferences, habitat, and favourite baits, and embark on an adventure to find that special spot and, after a great battle, be able to catch it. Become a master of the different fishing techniques and equipment to catch the enormous array of species available, always keeping realism as a fundamental premise. Multiple skin patterns and special fish. Will you manage to catch all the species in the game and their enormous variety of patterns and rare colourations? In addition to common fish, you can catch rare colour variants (albino, melanistic, or xanthic), other super rare colour variants called "epic," and even enormous legendary specimens. A place for your trophy fish. Are you wondering where to place your special catches and trophy fish? You have at your disposal the best trophy cabin ever created for a fishing game (and we're not the ones saying it!), a spacious residence with an aquarium, and a huge space where you can hang your taxidermies on any wall without predetermined spots. You have complete freedom to decorate your residence however you like! A solid present and a very promising future. As you know, the game is currently in early access and continuous development. We are implementing changes and adding many improvements to make the game the best fishing experience possible. There is still a long way to go until we reach version 1.0, but we also look to the future with great optimism. We have many ideas to implement, new tournaments, challenges, collectibles, achievements, adventures, new regions, new species, seasonal events... The future is very promising!
