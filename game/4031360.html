About this gameFull description from Steam+
SkyInFlames Feel the intensity of aerial combat in this military strategy game! Take control of advanced drone swarms and complex air defense systems in a military simulation. SkyInFlames puts you in charge of progressive weapon technologies, where your every move is part of a complex aerial operation. SkyInFlames is a real-time tactical strategy game (RTS). Defend cities and strategic facilities from strike drones. Command mobile SAM units, S-300 complexes, aviation, and engineering groups. Minimize civilian casualties and prevent the enemy from destroying critical infrastructure. IMPORTANT: Early Access The game is in active development. Currently, three game modes are available: Player vs AI, Sandbox and Multiplayer. Join our Discord community to find a co-op partner Objective: Keep facilities intact and prevent the enemy from gaining the upper hand. Protect your troops and lock down the skies. Units & Mechanics: Mobile anti-aircraft groups, S-300 (launchers and radars), engineering vehicles for repairs and ammo resupply. Realism: Limited fuel and missile supplies, fog of war, and detailed animations for maneuvers, takeoff, and landing. Key Gameplay Features Logistics: Deliver critically important supplies by ground or air. Construction: Build airfields and organize their defense against enemy attacks. Development: This is an early build demonstrating core mechanics. Ahead lies the addition of a PvE mode (vs. AI). Roadmap Note: This game contains military themes and combat simulation.
