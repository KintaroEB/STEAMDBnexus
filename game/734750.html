About this gameFull description from Steam+
Mine gems, collect pickaxes, battle bosses, and explore treasure-filled biomes! Elevate your idle gaming experience with PickCrafter, now available on desktop! Smash blocks and reap the rewards in this captivating pixel-style clicker. Forge Legendary Pickaxes and gear to dominate the world of Runaria. Prestige through Extravagant, Transcendent, Preposterous, and Legendary tiers. Craft intricate strategies with the new Ascension Skill Tree, and etch your name into the annals of legends! Set forth on a journey to discover all the Biomes, from the snow-clad Tundra to the alien landscapes of the Moon and Mars. Unearth their mysteries and uncover hidden treasures! Unleash your inner strength with unlockable Abilities. Forge powerful equipment and supercharge your furnaces. Craft, upgrade, and dominate in PickCrafter! Challenge your friends, climb the leaderboards, and complete over 120 remarkable achievements. Rare, Epic, and Legendary items await you! Download now and become the ultimate treasure hunter in this endlessly addictive adventure. Your fortune awaits! Join our Community Discord Server to suggest exciting new features and share your invaluable feedback!
