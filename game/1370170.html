About this gameFull description from Steam+
Diorama Builder lets you build and collect beautifully voxel crafted mini-worlds. Explore your creations, and immerse yourself in diverse themes, from contemporary cities to medieval castles. Enjoy the ultimate diorama experience with exclusive content and seamless gameplay! Effortlessly explore and curate your diorama collection with ease. Become the ultimate diorama connoisseur, mastering every miniature world! Play with your mini-worlds and uncover hidden interactions. Discover the secrets that bring your dioramas to life! Choose among your collection the diorama that you want to build. Mount piece by piece and explore dioramas on your own terms! Enjoy seamless gameplay with intuitive camera controls. Easily hide and reveal diorama pieces for a perfect view! COMPATIBLE WITH THE NEW TILT FIVE™ AUGMENTED REALITY SYSTEM! Really feel like you are putting together a virtual Diorama, piece by piece as if it were there in front of you. For the Tilt Five™ system, modifications designed to improve immersion in the game have been added, including: Navigate the Diorama using the Tilt Five™ wand, with full control of the scenery view, rotating, zooming, and panning; The fourth, which is the game's MENU, was positioned below the Tilt Five™ board, giving the feeling of interacting with a model and choosing options on the objects inside it; After finishing the Dioramas, interact with the Tilt Five™ wand on some objects in the scenery and watch them animate and come to life.
