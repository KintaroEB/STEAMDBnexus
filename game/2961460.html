About this gameFull description from Steam+
Please refer to the last page for the demo save file inheritance method (inheritance can only be performed after the February update, and the trial version will no longer be available after April 7th). The ancient sacred tree rooted in the Kingdom of Karen, bestowing blessings upon people, is the faith of the entire nation. The noble royal family and the Saint Valtine Church, wielding power, are staunch supporters of the sacred tree. The story begins with the Sacred Tree Festival in the Kingdom of Karen, an annual festival. On this day, to thank the sacred tree for its blessings to Karen, the Saint Valtine Church and the nobility organize the festival and proclaim the divine revelation. After spending a few days in daily life with her sharp-tongued maid, Ela, and the yandere nun, Sissy, Hekate attends the Sacred Tree Festival with Ela. Hekate, known as the "Child of the Sacred Tree," beloved by everyone around her due to her sacred tree lineage, seems poised to become an excellent paladin effortlessly. However, a major incident occurs during the Sacred Tree Festival. After this incident, the environment around Hekate undergoes a drastic change, and she herself becomes confused about her beliefs. The noble royal family, the Saint Valtine Church, and the rebellious Truth Liberation Army. In the mutual struggle of these major forces, can Hekate overcome her confusion, stand firm in her beliefs, and make choices without regrets—this is the story of a girl who fights with faith even if she loses everything. Rich storyline, featuring an ultra-extended campaign with over 400,000 words of text. △ hundreds of unique cards, and a variety of card builds. △ Exquisite skeletal models and skill effects. Each card has a completely different battle performance! △ Unique alchemy system.
