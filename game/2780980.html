About this gameFull description from Steam+
Lockdown Protocol is a first person social deduction game, combining real time action and communication, 1 to 16 players. In this game, players have to work together to collaboratively complete tasks in time. While most players will do their best to complete this objective and ensure victory, a small portion of dissidents will try to stop them at all costs, without getting caught.
