About this gameFull description from Steam+
Recruit a hero to lead your army, and use spells while they fight for you. Mage & Monsters 2 is an active auto battler, where you choose to either enhance the strength of your hero, reinforce the might of your army, or increase the power of your spells. Features: - Choose between 18 different heroes to fight for you. - Over 100 different upgrades. - 13 unique spells you can use in battle to help your army. - 25 different units you can recruit, and over 50 enemies. - 3 different maps each with an unique challenge. Cover Art by Dean Spencer.
