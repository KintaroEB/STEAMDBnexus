About this gameFull description from Steam+
Order of Kings is an MMO game mixing RTS and SLG gameplay. The realistic 3D terrain gaps will affect your deployment tactics and strategy decision-making. You can simultaneously command multiple troops, and every troop has an individual attack range. Combining the physical collision effect, every clash will become a test of your strategy and micro-control skills. Show your military talent on the epic battlefield of Order of Kings! Order of Kings uses full real-time battle gameplay. You're free to move your troops, command your soldiers with unique abilities and attack ranges for flexible tactics such as attack, dodge, focus fire, flank, etc. Build your dream team with different types of soldiers to showcase your art of war. Hide troops in forests, defend from high ground, or hold tight passes for a counterattack...... Every terrain has its own value. Seize key spots and utilize high ground vision to control the field, providing you with diverse tactical experience! Each troop has a true physical collision effect; formation isn't just for looks. In open battles, grab choke points and block key routes, using long-range attack to suppress enemies. When defending your city, lure enemies in and trap them to gain an advantage. Use movement and tactics to find the best way to win enemies stronger than you. Every commander has unique strategist skill effects. Damage enhancement, defending expert, healing, and more. Deploy them in the strategist position to freely activate their unique effects. Switch up your strategists depending on the battlefield situation; rushing or turning movement is up to you! Order of Kings brings a real siege battle experience. Breakthrough city walls, street fights, and city occupation. As the attacker, use a siege ladder to climb the walls and break in quickly. But watch out for the city garrison using tunnels to harass and counterattack; the battlefield can be flipped at a moment. Choosing the siege strategy wisely to perfectly win for you and your alliance. The battlefield is ready, awaiting your arrival! Assemble your friends and jump into Order of Kings to write your own legend!
