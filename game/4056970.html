About this gameFull description from Steam+
Make your desktop come alive and express your style! Stunning video wallpapers, customizable mouse pointers, and flexible desktop icons—your desktop will never be boring again. Dynamic Video Wallpapers: Bring your desktop to life with motion graphics, making every startup a fresh experience. Personalized Desktop: Swap mouse pointer sets freely and customize desktop icons to suit your style. Flexible Adaptation: Supports common screen ratios and multi-monitor setups, displaying video wallpapers naturally. Workshop: Download incredible creations from players worldwide or share your own unique wallpapers. Smart Performance Management: Wallpapers automatically pause during fullscreen games to keep your system smooth. Easy Video Uploads: Set any video as your wallpaper in an instant. Turn your desktop into more than a background—it’s your personal stage.
