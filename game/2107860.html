Meanwhile, Yggdra, a lone girl who escaped With her kingdom's last hope in hand, head to the remotest land on the southern tip of the continent. She arrives and meets a boy, the king of thieves, Milanor. The battle to liberate Yggdra's homeland begins with that encounter... ■Collaborate with allies! "Union system!"
