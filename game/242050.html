About this gameFull description from Steam+
DISCOVER ASSASSIN'S CREED IV BLACK FLAG, THE CLASSIC PIRATE ACTION-ADVENTURE GAME FROM 2013. LIVE THE GRITTY REALITY BEHIND THE PIRATE FANTASY Become Edward Kenway, a charismatic pirate captain trained by Assassins. Effortlessly switch between the Hidden Blade of the Assassin Order, four flintlock pistols, and dual cutlass swords. Stand amongst legendary names such as Blackbeard and Calico Jack as you establish a lawless Republic in the Caribbean, where corruption, greediness, and cruelty are commonplace. BECOME THE MOST FEARED PIRATE IN THE CARIBBEAN Command the Jackdaw, and strike fear in all who see her. Plunder and pillage to upgrade your ship with ammunition and equipment needed to fight off enemies. Attack and seamlessly board massive galleons and embark on an epic and infamous adventure. EXPLORE AN OPEN WORLD FILLED WITH OPPORTUNITIES Discover a large and diverse open world. From Kingston to Nassau, explore unique locations where you can live the life of a pirate: looting underwater shipwrecks, assassinating Templars in blossoming cities, searching for treasure in lost ruins, and more. DIVE INTO A MULTIPLAYER EXPERIENCE Put your assassination skills to the test. Discover a brand-new set of pirate characters and explore exotic and colorful locations.

Recommended

Recommended: OS *: Windows Vista SP2 Processor: Intel Core i5 2400S @ 2.5 GHz or better or AMD Phenom II x4 940 @ 3.0 GHz Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: Nvidia GeForce GTX 470 or AMD Radeon HD 5850 (1024MB VRAM with Shader Model 5.0) or better Storage: 30 GB available space Sound Card: DirectX Compatible Sound Card with latest drivers Additional Notes: Supported video cards at the time of release: Nvidia GeForce GTX 260 or better and GeForce GTX 400, GTX 500, GTX 600 and GTX 700 series.
