About this gameFull description from Steam+
Team up with old and new friends Investigate Alderwood Island’s secrets together! Meet new friends out on the trails, group up to explore Ride Islands for a change of pace, or compete together in group races. Explore the island and all it has to offer in galloping getaways and discover why this land has always been a special place for horses. Uncover an occult mystery As Alex Evans, your homecoming to Alderwood Island is anything but ordinary. A string of mysteries has set the entire island on edge. Your mother has vanished suddenly without a trace and a body was discovered in the woods. Together with old and new friends, investigate these incidents and unearth a conspiracy nobody saw coming… Take the reins and explore a breathtaking open world The island is home to scenic vistas where beauty and wonder are found with every step! Discover beautiful nooks and crannies as you wander cozy woods, peaceful beaches, and rolling mountainside hills. What secrets will you and your steed uncover as you roam this splendid island? If you’re looking for a change of pace, hop on the ferry to explore a scenic Ride Island with a group of friends! Compete in races and train to become a champion Group up with friends and compete in races that take you across the island. From challenging forest treks with tight turns, to scenic river crossings and intense jumping courses - with over 15 to choose from, there’s something for everyone! Will you and your horse leave your mark on the leaderboards and become the reigning champion? Express yourself and show off your style Design the character of your dreams! Customize your horse and your character by choosing from a variety of clothing, tack, and equipment options. More new items are added with regular updates. Acquire new accessories by helping village factions and even upgrade gear to enhance your performance.

Content notice

Equinox: Homecoming is an online MMORPG where players uncover an occult conspiracy while investigating a murder and their mother's disappearance. There is no combat, but we do depict unsettling content such as blood, bones, animal remains, and a human corpse throughout cinematic cutscenes and in specific locations. Caution is advised for Photosensitive Epilepsy, Mature Language and references to Violence, Gore, and Alcohol and Drug/Marijuana Usage.
