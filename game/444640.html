About this gameFull description from Steam+
Play the top-rated tower defense franchise in this free head-to-head strategy game. It's monkey vs monkey for the first time ever - go head to head with other players in a Bloon-popping battle for victory. From the creators of best-selling Bloons TD 6, this game is specially designed for intense multiplayer combat, featuring over 50 custom head-to-head tracks, incredible towers and upgrades, all-new range of powers, and the ability to control bloons directly and send them charging past your opponent's defenses. Easy to learn, hard to master - this game offers endless fun for casual and competitive players alike! Check out these awesome features! * Head-to-head two player Bloons TD * Over 50 custom Battles tracks * 22 awesome monkey towers, each with 8 powerful upgrades, including the never before seen C.O.B.R.A. Tower. Multiple ways to play! * Assault Mode - manage strong defenses and send bloons directly against your opponent * Defense Mode - build up your income and outlast your challenger with your superior defenses * Battle Arenas - Put your medallions on the line in a high stakes Assault game. Winner takes all. * Card Battles - Build the ultimate deck to slam your opponents in this unique twist on bloons TD gameplay.
