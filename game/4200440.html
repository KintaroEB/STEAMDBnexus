About this gameFull description from Steam+
Build Your Vehicle Strip down Junk, salvage engines, radiators, and various parts. Every component matters. Mix fluids, manage resources, and transform scattered scrap into a road-worthy beast capable of keeping you alive. The road never ends. Neither do they. Survive the wasteland in Nightmare Drive, a post-apocalyptic driving survival game where your vehicle is your lifeline. Scavenge for parts, customize your ride, and keep moving—the dead roam these roads day and night, and they never rest. Endless Roads, Infinite Danger Procedurally generated highways stretch endlessly. Explore abandoned structures, discover supplies, and push deeper into the unknown. But stay alert—every stop is a risk, and the dead are never far. Outlast the Dead They never sleep. Cut through relentless zombies, manage your fuel, and pray you get enough resources. Every mile survived is a victory. Every breakdown could be your last. Keep driving — the next breakdown could be your last.
