About this gameFull description from Steam+
Deceit 2 is the online action PvP experience where your capacity for trust and deception are put to the test. Join an ancient Ritual run by a twisted Game Master, claim your Role and work out how you can Escape. Do you have the skills to work out who’s with you and who’s against you? Three Teams go head-to-head; the Innocent, Infected and Cursed. However, nobody knows who is on their team and therefore: trust no one. Team up when it suits you, lie when it doesn't, and survive however you can. Every decision shapes how the nightmare unfolds, making every Ritual a unique story to tell. The Ritual plays out across 5 unique locations, each steeped in atmosphere and danger. Each Ritual Site offers distinct layouts, challenging Tasks, and ultimately requires varied strategy if you're wanting to Escape before being Banished. Around the Ritual Site you'll find Tasks which the Innocent must complete in order to Escape. The completion of these Tasks also unlock Items for the group at the Shop. Choose from Investigative, Offensive and Defensive tools which multiply the chaos, making each visit back to the Shop highly impactful. Climb the Ranks and prove your dominance in competitive play. You'll be rewarded in your skills by unlocking exclusive rewards. Rise through Seasonal Leaderboards and challenge the strongest opponents to become a true Master of Deceit. Participate as 1 of 9 Subjects trapped within the Ritual. Find your favourite, collect unlocks for them through Level Ups and customise their look inside the Collection.
