Join fast-paced action-packed combat. Play Combat Master -- the ultimate FPS experience on your PC and Mac. Best-in-class multiplayer gunfight. Next-level performance. And a whole lot more. Combat Master is designed for extreme performance on low-end hardware, so please don't judge graphics that hard ')) Whether you're a tactical professional, or simply love the tactical aesthetic, Combat Master provides a shooter gameplay that focus on the quality and innovation you're looking for.
