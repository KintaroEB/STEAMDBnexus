About this gameFull description from Steam+
MR FARMBOY is a colony farming game with automation. Build your dream farm, grow crops, raise animals, attract villagers, and hire workers to automate everything. 🌱 Grow and Harvest Crops Plant, water, and harvest a variety of crops on your dream farm. 🐓 Raise Animals Build Coops to raise chickens, ducks and geese, Barns to raise pigs, sheep, and cows. 🏗️ Build Your Farm Construct Markets, Houses, Warehouses , Coops, Barns, and more to grow your farming empire. 🛠️ Automate Your Farm Hire Farmers, Gatherers, and Carriers to fully automate production and logistics. 💰 Sell to Villagers Attract villagers to your farm and sell your goods to them. 🧘 Relax and Strategize Enjoy the atmosphere while optimizing your farm’s automation.
