About this gameFull description from Steam+
Memoriapolis is a historical city builder where your city grows across 5 ages from Antiquity to the Industrial Revolution. Place the buildings. The city grows organically and districts expand. Manage resources, navigate faction demands, and guide your dynasty to its final goal : building one of three Industrial Revolution Wonders — the Eiffel Tower, Neuschwanstein Castle, or the Statue of Liberty. Relaxed or challenging : difficulty settings adjust objectives, faction pressure, and resource balance to match how you want to play. Banners & Wonders is the biggest update to Memoriapolis since launch. Factions now have personalities: Altruist, Warrior, Expansionist, and more. They don't all want the same thing, and they won't all negotiate the same way. Your dynasty's goal : reach the Industrial Revolution and build one of three final Wonders — the Eiffel Tower, Neuschwanstein Castle, or the Statue of Liberty. The city is more open than before. No more cycle limits. No more closed districts. New maps, Steam achievements, difficulty levels have also been added and more! At the heart of it all : the Dynastic House that grows with your city across every age. Build your city across 5 ages Memoriapolis spans five historical eras : Antiquity, the Middle Ages, the Renaissance, the Age of Enlightenment, and the Industrial Revolution. Each age transforms your city with new architecture, new challenges, new Monuments to unlock. Watch your city grow organically No rigid grid. Your city develops naturally, expanding through districts shaped by your choices and your citizens.  No two playthroughs look the same. Build monuments from history Construct iconic landmarks like the Library of Alexandria, Windsor Castle, Leaning Tower of Pisa, Palace of Versailles and more to give your city a unique identity through time. Interact with factions Factions have personalities — Altruist, Warrior, Expansionist, and more — and they have demands. You can accept proposals directly, negotiate terms, or choose to favor one faction over another. Engage as much or as little as the game calls for. How is Memoriapolis different from other city builders? Your city grows organically and you guide your dynasty across the ages. Factions add depth but you are free to engage with these systems at your own pace. I played Memoriapolis before, what has changed? Banners & Wonders is the biggest update since launch. Factions now have personalities, construction is more open, new maps have been added, and your dynasty now has a final goal : build one of three Industrial Era Wonders. Is Memoriapolis easy? Yes and no! Select your difficulty at the start of a run: resources, faction pressure, objectives and more… all vary depending on the level you choose. Is Memoriapolis replayable? Each playthrough is different thanks to different maps, different factions, different Wonder to build at the end. The organic growth of your city means no two runs look the same.
