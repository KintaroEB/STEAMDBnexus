About this gameFull description from Steam+
Stomp on your rivals and aim for 1st place! Speed off into a 40-player survival race where fast-paced action and fierce strategy collide! Faaast Penguin is a survival action racing game for up to 40 players where mastering attacks and shortcuts is the key to victory. ○ Stomp, Jump, and Boost Speed! The Attack That Decides the Race Simply sliding isn't enough to win. Stomp on rivals and objects with an attack to boost your speed and do a big jump! One split-second decision can turn a race upside down. Pick your moment, leap into the air, and take the top spot at once. This clash of speed, skill, and strategy is the true thrill of Faaast Penguin. ◯ Find Shortcuts All Across the Stage! Shortcut routes are scattered throughout the courses. Chain attacks to reach routes that would otherwise be impossible to access. Nail it and you'll blast past your rivals, but overdo it and you could go off course. Play it safe or go all in. As your skills improve, new racing lines open up, creating a deep and rewarding racing experience that keeps getting better! ◯  Seize the Moment and Activate Your Ultimate! Collect cans of sardines to activate your Ultimate and become invincible for a limited time! Dive into shortcuts! Send your rivals flying. Destroy gimmicks. Catch up in the final stretch. The possibilities are endless. Analyze the situation and create a strategy that's uniquely yours. ◯ Master Each Course's Unique Gimmicks! Master special course gimmicks and outsmart your rivals. Collect water and soar through the skies in Miami, ride monkeys through the Amazon, unleash ninja skills with scrolls in Japan, and perform snowboard tricks on snowy peaks! 16 worlds and over 64 courses await you. ◯ Survive With Your Own Skill It's a 4-course survival race where only the best advance. Race through each stage at top speed and rack up points to claim overall victory! ◯ Enjoy It Solo or With Friends Hone your skills solo or compete against your friends. There's solo play, party play with up to 4 players, and even custom matches with up to 40 players! It's a cross-platform game, so you can play with anyone regardless of platform! ◯ Extensive Player Customization Freely customize your costumes, carts, ultimate rides, and emotes! It won't affect performance, so rock your style and dive into the penguins' vacation! [Penguin Climbers] ◯ The plane is leaving without you?! Time for a great escape! During a fun snowy mountain tour, a cliff collapses! It's a disaster! The penguins' desperate (or not) climb begins as they race back to the plane about to depart. Climb walls with an ice axe, occasionally rescuing friends with a fishing rod, and forge your way through the harsh surroundings. Team up with friends to reach the summit, or challenge yourself solo for a more disciplined climb. Will you safely reunite with the tour conductor and snap a memorable photo? ◯ To snowy mountains, jungles, and starlit skies! Explore a mysterious island. The penguins have arrived at the mysterious Enigma Islands, where the terrain changes daily. From giant bird nests and mysterious mech penguins to fishing spots, there are plenty of fascinating places to explore. Collect items, defeat enemies, and experience survival action unlike any race!
