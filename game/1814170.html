About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Lingo Lingo is a first person exploration game specializing in word puzzles and impossible spaces. As you complete its challenges, Lingo's world will slowly unfold around you, revealing a vast web of interconnected locations. With its hundreds of puzzles and dozens of hidden secrets, Lingo offers a one-of-a-kind gaming experience. Key Features Nonlinear gameplay - go where you want Keyboard required - dive deep into language Open world - an enormous space to explore and master
