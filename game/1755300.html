About this gameFull description from Steam+
Smart Factory Tycoon is a tycoon management game about running your dream factory with robots. Design your dream factory, plan all steps of production, research new technologies and become a tycoon! Machines & Robots Purchase new machinery and unlock advanced machines to produce every type of products in the market Purchase new robots with varying colors and watch them coming to your factory by different transportation options: balloon, mine cart, boat, parachute, PWC or bus Use the skill points to level up your robots (Movement speed, processing speed, charge capacity, oil capacity) Purchase chargers, super chargers, wireless chargers, and lubricators for your robots rooms Upgrade your machines, solar panels, wind turbines, chargers and lubricators for faster production and increased efficiency Fully automated machine-maintenance by drones Production & Market Plan all steps of the production Purchase high quality raw material to produce healthy and durable products Analyze the market to be aware of trending products Check the general market categories (Basic-Medium-Master) and Special Orders Increase your health, durability and sustainability levels to be eligible for some products in the market Sustainability & Economy Purchase wind turbines and solar panels to reduce electricity costs and increase your sustainability Plant trees in your gardens to be a sustainable factory Earn more money and XP to take the first place in the leaderboard Do the math and be aware of your expenses! Don't go bankrupt! Invest & Research & Development Research new technologies to improve your factory Purchase new buildings and deliver your products by rail, sea, and air Build new rooms for robots and production Place your product zones and define their types as general or export Achieve milestones to earn more XP and level-up your factory
