About this gameFull description from Steam+
Endless Coffee Update! (Survivors-like Roguelite) Endless Coffee is a massive free update for Astro Prospector, including a new mode with 5 new weapons, 200+ new upgrades, 9 different ships, new enemies and a thousand ways to break the Universe! Build your own ship, survive 21 Days and destroy the source of SpaceCorp. Then, go Endless, find powerful synergies and try to get as far as possible! Endless Coffee Features New survivors-like, ship builder mode. Survive 21 Days and go Endless. Reach as far as you can! Master 9 different ships, each one with unique gameplay. Unlock new skills by levelling up and winning with each ship. 420+ upgrades to discover. Dozens of hours of playtime, find clever combinations, break the Universe! -------------------- Story Mode (Incremental) Astro Prospector is an incremental bullet hell about mining cofferoids and fighting the evil SpaceCorp. Collect resources, upgrade your ship and become unstoppable. Mine cofferoids from SpaceCorp's fields and steal their AstroCoffee seeds. Explore and upgrade the skill tree to increase your ship power. Enemy Machines will try to stop you. Dodge their bullets and destroy them. F*ck SpaceCorp! Story Mode Features Addictive incremental progression. 25 Zones with unique enemies and bosses. Unlock new skills by completing tasks and destroying SpaceCorp Machines. 200+ upgrades to discover. Short and intense experience (~5.0hr playtime). Discord Join our server Discord, so you can stay tuned and give us feedback. You can find the link of the sidebar! Warning! This game contains flashing lights that may make it unsuitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or other photosensitive conditions. Player discretion is advised!
