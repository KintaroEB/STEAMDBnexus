About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to a world of wonder and darkness, where reality and dreams intertwine and secrets lurk around every corner. Pocket Mirror is a surreal indie adventure game that will take you on a journey through a mysterious realm where nothing is what it seems and everything is connected. As a young girl with a clouded past, you wake up in a strange and eerie mansion with nothing but your pocket mirror in hand. In search of your memories, you must explore the twisted corridors and rooms of the mansion, solve puzzles, interact with unique characters, and uncover the truth behind your identity and the sinister forces that are at play. Will you be able to face the truth? With its hauntingly beautiful art style, mesmerizing soundtrack, and captivating storyline, Pocket Mirror is a unique and unforgettable gaming experience that will challenge your mind, touch your heart, and leave you breathless. Features A surreal and atmospheric world full of mysteries and wonders to discover Intriguing puzzles and challenges that will test your skills and logic A cast of memorable and complex characters with multiple paths and endings, depending on your choices and actions A hauntingly beautiful soundtrack that will immerse you in the game's atmosphere A rich and deep lore that rewards exploration and curiosity An unforgettable journey of self-discovery, loss, and hope You shouldn't ever let it go, do you understand?
