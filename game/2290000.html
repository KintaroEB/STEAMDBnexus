About this gameFull description from Steam+
Make yourself comfortable and build a gorgeous Kingdom in TerraScape. Place buildings strategically for score and exciting new unlocks in a peaceful and atmospheric world. No time pressure here, take your time and puzzle away! Place your Keep and start growing your kingdom by choosing your first Deck of buildings. Strategically place your buildings to unlock more buildings, more draws, and new Keep upgrades to choose additional Decks that synergize well with what you already have. Each building benefits from different influences, be it other buildings and natural resources nearby, or the right biome to place it on. Earn points by collecting as many positive influences as possible, but beware: there are also negative influences! Watch your kingdom grow from a humble hamlet to a majestic metropolis and make sure that you always have a steady supply of points for your next draw to keep the game in motion. Let yourself be captivated by the unique graphic style and the lovingly designed landscapes. Observe the birds, the bees and the citizens of your flourishing and idyllic kingdom while listening to our relaxing soundtrack. In TerraScape, you define the tempo, so sit back, relax, and experience the cozy city puzzler vibe. Combine buildings in special patterns to merge mighty complexes. These Merged Buildings are impressive structures that have stronger influences and can reward you with bonus points, extra cards or even trigger special effects! Find over a dozen Merged Buildings and utilize them to the fullest! Multiplayer Get into engaging multiplayer matches with your friends! Develop your own strategies and defeat the plans of your opponents in Versus mode. Take advantage of strategically placed buildings and build up your point lead round by round. Or coordinate strategies with each other in Coop mode and max out your kingdom by working together. Those seeking a challenge can conquer the highscores by strategically planning and carefully building their settlement to earn a place on the global leaderboards. What does TerraScape offer: Unique blend of City Builder and Puzzle Game Relaxing and atmospheric gameplay Many unlockables and more to come 40+ Buildings 15+ Combination-Buildings Highscores & Leaderboards High Replay Value Great Soundtrack Coop & Versus Multiplayer What does TerraScape NOT offer: Resource Management 4x Strategy Citizen Simulation Combat Free Deck building Kingdom Mode – Our main mode: Play until you run out of points and buildings Scenarios – Play with special conditions and goals to achieve Puzzle Terras – 40+ progressively more difficult levels: Place fixed sets of buildings in the most optimal way Weekly Challenges – Stay at the top of the leaderbords of these Puzzle Terras while they are active to become immortalized! Coop Multiplayer – Play Kingdom Mode together with up to 4 players Versus Multiplayer – Up to 4 players face each other to see who achieves the Target Score first! Creative Mode – Build your kingdom without limits and with everything available. Just build away!
