About this gameFull description from Steam+
Shakes & Fidget is a hilarious fantasy role-playing satire and at the same time, an award-winning RPG! Sounds crazy? With over 50 million enthusiastic players, it ranks among the most celebrated online role-playing games worldwide! Create your own comic hero and conquer the top spot in the hall of fame! No easy feat, as real players have set their sights on the same goal. But together with your guild companions, you'll become stronger, more invincible, and win a ton of epic loot! Accept quests, embark on exciting adventures, brawl with monsters and other heroes, grow stronger, earn honor, and become a living legend! Poisons enemies with a toxic tincture for several rounds. Blocks attacks with the shield, can heal or deal more damage. Summons undead minions who sacrifice themselves in battle. Takes the form of an eagle or a bear. Strengthens in battle by playing the harp. Has an elixir that can revive. Carries two weapons and always strikes with both. Can dodge enemy attacks. Gets into a frenzy and can therefore attack several times in a row. Carries a shield that can be used to block attacks. Negates the special abilities of most other classes. Unleashes an arcane comet at the opponent at the beginning of each battle. Unique comic look and humor Thousands of weapons and epic equipment PVE alone and with friends and also PVP against other players Exciting quests and scary dungeons Free-to-play and regular free updates A one-time registration via Steam, Facebook Connect or an email address and password is required, unless you already have a user account.
