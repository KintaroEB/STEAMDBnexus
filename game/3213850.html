About this gameFull description from Steam+
MAKE STUDY AND WORK MORE ENJOYABLE Spend your time at the desk alongside your own characters, friends, players from around the world. Focus together, stay motivated, or just enjoy some quiet company. FOCUS TOGETHER! MULITPLAYER ROOMS FOR UP TO 16 PLAYERS Make avatars hang out together, share what you're working on, greet each other with emotes, and enjoy peaceful focus sessions together. CREATE YOUR FAVOURITE CHARACTERS Create characters with different hairstyles, outfits, and colors, then place multiple avatars throughout your room. Take photos, switch poses and expressions, and let your avatars change animations between focus and break sessions. BUILD SPACES FROM YOUR IMAGINATION Build cozy workspaces, art studios, rainy-day libraries, ruins, rooms for your favourite bands ... Freely place furniture and decorations, customize colors, and add your own images and GIFs to create spaces that are uniquely yours. PRODUCTIVITY TOOLS, LOFI MUSIC Practical tools to support your daily work and study sessions, including: Pomodoro Timer Countdown and Count-up Timers To-Do Lists Daily Habits Journal Focus Statistics Set the mood with lo-fi music, ambient sounds, weather effects, and different times of day. AVATAR AS DESKTOP COMPANION Mini Window Mode that make avatar stays in the corner of your desktop and work with you. DISCOVER AND VISIT NEW SPACES Publish your own spaces, visit rooms created by other players, or share and download creations through the Workshop. With countless spaces created by the community, every day can feel like working somewhere new. Watch Party Watch YouTube together with friends using Watch Party. Face Tracking Use face tracking in solo mode to bring your avatar to life. Stream, record videos, or create content using your gogh avatar. More Features Photo Mode with support for transparent backgrounds and green screen videos. Create your own playlists using local music files. Stream virtual camera footage onto supported room items (single-player only). Meet Kuroda 42, a mysterious little companion that evolves as you focus. Native support for Apple Silicon Macs.
