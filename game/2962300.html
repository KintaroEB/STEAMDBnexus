About this gameFull description from Steam+
Royal Quest Online is a free-to-play MMORPG that seamlessly combines elements of captivating fantasy and futuristic technology. Begin a crusade against the forces of evil! Defend your kingdom, earn titles, hoard riches and conquer castles! THE CHOICE IS YOURS Imagine a team consisting of a sniper armed with support cannon, a mage casting defensive shields, a mercenary assassin throwing poison bombs, and a crusader spreading the holy light while riding an armored wolf! Royal Quest Online offers these and hundreds of other roles for you to fulfill. Discover a world full of goblins, werewolves, vampires, orcs and even dragons. Visit enchanted living forests, dungeons full of deadly traps, magnificent palaces, hulking fortresses, forlorn laboratories and even islands in the sky. Get to know this magic world and then conquer it! Lands of Elenia truly are a sight to behold and they’re just made for adventure! Join magic orders and clandestine organizations, meet the native inhabitants of Elenia and dig through the echoes of past civilizations, ravaged by the horrendous cataclysms. All that and more leads to thousands of quests replete with valuable rewards. YOUR PATH TO GLORY There’s no single right way to the top. Play, aim big, and never stop, until you get what you want! Become a wandering monster hunter or a mercenary doing regular jobs. Sign up for Royal service or make a fortune selling rarities. Vanquish competition in the arena and wrest yourself a castle. Go as far as your imagination allows you! GUILD CLASHES You live in the kingdom — you follow its laws. Become a member of an existing guild or create your very own to then fight other guilds over massive fortresses. Owning a castle is not only honorable, but also highly profitable, as castles generate regular income. Start with one castle and keep expanding your holdings!
