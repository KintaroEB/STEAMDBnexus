About this gameFull description from Steam+
About This Demo Play as Hendra, a man on a mission for revenge. Infiltrating a hidden, illegal factory, you'll come face-to-face with Jagal, one of those responsible for your family’s murder. Engage in close-quarters combat and uncover dark secrets as you take the first steps on your path to vengeance. Story Mission Experience a compelling, demo-exclusive mission where Hendra infiltrates a dangerous illegal factory to confront Jagal, the man responsible for your family's murder. Navigate intense close-quarters combat, face deadly enemies, and uncover the dark secrets within this criminal operation. Arena Mode Test your skills in arena mode, where you’ll face relentless waves of enemies. This fast-paced combat experience challenges you to hone your tactics and strive for high scores unique to the demo. Limited Outfit Selection Personalize Hendra’s appearance with a selection of demo-only outfits. Each option allows you to style Hendra to match your gameplay approach, giving a glimpse of the customization available in the full game. Achievements Unlock up to 10 demo-specific achievements tied to the story mission, arena scores, and combat challenges, encouraging you to fully explore and master the demo experience. Key Features Demo-exclusive story mission with unique objectives and enemies Arena mode featuring wave-based combat and high score challenges Limited outfit customization to personalize Hendra’s look Unlockable achievements tied to the story mission, arena scores, and combat challenges

Content notice

Our game features intense and graphic depictions of violence. Players will engage in combat scenarios that include blood, gore, and dismemberment. The violence is frequent and integral to the gameplay experience, with detailed and realistic portrayals of combat injuries. The storyline includes scenes of significant violence that some players may find disturbing. This includes brutal hand-to-hand combat, use of weapons resulting in graphic injuries, and scenes depicting the aftermath of violent confrontations. We want to ensure that players are aware of the mature themes present in our game so they can make an informed decision about their play experience.
