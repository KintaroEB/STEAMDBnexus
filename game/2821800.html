About this gameFull description from Steam+
Join Our Elite Platform Manager Team at DrillCore! Are you ready to lead a team of exceptional talent in one of the most challenging environments known to humankind? At DrillCore, we're not just mining minerals; we're safeguarding the future. We're looking for strategic minds and resourceful leaders to manage our cutting-edge operations. Do you have what it takes to make every decision count in the race to profits & earnings against climate collapse? Your 'Exciting' Mission: Lead, Manage, Innovate Strategic Leadership As a Platform Manager at DrillCore, you'll oversee a dedicated team of miners, carriers, and guards. Your mission? Navigate them through perilous missions to extract vital minerals and power our drill platform. Every strategic decision will test your planning and resource management skills. You’ll report directly to your Sector Manager, he will make sure you keep the operation running smoothly, whatever the pressure you’re under. Operational & Safety Excellence Nighttime is when the real excitement begins. This is when the planet's fauna usually clash directly with our company’s safety commitments. Your job? To maintain profits while safeguarding both the lives of your crew and the biodiversity of the planet you’re drilling. Sometimes tough choices need to be made, and that’s why DrillCore equips you with state-of-the-art defensive turrets. Remember, it's NOT a crime against biodiversity if it’s self-defense. Innovation and Adaptability At DrillCore, no two challenges are the same. Unpredictable wildlife, evolving requirements, and ever-changing technologies will keep you on your toes. Adapt swiftly, allocate resources efficiently, and develop innovative strategies to overcome the unexpected. Your success unlocks new career opportunities and advancements, pushing the boundaries of what's possible.
