About this gameFull description from Steam+
Japan Stigmatized Property 2: Surveillance Horror Case Files To All Personnel: We appreciate your dedicated efforts in the previous surveillance operation. Please be advised that the risk level for the current assignment has significantly increased. You are required to exercise extreme caution while performing your duties. You are an observer for the "Japan Stigmatized Property Association" (JSP). Your duty is to monitor surveillance cameras installed in designated properties and check for any anomalies. Upon discovering an anomaly, you must report it immediately. Repeated failure to report or excessive false reports will result in "Mission Failure." Conversely, maintaining successful surveillance from 00:00 to 05:00 constitutes "Mission Success." 【Operational Features】 Real-World Locations: You will be assigned to properties that actually exist in Japan. Randomized Anomalies: Irregularities occur randomly. Camera Modes: Be vigilant for phenomena visible only through specific equipment (Night Vision or Standard Camera). 【Examples of Anomalies】 Movement of furniture; disappearance or appearance of objects. Intrusion by human figures or spiritual entities. Lighting malfunctions; floating orbs. Bloodstains on walls; video feed noise/interference. "Something" beyond imagination. 【Shift Details】 Assignment: 4 total properties. Hours: 00:00 – 05:00 (Midnight shift). Difficulty: Selectable between "Normal" and "Hazard Pay" (Hard). 【Others】 Streaming and broadcasting while conducting duties is permitted. We place no restrictions on monetization or Super Chats. Submission of permission requests is not required for either individuals or corporate entities. Please commence your duties.
