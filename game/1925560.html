About this gameFull description from Steam+
EXPERIENCE FLIPPING, TRICKING, PARKOUR, AND CLIFF DIVING LIKE NEVER BEFORE Tricking 0 is a physics-based sports simulation that lets you perform insane flips, master tricking combos, parkour lines, and dive off cliffs in full control. Inspired by the world of extreme movement sports, the game blends tricking, parkour, gymnastics, tumbling, cliffjump and dods mechanics into one fun and challenging experience. If you love movement, acrobatics, extreme sports or if you wonder how it feels to do a backflip, this is your game ! 🎮 FEATURES FLICK-IT INPUT SYSTEM - 170+ TRICKS & MORE The Flick-It input system allows you to perform over 170 unique tricks, with more than 300 planned for the full release. Using the right joystick and triggers, you can intuitively tricks, all without memorizing complicated button combinations SEAMLESS TRICK TRANSITIONS – INFINITE COMBOS In Tricking 0, every trick connects seamlessly with every other trick, allowing you to go from a finite amount of tricks to an infinite amount of combos. The more you play, the more you can experiment with transitions, creating smooth tricking combos or powerful tumbling passes PARKOUR & ENVIRONMENT INTERACTION Trick off flat ground or take it to the next level by integrating parkour-style vaults, and wall flips into your combos. The game's physics adapt your tricks to different heights and surfaces, giving each location a unique feel. Explore the environment, experiment with different spots, and push the limits of what’s possible FREEFALL & CLIFF JUMPING For huge jumps and cliff dives, activate the active ragdoll system. Control your character in mid-air, using your joysticks to control your arms to balance or initiate and stop twisting. With some practice this system lets you master cliff jumping and even dødsing. Offering complete freedom to express yourself in the air X-RAY BONE BREAKING EFFECTS – FAIL IN STYLE When things go wrong, Tricking 0's X-ray bone-breaking effects and ragdoll physics make every fail hilariously brutal. Flip big—or crash hard REPLAY SYSTEM – CAPTURE EVERY TRICK Perfect your tricks & lines, and watch your best moments from every angle in slow motion CUSTOMIZATION – MAKE IT YOURS Customize your character with body proportion sliders and a selection of clothing options. The current choices are just the beginning, future updates will bring a wide range of outfits, accessories, and gear from top tricking and parkour brands LOCAL MULTIPLAYER & GAME OF TRICK Challenge your friends in local multiplayer or face AI opponents in skill-based trick battles Game of Trick – Like H.O.R.S.E or S.K.A.T.E. One player sets a combo, and everyone else must replicate it. Fail, and you take a letter. The last one to spell "TRICK" wins Game of Add on – Start with a single trick, then each player adds a new move to the combo. Mess up the full sequence, and you take a letter. The last one to spell "TRICK" wins GAMEPAD DESIGN Designed to be played with a gamepad, Tricking 0 offers the best experience when using a controller. The intuitive Flick-It system and the freefall mode are optimized for analog sticks and triggers, giving precise control over your flips, twists, and bails 🚀 FUTURE OF TRICKING 0 Tricking 0 is evolving beyond just a sandbox for flipping. The full version will include : ✅ Career Mode & Progression – Start as a beginner and rise to become a legendary athlete ✅ Online Multiplayer – Train, compete, and session with your friends ✅ More Tricks and Maps: With 170+ tricks already in the game, there’s more to come! New maps, spots, and tricks are on the way ✅ Expanded Customization: Unlock more outfits and gear to make your character stand out 👥 JOIN THE COMMUNITY! Tricking 0 is still in development, and your feedback is essential! This Alpha version is a work in progress, with frequent updates adding new tricks, maps, game modes, and features. I'm actively listening to the community and doing my best to respond to your feedback. Join our Discord server to share your thoughts, report issues, and suggest ideas Let's make this game as great as it can be! Follow @tricking.videogame on Instagram for regular development updates and news on the latest game additions
