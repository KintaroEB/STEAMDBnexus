About this gameFull description from Steam+
Everything is Woven in the Mist. The gods have fallen, and the Gyldenmist is consuming the world. Revived by a mysterious girl named Dew, you are thrust into a bleak world to harvest Gyldenblood and mend the shattered Web of Fate. Scavenge, fight, and extract in a brutal cycle of survival to bring a flicker of hope to this forbidden land. Mistfall Hunter is a third-person PvPvE extraction ARPG blending bleak dark fantasy aesthetics with intense, visceral action. Step into a forbidden realm consumed by the mist. Caught in a ruthless loop of high-stakes looting, deadly combat, and desperate extractions, you must fight for your life in the shadows to experience the ultimate dark fantasy survival test. This intricate dark fantasy world hides precious Gyldenblood and forgotten relics. It also serves as a brutal battleground for melee skirmishes against both horrific, corroded creatures and cunning rival Gyldhunters. Every battle is a deadly gamble where death strips you of all your spoils. To return to the safety of your camp, you must carve a bloody path to a Returner Woodling. Will you secure your hard-earned loot, or risk everything for greater glory? The Web of Fate does not judge. It remembers. Engage in fluid, third-person action where every strike matters. As a Gyldhunter, string together devastating combos to seize the upper hand in high-stakes PvPvE encounters. A precision free-aim system adds profound depth to the battlefield—target your enemies' weak points and immerse yourself in the brutal clash of heavy steel and arcane arts. Deploy a diverse arsenal of tactical items to outsmart your prey, letting split-second reflexes and cunning strategy dictate who survives. Six distinct classes define your path through the mist, each mastering two unique weapon stances. Adapt to the chaos of the mist by seamlessly swapping fighting styles mid-battle to dominate any encounter. Dive into deep customization with expansive talent trees, tailoring your loadout using dozens of rare gem affixes and a vast array of active skills. Break free from rigid class restraints—whether you favor relentless assault, elusive maneuvering, heavy crowd control, or unyielding defense, you define the rules of your hunt. Stalk the mist as a lone wolf, or band together in a three-player squad. In this hazardous realm, fighting back-to-back with your companions is the surest way to crush towering monstrosities, outmaneuver rival hunting parties, and guarantee a safe extraction. The rules of survival shift in every season. The battlefield beneath the Gyldenmist constantly mutates, unleashing new classes, forbidden arcana, uncharted zones, and ancient relics to keep the hunt fresh and fiercely competitive. With zero pay-to-win mechanics and no paid stat boosts, victory is earned in blood—decided solely by your mastery of the hunt.
