About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Dream Westward Journey: Unparalleled" is a side-scrolling adventure action RPG game with a theme of Journey to the West. The story is based on the first part of the Dream Westward Journey series, "Dream Westward Journey: Seven Demon Kings", and combines the game art and technology of the official sequel "Dream Westward Journey: Unparalleled". It tells the story that after the successful quest for the scriptures, King Bull is released from the alchemy furnace and gathers his old subordinates to cause chaos in the Eastern Continent of the Gods. Sun Wukong defeats the seven demon kings one by one to protect the Eastern Continent of the Gods. ---As the Dream Begins--- In the spring of 2011, the weather was slightly chilly. Six young people gathered in a small, dark room of just a few square meters to sketch out their dreams. Just like the four disciples in "Journey to the West" who endured eighty-one tribulations to reach the Western Heaven, the journey of pursuing their dreams was not smooth sailing. They created, implemented, tested, refined, and repeated. Thus, "Dreaming Journey to the West: The Seven Demons Chapter" was born. The Black Robed Man rescued King Bull Demon from the alchemy furnace and gathered the other six demons to occupy the Eastern Shengzhou. Sun Wukong and Tang Seng embarked on an adventure to save the world. Thanks to the players' love, "Dreaming Journey to the West" attracted its first batch of players. We met because of our shared love for "Journey to the West", side-scrolling games, and the then very nascent "Dreaming Journey to the West", and this became the impetus for the small studio's continuous growth. We were no longer content with a simple system, a few characters, and a single gameplay. Thanks to the strong support of the players, the dream-making journey became unstoppable. In the autumn of the same year, "Dreaming Journey to the West: The Ten Courts of Hell Chapter" was born. It had been developed for less than three months, and Zhu Bajie joined the dream-making journey. The main storyline of the Hell chapter was constantly improved, and the magic weapon system took shape. The story of "Dreaming Journey to the West" became more full-bodied. The winter of 2011 was a sunny winter. "Dreaming Journey to the West: The Heavenly Rebellion Chapter" bloomed amid the expectations of many. In a night attack on Kunlun Mountain, Sha Seng appeared in time. After obtaining the scriptures, the four disciples finally reunited and embarked on an adventure to save the world. We added the Xuanhua Gourd and the pet system, and also included the online version of Kunlun Mountain that had been delayed. There was always a mystery in the plot: Where did the Jade Emperor go? Unfortunately, in this memory, this question remained unanswered... ---Journey West Again--- Time flies. "Dreaming Journey to the West" has been accompanying the immortals for 14 years without our realizing it. Looking back on these 14 years, there have been laughter, tears, emotions, and regrets... The once naive you sitting in front of the computer might now be the same age as those six young dream chasers, shouldering the responsibility of supporting your family. Are the friends who once fought side by side still by your side? "Dreaming Journey to the West: Unparalleled" is a nostalgic work of "Dreaming Journey to the West" and marks the first attempt of the Dreaming Studio to enter Steam. We have heard some immortals say, "Dreaming Journey to the West held me in my childhood!" We believe that "Dreaming Journey to the West" holds a very important place in the hearts of many immortals. Maybe during the midday break, or as the evening sun sets, or in the leisure time after finishing homework, having "Dreaming Journey to the West" by your side is an honor for the Dreaming Studio that created this world. The story of this game begins with "Dreaming Journey to the West: Seven Demon Kings". We have combined the game art and technology of the mobile masterpiece "Dreaming Unparalleled" to recreate those once naive levels. Through familiar characters, music, and art, we aim to help the immortals who loved "Dreaming Journey to the West" in their childhood relive their memories. When Sun Wukong's golden hoop rod stirs the clouds of the Heavenly Court once again, and the bone blade of the White Bone Spirit clinks with the familiar sound, you will find that the moonlight of the Rainbow Tower is as bright as it was back then, and the ripples of the Shichuan River still reflect the image of the young boy from those days. This time, we want to tell the story of "Dreaming Journey to the West" to its end. Would you like to listen? ---Game Features--- [Original Team Reunites, Continuing the Saga] "Zao Meng Xi You: Wushuang" is developed by the original team of Zao Meng Studio. As a nostalgic work of the "Zao Meng Xi You" IP, the game features Sun Wukong as the protagonist and recreates classic levels from "Zao Meng Xi You: Seven Demon Kings". With the latest game art and technology, it offers a refreshed combat experience while retaining the strong childhood flavor. When I was a child, I roamed the skies and the earth. A decade or so ago, on the way home from school in the evening, my friends and I would huddle together, whispering things like "The final form of the Bull Demon King is too hard to defeat" and "I got the Ruyi Golden Cudgel". Back then, we never imagined that these fragmented conversations would become a shared code of our youth. The days when we took turns playing "Dream Westward Journey" side by side symbolized the most sincere friendship of our childhood. "An ethereal light pierces the vast expanse of the universe, and the staff in his hand is no less. It can be long or short, used as one pleases; it can be wielded horizontally or vertically, stretched or retracted at will." Just as the poem describes, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven symbolizes a positive image of justice and bravery in our hearts. And it is "Dream Westward Journey" that has transformed the story of "Journey to the West," which is etched in our DNA, into an adventure that can be touched by our own hands, allowing us to play as Sun Wukong and personally slay all the evil in the world. Fourteen years later, a chance encounter with a celestial being Today, when Sun Wukong returns to Flower and Fruit Mountain, the familiar background music plays again. Whose heart is stirred? As the first game of the "Dreaming Westward Journey" series to be launched on Steam, we wonder if you are surprised to see it here. No matter, let's dream together this time!
