About this gameFull description from Steam+
🚀 Rail Grind Into the Ultimate VR Multiplayer Shooter: Hyper Dash Step into Hyper Dash, the free-to-play VR sensation where teamwork, speed, and strategy collide. Experience pulse-pounding action across diverse maps and game modes that will keep you coming back for more! 🏃Master the Art of Movement Speed and agility are your greatest allies. Dash, sprint, and rail grind your way through intense arenas, outmanoeuvring opponents and securing objectives to lead your team to victory. 🎯Endless Game Modes, Infinite Fun Challenge yourself across different game modes, including: Payload Domination Control Point (Team) Deathmatch Capture the Flag Elimination Ball Mix it up by adding mutators -like Instagib or Low Gravity- to your custom matches or use the server browser to check out what others have created. ⏱️Action Without the Wait In Hyper Dash, you can jump straight into the action. With compact maps, fast respawns, and steady pacing, the game ensures you spend less time waiting and more time immersed in the thrill of combat. It’s designed for players who appreciate quick, engaging gameplay sessions. 🎮 Ready to Dash? Get ready to dash, grind, and dominate in the most exhilarating VR shooter around. The arena awaits—will you rise to the challenge?
