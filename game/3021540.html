About this gameFull description from Steam+
400 years from now, Earth has perished, and humanity has made its home among the stars. The struggle for a place in space has left a lasting legacy of internal conflicts and brutal faction wars. The galaxy is divided among 10 warring factions, each with its own agenda. Factions like the Children of the Everliving seek eternal life at any cost, while MPL aims for profit, and Tianchao combats corruption with vast spy networks. The Abyss The Abyss appeared without warning, causing chaos in the Frontier. As a commander, your ultimate goal is to discover the secrets of the Abyss and prevent it from consuming everything in its path. This exploration-rich game mode lets you choose your path and make crucial command decisions that determine your rewards. Game Modes and Challenges PvE Content: Enjoy a story-rich campaign and tackle challenging bounties. PvP Arena: Test your skills against other players in strategic space battles. The Vault: Join an alliance and raid the vault for valuable resources. The Abyss: Uncover secrets and make pivotal decisions in this exploration-focused mode. Recruit Unique Pilots Collect over 100 ships, each with unique abilities and backstories. Upgrade your fleet and pilots with powerful enhancements to dominate space. Deep Customization Customize your ships with a variety of weapons, shields, and software. Utilize up to 6 gear slots and collect powerful implants for limitless possibilities. Tailor your fleet to your strategic needs and battle preferences. Master Your Tactics Use strategic tactics to defeat powerful enemies and progress towards becoming a legendary commander. Plan your moves carefully to create the perfect fleet combination, and choose formations based on your ships’ unique targeting patterns to maximize your battle effectiveness. Limited-Time Events Engage in exciting events and themed holiday bounties. Participate in community-wide challenges and step into the commander's arcade with mini-games like Gelecek Survivor. Explore the Galaxy In Starborne: Frontiers, the vast galaxy is your battleground. Use strategy and tactics to navigate through the sci-fi universe, conquering challenges and building your legacy among the stars. Experience the thrill of strategic space battles as you command your fleet in this sci-fi adventure. Master the art of strategy and immerse yourself in a sci-fi epic where your choices shape the fate of the galaxy. Engage in strategic planning and sci-fi battles to become the ultimate commander in Starborne: Frontiers. Strategy is essential in every aspect of this sci-fi universe, and your tactical decisions will determine your success.
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

Our game features rich and immersive storytelling, which includes mature themes within the backstories of certain units. These narratives may contain depictions of violence, trauma, and other sensitive topics. Specifically, some character histories explore difficult subjects such as: Violence: References to physical confrontations and battles are present within the context of the units' past experiences. Trauma and Psychological Struggles: Characters may have endured various hardships, including the psychological aftermath of war and personal loss. It is important to note that these mature themes are woven into the fabric of the characters' backgrounds to provide depth and realism to their stories. They are not depicted graphically within the gameplay itself. However, players who may have sensitivities to these topics are advised to consider this information before engaging with the game. We believe that these elements contribute to a more compelling and nuanced narrative experience, adding layers of complexity to our characters and their journeys.
