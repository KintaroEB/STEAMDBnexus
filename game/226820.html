About this gameFull description from Steam+
A decaying southern mansion holds secrets to finding a missing bride. Uncover the betrayal and dark romanticism of the family estate’s history. The investigation startles at every turn as you sleuth in the old graveyard, where spiders, skeletons, and a dearly departed ghost create supernatural hauntings surrounding your every move. Some Families Keep Deadly Secrets! Jessalyn Thornton’s fateful sleepover at the abandoned Thornton estate was supposed to be a pre-wedding celebration, but the fun ended when she disappeared. While her family searches for clues, others refuse to speak about the estate’s dark past. Did something supernatural happen to Jessalyn, or is someone in Thornton Hall holding something besides family secrets? ⚠️ATTENTION MAC USERS: This game will not run on Mojave, Catalina, Big Sur, Monterey, Ventura, or higher Mac operating systems.
