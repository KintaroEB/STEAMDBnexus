About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Heartopia, a multiplayer life simulation game crafted for creativity, freedom, and peace. Build your dream home, explore a wide range of hobbies, and forge warm connections with friends in a town full of endless possibilities. [Game Features] ◆ Let's Connect! Create Unique Memories Chat with the charming residents of Heartopia Town, hang out with old friends, and meet new friends every time you log in! ◆ Let's Explore! Gardening, Fishing, Birding... Hobbies are yours to enjoy, not chores. In Heartopia, feel free to develop six major hobbies, including fishing, cooking, gardening, birdwatching, and more. ◆ Let's Build! Design Your Dream House Whether you dream of a cozy cottage or a magnificent mansion, Heartopia gives you the tools to turn your vision into a reality. You can also invite your friends over and build your dream home together. ◆ Let's Dress Up! 1000+ Daily Outfits Mix and match casual wear, elegant gowns, and whimsical costumes to create the perfect look for any occasion. Express your mood and show the world who you are. ◆ Let's Pet! Adopt Cats and Dogs Meet your very own animal companions in Heartopia! While each of your furry friends may come with different colors and personalities, they can all bring you warmth and comfort.
