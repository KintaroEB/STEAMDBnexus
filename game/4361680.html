About this gameFull description from Steam+
Home Store Simulator is a first-person store management simulation where you build, manage, and grow your own home goods store. Design your store to be both efficient and visually appealing. Determine where products are displayed, organize your store layout, and ensure a smooth shopping experience for your customers. Running out of stock? Keep your store supplied with new products and stay on top of the latest trends to attract more customers. Take cash and credit card payments and make sure every customer leaves your store satisfied. Expand your store by unlocking new sections to increase capacity, boost profits, and grow your business. Personalize your store to make it unique. Rename your store, paint the walls, customize the interior, and more.
