About this gameFull description from Steam+
From Mining to Masterpiece Mine ore, process it into fragments, and refine them into valuable gemstones. Build and optimize your production line from extraction to cutting and polishing. A stable supply and efficient management will determine how fast your operation grows. Machines and Flow Build your production chain using a variety of machines and utilities. Place equipment with intent, connect each stage, and keep materials moving without stalls. As new machines unlock, you can expand into parallel lines, higher throughput setups, and smoother automation. Expansion and Growth Grow from a compact mining facility into a larger operation with greater capacity and more processing options. Expansion is not just about adding space. It is a chance to redesign your workflow, remove bottlenecks, and improve overall stability. Employees Bring in employees to handle routine work so your operation stays active while you focus on planning, upgrading, and improving efficiency. Build it step by step, improve the flow one bottleneck at a time, and enjoy the moment when a rough ore pipeline turns into a reliable gemstone factory.
