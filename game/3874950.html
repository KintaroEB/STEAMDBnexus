About this gameFull description from Steam+
Bit Buddy reacts in real-time to your keyboard and mouse activity. Every click and movement brings an adorable response from your little companion. Even better, each interaction gives you a chance to unlock new decorations, with no extra effort required! With 600+ unique accessories and pet skins to collect and customize, you can give your Bit Buddy a one-of-a-kind look that matches your style. Bit Buddy automatically logs your daily and historical keyboard and mouse activity, so you can easily track how much you’ve typed or clicked. Whether you're working or studying, Bit Buddy stays by your side on your screen—your loyal digital coworker, always there with you. 💻 Instantly responds to your mouse and keyboard actions: 🎤 Reacts to your microphone input in real time: 🎁 Each use of your keyboard or mouse may reward you with decorations, no extra steps needed! And finally, want to see your character in Bit Buddy? Join our Discord and let’s make it happen together! Privacy Policy Bit Buddy does not collect any of your device or personal information. Any keyboard or mouse usage data is stored locally on your own device and is never uploaded anywhere. Other than the required communication with Steam (for things like purchase verification, achievements, or cloud save), the game does not perform any other network communication.
