About this gameFull description from Steam+
Cozy Marbles is a comfy game about building marble runs. Using a sampling of bits and pieces, fit together the marble course you’ve always wanted to build and play with. Unwind to a bespoke lo-fi soundtrack and roll with the nostalgia. In the Cozy Marbles demo, build your marble course in a mellow Living Room using a selection of track pieces from the Toybox. Cozy Marbles is a little game with a big brain. Plan and experiment with all sorts of marble constructs, from marble rollercoasters to wacky apparatus. Watch your marbles flow through carefully laid routes… or fly way off track! Assemble a marble course, drop your marbles and watch them race to the end. Use checkpoints to split the track or set up multiple start and finish lines wherever you want. Make your race sweet and simple, or huge and complex with tons of laps and hundreds of marbles! May the best one win. The next step after perfecting your marble masterpiece (or diabolical marble trap) is to share it with the world. Export and import courses to show off your build and try everyone else’s. If you’re a streamer, use Twitch integration to let Twitch Chat drop marbles into your course and join your race!
