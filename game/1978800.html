About this gameFull description from Steam+
Lights, Camera, ACTION! Experience the adrenaline-fueled intensity of classic first-person shooter action with AQtion, the ultimate evolution of the beloved mod, Action Quake 2. AQtion combines the latest engine enhancements with the signature gameplay mechanics and updates from the Action Quake community, delivering a nostalgic yet revitalized gaming experience. Engage in fast-paced, skill-based combat where every move counts. With realistic weapon handling, limited ammunition, and a focus on tactical gameplay, AQtion tests your reflexes and strategic thinking like never before. Master the art of precision shooting, maneuver through dynamic environments, and outsmart your opponents in heart-pounding battles that will keep you on the edge of your seat. Featuring regularly updated game modes and features, engine enhancements and an active pickup game community, AQtion brings the classic Action Quake 2 experience into the modern era. Jump into the action and unleash your skills in intense multiplayer matches or thrilling single-player challenges. If you're an old school veteran to first person shooters, this is the Action Quake 2 you remember, with the original charm and modern enhancements you would come to expect. If you're new to the scene, welcome to what classic first person shooters are all about!
