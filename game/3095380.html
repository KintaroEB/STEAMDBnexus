About this gameFull description from Steam+
Embark on an epic journey in Ninja Rift, an immersive RPG where you create and customize your own ninja. Level up your skills, master powerful techniques, and join forces with clans and crews to dominate the battlefield. Challenge other ninjas in intense battles, prove your prowess, and rise to become the ultimate ninja warrior. In Ninja Rift, the path to greatness is paved with strategy, skill, and unwavering determination. Will you conquer the rift and claim your place as the best ninja of all? Join now and begin your adventure!
