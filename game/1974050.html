About this gameFull description from Steam+
Brand-New Season “Afterlight” Launches July 16! The Night Watch Begins — Rewards Await The new Season arrives with a full lineup of events. Log in daily to claim a Legendary Battle Pactspirit Selection Pack, and from the second week on, checking in also earns you a 10x Battle Pactspirit draw. Complete Season event and objective quests for even more rewards, including rare Season Drop Pactspirits, Pactspirit Crystals, Exclusive Weapon Appearances, Activation Medium: Motionless, and a generous supply of Tax Vouchers! Seasonal Mechanic: Afterlight As night falls, the Guiding Lantern flickers to life. Purify the restless Wraiths gathering along the procession's path and collect scattered relics and high-value loot. Watch for golden reward monsters, special coffins, revived Wraiths, and mysterious bosses: surprise encounters that make every run more thrilling and more rewarding! New Hero Trait: Dance of the Deep Tide Whisper Selena arrives with the brand-new Hero Trait Dance of the Deep. Built around Terra skills, her flowing combat rhythm lets the Crimson Tide move with her every step, opening fresh build possibilities amid dazzling, deadly spins! New Progression: Nether King's Divinity Nether King's Divinity introduces a new kind of Divinity Slate. Brand your Divine Remnants to cultivate unique Divinity forms, then harness special effects like Judgment, Contamination, and Banishment to discover new build paths and craft slate combinations all your own! New Challenge: Divine Ascent The all-new challenge mode Divine Ascent is here! Climb ever-higher floors against a tense three-minute timer, defeat floor bosses, clear random events, and claim dense first-clear rewards. Then carve your name into both the First Clear Rankings and the Infinity Rankings! System & Visual Upgrades Across the Board Lunaria goes core, now faster and easier to build around. Renewed Memory becomes permanent in streamlined form, and the Dream Interpretation system has been retired in favor of a reworked gear affix experience. Quality-of-life upgrades arrive in force: Trade House price checks, Quick Buy, One-Tap skill leveling, mouse cursor size settings, and separate brightness and transparency controls for effects. Ember's Rest, the Netherrealm Device, select hero models, and gear icons have all received visual upgrades, delivering a smoother, more immersive new Season experience! FAST & THRILLING Battles With no stamina and no cooldowns, smash down incoming waves with melee attacks, blast off magical explosions and draining pools, or snipe down ranged enemies. Grind with your own battle style! Collect ENDLESS Loot Unlimited drops from battles to upgrade build styles and establish your own collection. Prove your grind power by showing off in the in-game free market. Build UNLIMITED Playstyles With unique heroes, 24 talent tabs, 200+ legendary gears, 240+ powerful skills, Try out infinite playstyles or strategic possibilities on hero builds. Build your very own hero! Trade AT WILL Utilize the Trade house to be a part of a booming economy with an infinite amount of hero builds to trade. One hunter's trash might be another hunter's treasure!
