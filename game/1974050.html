About this gameFull description from Steam+
Brand-New Season “Tartarus” Launches Oct 16! Warden's Welcome Gifts Check in every day to collect the new season's Rare and Magic Drop Pactspirits, and sign in for seven days straight to claim a Legendary Drop Pactspirit Selection Pack. Season events unlock 30 Legendary Battle Pactspirit draws, Rare seasonal Drop Pactspirits, upgrade Insignias, and an exclusive weapon appearance. There's also a limited-time event that grants Cloudgatherer - Surging Gold and Happy Chonky, plus the new Pactspirit Rally feature—everything a rookie Hunter needs to become a proper Warden! Seasonal Mechanic: Tartarus The inmates have escaped across the Netherrealm, and behind these walls, only one rule matters. Become the Warden of Tartarus: hunt down the fugitives, take their Prisoner Nameplates, and run the prison your way over a 20-day sentence. Pit inmates against each other—Mammoth > Lion > Wolf > Colossal Rat—feeding the strong until Chromatic Prisoners emerge. Torture Instruments and sudden Prison Incidents keep every patrol unpredictable. When the 20 days are up, the riot begins: crush the beasts in the central prison complex and claim your Warden's Caches! New Hero Trait: The Ultimate One Spacetime Witness Youga gains a brand-new Hero Trait: The Ultimate One. This dark mage from a doomed timeline no longer summons parallel versions of himself to fight at his side—he hunts them down and takes their power. Drive the Clockwork Core and freely combine three development paths to become the final answer on every timeline! New Progression: Followers The brand-new Follower progression system is here. Letters of Introduction from the interrogation room bring a roster of hardened allies who volunteer to fight for you in the Netherrealm. They wield their own exclusive Tartarus Skills, and every skill drop arrives with random Support Skills attached—the higher their rank, the more skills they can equip. Recruit Ultimate Followers and raise the perfect partner for your build! System & Visual Upgrades Across the Board Afterlight goes core and gains its own Talent Constellation, and Nether King's Divinity drops far more often—and can now be exchanged. Supreme Showdown introduces the new Star's Fall mechanic and a rotating boss lineup. The Netherrealm map pool, Trial: God of War, God of Machines, and Outlaw have all been improved, and the Fire Lord “Flame Forger · Burning Heart” gets an upgrade too. Memory Revival is no longer a gamble—you can reroll the result. The Trade House adds condition filters and preset sharing, compasses and map materials can be favorited and saved as presets, and loot drops resolve much faster. Three familiar heroes get refreshed models and skill effects, campaign stages and plenty of audio have been overhauled, delivering a smoother, more immersive new season! FAST & THRILLING Battles With no stamina and no cooldowns, smash down incoming waves with melee attacks, blast off magical explosions and draining pools, or snipe down ranged enemies. Grind with your own battle style! Collect ENDLESS Loot Unlimited drops from battles to upgrade build styles and establish your own collection. Prove your grind power by showing off in the in-game free market. Build UNLIMITED Playstyles With unique heroes, 24 talent tabs, 200+ legendary gears, 240+ powerful skills, Try out infinite playstyles or strategic possibilities on hero builds. Build your very own hero! Trade AT WILL Utilize the Trade house to be a part of a booming economy with an infinite amount of hero builds to trade. One hunter's trash might be another hunter's treasure!
