About this gameFull description from Steam+
Angels Online Global marks a major milestone in the franchise's 20 year history. This edition faithfully preserves the game's classic gameplay and world while delivering a fully upgraded experience for modern players. Veterans can relive the pure excitement of their original adventures, while smoother combat, refined progression, and global servers open the door to a new era of cross-border exploration. The four major factions reunite once again. Fight for honor, loyalty, and belief as iconic faction-based battles return in their most refined form. A highly flexible skill system allows players to freely combine abilities from a vast skill pool, creating personalized builds and defining their own combat roles. An extensive wardrobe system lets players shape their look without restrictions. From cute to heroic, simple to extravagant, your style is entirely yours. Totem battles, personal housing, pet cultivation, mech mounts, and a variety of mini-games all make a full comeback, bringing classic gameplay together in one complete experience. With optimized level-up efficiency, early-game progression and training time have been significantly reduced, allowing players to access core and endgame content with ease. Significantly increased bag space removes restrictions on dungeon runs and loot collection, making exploration smoother and more enjoyable. Built-in real-time translation supports multiple languages, enabling seamless communication with players from around the world. Costumes now drop directly from dungeons, making fashion collection more accessible and enhancing the thrill of exploration and challenge. Unused outfits can be dismantled and fused to enhance character attributes, turning your collection into real strength and ensuring no resource ever goes to waste.
