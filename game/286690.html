About this gameFull description from Steam+
In 2013 the world was devastated by an apocalyptic event, annihilating almost all mankind and turning the earth’s surface into a poisonous wasteland. A handful of survivors took refuge in the depths of the Moscow underground, and human civilization entered a new Dark Age. The year is 2033. An entire generation has been born and raised underground, and their besieged Metro Station-Cities struggle for survival; with each other, and the mutant horrors that await outside. You are Artyom, born in the last days before the fire, but raised underground. Having never ventured beyond the city limits, one fateful event sparks a desperate mission to the heart of the Metro system, to warn the remnants of mankind of a terrible impending threat. Your journey takes you from the forgotten catacombs beneath the Metro to the desolate wastelands above, where your actions will determine the fate of mankind. But what if the real threat comes from within? UPDATED FOR STEAM Experience the post-apocalyptic depths of the Moscow Metro like never before with the Next-Gen Update. METRO 2033 Redux has been enhanced with numerous visual and technical improvements, including 4K Textures; TSR, DLSS and FSR support; increased Geometry and Texture details; improved PBR materials; spatial audio support; improved Animations and sound quality, and more! METRO 2033 Redux also adds supports for the Xbox Controller Impulse Triggers and DualSense controller Adaptive Triggers, immersing you further in the fight for survival. Game Features METRO 2033 Redux is the definitive first chapter in Artyom’s epic journey – the 2010 cult classic, rebuilt in 2014 using the METRO Last Light engine and gameplay framework, and now further updated to take advantage of modern hardware while preserving the gameplay of the original Redux experience Brave the horrors of the apocalypse, and immerse yourself in the Moscow Metro – equip your gasmask and an arsenal of hand-made weaponry as you face the threat of deadly mutants, human foes, and supernatural phenomena in one of the most atmospheric worlds in gaming Two unique Play Styles : ‘Spartan’ and ‘Survival’ – approach the campaign as a slow burn Survival Horror , or tackle it with the combat skills of a Spartan Ranger in these two unique modes The legendary Ranger Mode returns – dare you play the fearsome Ranger Mode? No HUD, UI, deadlier combat and limited resources combine to create the ultimate immersive experience Based on the internationally bestselling novel series by Dmitry Glukhovsky
