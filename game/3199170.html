About this gameFull description from Steam+
Gather your friends and dive into the action-packed Battle Royale, Squad Fight, or Hot Zone like there's no tomorrow! Blood Strike offers a wide range of playable Strikers, each with a unique active and passive ability letting you deploy drones, shield walls and everything in between. Customize your weapons to your liking and get ready to prove that you have what it takes to come out on top! Adrenaline Packed Combat Having the guts to pounce on your enemies and pursue them in tense firefights is key to your success in Blood Strike. Tactical decisions matter, but don’t expect to camp a spot unchallenged! Meet The Strikers & Their Deadly Abilities Pick your favorite hero each with their unique active and passive skill and bring defeat to your opponents! Enhance Your Arsenal Take it to the next level by customizing your weapons and selecting unique loadouts. Team Up For The Perfect Strike Blood Strike lets you team up and play team-based modes quickly. Finding your friends, adding them to your squad and jumping in a firefight as a team couldn't be easier! Intuitive Controls. World Class Precision. Blood Strike offers the smoothest aiming, shooting, running, and gliding experience. Enjoy smooth graphics and responsive controls, even on low-end machines, and fully immerse yourself in the action without hardware worries. Let nothing stand between you and that perfect victory.
