About this gameFull description from Steam+
Pure Badminton brings the speed, precision, and energy of real-world badminton to life. Experience the fastest racket sport in the world like never before, in a realistic and accessible game. 🏸 REALISTIC GAMEPLAY & PHYSICS Authentic shuttle physics and realistic player animations recreate the flow and intensity of real matches. Expect a challenge that feels natural, with performance shaped by positioning, timing, and shot selection. 🏆 CAREER MODE: BUILD YOUR JOURNEY Start from the bottom and rise through a full career mode, competing in the world's biggest tournaments. Train, improve, and climb a ranking system inspired by real-world competition. Develop your playstyle naturally: your skills evolve based on how you train and compete. 👥 LOCAL MULTIPLAYER (UP TO 4 PLAYERS) Play singles or doubles with your friends. Pure Badminton supports local multiplayer for up to 4 players. Whether you're a competitive player or a lifelong fan, it’s time to bring your skills to the digital court.
