About this gameFull description from Steam+
Suncraft is a cozy pixel art adventure focused on growth and restoration. Step into a peaceful world where every tool you craft brings a new spark of life to the village. Settle into a relaxing rhythm of progress as you watch the village return to the glory it once had. Do you want to enjoy the atmosphere of Suncraft? Wishlist Suncraft today to be notified at launch! CORE GAMEPLAY Explore and Gather Journey through the land and collect materials while unlocking new biomes. Craft and Rebuild Build workstations, construct machines, and craft components piece by piece to repair the village and revive the land. Upgrade and Automate Unlock new gear, master powerful skills, and recruit loyal companions to help you along the way. QUESTS AND MYSTERIES Complete objectives for villagers to uncover the story of the village.
