About this gameFull description from Steam+
Lead your troops to victory in a huge open world set in the Vietnam War. Play as U.S. or Vietcong / N.V.A. on an ever changing battlefield where other units move and fight independently of player. Command your squad to follow, stay, attack and change formations etc. Missions of various types are provided based on the situation in the area. However, there is no story or main mission progression. Use radios to request airstrikes, artillery support, reinforcements and supplies. Patrol the Mekong delta and lush river valleys. Search and destroy the enemies in dense jungles and defend the hilltop fire bases. Protect loyal villages from enemy infiltration or teach them the price of betrayal. Build perimeter defenses. Eat, drink and smoke to calm the nerves, or pop pills to feel energized. Keep an eye out for booby traps, tigers, snakes and critters as you root out tunnels and hidden fortifications in the jungles. Avoid capture or become a P.O.W. Bomb the Ho Chi Minh Trail or lead special forces across the border into Laos and Cambodia to disrupt enemy supplies. The game features one huge 1000+ km2 map that covers North and South Vietnam, and large parts of Laos and Cambodia with hundreds of manually built bases and villages. Fly jets or gunships, or man the door guns. Pound the enemy using tanks and artillery and a vast arsenal of weapons. Helicopters: UH1 Huey (4 variants), AH1 Cobra, CH47 Chinook, OH6 Little Bird, H13 Sioux, CH54 Skycrane, H21 Shawnee, H34 Seahorse, AH56 Cheyenne, Mi8. Airplanes: F4 Phantom II, F5 Tiger II, F105 Thunderchief, O1 Bird Dog, O2 Skymaster, OV10 Bronco, A1 Skyraider, A4 Skyhawk, A6 Intruder, A37 Dragonfly, PC6 Porter, B52 Stratofortress, MIG17, MIG21. Land Vehicles: M48A3 Patton, M551 Sheridan, M42 Duster, M113A1 ACAV, M132 Zippo, M50 Ontos, M151A2 Jeep, LVTP5, M106 Mortar, M107 SPG, M88 ARV, M274 Mule, M548, M577, M54 Trucks, M55 Quad 50cal, V100 Commando, D7E Bulldozer T55, T34/85, PT76, BTR60, Gaz69, ZIS151 Trucks, BM21, ZSU57-2, ZU23. Boats: PBR mkII, PCF mk1, ASPB, ATC, ATC(H), Sampans Disclaimer: I don't have any website nor do I plan to make any, so please don't interact with any sites pretending to be me!
