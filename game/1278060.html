About this gameFull description from Steam+
Orqa FPV.SkyDive is a Free-to-Play Drone Racing And Freestyle Simulator, and your gateway into the world of FPV drones. Experience an immersive & realistic flight simulation through the eyes of an FPV pilot. Practice your skills and participate in races, challenges or your own freestyle sandbox tricks as you master your flying skills in both single-player and multi-player. A Realistic Simulation Optimised and realistic drone flight physics for the most realistic flying experience. A variety of maps in a vibrant 3D world environment The Possibility of Flying Micro Drones and Regular FPV Aircraft Versatile Gamemodes Racing - Develop your maverick racing skills in a high-speed FPV adrenaline fest Freestyle - Practice your tricks and flicks in a sandbox environment Contest - Participate in global racing events with leaderboards Flight School - Master the art of FPV! Elevate your FPV skills in fun and interactive way Track Builder - Build your dream track Seamless Controller Integration Seamless integration with your favourite controllers Calibrate and fine-tune your rates Native integration with Playstation, Xbox & ORQA FPV.Ctrl controllers Multi-player & Community Compete with other pilots in online game modes Join a growing community of FPV pilots Collect Flight Points and rank up Miscellaneous Platform agnostic. Run FPV.SkyDive on PC, Mac, or Linux devices
