About this gameFull description from Steam+
Strayed is a fast paced open world VR survival game where every session feels like a new adventure. Start with nothing on a fresh island, gather materials, craft weapons, build bases, and battle other players for the best loot. You start washed up on the beach with only a rock and a torch. Gear up fast and pay attention. Early choices can shape your entire run. No island is ever the same. Explore huge procedurally generated environments full of beaches, forests, mountains, and abandoned monuments. Gather resources, craft gear, and build a base to protect your loot. Upgrade tools, unlock new weapons, and design your own defenses. Work alone or team up with friends for bigger goals. Other players are always out there. Some players want to team up. Others want your loot. Choose who to trust, stay alert, and make your own story every time you spawn. Key Features Procedurally generated islands VR combat with guns, bows, melee, and throwable items Craft weapons, tools, armor, and more Build bases with doors, traps, and electricity Operate vehicles: ATVs, boats, and helicopters Proximity voice chat & teams Active high population servers with regular wipe schedules Monthly updates with new content and improvements Early Access Strayed is still growing and improving. We update the game every month with new content, features, and bug fixes. Some systems will evolve throughout EA. Your ideas and feedback help shape the future of Strayed. Thank you for being part of the community.
