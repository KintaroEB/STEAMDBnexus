About this gameFull description from Steam+
Pumpkin Woods is a witch-themed open world survival crafting sandbox set in a strange and unforgiving forest. Gather magical resources, brew potions, trade with merchants, roam around in your traveler wagon, explore mysterious landmarks and manage your health, happiness, hunger, and thirst to survive. ❗ IMPORTANT ❗ 🖱️ Mouse & keyboard only 🖥️ Designed for 16:9 resolutions 🤝 No handholding. Explore, experiment, and figure things out ⚰️ You will die... but each attempt makes you wiser 🔒 Secrets are everywhere, if you know where to look Customize your witch attire, furnish a secluded cabin, adopt a companion cat or unicorn, and travel by foot or enchanted broom through a world shaped by dynamic weather and procedural generation. A chill mode is available for players who want a more relaxed pace.
