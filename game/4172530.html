About this gameFull description from Steam+
A WORLD BORN OF MYTH AND WAR ・ ᛅ ᚹᛟᚱᛚᛞ ᛒᛟᚱᚾ ᚢᚠ ᛗᚨᚦ ᛅᚾᛞ ᚹᛅᚱ When the gods fell silent and mortals rose to claim their place, the world of Legend of YMIR was forged in fire and frost. This is where your saga begins, as a warrior bound by fate who must reclaim the power once held by the divine. From frozen ruins to the sacred peaks of Asgard, every step reve als fragments of forgotten legends. Explore lands scarred by ancient battles and hidden betrayals, uncover long-buried secrets, and witness a world on the verge of rebirth. MASTER THE ART OF COMBAT ・ ᛗᛅᛋᛏᛖᚱ ᚦᛖ ᛅᚱᛏ ᚢᚠ ᚲᛟᛗᛒᛅᛏ Victory in Legend of YMIR belongs to those who master control. Every strike, dodge, and counterattack responds to your timing and instincts. The hit-confirmation combat system rewards precision and awareness, allowing you to read your enemy’s movements and react with purpose. Combine abilities, unleash powerful skill chains, and stand with allies in challenging raids that demand strategy and coordination. In the decisive moments when perfect timing changes the flow of battle, you will feel the full force of action combat. WAR THAT SPANS REALMS ・ ᚹᛅᚱ ᚦᛅᛏ ᛋᛈᛅᚾᛋ ᚱᛖᛅᛚᛗᛋ Fight for your server’s pride in Server Battle, the massive conflict that defines Legend of YMIR. Warriors from distant realms gather on the same battlefield where unity, tactics, and courage rise above raw strength. Each campaign spans two weeks and forges new rivalries as shifting alliances test every warrior’s resolve. Capture strongholds, seize the Crown, and lead your forces through strategic coordination. Every victory brings your realm closer to the greatest trial of all, the YMIR Cup. YMIR CUP: THE ESPORTS STAGE OF LEGENDS ・ ᛁᛗᛁᚱ ᚲᚢᛈ ᚦᛖ ᛖᛋᛈᛟᚱᛏᛋ ᛋᛏᛅᚷᛖ ᛟᚠ ᛚᛖᚷᛖᚾᛞᛋ Only servers that triumph through the trials of Server Battle may advance to the YMIR Cup, a global tournament created to decide the strongest realm in existence. The YMIR Cup unfolds offline in an intimate esports setting where the clan leaders of the victorious clans gather to represent their regions. This arena becomes a crucible where pride, strategy, and legacy collide. This is the moment when legends rise. Claim victory, honor your realm, and carve your name into the enduring saga of YMIR. RULE YOUR OWN WORLD ・ ᚱᚢᛚᛖ ᛁᛟᚢᚱ ᛟᚹᚾ ᚹᛟᚱᛚᛞ For leaders who seek a destiny beyond the battlefield, the Partner’s Server grants the rare chance to build an entire realm. Only selected operators earn this honor, gaining the authority to name their world, create its laws, and guide a community shaped by their vision. Within these realms, every rule forged and every event created becomes part of a living tale shaped by its leader. Your decisions influence the identity of your world and the warriors who call it home. Lead with wisdom or command with unwavering resolve. The legacy of your realm begins with you, and its story will echo across the world of YMIR. UNREAL ENGINE 5 IN FULL GLORY ・ ᚢᚾᚱᛖᛅᛚ ᛖᚾᚷᛁᚾᛖ 5 ᛁᚾ ᚠᚢᛚᛚ ᚷᛚᛟᚱᛁ Legend of YMIR brings the vast beauty and fierce spirit of Norse mythology to life through Unreal Engine 5. Frozen wilderness stretches to the horizon, towering strongholds rise from ancient stone, and forgotten sanctuaries echo with the remnants of lost power. Cinematic lighting, richly detailed environments, and sweeping vistas create a world that feels alive with myth and danger. Every battlefield tells a story, and every path hints at hidden tales waiting to be uncovered. From close-quarters duels to large-scale wars that shake the land, each encounter blends spectacle and immersion to draw you deeper into a realm shaped by ages long past. KEY FEATURES ・ ᚲᛖᛁ ᚠᛖᚦᚢᚱᛖᛋ Skill-Based Action Combat: Real-time battles that reward timing, mastery, and precision. Server Battle Warfare: Massive inter-server conflicts that determine the fate of entire realms. YMIR Cup Global Tournament: Rise to a world stage and prove your server’s dominance. Lead and Create: Shape your own realm and community within the Partner’s Server system. Norse Mythology Reimagined: Explore a world inspired by ancient legends and forgotten gods. Cinematic Storytelling: Uncover hidden histories and witness the rise of mythic tales. Unreal Engine 5 Visuals: Experience a world of beauty and danger brought to life in stunning detail.

Content notice

Legend of YMIR contains fantasy-themed combat involving weapons, monsters, and magical effects. All violence is non-realistic and does not include gore, dismemberment, or explicit injury detail. The game does not contain any sexual content, nudity, drug-related themes, or depictions of self-harm. Online interactions and player chat are included, and gameplay may involve cooperative or competitive combat within a dark-fantasy world setting.
