About this gameFull description from Steam+
VRidge connects your mobile device to your PC, transforming it into a powerful SteamVR-compatible virtual reality headset. It will then stream SteamVR apps to your mobile device as a high-performance video with full head and controller tracking. All you need is a mobile or cardboard VR viewer to enjoy the experience! Play with limited session time to try out VRidge before purchase with this demo. Getting started Install RiftCat client application on your computer via Steam. Install VRidge on your mobile device and place it inside your mobile VR viewer. Launch both apps. Follow the built-in instructions in the RiftCat application to connect them via WiFi. Enjoy!
