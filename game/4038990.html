About this gameFull description from Steam+
Bring more warriors from 《Crusaders Quest》! Crusader Quest : Mini(CQ:Mini)is a hands-on clicker game with your work and play. Besides the warriors and costumes in the original Crusader Quest, There are also original characters that you've never seen before, so make sure you get them! [Crusader Quest] The warriors of Crusader Quest are back in a cute mini-mini! Collect all the Crusader Quest warriors and costumes You can battle the bosses of Crusader Quest. Original characters and costumes that only appear in this work appear. [Clicker game] Press keyboard, mouse, play game, and work naturally     The warriors can react, and acquire new warriors and costumes. The battle is automatic. Of course, you can click directly and fight. As you battle, you can meet the bosses of Crusader Quest one after another. You can get the original warriors and costumes that you can only see in this game [Features] You can select a variety of backgrounds that appear in the original. Backgrounds can be turned on/off. You can freely drag and place it anywhere you want and proceed. The size can be changed freely in five stages.
