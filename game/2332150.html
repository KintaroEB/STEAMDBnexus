About this gameFull description from Steam+
“Oh, a visitor? We haven’t had one in quite some time…” Waking up on a deserted island, you are greeted by a mysterious woman who asks you to take care of her frogs while she is away. ​However, she vanishes the next day, leaving you with only a hand-written note and some old tools. With nowhere to go, you have but two simple tasks: Escape Frog Island Accept the role of caretaker of Frog Island In this spiritual successor to the classic creature collection games like Neko Atsume, build the island of your dreams, as you discover cute and curious frogs, and uncover the island's many hidden secrets, to try and piece together the past life you've somehow forgotten. Meet over 60 unique frogs, from the bashful to the bold. Each frog has its own personality and style, food preferences, and special mementos and gifts! Dress frogs with accessories, play interactive minigames with them, and keep the frogs of your island happy as you fill out your compendium to discover more! Unravel the island's history, and discover the key to your escape, all while forming lifelong bonds with the island's magical and divine frogs. The true answers lie hidden within the island, waiting for you to uncover them. Transform a once deserted island into a beautiful frog sanctuary. WIth over 250 toys and decorations, and free-form placement, decorate your island your way, to help create the perfect home for your curious new froggy friends. Discover and collect 60+ adorable frogs, each with unique personalities and looks, and some super secret frogs as well! Explore the diverse wildlife of the island, with 60+ bugs, critters, and fish for you to catch! Farm, cook, fish, and forage during your day-to-day caretaker activities Discover 10+ hand crafted environments on the island, from a fiery volcano, to a magical field of flowers! Build the island of your dreams, with over 250+ toys and decorations to fulfill all your decorating needs! Dress your frogs with cute accessories, over 60+ to unlock! Feed, sing songs, chat, clean, and so many more ways to interact with your frogs Capture your favorite moments on the island with your camera, and save them in your photo album! Features a story-driven narrative, special side quests, and other fun and whimsical events to discover! And so much more! Hi I’m DevFrog! Frog Island is my first ever video game! For the past 3 years, I’ve been sharing my development progress online, so if you want to see how Frog Island is made or want to share ideas, follow me on socials (the links are on the right side of the steam page).
