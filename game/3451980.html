About this gameFull description from Steam+
Soul's Remnant is a free to play 2D platformer MMORPG by Chaomoon, with a focus on social gameplay and exploration. Customize your skills with our classless system, team up with others, and defeat monsters, bosses, and dungeons together! Venture into the mysterious world of Soul's Remnant with friends or meet new ones along the way - just watch out when night falls! Game features: 2D platformer MMORPG with a cute and colorful pixel art graphics style Large world to explore with lots of monsters, bosses, dungeons, and secrets to find! Classless skill system and various stats to build your character with Lots of equipment of various rarities to find and upgrade Challenging dungeons and bosses for your (and your friends) to conquer Guild system with various perks for you and your guild to unlock Full crafting and gathering system, with many recipes to unlock and items to craft Comprehensive quest system that provides various fun missions to complete for rewards Befriend 10 villager NPCs and learn their stories! Each with their own unique personality Lots of player-driven and party-based content, such as parties, party grinding, housing, and player shops for a full player-driven economy! Full soundtrack of 40+ originally composed songs, all written, composed, and produced by Chaomoon Made by a small indie team! Hi, I'm Chaomoon! For 8 years (from 2016 to 2024), I developed Soul's Remnant alone as my passion project. Over years of hosting public playtests, the game developed a community of dedicated players, and grew to be something larger. Now, working towards bigger and better versions of the game, the team has expanded, with Rajah joining the team since 2025 to help with future updates and development! Developed with community in mind! Community feedback has always been a crucial part of Soul's Remnant's development, so feel free to join our community and leave feedback for us, as we continuously work towards making the game even better!
