About this gameFull description from Steam+
Journey back in time to where it all began! Kingdom Rush 6: Genesis brings a new chapter to the beloved tower defense saga, packed with the charm fans fell in love with. Jump into a portal in time to rewrite history and stop young Vez'nan before darkness takes hold! Return to the original Kingdom Rush lands, at a time when legends were just beginning. Recruit familiar faces, revisit iconic battlefields and build strategic defenses to outsmart enemy waves. 18 Handcrafted Campaign Stages Travel across three landscapes of early Linirea: the Citadel, the Stormcloud Depths and the far lands of Arcania. Each stage is handcrafted piece by piece, from its unique mechanics and paths to the hidden Easter eggs waiting in every corner, so no two battles ever feel the same. Standing between you and Linirea's future are 40+ enemies from 5 factions and 6 colossal bosses. 5 Ways to Conquer Every Stage Beyond the main campaign, every stage unlocks 5 game modes that change the rules entirely. Our favorite? Classic Rush. Go back in time to 2011 and take on each stage with the original Kingdom Rush arsenal. Switch things up: survive one devastating wave in Iron Challenge, outpace enemies in Blitz, lead four heroes with no spells in Hero Party, or trust only your towers and spells in Spell Rush. Lead Legends Old and New Reunite with beloved characters and meet new allies. Choose your dream team from 12 heroes, 15 towers and 9 spells, mixing classic favorites with brand-new faces to find the synergies that suit your strategy. Then customize your army like never before, with individual upgrade trees for every hero and spell, plus global tower upgrades you can retune between stages. 15 Years Holding the Line Fifteen years after the first creep marched down the road, Kingdom Rush returns to the lands where it all began, with bold new twists, epic fights and the signature Kingdom Rush humor. Along the way, you'll encounter surprising returns, memorable challenges and our most ambitious adventure yet. Made with love in Montevideo, Uruguay, down to the last detail. Our team keeps making new campaigns, stages, heroes, spells and towers coming this year and beyond. Defend the past to change the future! Game Features: 27 upgradeable characters: 15 Towers with special skills: Create your own strategy by combining different towers to get the most of their synergies. 12 Epic Heroes: Play with 2 heroes in each stage, upgrade them to unleash their powerful abilities and bolster your defense. Who knew heroes could be such close roommates? 9 Powerful Spells: Equip your spells for you to use in critical moments and turn the tide of the battle. 3 different landscapes across Linirea, to explore and alter the past. 18 Campaign stages, each offering their unique challenging hordes of enemies and their stage mechanics. Each one offers a new adventure! Game modes. Who doesn't love a good challenge? 50+ Achievements: show off your skills and unlock rewards! 40+ different enemies to test your mettle: 5 enemy races and 6 colossal boss fights of epic proportions. Who will prevail: you or the baddies? Easter eggs and Ironhide's usual humor. What's a game without hidden surprises and a good laugh?
