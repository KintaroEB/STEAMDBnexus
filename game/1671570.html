About this gameFull description from Steam+
This game contains real-life knowledge of plants 🌱 🍄 recipes 🥘 and herbalism 🍵! Harness the power of nature to rebuild your home in Out and About, a cozy foraging adventure! A storm has blown through the town of Portobello. To help this little community get back on its feet, you’ll need to learn all about the wild surrounding landscape. Learn to identify 92+ real life plants and fungi! From plants to mushrooms and seaweeds, foraged from different biomes and across different seasons. Currently covering spring and summer plants - more plants will be added with the 1.0 release in Q4 2026. Cook delicious recipes and brew herbal remedies to help the townsfolk. All 150+ recipes in the game are real, and we'll give you step-by-step instructions on how to make them at home! Explore an open natural world, from dense forests and wide grasslands to beautiful coastal beaches and wetlands. Customize your character and discover different hairstyles and outfits. Make friends with the charming characters around the town and become part of the community! ✨ Wishlist now! ✨
