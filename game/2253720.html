About this gameFull description from Steam+
Become a Business Tycoon Start with a small company and turn it into a global business empire. Build factories, stores, farms, research centers and logistics networks. Produce goods, negotiate contracts, analyze markets and compete with thousands of other players in a living player-driven economy. Player-Driven Economy Buy and sell products in a live marketplace where prices are determined by real player activity. Deep Supply Chains Transform raw materials into advanced products through complex production chains spanning multiple industries. Multiple Industries Operate in agriculture, energy, retail, fashion, automotive, aerospace, restaurants and many more sectors. Multiplayer Trading Build long-term partnerships, negotiate contracts and create your own business network. Real Business Decisions Analyze profits, optimize production, manage cash flow and respond to changing market conditions.
