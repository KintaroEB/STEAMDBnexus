About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to the World of Puzzle Pirates! Puzzle Pirates is a complete pirate world powered by puzzles.  Every activity in Puzzle Pirates is a unique challenge: Try your hand at sailing, gunning, hauling treasure, and many more as you voyage across a vast ocean filled with treasure, mysterious Brigand Kings, and monsters. Earn booty and start trading, build a trading empire, and end up governor of your own island! Sail the seas with your friends and pillage brigands and merchants aboard your very own pirate ship! Play more than 20 puzzles and mini-games, representing various piratey exploits, from swordfighting and sailing to shipbuilding, foraging, and digging up buried treasure. Explore a living world filled with thousands of other players! Hunt dangerous Sea Monsters and voyage to lost treasure-filled realms, such as the Haunted Seas or the Cursed Isles! Customize your pirate with a bounty of different clothing items and equipment! Run your own shop and produce weapons, ships and other goods for sale. Band crews together under a mighty “Pirate Flag” and become governor of a pirate haven. Puzzle Pirates is free to play, no subscription is required to enjoy everything the game has to offer!
