About this gameFull description from Steam+
Saleblazers is an adventure shopkeeping simulator. It's up to you to decide how you want to thrive in this world! Cook and Eat Food to Become Stronger Combine ingredients to cook amazing meals to help you on your journey. Go Fishing for New Stuff Travel the lands and find a variety of fishing spots to catch the biggest fish... and sometime rare items! Sell Anything at your Shop If you pick it up, you can sell it! Use Everything as a Weapon Swords, guns, spells... or chairs!? Survive... Use your skills to survive a deadly island full of human factions and deadly monsters. Make sure you loot your enemies! KEY FEATURES Play solo or bring up to 7 friends Over a thousand cool items to discover with hundreds of powerful attributes Create interesting builds that fit your playstyle through player stats, equipment, and items An immersive world with villages, quests, factions, and police Character classes that offer unique experiences Build and run multiple shops across the island Hire employees Dedicated server (see Steam Community guide for details) Verified on Steam Deck!
