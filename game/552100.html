About this gameFull description from Steam+
In Brick Rigs, you can become anything: a firefighter racing to the scene, a pilot in intense aerial combat, or a police officer in a high‑speed chase. Build, play, experiment, destroy. You decide what, and how, you want to play. The only rule is that it has to be fun. Create your own vehicles, machines, creatures, or buildings. In the Brick Rigs Editor, there are no limits to your imagination. Ever dreamed of a flying submarine? With a little patience, you can build almost anything and try it out directly in the game. The powerful editor lets you create detailed models with realistic, functional mechanics. No patience to build your own vehicle? No problem. Ambitious Brick Rigs players have already filled the Workshop with a whole universe of fantastic builds. From detailed minivans to giant transport planes and entire skyscrapers, there's something for everyone. Discover it all now in the Steam Workshop. Yes, building your own vehicles is really fun. But let's be honest — destroying them is just as much fun. With dynamic destruction physics, every crash looks especially spectacular. See how far you can make those bricks fly. Brick Rigs has a large, passionate community. Play with friends, team up, or experiment on your own. From roleplay servers and high-speed chases to massive multiplayer chaos, players are constantly creating new ways to play. Anything is possible in Brick Rigs — as long as you're having fun! Bob is the face of Brick Rigs. He's been there from the very beginning and has experienced a lot: adventures, disasters, parties, and much more. And what can Bob actually do? Pretty much nothing. But he's a really lovable guy.
