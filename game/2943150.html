About this gameFull description from Steam+
SNØ is a simple game about downhill skiing. Carve your way down procedurally generated mountainscapes and try to score as many points as possible by pulling off high-risk proximity maneuvers and tricks. Mastering the controls and terrain is key to achieving high scores, making every run a test of skill and flow. SNØ offers endless procedural mountains to explore. Choose whether you want to chase the top spots on the leaderboards, or just enjoy the ride in Zen mode. Message from the creators: SNØ is made in Norway by two people. We designed it to capture the essence of downhill skiing in a minimalistic and playful way. SNØ is perfect for short gameplay sessions, focusing on rewarding gameplay systems, instant action and smooth controls. Special message to all Steamdeck users: We have optimized SNØ for the Steamdeck. The game runs at 60-90 FPS on High quality settings. Enjoy. Things you will find in this game Fast and smooth freeriding gameplay Endless procedurally generated mountains Three ride styles: Skis, Snowboard and Sit-ski Countless skins requiring skill to unlock Share and revisit mountains by seed Full keyboard and gamepad support Optional Zen-mode - No scoring, just you and the mountain Online global and friends leaderboards Visit top scoring mountains directly from the leaderboards First person, third person and drone camera modes Photo mode Things you will not find Online multiplayer Prepped slopes Loading times
