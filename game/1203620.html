About this gameFull description from Steam+
RISE AS A FLAMEBORN You awaken as the last hope of a fallen kingdom. Forge your hero through a deep skill tree - armored juggernaut, deadly ranger, devastating battlemage - or blend them into a playstyle entirely your own. EXPLORE A VAST WORLD OF WILD BEAUTY Discover breathtaking landscapes and the secrets of a fallen civilization. Journey through lush forests, sun-scorched dunes, snow-capped peaks, and ruins swallowed by the Shroud. Swing across chasms with your grappling hook, glide from dizzying heights, and dive beneath the waters to reach places no road will ever lead to. SURVIVE AND GROW Live off the land - hunt, farm, fish, and scavenge rare materials. Rescue survivors from the Shroud, and your lonely hut grows into a bustling settlement of craftspeople and townsfolk, unlocking new recipes and gear. Survival here rewards preparation and creativity. FIGHT YOUR WAY Grow strong enough to face what hunts you. Wield greatswords, channel devastating magic, specialize deeply or mix archetypes through skill-based combat and a talent system that is both easy to pick up and deep to master. BUILD WITHOUT LIMITS Claim your place in Embervale - if you can dream it, you can build it. Raise towering fortresses, carve homes into mountainsides, reshape the terrain, and furnish every room down to the smallest detail. Voxel-based building gives you extraordinary freedom to make this world your home. CONQUER THE SHROUD Take the fight back to the enemy. Descend into the deadly fog that threatens all life. Face its most fearsome creatures, claim materials found nowhere else, and free Embervale from the Shroud's grip. FORGE YOUR LEGEND - SOLO OR WITH FRIENDS Walk this path alone or in co-op with up to 16 players. Fight, build, farm, or explore: every role matters in rebuilding the kingdom. DISCOVER ENDLESS ADVENTURES When the campaign ends, your story doesn't. Dive into worlds and dungeons created by millions of players, or share your own through our Adventure Sharing feature.
