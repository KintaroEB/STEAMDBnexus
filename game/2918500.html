About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to our Discord channel: haAuNAhBsg Sprawl out like a starfish on the sun-baked beach and just zone out. Soak up the warm rays and listen to the rhythmic lull of the waves. Today, tomorrow, or even all day long—if you want to, you don't have to lift a single finger. Cruise through fragrant flower fields or just lounge on a bouncy mushroom for hours. There are no deadlines, no timers, and absolutely no rush. Your time here is yours to spend on pure, unadulterated joy. Plant a seed, get something bizarre! You’ll be harvesting everything from vibrant blooms to truly derpy crops. They’re weird, they’re quirky, and honestly? They’re impossible not to love. Every nook and cranny of the island is packed with cheeky secrets. Rummage through every corner to collect useless but adorable toys. Because let’s be real—being cute and happy is the only thing that matters. Bring your best friends along for the ride! Sweep them right out the door with a broom, or drag them into the water for a dip. Even the simplest moments become memories you'll treasure when you're together!
