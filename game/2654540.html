About this gameFull description from Steam+
Story background The story of Xiantu starts from an unnamed island. You, with exceptional talent, are originally cynical and neglectful of cultivation. Entrusted by the immortal, you embarked on the path of cultivating immortals to the immortal world. Exploring in the wilderness of Zezhou, gradually discovering the truth of the "Immortal Path". Explore the Continent The path of immortality is long, and you have left the nameless island and set foot on the barren continent. Explore the mainland, discover other sectarian forces, and advance the development of the story. Collect various resources for self cultivation and building a sect. Encounter various random events and obtain unexpected treasures. Simulated operation While exploring, you will also manage your own sect. Collect reputation and sect resources to upgrade and build the sect. Use different sect buildings to continuously produce various required props. The powerful sect will assist you in going further in the path of immortality. Build a team As you move forward on the path of immortality, you will encounter many characters. Different characters have different talents, professions, and skills. Recruit the characters you need and freely form your team. A reasonable combination of lineups can unleash greater power. Role development Even characters from the same profession may end up being vastly different. Each character can choose one of six different paths. Each character can practice different techniques and obtain different improvements. Special: The protagonist can freely combine skills from various professions! Ascending the Fairy Realm After your feathering and soaring, you will head towards the fairyland at the end of the world. Explore within the realm of immortality and discover the truth about the 'immortal path'. The fairyland is far from the end, there is still a lot of content waiting for you to explore
