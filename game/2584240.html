About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to the Moonlight Peaks: Decorating Demo Experience life as a vampire in the enchanting town of Moonlight Peaks! In this heartwarming life-sim, you'll embark on a journey to prove to your skeptical father, Count Dracula, that even the undead can lead lives filled with compassion. Key Features: 🏰 Immersive Decoration: Dive into the art of interior design as you transform your vampire abode. Choose from a range of exquisite furniture, decorations, and themes to create a haven that reflects your unique style. 🎨 Personal Expression: Express your creativity and identity through your home decor. Every choice you make tells a story, making your dwelling a reflection of your journey and growth. 🍃 Foraging and Crafting: Explore the enchanting landscapes around Moonlight Peaks to gather resources for crafting unique decorations. Let the world inspire your designs. 🧪 Potions and Spells: Unlock the mysteries of ancient potions and spells. Use your newfound skills to enhance your surroundings and bring an extra touch of magic to your home. 🕰️ Limited-Time Preview: Enjoy a sneak peek into the enchanting world of Moonlight Peaks with a special decorating demo. Immerse yourself in the magic and possibilities that lie ahead.
