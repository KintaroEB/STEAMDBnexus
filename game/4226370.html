About this gameFull description from Steam+
My Text Immortal World This first commercial text-based immortal cultivation online game is crafted by two independent developers. We cherish the pure gaming experience of classic online games from the early millennium era, and have polished this masterpiece of immortal cultivation centered on high freedom, true randomness, and pure loot drops. It features no AFK gameplay, no offline solo mode, and fully online interaction. Limited by development resources, the game still has many areas awaiting optimization. We sincerely welcome every player’s feedback, and wish to refine this original vision of immortal cultivation together with all fellow cultivators. We’ve also prepared an official Discord community. Core Immortal Cultivation Experience: Boundless Freedom Master online cultivation, spirit herb planting, and pill refining. Earn Sect Contribution by completing sect quests and submitting rare treasures, then exchange it for a vast collection of cultivation manuals and skills. In your spare time, explore trial zones, challenge world bosses, venture into secret realms, conquer the Star Chessboard, chat and duel with fellow immortals, or compete in sect tournaments to win fame and abundant rewards! Four Unique Sects, Distinct Cultivation Paths Choose from four exclusive sects, each with a unique innate heart method and tailored cultivation direction—no restrictions on sect selection, pick your perfect immortal path freely: Sky Sword Sect: Specialized in sword-based skills with 30% armor penetration. Innate heart method Maxed Sword of the Enlightened Heart boosts physical/magical critical strike chance by 30% and reduces enemies' physical/magical defense by 45%. Scarlet Cloud Sect: Ideal for fire/lightning skills. Fire skills deal pure magical damage with stackable burn effects; lightning skills blend physical and magical damage with dominant crowd control. Innate heart method Maxed Scarlet Flame Thunder Tactic grants 150 Fire/Lightning Attribute and 150 Fire/Lightning Resistance. Flowing Moon Sect: The top choice for water/earth skills. Water skills scale damage with True Essence, focusing on slow, healing and buffs; earth skills scale damage with your own defense—the higher your defense, the deadlier your attacks. Innate heart method Maxed Flowing Moon Scripture provides 150 Water/Earth Attribute and 150 Water/Earth Resistance. Spirit Treasure Hall: Specialized in enhancing magical treasures. Innate heart method Maxed Spirit Treasure Control Scripture increases magical treasure ATK/DEF by 20% and True Essence regeneration by 1%, while boosting treasure usage times. No restrictions on skill selection—create your personalized build freely. Core Sect Gameplay: Immersive Immortal Daily Life Sect Main Hall: Donate resources to upgrade your sect and gain personal contribution, then rise through the ranks from a handyman disciple to a core disciple. Complete quests from the Examination Elder for more contribution, and compete in Outer/Inner/Elite Sect Tournaments to defeat rivals and claim generous ranking rewards. Sect Treasure Shop: Exchange Sect Contribution for basic cultivation manuals, skills, herbs and pills, or purchase functional items and stylish costumes with Immortal Jade (premium currency). Sect Alchemy Room: Refine all kinds of pills by unlocking corresponding pill recipes and upgrading your alchemy level—all alchemy materials are farmable in-game. Sect Cave Abode: Your exclusive spot for online meditation and cultivation. Upgrade the cave to speed up cultivation progress (requires matching sect rank). For time-strapped players, the 100x Speed Secret Realm is available for fast cultivation, and you can claim free Secret Realm Tokens daily based on your sect rank. Sect Trial Zones: Unlock trial zones by reaching specific cultivation realms. Explore via grid-flipping to collect spirit stones, equipment/treasure shards and heavenly materials. Encounter demonic beasts, defeat them and loot rare gear and items. Sect Spirit Plantation: Every disciple starts with 10 spirit fields, with more unlocked as your sect rank rises. Upgrade spirit fields to plant higher-tier spirit herbs and cultivate your own alchemy materials for self-sufficiency. Equipment & Magical Treasures: Endless Build Customization Equipment System: Realm-Aligned, Quality-Progression Equipment is divided into 10 tiers, corresponding to the 10 major cultivation realms—each tier has Early/Mid/Late stages for different cultivation levels. All tiers feature 12 pieces of gear (6 weapons, 6 armors), each with 5 quality ranks: Low, Mid, High, Mystic and Heavenly. Every piece of equipment has base attributes (upgradeable) and affix attributes (reforgeable)—zero build restrictions. Earth skill players can equip shields to stack defense and maximize damage; sword skill players can choose swords, broadswords or spears for burst damage. Customize your gear fully to match your cultivation path. Magical Treasure System: Offense & Defense, Auto-Combat Magical treasures share the same tier (10) and quality (4) system as equipment, with upgrade and reforge options. Unlock and use them by reaching the required cultivation realm (consumes True Essence in combat). Treasures are split into Offensive and Defensive: Offensive treasures auto-attack enemies by consuming True Essence at set intervals—solo all foes with powerful offensive treasures without manual operation. Defensive treasures generate a protective shield for you—enemies can’t break through with a strong enough shield. Combine offensive and defensive treasures to forge your unique immortal combat power. Cultivation Manuals & Skills: Unlimited Free Combination Unlock dozens of heart methods and active skills in-game, all divided into 5 tiers (higher tiers = stronger effects). Zero restrictions on learning—no class or sect limits, build your character freely! Heart Methods: Passive effects that take effect immediately after learning, boosting your power permanently. Active Skills: Equip to use in combat (6 skills max per character). Categorized by attribute: Sword (Physical), Fire (Magical), Lightning (CC), Earth (Defense), Water (Healing & Support). Coordinate with teammates in party play for seamless battles. All skills are farmable via in-game systems, while rare high-tier skills drop randomly from world bosses—your luck decides everything. Free Trading, Real Player Economy The game features an Auction House with unlimited free trading—all materials, items, equipment, treasures and skills can be listed for sale, enabling true player-driven economy and farming for profit! Loot rare Mystic-tier treasures/equipment or Heavenly/God-tier skills from boss fights and secret realms, list them on the Auction House for a large amount of Spirit Crystals (in-game currency), which can also be freely traded on the market. Your luck and hard work both pay off! Fair Play Notice: To maintain a fair trading environment and protect players' legitimate interests, large-scale profit-making with bot/ studio accounts is strictly prohibited. Violators will face permanent account ban without warning. Diverse Gameplay, Rich Immortal Journey Beyond the core cultivation gameplay, the game offers a wealth of fun features: real-time chat, player duels, popularity & charm ratings, exclusive titles, customizable costumes, server-wide leaderboards, spirit pattern forging, secret realm exploration and more. Discover endless immortal cultivation fun in the game yourself! Core Game Declarations This is a fully online text-based MMORPG—no AFK auto-play or offline single-player mode, online play required at all times. All in-game items, equipment, skills and treasures are 100% random drops—no manual interference, no pity system, luck is everything. Our development team will continuously collect player feedback to optimize game content and experience. Thank you for your support and understanding!
