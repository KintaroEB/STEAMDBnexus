About this gameFull description from Steam+
Build your city: Global City's graphics stand out - be amazed by the amazing appearance of your residential neighborhoods, enormous malls and tall buildings. Collect resources: Everything you need to build a city is right under your feet. Well, buried in the ground, actually! Collect natural resources and raw materials for producing building supplies, prepare lumber and produce plastic. Basically, you can collect or create all the resources your city needs. Don't forget the trade you can do in Global City, too! Reach new heights in building and trading! Complete tasks, collect taxes and grow the population: Complete fun tasks and get excellent rewards that help you progress through the game's story. The tax system lets you earn even more. It's simple: the higher your city's population, the more taxes you can collect! Filling the budget lets you further develop your city and build new projects. Chat with friends: City building is more fun with friends. So, you'll be excited to have the chance to build a friend community, where you can chat with each other, swap resources and give each other a helping hand. Co-op competitions and great prizes add to the fun! ● Complete fun quests based on your city's growth ● Build cool buildings and a wide variety of sites ● Earn achievements and rewards ● Develop everything, from agriculture to space flight ● Build the city YOU want!
