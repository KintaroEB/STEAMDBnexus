About this gameFull description from Steam+
World of Warships: Legends—the renowned console naval shooter, now on PC. FAST BATTLES AND BIG GUNS In World of Warships: Legends, fast-paced action blends with tactical depth. Victory is earned through strategic play, smart positioning, and coordinated teamwork. As a Captain, you can choose from more than 600 historically inspired warships, including legendary vessels from WWII like Yamato, USS Laffey, USS Texas, and many more. YOUR NAVAL PLAYSTYLE The pace of battle is yours to control, with four ship types that play very differently. Destroyers rely on speed and stealth, battleships on armor and firepower, and cruisers on versatility, while aircraft carriers influence the fight from above, directing air squadrons to project power anywhere on the map. With hundreds of warships from multiple nations—including the USA, UK, Japan, Germany, Italy, and the USSR—there’s always a ship to match your playstyle. ELITE LEADERSHIP Lead your fleet with over 150 historical and fictional Commanders. Each one features a unique skill tree, allowing you to create specialized builds that fit your ship and your playstyle. If you want a different look, use Commander guises with unique visuals and voiceovers. Customize your Commanders and find the perfect combination to win. UPDATES & MODES We release major content updates almost every month to ensure there is always something new to explore. Discover everything from powerful new ships and Commanders to unique game modes, ranging from casual PvE events to intense PvP battles. Take part in special collaborations with iconic franchises such as Star Trek and Azur Lane, bringing themed ships, Commanders, and cosmetic content into the game. Complete mission chains, progress through the battle pass, and experience epic War Tales—there is always fresh content for every Captain to enjoy. CROSS-PROGRESSION With cross-progression, you can link your console or mobile account to Steam without losing progress. Team up with friends on PC, console, or mobile and dominate the high seas together. Some account linking rules may apply—check the FAQ in the community discussions for details. LEARN. ADAPT. PREVAIL. Legends is beginner-friendly, but prepare for a journey with true tactical depth. While the basics are easy to learn, the game offers many levels of strategy for those who want to dive deep. If you have questions, our in-game wiki, video tutorials, and community channels provide all the answers you need. Play exactly how you want with full support for keyboard, gamepad, and handheld devices, including Steam Deck.
