About this gameFull description from Steam+
Experience the challenge of cooking, but now with only one arm! Grab a friend and you'll have two arms in total. One-armed cook is a chaotic co-op cooking game for up to 4 people. Work as a team or cook on your own, set your own pace, and don't let the stress of a full restaurant catch up to you. The goofy physics-based aspect of the game gives one-armed cook a new twist. Take orders from customers With many different dishes for the customers to order you will never run out of something to do. Prepare and cook your food Simple and intuitive recipes make the experience much more fluent But should you ever be in doubt, you can always check the recipe book Mess around. have fun with friends and create chaos with the physics
