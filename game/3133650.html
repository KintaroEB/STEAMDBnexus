About this gameFull description from Steam+
Build up a fantasy city and fill it with hand-drawn characters, each with their own unique personalities and skills. Send them on quests to expand your influence and clear out nearby monster dens that threaten your city. Help them grow and evolve, all in the pursuit of gold! Create a thriving city around monster-filled dungeons. Bring in wealth from exploring and dungeon delving, then use it to shape the town to your wishes. Add markets, taverns, gambling halls, and guilds to attract even more heroes to fight for your cause! Gold-thirsty adventurers will come from all corners of the world. Humans, beasts, mothmen, the undead, and more may join your cause. Recruit them all, or only your favourites! These adventurers won’t work for free, their loyalty lies with gold! You’ll have to recruit them with promises of gold and glory, then send them on quests to kill, defend, explore, and more. Your pet may be filled with divine power, but it still needs guidance. Pet, brush, and discipline it so it can grow, evolve, and learn powerful spells! Being near dungeons provides easy access to loot—but it also puts your kingdom at risk of monster attacks. Cast spells to help your heroes defend their home, level up, and unlock powerful new gear and abilities as your city continues to grow! WISHLIST NOW!
