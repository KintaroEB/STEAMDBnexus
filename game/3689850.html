About this gameFull description from Steam+
In a chaotic Middle-earth, war’s shadow looms over the land, people retreat into castles. A powerful dark sorcerer has opened the Gates of Hell, summoning a demonic legion. The balance of magic is crumbling, ancient walls trembling under the infernal onslaught. Knights! Draw your swords, defend the castle together! -A New Height in Puzzle Tower Defense More than a battle of defense and attack, it’s a clash of courage and strategy. Heroes and towers fuse seamlessly, offering fresh tactical combos and a thrilling combat experience, micro-control RTS hero, strategize like a master, and then embrace the thrill of heroism. -Expand Territory, build Roads Expand Territory, then build Roads, road cards help you controlling the enemy’s path to the castle. Tip: Within your tower's range, the longer the enemy's path, the higher your chances of success. -Free RTS Battles on the World Map On a world map, unleash RTS (Real-Time Strategy) battles, leveraging the unique terrain features of each vast region for strategic layouts. Seize resources, engage in real-time combat with countless players, and dominate the battlefield! -Unite with Players Worldwide A 3D planetary map hosts players across the globe. Unleash your social savvy, form alliances, grow stronger, and conquer the planet together!
