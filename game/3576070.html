About this gameFull description from Steam+
From Legacy To Legend For 20 years, Sudden Attack has been cherished by players, and now we are embarking on the new phase of our endless journey. Step into the intense battles of Sudden Attack Zero Point, a free-to-play FPS that delivers a fast-paced shooting experience. Experience diverse combat styles through a distinctive gun customization system. Dominate classic bomb defusal missions with your tactics, and stay tuned for more modes coming soon. Don't forget to explore the Black Market to trade items that you earned through gameplay and collect the items you need for your arsenal. Tailor your weapons to match your style and team up with friends to secure victory! Fast-Paced Combat Nowadays, FPS games can often be overwhelming and time-consuming. We understand that not everyone can indulge in all-day gaming or wander around in an unnecessarily large map only to be eliminated in seconds. Sudden Attack Zero Point offers quick, action-packed battles, allowing you to enjoy intense gameplay and fully embrace the thrill of combat without unnecessary delays. Customization For Victory Unleash your creativity with an extensive weapon customization system, letting you build the perfect gun for your playstyle! Customize various gun attachments to your liking, including weapon recoil, reload speed, appearance and more. Through gun customization, even the same weapon can offer a unique combat experience, allowing you to enjoy diverse and exciting battles. Stand out on the battlefield with a weapon that's truly yours! Classic Tactical Experience Engage in strategic combat focused on bomb defusal modes, where teamwork and tactics are essential for outsmarting your opponents. Each match demands precise coordination and communication to ensure victory. In addition to these thrilling bomb defusal modes, a variety of other game modes await. Strategize with your team and dominate your enemies! Trade & Gear Up! Participate in the Black Market, where you can trade weapon skins, attachments, and other items that you earned in the game with fellow players. Upgrade your weapons and customize your setups to suit your preferences, giving you a competitive edge. With the right modifications, you can truly dominate the battlefield with your favorite weapon!
