About this gameFull description from Steam+
Dinoblade is an intense action RPG set in a prehistoric world forever changed by a cataclysmic event, one that redefined the very nature of Dinosaurs. Step into the role of a young Spinosaurus, wielding a colossal Great Sword forged from ancient power. Confront rival dinosaurs, some armed with brutal weapons of their own, all in a desperate fight to prevent a looming extinction. Venture across a sprawling world filled with awe-inspiring biomes, from dry canyons to dense, mist-shrouded jungles. Each region is ruled by a ferocious Alpha, an apex predator who guards the path forward. Defeat them, or be devoured. Every victory unlocks strength. Master new skills, unleash devastating abilities, and forge your own combat style. Chain together powerful combos, time your dodges, rolls, and parries, and discover legendary weapons hidden across the lands. At the heart of this savage world lies a mystery — one that could save your species, or doom them forever. Rise as a Legend, conquer the wilds, and decide the fate of your home. Become the blade and rewrite history before your world is lost to the shadow of extinction. Conquer the Challenge: Defeat the final boss to unlock the ultimate Boss Rush Mode
