About this gameFull description from Steam+
The Manager The Manager is the office's ultimate authority figure, tasked with ensuring productivity and firing the Slackers hiding among the Specialists. Slackers The Slacker's goal is to goof off without getting caught. Whether it’s playing Minesweeper, or taking a dump, Slackers must blend in to avoid the Manager’s wrath. Specialists Specialists are the backbone of the company, assigned various tasks that keep the office running smoothly. But even the hardest workers know that productivity sometimes requires a coffee break. Gameplay Mechanics Objective-Based Gameplay: Specialists are tasked with completing objectives to contribute to the company's success, whereas Slackers engage in activities to appear busy while avoiding actual work. Social Deduction and Strategy: Players utilize proximity voice chat for real-time discussions, alliances, and accusations, making social interactions crucial to gameplay. Managerial Oversight: The Manager must observe, deduce, and decide who to fire, with each choice potentially altering the game's balance. Experience the Office Like Never Before:
