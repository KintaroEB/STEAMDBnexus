About this gameFull description from Steam+
English localization only available in Demo. Dynasty crumbles. The land fractures. You're one of many orphans, taken in by distant kin. On reaching adulthood, you become a constable, whether for survival or ambition. Long Knives is a low-fantasy wuxia text-based adventure with TTRPG mechanics and deep narrative branching. Key Features TTRPG-inspired. Second-person narrative. Customize attributes. Choose pre-orphan background. Allocate points. Proficiencies affect skill checks. Uses an 18-sided die, not D20. Visible and hidden checks. Reload anytime. Personalized Experience Every line of dialogue is your choice. Story branches respond to hidden variables.
