About this gameFull description from Steam+
Welcome to Tiana's Fishing Diary—a cozy desktop idle game! Explore seven distinct maps, each with its own style and charm. Collect fish with unique skills, run your aquarium, and combine their abilities strategically to maximize profits. Upgrades Seek out skilled trainers to level up your fishing abilities and unlock the rarest catches. Maps Discover seven distinct maps, each with its own style and charm. Collection Fill your fish codex! Collect 150+ species ranging from Common all the way to Legendary. Manage Your Aquarium Fill your aquarium with your catches and watch the gold roll in. Mix and match fish skills to maximize your earnings! Customization Dress up your character with tons of hairstyles, clothes, and fishing rods. Put together your perfect style! Modes Passive Mode: You can idle the game and wait for fish to bite automatically. Active Mode: You can actively consume stamina to activate the "All-Out Moment" state and enter manual control mode. If your hand speed is fast and accurate, you can catch a large number of fish in a short time.
