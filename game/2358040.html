About this gameFull description from Steam+
Magisterium: Fantasy Craft is a third-person single and co-op medieval fantasy life simulator with survival and RPG elements. In Fantasy Craft you take a part of the main character who lost his father and had to move to his uncle to the village of Lovelydale to start a new adult life. Learn all the crafts and become a master in alchemy, potion-making, blacksmithing, carpentry, hunting, fishing, cooking, tailoring. You will meet your old friends and get acquainted with new people in the village. You can explore magnificent places full of mysteries. Your incredible journey to the fantasy world starts here. CUT DOWN TREES AND MINE MINERAL RESOURCES To build a house, different workbenches and craft various items you should chop trees, mine mineral resources. BUILDING Build your-self a nice comfortable house, furnish it and place a lot of workbenches there. HUNT, FISH To survive in this wonderful fantasy world, you will have to hunt and go fishing. Those animals are extremely crafty and dangerous. MAPS AND COMPASS READING Feel a real immersion into the survival game! There were no google maps in ancient times😊. So, there will be no markers on the maps you have to learn how to use compass and read maps. Buy a compass and various maps from traders. COOK Fell like a real chef and go cook some delicious sophisticated food to get all sorts of buffs. GARDEN We heard that failure of crops led to the rise in prices for fruits and vegetables. It will be better to do gardening to get enough vegetables and fruits for cooking and winery. WINERY You can’t imagine a good dinner without a bottle of a nice wine in the end of a hard-working day. So maybe it’s time to build your own winery to craft some wine, rum, grog or beer? They say that this business pays off nicely😉. POTION-MAKING If you want to increase your health, stamina, weight, make woodcutting and mine-digging faster and more efficient, increase your critical damage and your armor - you should master the art of potion-making. CHALLENGING QUESTS Interact with diverse NPC, implementing various quests and tasks in order to get free blueprints, money and XP. TRADE WITH NPCs You can earn money by crafting things and selling it to NPC or traders on the market. BLACKSMITHING As long as you stay in the village or on your land you are in no danger, but exploring new enchanted locations full of dangerous species will require excellent weapons. Take a look at blacksmith’s goods and chose the best blueprints of a weapon to fight the monsters. TAILORING Don’t tell me you are going to explore the world without a good armor! Pay a visit to the best tailor in the village, I saw a lot of blueprints of some fine clothing to give you great protection in a combat. GO TREASURE HUNTING If you want adventures, you can go treasure hunting to magnificent secret places, but you have to fight the bizarre monsters, protecting those treasures. LOCKPICKING All the containers like chests and cupboards, that have valuable things inside like coins, lockpicks, potions, expensive tableware, food etc., are locked, make sure you buy a lot of lockpicks when going on missions into dangerous blocked areas. All locked containers have 3 difficulty levels: beginner, intermediate and master. Good luck with lockpicking 😊. Features in the game: • Beautiful picturesque and dangerous open world locations. • Alternation of day and night and weather conditions changes. • The ability to switch off the night cycle and set your hunger and thirst parameters to the minimum in the advanced settings. • Interactive environment that gives you the opportunity to chop trees, collect sticks, stones, to mine ores and minerals, pick up plants, loot containers, lockpick chests and cabinets with valuable items and so on. • A few variants of house building. • Researching system helps you to open new blueprints in the workbenches, you should examine plants, berries and mushrooms in the researching table to get the scrolls with recipes for future potions. • Brewing various potions increasing health, strength, armor, weight, crit and a lot more. • Massive quantity of blueprints in the game that involves a lot of craft. • Craft various equipment, tools, weapons, amour, furniture and so on. • Interesting farming system that gives you an opportunity to cook more complex dishes. • Interaction with diverse wild animals and mysterious monsters. • Multiple fishing zones with common and rare fish. • Levelling up character’s skills. • Interaction with NPCs including quests and trade. • Balanced trading and economy system. • A lot of quests to make loads of money. • A possibility to expand your land by buying the near-by plots of land. • A possibility to set complex equipment in order to mine iron, coal and copper on your land. • A variety of well-protected caves with essential and rare resources.
