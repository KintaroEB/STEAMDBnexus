About this gameFull description from Steam+
Skillshot City is a chaotic battle royale with roguelite skill builds and infinite revives. Loot, shoot, cast and drive through a GTA-style city to level up wild skills and create clever combos on the fly. If you die, keep fighting outside the circle and try to revive before the final showdown! There are no queues and no downtime. Just non-stop action. Play solos, duos, or trios with friends and climb the ranks! DRAFT GODLIKE SKILL SYNERGIES Fight for skill points to level up fast. Each level up gives you a choice of 2 randomly generated skills. Do you take Invisibility or Bouncing Bullets? Teleportation or Regeneration? Like a roguelite this makes every match play different. You need to think on your feet to combo skills together and create the most broken build possible before the final showdown! UNLOCK SKILLS & NEW CLASSES Unlock new abilities between matches. There are over 100 unique skills, classes, and round modifers to unlock. Once unlocked you can equip skills to your build to make them appear more often in-game. Join the community to theorycraft thousands of possible skill combos! FARM COPS, GANGS, MISSIONS & MORE Inspired by MOBAs, the faster you farm the stronger you become. You can do missions, win supply drops, fight gangs, explore for rare loot, and if you're feeling risky - aggro the cops. But remember, the circle is shrinking and death will set you back a lot. You have to balance risk with reward to level up as much as possible while still surviving to the final showdown. 3 ULTRA FAST ROUNDS While other BRs have one long round, Skillshot City has 3 ultra-fast rounds - with a high-stakes showdown every 5 minutes. The winners of rounds 1 and 2 get to choose unique modifiers that affect the rest of the match, and the winner of round 3 wins the game! ZOMBIE REVIVALS Your skill build progresses from round to round, and if you die you can keep playing too. You'll play as a zombie and can loot for the next round - or hunt down a survivor to revive. No queueing time, no getting booted to the main menu, just pure refined non-stop action. OTHER COOL FEATURES A singleplayer / coop survival mode with leaderboards and custom difficulty modifiers Choose from 8 classes, each with a unique playstyle and strategy Design skill builds that adjust the pool of skills you'll choose from in-game Crazy challenges, groovy cosmetics, and stats tracking to show off your favorite skills Small download (under 1GB) and instant join times mean you can start playing in minutes!
