About this gameFull description from Steam+
Prepare for your grand adventure, Arisen! In this app, you can create your characters for Dragon's Dogma 2. Customize the looks of your main character, the Arisen, and the looks and inclination of your companion, your Main Pawn. You can store up to 5 characters of each, the Arisen and your Main Pawn respectively. The character data created in this app can be transferred to the base game of Dragon's Dogma 2, available separately. In the main game there will be additional graphic options available.
