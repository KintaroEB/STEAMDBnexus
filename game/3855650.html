About this gameFull description from Steam+
The game is set in the First General Hospital of Yuanwang City, located in the eastern region of a certain country. Two players can take on the roles of the Lin siblings and experience a series of mysterious events in this hospital. The gameplay is designed to fully accommodate dual-player mechanics, featuring encounters such as monster chases, mind-bending puzzles, endless corridors that require both players to gather different clues to proceed, escape rooms, and thrilling dual-player boss battles! Through conversations with other individuals (or creatures) in the hospital, as well as textual and video content, uncover the secrets hidden within this hospital! The Lin siblings On their 16th birthday, their mother was brutally murdered. While fleeing toward the subway, they were caught in a mysterious "Black Flame Incident" and fell unconscious on the spot. When they awoke, they found themselves in an eerily familiar hospital. Rich puzzles and battles More thrilling experiences await you and your friend to unlock together~!
