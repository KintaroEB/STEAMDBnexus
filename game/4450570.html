About this gameFull description from Steam+
This Is Golf is is first-person golf game where you use the mouse to shape the perfect shot. Scout the terrain Although the shot happens in first person, you can walk around in third person to see what the hole looks like or to spectate your friend's "crazy new stinger". Clubs You can pick from a wide variety of clubs, from the 5 iron to the 9 iron, pitching wedge, driver or putter, and they all have the own purpose. You will learn to choose right the more you hit with them. Procedural holes Every hole is procedurally generated. Multiplayer You can play in lobbies of up to 8 players.
