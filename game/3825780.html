About this gameFull description from Steam+
WonderKing is a side-scrolling MMORPG that seamlessly blends classic 2D nostalgia with modern combat systems. Experience the thrill of active manual combat with explosive combo actions, while enjoying the ultimate convenience of idle progression when you are away. From immersive story-driven scenarios tailored for solo adventurers to massive instance dungeons and epic boss raids requiring seamless teamwork—the world of WonderKing is filled with endless content. Packed with impactful combat and deep progression systems hidden beneath a friendly, emotional art style, your legendary journey begins right now in the world of WonderKing! 1. Diverse Class System WonderKing features a wide array of distinct classes, each possessing unique characteristics and tactical roles on the battlefield. Start your journey by choosing one of the four base classes—Swordsman, Mage, Archer, or Thief. As your character grows, unlock advanced advancements through the Class Change system, revealing powerful skill sets that allow you to fully customize your preferred combat style. 2. Epic Boss Raids with Allies Form the ultimate party, combine class-specific synergy skills, and coordinate with your allies to conquer formidable bosses. Defeating these powerful foes rewards you with top-tier equipment and valuable loot essential for boosting your character's stats. 3. Tailored Progression & Raid Guides Never lose your way with our optimized content guide, perfectly tailored to your current level and Combat Power (CP). From recommending the best hunting grounds for your current stats to detailing recommended CP and clear rewards for stage-by-stage boss raids, you can track your path to maximum efficiency at a glance. 4. Test Your Limits in the Tower of Trials Prove your true strength in our ultimate tower challenge content. As you ascend higher, the enemies grow significantly stronger—but so do the daily rewards that await you at every milestone. 5. Infinite Customization Express your unique identity by freely customizing your character’s appearance with a vast collection of stylish avatar outfits. Amplify the fun with our distinct Pet System, featuring charming companions that accompany you on your adventures. Mix and match your favorite avatar coordinates with adorable pets to establish your own iconic style in WonderKing! 6. Vast End-Game Content & The Joy of Collecting For adventurers who crave continuous challenges, WonderKing offers deep, layered end-game progression. Test your ultimate limits through equipment enhancement and attribute optimization. Dive into a rewarding Collection Book system where you hunt monsters across fields to collect Monster Cards and earn permanent stat increases. Discover a world that perfectly satisfies both the thrill of action and the joy of continuous growth!
