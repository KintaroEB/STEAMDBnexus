About this gameFull description from Steam+
Summoners War: Chronicles is a summoning RPG that explores the conflicts and choices that shaped the world found in the original Summoners War: Sky Arena. Choose one of six Summoners—Orbia, Kina, Cleaf, Soleta, Heath, or Jin—to explore vast continents, summon Monsters, and battle against the darkness seeping through the Rift of Worlds. KEY GAME FEATURES Choose Your Character Each Summoner has a unique role and skill set. Choose the one that suits your playstyle and embark on your journey! Fight Alongside Your Monsters Summon a team from over 550 Monsters and join them on the battlefield for the first time! Action RPG Combat Evade enemy attacks, command your Monsters, and cast spells in real-time combat encounters. Endless Adventure Explore a vast world comprised of multiple continents. Game Modes Delve into loot-filled Dungeons, climb the many-floored Ascensions, and take on Raids with friends! Then head to the Arena to compete against other players! Summoners War Reimagined Experience the expanded universe of Summoners War, and immerse yourself in the untold story of the Rahil Kingdom.
