About this gameFull description from Steam+
Last Man Sitting is an over-the-top roguelite action shooter inspired by the ‘00s when FPS peaked. Pick your loadout, level up, draft Power Ups, and unlock new Chair upgrades while you grind through the workspace and survive against progressively harder waves of sentient office appliances in singleplayer or co-op multiplayer. Found an imba combo? Use it on your friends in the PvP arena mode! Shoot, hack, slash, grind, and draft your way to the top and any leaderboards. Thrive under pressure: Experience intense skill-based grind-and-gun third person action and survive through waves of relentless bot onslaught Manage challenging stakeholders: Take on different enemy types, from grunts to minibosses and finally major bosses Develop a broader skillset: Gain XP to level up and draft from over 200 Power-Ups to unlock new synergies and builds Position yourself for future opportunities: Customize your load-out with Chair Upgrades, different weapons and abilities PvP Strategic alignment sessions: Climb the ladder in the PvP arena mode where you can challenge your peers in a four-player king-of-the-hill tournament style
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

Game includes shooting robot-like NPCs in PVE, and human-like characters during PVP gameplay. There are some mild gun impact splashes and explosions when shooting other characters followed by blood splatter. The player character explodes when dying - which may include explosion of blood on the screen, however game provides option to disable this effect.
