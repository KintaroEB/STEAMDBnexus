About this gameFull description from Steam+
***COLLECT (OR KILL) WILDLIFE FOR REWARDS IN THE NEW UPDATE*** Amazing Frog? is an openworld, sandbox, GTA style version of Swindon. Use bonkers physics to throw ridiculous, farting ragdoll frogs all around town and beyond the city walls. Explore Swindonshire and unlock new worlds, skins, weapons, vehicles and challenges in the Quest for the Magical Mystery Toilet! Start the fun in single player or play up to 4 player split-screen with your friends. Play co-op or combat or just do your own thing - its up to you! Its a Big Big world out there, with lots to discover, you can even explore the moon. Watch out for any dangerous creatures! Also this is a little reminder that not all frogs want to be your friend. It doesnt hurt to get very rich, so load up the Bank of Swindon. Also, you never know what we might add next and what items may come in handy. So, collect everything from berries and other produce, zombie flesh, jam jars, money, skulls, fish or dead pigeons ...EVERYTHING! (… there are so many items you can put in your inventory) Don’t forget to use a vacuum for faster bulk collection! You will find there are lots of ways to earn money in Swindonshire! You can also try driving a Taxi or Collecting trash on 'Bin Day'. Look out for other Jobs around Swindon. If it fits, it can flush! Try Flushing everything to discover the transformative effects of the Magical Mystery Toilet. Repeat flushes can intensify the magic ( just like in real life ).
