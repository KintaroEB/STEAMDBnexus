About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Do you believe in monsters?" ” If the person closest to you, the person you trust most, the person you can't be separated from, the person you share a bed with, the person most important to you, the person most beloved to you, the person fused into your very bones and blood, the person most reliable, the person most indispensable, the person as essential as lips to teeth—were replaced. ... One stormy night, May watches her soaked husband at the door. Rainwater seeps in from under his feet. He smiles, same as ever. But something is different. A black cat back from the dead, surveillance cameras appearing from nowhere, birds gathering everywhere... This sudden wave of strangeness makes it harder and harder to tell reality from hallucination. Meanwhile, the courier who keeps showing up and the female officer investigating alone each bring their own secrets into her life. In this light horror narrative adventure of food, love, and death, players will see through May's eyes, unmask the one who shares her bed, and reach different relationships and endings through their choices. Main Characters [Labyrinth of the Heart] May Her naturally curly hair is the only memento her mother left her. She once wore it long, but perhaps it was too hard to manage, so she took scissors and cut it short herself. She has cut away many things, including memories she wanted to forget completely. But leaving didn't help, and alcohol only offered fleeting blankness in her mind, followed by side effects she couldn't withstand. She has lost far more than she ever bargained for. Her whole life has been a flight, yet she never gets anywhere. [Bones of Love] August He started wearing glasses in sixth grade. They became like an external organ, helping him see the world more clearly. But seeing too clearly isn't always a good thing, so August chose silence, chose to follow blindly. He shut down his senses, refused to solve the obvious problems, and what he got was rot—even his bones became flower food. August (?) Who am I? Where did I come from? Where am I going? He hasn't figured out any of life's three ultimate questions, so He wants a name, a home, a future. Eat a piece of love, and you gain eternal love—or so He believes. [The King Bows, The King Kills] Victor A simple, single-minded, always-smiling rookie courier. He looks like he might be hiding dog ears under his cap. So straightforward he has no filter. A little blood won't leave a mark on his innocent heart—it never has. That heart has always been an open book. Cherish that heart, and be just as honest with him. [Song of the Ivory Tower] Thelma No one knows how Thelma went from state trooper to neighborhood patrol, and no one cares. She tried to start a new life here, but things veered off course the moment she picked up that ID badge. Perhaps she shouldn't have been so stubborn, shouldn't have chased every thread, shouldn't have knocked on that yellow door. Game Features - 240,000+ words of text, approximately 5-6 hours per playthrough. - 3 story branches, 12 endings in total, including 6 character-specific endings. - Over 50 character portrait variations, 40+ storyboard CGs (excluding variations), and 6 story CGs. - Explore the stories of her husband August, Victor the courier, and Thelma the police officer, gradually uncovering their hidden secrets. - Shape May's attitude through dialogue and action choices, alter relationships, and drive the story forward. - Navigate between romance and dread toward May's ending (some of which count as good endings, depending on who defines "good"). Mature Content Description This work contains horror, death, violence, psychological distress, and depictions of eating that may cause discomfort. Please proceed according to your own comfort level. This story is purely fictional. Any resemblance to real persons, events, groups, or organizations is purely coincidental.
