About this gameFull description from Steam+
Upgrade your hacking prowess in this unique "Inaction" RPG/MMORPG.  Optimize your build by engaging with the dozens of stats and over 100 different affixes that can appear on gear.  Try to find the ultimate "Signature Affix" item providing unique gameplay mechanics and enabling builds that utilize unorthodox stat combinations. Join or start your own Syndicate with other players, sharing resources and creating powerful boost mechanics to empower your hacking and gathering activities. Participate in multiple daily community boss events where players fight against a world boss, while others provide support to the combatants to push for new records and powerful temporary buffs based on how many levels of the event are completed. Enjoy the ability to keep making progress while away, as your character will continue to hack virtual opponents and gather additional resources for up to 12 hours after logging out.
