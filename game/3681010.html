About this gameFull description from Steam+
Conquer the Crucible, Become Shogun Nioh 3 is the eagerly anticipated continuation of the dark samurai action RPG series Nioh. A deadly struggle unfolds amidst the devastation of an alternate Warring States period where bandits run rampant and yokai skulk about. Key Elements of the Game: • An open field that challenges you to the fullest This game features an open field filled with the sense of tension characteristic of the "Nioh" series while also allowing you to explore to your heart's content. In this dark samurai world, you will encounter formidable yokai, explore menacing villages where darkness lurks, and struggle against the ominous presence of "the Crucible." • Samurai and Ninja combat styles You can enjoy an instantaneous and seamless switch between two combat styles during battle, facing your enemies head-on with Samurai Style and using quick movements and special techniques with Ninja Style. Note: The product "Nioh 3 Digital Deluxe Edition" is also being sold. Please confirm that you have selected the correct product before making your purchase. Note: These images are taken from an in-development build of the game. The final version may differ. Note: - Demo save data will be automatically carried over upon launching the full game. (The demo save data must be saved on the same device used to play the full game.) - If the full game is started even once and save data is created, you will no longer be able to play the demo. - The demo completion bonus Twin-Snake Helmet, which can be obtained upon completing the demo by February 15, 2026, can also be obtained as a guaranteed item drop upon defeating Jakotsu-baba in the full game by February 15, 2026. Starting February 16, 2026, the item will become a rare drop from Jakotsu-baba.
