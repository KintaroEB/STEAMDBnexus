About this gameFull description from Steam+
"Ah, you scoundrel! Hurry up and study your lessons!" said the parents and put you under house arrest. Try to outsmart your parents and find a way to hang out with your friends. Immerse yourself in the role of a schoolchild whom the parents punished for a bad grade at school, and try to escape from home without being noticed by father and mother. Key mechanics: 3D first-person game. Stealth, hiding, don't get caught, and don't make noise! Puzzles, solve riddles, find items to plan your escape. Be careful, parents may notice open cabinets and doors.
Content & age ratingsSteam content notice · 2 regional ratings+

Content notice

The game presents a story of a strict and abusive family environment. The father is depicted as being intoxicated with a beer bottle, while the parents engage in harsh treatment of their son. Punishments include forcing the child to perform physical exercises, as well as physical abuse such as whipping with a belt and slapping. Mature content, such as the parents’ abusive behavior toward their child, is depicted in specific scenes and during interactions between the child and the parents throughout the storyline.
