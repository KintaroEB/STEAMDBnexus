About this gameFull description from Steam+
Join the adventure and become the master of the metro in a fascinating game that takes you into the mysterious underground world of the Prague capital city. Face challenges on the tracks from the cabin of the historic EČS or 81-71 train. Can you complete the entire route, transport all passengers, and avoid any delays? This game is not an ordinary train simulator. Here, you have complete freedom to choose what you want to do. Become a train driver and improve your skills, explore the underground tunnels, or help your colleagues with various tasks. If, as a conductor, you prove yourself by completing your route on time according to the schedule, you’ll earn some money. You can then spend it on various items. More ways to spend your earned money will appear in future versions. Likewise, future versions will bring more stations, trains, and tasks!
