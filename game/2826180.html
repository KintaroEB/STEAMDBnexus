About this gameFull description from Steam+
Discover Chill Pulse, where productivity meets immersive environments, offering a serene backdrop to your study and work sessions. Journey through time and space, from the vibrant energy of a cyberpunk city to the peaceful solitude of an ancient era, and the cozy confines of a modern cabin. Each setting is designed not just to enhance your focus but to subtly enrich your experience with a narrative essence, seamlessly integrated into your journey of productivity. Features: Varied Study and Work Environments: Select from an array of beautifully crafted scenes, each designed to offer a unique atmosphere that promotes concentration and efficiency, tailored to your personal preference. Integrated Productivity Tools: Make use of a built-in Pomodoro timer and customizable to-do lists to break down your tasks into manageable intervals, optimizing your study and work routines for better results. Ambient Soundscapes: Elevate your focus with a curated selection of music and ambient sounds, designed to complement the mood of each specific environment, aiding in concentration and relaxation. Dynamic Customization and Rewards: Personalize your experience by unlocking new environments, soundtracks, and features as you achieve your study and work objectives, keeping your sessions engaging and rewarding. Narrative Touches: Experience the settings through the presence of a protagonist, whose story unfolds in the background, adding a layer of depth to your productivity journey without overwhelming your focus.
