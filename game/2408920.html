About this gameFull description from Steam+
This is an immersive survival game that immerses players in a dangerous and unknown environment. In this game, you need to explore the vast world, search for food, water sources, and resources, while avoiding wildlife and other potential threats. The world in the game is a beautiful and cruel wilderness, and you need to learn survival skills such as building campsites, making tools, hunting, and gathering. You can cut down trees, fish, plant crops, and even collaborate with other players to tackle environmental challenges together. Main features: Free exploration: The map in the game is very large, and you can freely explore various terrains, from forests to mountains to deserts. Resource management: You need to manage food, water, physical and mental health. Reasonably allocate resources to ensure one's own survival. Dynamic weather: The weather can affect your survival condition. On cold nights, you need to start a fire to keep warm; On hot days, you need to look for shade. Wildlife: When encountering wild animals such as wolves, bears, and deer, you need to be careful when dealing with them. Some animals are friendly, while others will attack you. Multiplayer mode: You can team up with other players to build campsites and explore the world together.
