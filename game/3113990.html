About this gameFull description from Steam+
Our protagonist, Koga Nao, wakes up one morning in high spirits. The birds are singing... and a girl is with him. The girl, Toiro Kirame, he met the previous night at a college drinking party. While he'd only meant to take care of her after she'd had too much to drink, confusion and a gap in her memory leads to her mistakenly assuming their relationship and demanding he take responsibility by going out with her. However, the situation grows complicated when Nao's longtime crush, Nobuta Yua, responds to his many confessions out of nowhere and declares that he's now her boyfriend. While he now finds himself with two girlfriends, this is no joyous occasion. He has to manage his relationships with them without letting anyone know he's two-timing, while also dealing with his ever-changing and evolving friendships. Will he be able to navigate his way out of this sticky, romantic quagmire to reach a happy ending for everyone involved, or will the weight of his dishonest behavior drag him down to his demise? Find out in this messy, unpredictable, and drama-filled romantic comedy! Read through the drama-filled story of Koga Nao and his two girlfriends, Toiro Kirame and Nobuta Yua in this beautiful visual novel. Watch the romance unfold with the lovely standing art and backgrounds. Keep Your Secret! While two-timing both Yua and Kirame, it will be up to you to keep this juicy drama a secret! This is a tense, gripping story like you've never experienced before. Love with a Thrill Even something as simple as a date carries many risks, and compromises may need to be made. Will your mind linger on the girl you're on a date with, or will your thoughts wander to the other heroine and drop your focus?
