About this gameFull description from Steam+
Meh" is an engaging clicker game that puts you in control of collecting a variety of computer models. The premise is straightforward: click on the computer displayed on your screen to earn a random computer model at intervals. You can earn a new computer model every hour you play, adding to your collection, which includes a diverse array of computers ranging from classic desktops to sleek laptops and futuristic gadgets. The challenge lies in amassing all the unique models available, keeping players engaged as they strive to complete their collection. "Meh" offers a blend of casual fun and strategic clicking, making it an ideal choice for anyone looking to unwind while aiming for the ultimate goal of collecting them all!
