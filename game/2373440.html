About this gameFull description from Steam+
Tinker with the best gear and harness the power of intricate rune words to enhance your abilities and overcome formidable challenges. Every battle is an opportunity to showcase your expertise and optimize your loadout, ensuring you're prepared for the diverse range of content that awaits. Forge lasting friendships as you embark on thrilling adventures together. Engage in epic guild content, such as challenging dungeons and exhilarating dragon fights, where cooperation is the key to victory. Unleash your entrepreneurial spirit in the bustling Open Market, a vibrant hub where players can trade equipment and goods. Shape the dynamic in-game economy and get rich as you engage in thrilling transactions. Take a break from epic boss battles and delve into the realm of professions. Engage in the tranquil art of harvesting and fishing, where you can immerse yourself in the beauty of nature. With unique pixel art graphics, our game invites you to embark on a visually stunning adventure. Experience the freshly revamped starting location and explore various enchanting landscapes, from verdant elvish forests to mystical dwarven ruins.
