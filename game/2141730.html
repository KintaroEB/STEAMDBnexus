About this gameFull description from Steam+
Play alone, or team up with friends (or strangers!) in up to 6-player co-op. Enjoy hilarious moments as you watch them die from the horrors you encounter, as proximity voice chat carries their screams down the halls. Encounter dangerous entities from across the Backrooms as you push deeper through each level. Each entity has its own behavior and way of hunting the player. Learn what they react to and how to survive. Explore photorealistic environments, handcrafted to feel unsettling. Life like visuals and spatialized audio remove all barriers between you and the game. Start in the yellow halls of Level 0 as you descend deeper into the Backrooms. Every level brings its own environment, mechanics, and threats to survive. Even within the same level, each attempt brings different layouts, item spawns, and entity encounters. No two runs are the same. Backrooms: Escape Together is a co-op survival horror game featuring lifelike graphics, proximity voice chat, and horrifying entities. You and up to 5 friends must make your way through 11 fully procedurally generated levels using what's available to you to progress deeper into the Backrooms. Every level is a mini-adventure of its own, hosting unique gameplay, puzzles, lootable items, environments, and entities. Levels will be added constantly throughout Early Access as we grow the game into the best multiplayer experience to date.
