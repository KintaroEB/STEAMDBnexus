About this gameFull description from Steam+
HAELE 3D - Feet Poses Pro is an easy to use anatomy drawing reference tool designed for beginner to advanced artists. With a diverse range of editable options, it serves as a resource for learning how to draw human feet. Whether your art style is realistic, stylized, cartoon, anime or anything in between this is easy to use app complements tutorials and aids practicing your foot drawing or sculpting skills from sketch to finish. When it comes to feet that are wrinkled or smooth, slender or meaty, dirty or clean, wet or dry, veiny or not, HAELE 3D has the capability to craft the perfect pair of feet to meet your specifications. HAELE 3D - Feet Poser Pro renders realistic and stylized foot drawing reference poses and animations. Edit every detail down to wrinkles of the soles, nail polish, veins, fingerprints and even calluses. Many built in presets, custom backgrounds, colors, materials, lighting and many more. Features: - 50+ Preset poses - 50+ Preset animations - 30 Preset scenes - Custom posing - 11 Morph targets: weight, smooth, ankle size, ball width, heel width, flat soles, full arch, bunion, toe length, longer second toe, toe roundness, nail length - Editable skin material: wrinkles, dirt, wet, veins, details, reflectivity, tone, saturation, variation - Colors: preset skin colors, grayscale and skin tones, fully editable color regions - Nails: length, shine, tone, polish colors - Custom image backgrounds - Editable lights: 3 Point light (color, intensity transformation), Environment light (HDRI map, intensity), Ground light (color, intensity) - Environment: HDRI studio backgrounds, various environments, solid colors, - Floor on/off, color, reflectivity - Adjustable camera angle, location, position and field of view - Save and load scenes - Load presets
