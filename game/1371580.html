About this gameFull description from Steam+
Embark on an Epic Journey – Experience the Rich Tapestry of Diverse Cultures In a vast world forged by the Zhongzhou, Dongzhou, and Xizhou, nature’s wonders awaken as day turns to night. Discover dynamic ecosystems and immerse yourself in unique cultural traditions that promise an unforgettable adventure. Confront the mighty Jiuzhou, Hellas, and Shah civilizations, and choose your own path of development to master your strengths and shape your destiny. Unleash Your Creativity and Forge Your Empire Experience unparalleled sandbox freedom where diverse civilizations boast unique weapons, armor, and architecture. With over 1,300 crafting recipes at your disposal, create an empire that stands apart and opens up a world of endless possibilities. Recruit NPCs, Tame Wildlife Subdue and recruit NPCs, horses, animals large and small, and elite Beast Lords. Myth of Empires’ deep taming and breeding systems allow you to cultivate an army of man and beast that will help you with both combat and transportation, and aid you in your journey to becoming emperor. Fight Epic Battles on Land, Air, and Sea Myth of Empires features hundreds of different weapons, armor, and siege equipment from multiple civilizations. Construct massive fortresses to defend your territory, or take your armies to the battlefield and immerse yourself in thrilling ancient warfare! Engage in Cross-server Battles Establish administrative regions, fight for the position of county magistrate, and compete to expand your territory through Fortress Battles. Craft the perfect guild and utilize your diplomatic prowess to ensure its long-term stability. Diverse Civilization Growth Paths – Write Your Own Legend Choose from 25 unique development paths, leverage a 16-tier Nobility system, and unlock over 100 achievements. Our brand-new profession change system lays the foundation for a truly one-of-a-kind experience. Each civilization brings its own unique strengths, putting the power of choice in your hands! Play Multiple Gameplay Modes Offering Endless Possibilities Myth of Empires supports official servers, custom servers, as well as solo and hosted modes. Creative players are also able to use the game’s robust mod editor to tailor the game to their dream experience.
