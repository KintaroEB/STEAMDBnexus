About this gameFull description from Steam+
Build Your Own Empire! Empire City will amaze you with its incredible details and stunning graphics. Enjoy the views of luxurious residential areas, majestic temples, grand forums, and magnificent wonders of the world, creating a unique atmosphere of the ancient world. Gather Resources: Everything you need to build your city lies right beneath your feet. Deep in the Earth are valuable minerals and raw materials for construction. Extract marble, clay, wood, and process metal to create all the necessary resources. Utilize ancient technologies and knowledge to efficiently manage production and infrastructure. And don’t forget about trading in Empire City! Be a master in both construction and commerce! Complete Tasks, Collect Taxes, and Increase Population: Complete exciting tasks to earn generous rewards and progress more easily through the game’s storyline. Taxes will help you earn more: the larger your city's population, the more taxes you can collect! Increase your budget to develop the city and construct new buildings. Implement ancient technology innovations to boost efficiency and growth, extract valuable minerals, and build luxurious palaces! Connect with Friends: City development is better done together. Create a community of like-minded individuals, chat, exchange resources, and provide friendly support. Participate in competitions and win excellent prizes! Complete engaging quests as your ancient city grows Build unique structures and magnificent ancient landmarks Earn achievements and rewards Develop everything: from agriculture to the construction of great wonders of the world Use ancient technologies to enhance infrastructure Build your unique ancient empire!
