About this gameFull description from Steam+
Tangy TD: Hero Tower Defense, Class Based, Roguelite, Item Combining Strategic tower defense where you play a witch, place class-based towers, equip them with items that grant powerful abilities, combine them, create unique builds & deal ridiculous amounts of damage! Customize your builds with a deep stat system and giant skill tree! Move around Towers, Fight Big Bosses! Spend Skillpoints earned by defeating enemies in a giant Skilltree. Equip and Combine over 100 different items. Key Features: Three classes with unique skill sets. Defender, Archer and Healer. A Skill Tree for each class to progress through and grow more powerful. Endless Mode. Test your strategy against waves of enemies that become exponentially more difficult.
