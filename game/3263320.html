About this gameFull description from Steam+
Bring the glass to the customer! As two construction workers, you are transporting a glass window for a tall structure. On this journey you have to be careful with the glass because it is very fragile. Can you make it to the top without breaking it? Multiplayer: Get help from a friend to carry the glass! Invite a trusted friend to your lobby to carry the glass with you! Characters with physics We've added physics to every part of the characters to improve the gameplay! Levels Skyscraper & Lighthouse Game Modes: Normal, Hard (less checkpoints), Impossible (no checkpoints) If you want a more casual experience, you should try Normal mode. But if you believe you can make it to the top without breaking the glass, then hard mode or the impossible mode might be for you!
