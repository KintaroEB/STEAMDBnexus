Let's Patiti!

"Let's Patiti!" is a multiplayer party game that supports 2-4 players and can be played online or locally. In the game, you take on the role of an adorable little cat, engaging in a game of chase and cunning competition with your "Meow Meow" friends or AI in the "Meow Meow Forest".
